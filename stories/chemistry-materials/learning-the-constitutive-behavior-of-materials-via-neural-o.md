A data-driven constitutive modeling framework uses neural operators with causal attention to learn how materials deform and stress without requiring knowledge of internal state variables. Rather than specifying evolution equations for unobservable internal states, the approach treats materials as functional mappings from complete strain history to stress response. The model trains on full loading paths as function-to-function mappings, predicting complete stress trajectories in parallel. Causally masked attention restricts the model to consider only past material states while maintaining computational parallelizability. Spectral convolutions provide discretization-invariant frequency representations, and sinusoidal activations resolve strong nonlinear transitions in material behavior.
