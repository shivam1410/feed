Researchers built coding agents that solve complex robotic tasks, then used those solutions to train simpler robot learning models. The agents tackled problems requiring up to 30 minutes of continuous interaction, and a model trained on their generated demos completed long-horizon tasks on real robots. This creates a path where AI coders teach physical robots what to do.
