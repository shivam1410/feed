They diagnosed omni-modal agents' core bottleneck: planning fails because noisy multimodal observations clutter the dialogue history. Omni-Decision replaces messy history with an explicit evidence ledger tracking what's missing, confirmed, and conflicting. On OmniGAIA it reaches 81.4 percent accuracy at 43 percent of Gemini's cost. Clean reasoning from a compact ledger beats sprawling context.
