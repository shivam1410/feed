AutoCause automates causal discovery in environmental time series by recording expert decisions, providing principled defaults, and allowing domain overrides. It wraps four causal-discovery methods and grades links by method consensus. Tested on 145 datasets, majority-supported links proved more precise than single-method results on synthetic benchmarks, converting inconsistent expert practice into auditable, repeatable analysis while keeping causal interpretation with analysts.
