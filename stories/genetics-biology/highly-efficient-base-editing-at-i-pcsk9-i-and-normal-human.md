Researchers achieved highly efficient base editing at the PCSK9 gene in human embryos while maintaining normal development. Base editing precisely changes individual DNA letters without creating permanent DNA breaks. This work demonstrates feasibility of targeting disease genes with high efficiency in early human development. The ability to edit genes efficiently in embryos while preserving normal developmental processes opens possibilities for addressing genetic diseases.
