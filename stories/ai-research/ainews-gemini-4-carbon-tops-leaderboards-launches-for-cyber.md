Google DeepMind launched Gemini 4 Carbon, and it now takes the top spot on the Artificial Analysis benchmark. Its claimed cyber scores include 88.2% on SEC-bench Pro for finding vulnerabilities and 71.0% on CWE-bench for patching. For now, access is limited to trusted cyber defenders through the Fairwind Program. Wider developer access is promised as soon as possible, with no firm date. Pricing is murky, with $4/$20 standard pricing after a 50% launch discount. That matters because the strongest cyber model goes to vetted defenders first.
