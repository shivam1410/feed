Periodic Labs wants AI to discover new materials by running real experiments. The company launched last September. Its approach, which it calls synthesis superintelligence, uses reinforcement learning grounded in physical experiments. It pairs that with high-throughput labs, materials characterization, and simulations. The team argues that scientific discovery differs from math and coding. It also says failed experiments can be some of the most valuable training data.
