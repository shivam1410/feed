Over 1,000 employees from frontier AI labs (OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepMind, Meta, others) cosigned a letter requesting international cooperation to deliberately pace AI development. The letter warns that automating AI research could accelerate capability development beyond humanity's ability to understand or control resulting systems. Signatories request U.S. government support for international mechanisms managing pacing. They acknowledge competitive pressure prevents individual companies from slowing unilaterally. This matters because frontier developers themselves are expressing safety concerns about acceleration outpacing control mechanisms, making governance an urgent challenge.
