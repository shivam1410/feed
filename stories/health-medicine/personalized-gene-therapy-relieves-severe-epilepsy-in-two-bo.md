Personalized gene therapy has successfully treated severe, previously intractable epilepsy in two boys by selectively switching off one copy of a disease-causing gene. Remarkably, one child gained the ability to walk independently for the first time following treatment. The therapy targets individual genetic mutations causing disease, representing precision medicine applied to severe neurological disorders. This breakthrough demonstrates gene therapy's potential extends beyond controlling seizures to enabling profound functional improvements that transform patients' lives and independence, moving beyond symptom management toward restoration of lost capabilities.
