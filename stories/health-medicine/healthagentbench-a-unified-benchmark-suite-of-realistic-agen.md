HealthAgentBench presents a comprehensive evaluation framework with 54 healthcare tasks distributed across 7 categories to assess AI agent capabilities in complex clinical workflows. The benchmark reveals significant challenges where agents struggle: medical imaging analysis and compositional reasoning tasks requiring multiple clinical concepts. Conversely, the evaluation shows promise in electronic health record (EHR) data analysis tasks. By providing structured, realistic healthcare scenarios, the benchmark enables systematic assessment of frontier AI agents in medically relevant settings. These findings identify where AI agents need improvement before clinical deployment and where current architectures show sufficient capability for healthcare applications.
