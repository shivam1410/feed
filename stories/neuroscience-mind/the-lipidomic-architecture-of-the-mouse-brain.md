Researchers created a spatial lipidomic atlas of the mouse brain, revealing over 500 distinct biochemical territories mirroring the brain's cell types and neural connectivity. The atlas captures oligodendrocyte heterogeneity and ventricular zonation patterns. Notably, these lipid architectures remodel during pregnancy. This comprehensive map reveals how lipids organize in three-dimensional brain space, potentially explaining how lipid composition relates to neural function, health, and vulnerability to neurological disease.
