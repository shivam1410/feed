DriveZero combines a vision foundation model for perception with reinforcement learning for action selection in autonomous driving. Using closed-loop RL training, the system learns driving behaviors exceeding the scope of human demonstrations. This approach overcomes purely imitation-based limitations and advances autonomous driving capabilities.
