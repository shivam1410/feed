Using the James Webb Space Telescope, astronomers analyzed the atmosphere of WD 1856 b, an unusual terrestrial planet orbiting a white dwarf rather than a normal star. Transmission spectroscopy revealed hydrocarbons and aerosols in its atmosphere, constraining the planet's mass and thermal evolution. This discovery opens observational opportunities for studying planetary atmospheres around evolved white dwarf stars, previously inaccessible to detailed study, advancing understanding of planetary survival around different stellar types.
