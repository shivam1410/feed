The protein SIRT7 plays a critical role in maintaining female chromosome stability. In females, X-chromosome inactivation (dosage compensation) balances X-chromosome gene expression with the rest of the genome; SIRT7 safeguards this balance. The protein protects X-chromosome integrity and ensures proper dosage compensation between the X chromosome and autosomes. Loss of SIRT7 function disrupts this balance, compromising chromosomal stability and cellular function. This identifies SIRT7 as a key guardian of sex-chromosome stability in females, with implications for understanding genetic imbalances and disorders linked to X-chromosome dosage defects.
