An all-optical photonic time crystal was created by delivering intense terahertz light pulses onto specially engineered material hosting collective electron waves called plasmons. This approach rapidly and strongly modulates optical properties like refractive index in real time, achieving ultrafast temporal control of light behavior. Dynamic reshaping of optical properties enables new possibilities for signal processing at terahertz frequencies, pushing photonic technologies toward faster and more flexible systems.
