Researchers built a system that generates diverse training environments for language-model agents before defining outcome rules. They created 3,300 such environments for just a few cents each and trained a Qwen model on them, improving its agentic score from 0.204 to 0.815. The gains transferred to unseen problem types and external benchmarks, showing this matters because scaling agent training currently requires expensive custom environments and careful evaluation setup.
