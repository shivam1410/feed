China is now leading the world in testing next-generation CAR-T therapies. These engineered immune cell treatments are becoming a major frontier in cancer care, and China's faster pace of clinical trials gives them a significant advantage over other competing nations. That said, new regulatory changes could slow their momentum and level the playing field, reducing the advantage that speed currently provides.
