AMD acquired World Labs for 8.2 billion dollars, gaining Atlas, a model generating novel camera views from still images that solves sparse reconstruction in computer vision. Atlas outperforms specialized models and applies to robotics simulation and design. This matters because sparse 3D reconstruction is a bottleneck for embodied AI, design tools, and digital twins.
