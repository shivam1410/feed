Northern Hemisphere conifer trees planted in the Southern Hemisphere demonstrate unexpected adaptability, showing increased stem growth and physiological signals indicating reduced environmental stress. This suggests climate-appropriate tree species may thrive across hemispheres despite their evolutionary origin, potentially expanding reforestation options in regions facing tree loss. The findings have implications for large-scale afforestation strategies and understanding tree adaptation.
