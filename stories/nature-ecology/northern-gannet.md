Morris is a Northern Gannet (Morus bassanus), the only known individual of this Atlantic species in the Pacific Ocean. Morris appeared in the Farallon Islands 14 years ago and now resides in Pillar Point Harbor, California. Identifiable as the sole white bird with a yellow head in the harbor, Morris frequents areas near the commercial pier with Brandt's cormorants, becoming a local celebrity easily spotted by visitors.
