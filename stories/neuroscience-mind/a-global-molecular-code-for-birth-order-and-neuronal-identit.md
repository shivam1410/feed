A high-resolution developmental transcriptional atlas of the Drosophila melanogaster nerve cord reveals a shared molecular code that records the birth order of individual neurons and organizes neuronal diversity. By analyzing genome-wide transcription patterns across development, researchers identified genetic signatures that correlate with when neurons are born and determine their functional identity. This transcriptional code provides a biological "timestamp" embedded in gene expression that links developmental timing to neuronal properties. Understanding how birth order is encoded at the molecular level clarifies fundamental mechanisms of neural development, organizational principles in the nervous system, and how cellular diversity emerges from temporal sequences of birth.
