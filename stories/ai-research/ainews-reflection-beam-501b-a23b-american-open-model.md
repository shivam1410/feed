Reflection has launched Beam, a new text-only model with 501B total and 23B active. It is built for coding, agentic work, and science. The team says it was trained from scratch on 23.8 trillion tokens, partly from OCR of hundreds of millions of PDFs. Full weights under Apache 2.0 are due this month. Reflection compares it to Inkling, Nemotron, and GLM 5.2, but newer models like GLM 5.3 and Kimi K3 are generally ahead. Why it matters: it's a US-trained option for people who have been waiting on more open models.
