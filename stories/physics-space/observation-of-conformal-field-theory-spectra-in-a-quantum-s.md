Scientists using a quantum simulator—an array of optically trapped neutral atoms at a quantum phase transition—directly observed the universal excitation spectrum predicted by conformal field theory. This experimental confirmation validates decades of theoretical predictions and demonstrates quantum simulators' power for probing fundamental physics. The achievement bridges abstract theory and measurable laboratory observations, opening new pathways for testing quantum phenomena previously accessible only through mathematics, advancing fundamental physics and quantum computing.
