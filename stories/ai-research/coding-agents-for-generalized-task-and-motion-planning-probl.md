Researchers tested whether AI coding agents could solve a tough robotics problem: planning tasks where discrete decisions must respect geometric constraints. They evaluated Claude Code and other models on 28 simulated environments, generating 980 programs tested on 100 unseen instances each. The agents achieved 56% to 95% success compared to 47% for hand-engineered planners. Surprisingly, as problems got harder with more objects, the AI-generated programs stayed more reliable than expert code.
