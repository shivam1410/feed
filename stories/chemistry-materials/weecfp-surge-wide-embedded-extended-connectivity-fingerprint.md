WEECFP is a parameter-free 1024-dimensional molecular fingerprint with WEECFP-SuRGE transformer architecture using graph-distance-aware attention. The approach achieves top results on molecular property prediction: ranked #1 on five TDC ADMET tasks without external pretraining and outperforms classical fingerprints on MoleculeNet regression. The fingerprint is nearly lossless, recovering exact SMILES for 99.9% of in-distribution molecules and 98.93% cross-dataset. Structured, interpretable representations match or exceed black-box approaches.
