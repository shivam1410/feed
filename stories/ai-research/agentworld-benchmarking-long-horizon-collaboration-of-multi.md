AgentWorld is a benchmark for testing how well multi-agent LLM teams collaborate on long, complex tasks. It has 100 human-annotated tasks across 50-plus interaction rounds in an MMORPG. The best model achieved only 52 percent task success. Common failures included communication breakdowns and role confusion. This matters because it reveals how AI agents actually coordinate, not just how they perform individually.
