High-resolution satellite mapping reveals seagrass meadows worldwide experienced significant loss and degradation since 2019. Most remaining seagrass ecosystems fall outside protected marine areas, leaving them vulnerable to continued damage from coastal development and climate change. Findings underscore urgent need to expand marine conservation and integrate seagrass protection into climate policies, as these ecosystems sequester carbon and provide essential marine habitat.
