Researchers showed modern LLMs can work asynchronously, processing new inputs while reasoning or acting, unlike the sequential read-think-act cycles they typically follow. They built a framework with overlapping memory states and tested Qwen 3.x on streaming video understanding, videogames, and monitoring tasks without any retraining. The models adapted to true concurrency.
