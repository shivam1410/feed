Gene expression patterns uniquely identify neuron cell types and predict their long-range projection targets within distant brain regions. In the mouse visual cortex, multimodal analysis linked transcriptomic profiles to neuronal morphology, electrophysiology, and connectivity of excitatory neurons. This integration shows that a neuron's molecular identity—its expressed genes—directly encodes its anatomical circuit role and projection destinations. The finding bridges molecular biology and circuit neuroscience, suggesting that cell-type identity deterministically shapes neural connectivity and function.
