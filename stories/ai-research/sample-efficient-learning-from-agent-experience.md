Experience Distillation internalizes agent interaction histories into model weights without further environment interaction. On 749 software-engineering tasks and six text-adventure games, it retains at least 64.8% of in-context learning gains, far exceeding direct supervised fine-tuning (3.8%). Experience Distillation followed by in-context learning from trial-and-error matches classical reinforcement-learning baselines with at least 9.6x fewer environment samples, making it practical for sample-constrained agent learning scenarios.
