Scientists have developed all-perovskite tandem solar modules using nanocrystal-tailored recombination mechanisms. This approach enhances the efficiency of photovoltaic devices by optimizing how charge carriers recombine within the perovskite layers. The advancement represents progress toward more efficient solar energy conversion using tandem multi-junction architecture with perovskite materials, potentially improving commercial viability of perovskite-based solar technology.
