A diffusion-based atmospheric reconstruction system uses composable observation interfaces to combine sparse radiosonde, aircraft, and dense surface-station data. The combined R+A+S interface reduced RMSE by 9.24% against ERA5 across all 13 atmospheric variables over CONUS compared to radiosonde-only conditioning. Interfaces were developed on 2019 data and evaluated throughout 2020 without retuning.
