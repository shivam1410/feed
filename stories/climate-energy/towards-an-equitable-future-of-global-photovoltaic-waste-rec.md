Global photovoltaic waste will reach 297–402 million tonnes by 2060, requiring regionally adapted recycling strategies and international cooperation for equitable, scalable PV waste circularity. Current recycling infrastructure cannot handle this volume of material from proliferating solar installations. Comprehensive waste management frameworks addressing environmental sustainability and resource recovery are essential as renewable energy deployment accelerates globally.
