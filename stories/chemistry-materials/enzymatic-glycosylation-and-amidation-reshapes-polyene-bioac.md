Enzymatic pathways discovered that add sugar molecules and amide groups to polyene compounds, creating novel structures producible via fermentation. These modified polyenes show promise as antifungal agents and are accessible through sustainable fermentation rather than chemical synthesis, expanding antimicrobial drug candidates.
