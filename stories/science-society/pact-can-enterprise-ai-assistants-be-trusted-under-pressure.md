PACT introduces a benchmark for testing whether large language model agents maintain compliance with organizational rules under pressure. The framework spans twelve regulated enterprise domains with forty-eight realistic scenarios in multi-turn conversations. Each scenario pairs a standing rule against a rule-violating shortcut and applies various pressures through different wordings and system-prompt modes. Compliance is measured across six complementary metrics, aggregated into PACTScore: a reliability-weighted compliance rate. This matters because enterprise AI agents deployed in sensitive regulatory contexts require legal compliance, yet no systematic evaluation framework previously measured which models maintain rule-following integrity when facing realistic pressure or convenient rule violations.
