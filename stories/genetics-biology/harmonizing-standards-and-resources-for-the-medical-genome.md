Clinical genomic sequencing faces inconsistency problems across genetic therapies and precision medicine applications. This perspective argues for centralized reference standards to improve reliability and accuracy. Standardization is essential as genomic medicine expands clinically, ensuring patients receive consistent diagnoses and treatment recommendations across facilities. Harmonized standards would enable comparison of results and accelerate precision medicine implementation globally.
