Connectomics mapping combined with electrophysiology and computational modeling of electric fish cerebellum-like neural circuits reveals how synaptic plasticity distributed across multiple network layers enables fast, accurate, noise-resistant sensory learning. This distributed learning strategy differs from centralized approaches. The principles governing efficient neural learning in fish may generalize across species and inform artificial intelligence design.
