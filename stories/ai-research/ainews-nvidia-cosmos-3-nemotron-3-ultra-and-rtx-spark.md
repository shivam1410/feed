NVIDIA released Cosmos 3, an omnimodal model unifying language, image, video, audio, and action in a Mixture-of-Transformers architecture. The family spans Nano (16B: 8B reasoner + 8B generator) and Super (64B: 32B + 32B) configurations with specialized Text2Image and Image2Video finetunes achieving new open-weight SOTA in image and video generation. Simultaneously, NVIDIA announced Nemotron 3 Ultra, a 550B-A55B parameter open-weight LLM claiming new US SOTA. RTX Spark, a 1-petaflop personal supercomputer, was also previewed with launch partners including Microsoft.
