AlphaGenome Atlas represents a comprehensive database that predicts the functional effects of every possible single nucleotide variant in the human genome. This systematic mapping captures how individual DNA changes influence biological function across all human genetic variation. Such a resource enables researchers to prioritize genetic variants associated with disease, understand population genetic diversity, and accelerate discovery of gene-phenotype relationships across the complete human genetic landscape.
