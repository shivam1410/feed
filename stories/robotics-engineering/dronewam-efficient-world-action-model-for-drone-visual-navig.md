Researchers developed DroneWAM, a prediction model that helps drones anticipate how their actions change visual observations. Using a JEPA architecture with adaptive rollout, it reduced computation from eight steps to 4.58 while improving accuracy. This efficiency is crucial for drones operating under tight power and computational constraints.
