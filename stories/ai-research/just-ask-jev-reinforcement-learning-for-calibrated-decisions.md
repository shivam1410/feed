Jev is an AI model trained with reinforcement learning to detect when language models misbehave. It answers multiple questions about one input in a single pass, scoring alignment failures in ten categories: sycophancy, jailbreaks, deception, hallucination, and others. Testing achieved 0.886 median AUROC zero-shot, beating supervised baselines. This matters because catching AI failures before deployment protects users from harmful outputs.
