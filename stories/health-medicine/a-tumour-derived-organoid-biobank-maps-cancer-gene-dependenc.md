Researchers developed a clinically annotated multi-omic organoid biobank from 256 patients across 5 cancer types, with matched patient tumor samples. The organoid collection includes genomic, transcriptomic, and proteomic data, enabling systematic mapping of gene dependencies and identification of subtype-specific therapeutic vulnerabilities. By tracking organoid evolution under treatment, the study reveals how cancers respond and adapt to therapy. This resource combines the physiological relevance of patient-derived organoids with comprehensive molecular profiling, creating a powerful platform for understanding cancer biology and discovering precision oncology strategies.
