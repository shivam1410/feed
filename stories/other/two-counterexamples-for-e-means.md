e-means (Bessa et al., 2026) combines exponential averaging and cluster revival. We give two counterexamples to its published recipe with higher final loss than standard k-means (Lloyd, 1982) from identical initial centers. Mass-based revival can destroy a global optimum and make the ratio of expected losses unbounded even with k-means++ seeding.
