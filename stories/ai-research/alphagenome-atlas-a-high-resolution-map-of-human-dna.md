DeepMind built AlphaGenome Atlas, a vast database that predicts the effects of every possible single letter change in human DNA on disease and traits. The atlas covers the entire human genome's three billion genetic variants systematically. This gives researchers and doctors a complete, searchable map of genetic variation and exactly how each variant affects human health, disease risk, and traits.
