Proteomic analysis of bone fragments and teeth from Southwest China identified multiple Denisovan remains and revealed details about their evolution and physical characteristics. Ancient protein sequences extracted from the fossils confirmed Denisovan identity and provided genetic insights into their phenotype and evolutionary relationships. The discovery fills a significant geographical gap in Denisovan distribution documentation across Asia. This matters because it expands knowledge of when and where this extinct human species lived, illuminating human evolution patterns and demonstrating how protein analysis can identify archaic human remains even when DNA is degraded or absent.
