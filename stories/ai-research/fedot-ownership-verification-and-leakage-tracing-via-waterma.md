Federated latent diffusion models enable collaborative model training but face watermark attacks and model theft threats. FedOT introduces chunked watermarking and latent vector transformation to enable ownership verification and trace model leaks while resisting watermark removal. This approach maintains model performance while protecting intellectual property. As collaborative AI training scales, protecting federated model ownership prevents malicious actors from removing copyright traces and claiming unauthorized ownership.
