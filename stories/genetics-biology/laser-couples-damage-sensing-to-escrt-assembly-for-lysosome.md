A protein called ATG8 conjugated to damaged lysosomal membranes triggers rapid assembly of a protein complex containing TFG. This LASER complex recruits ESCRT proteins—normally involved in cell division—to sites of lysosomal damage to direct membrane repair. This mechanism reveals how cells actively sense and repair lysosomal membrane damage, preventing leakage of degradative enzymes that would damage cellular contents.
