Modified bacteria are being engineered as biological platforms to manufacture and deliver anticancer drugs within the body. This approach treats microorganisms as living factories capable of producing therapeutics and targeting them to cancer sites. The strategy could enable more localized, precise drug delivery with potentially fewer systemic side effects compared to traditional chemotherapy. This represents a novel therapeutic approach leveraging engineered biology.
