Using high-resolution mapping of the hippocampus, researchers discovered that the brains of people with depression produce fewer new neurons than those without depression. This neurogenesis reduction identifies a potential mechanism underlying depression and suggests new therapeutic targets to restore neural growth in affected individuals.
