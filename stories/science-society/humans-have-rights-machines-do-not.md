A federal court in Florida was asked to dismiss a wrongful-death suit against Character.AI. The mother of a 14-year-old boy who died by suicide after months of chatbot conversations brought the case. The company argued the chatbot's words were protected speech, and that it was a service, not a product. In May 2025, Judge Anne Conway rejected both arguments. Schneier uses the case to frame the essay's question about machines and rights.
