Anthropic's run-rate revenue—an annualized projection of current monthly revenue—hit $47 billion following their Series H funding announcement. This represents explosive growth: the company moved from approximately $9 billion at year-end 2025 to $14 billion in February 2026, then $30 billion by April, and $47 billion recently. Run-rate revenue has grown roughly 10x annually over the past three years. Axios CEO Jim VandeHei noted that no company in any industry or era has scaled organic revenue this quickly at this scale, reflecting unprecedented enterprise adoption of Anthropic's Claude AI model.
