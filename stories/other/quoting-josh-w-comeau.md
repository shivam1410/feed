Course creator Josh W. Comeau reports his latest course tracking to sell roughly one-third of typical launch copies, with revenue down significantly compared to last year. Multiple course creators report 50%+ revenue declines with fewer people engaging with content. Comeau attributes the downturn to dual AI pressure: people worried about developer job stability avoid spending on skill development, while LLMs provide free personalized tutoring, reducing incentive for paid courses. LLMs also train on creator content without consent or compensation, further eroding the economic foundation of professional educational content.
