Microsoft released MAI-Thinking-1 (1T parameters, 35B active, reasoning-focused) and MAI-Code-1-Flash (137B parameters, 5B active, GitHub Copilot optimized). Both trained from-scratch on commercially licensed data without third-party distillation (though this claim requires verification per embedded updates). MAI-Thinking-1 reportedly preferred over Sonnet 4.6 in blind human evaluation. These compact, specialist models represent Microsoft's strategy delivering high performance with lower inference costs through efficient, licensed-data pretraining.
