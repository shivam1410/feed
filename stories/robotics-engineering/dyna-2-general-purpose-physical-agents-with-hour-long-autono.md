Dyna Robotics has a robot that folded restaurant napkins for 24 hours straight at a 99.4% success rate. The model behind it, Dyna-2, was pre-trained on one million hours of human video. It combines predicting future video with robot actions. The team says robots can learn new tasks from as little as 45 minutes of demonstrations. At Din Tai Fung, its robots fold thousands of napkins daily. The real challenge, the company argues, is reliable, long-horizon autonomy in the real world.
