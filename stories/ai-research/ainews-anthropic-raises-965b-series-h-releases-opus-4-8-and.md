Anthropic raised $65 billion in Series H funding at a $900 billion pre-money valuation, with revenue run-rate reaching $47 billion (up from $9 billion in December). The company released Opus 4.8, addressing community concerns about prior versions and achieving state-of-the-art performance on economically significant benchmarks. More significantly, Anthropic introduced Dynamic Workflows (ultracode), a massively parallel feature in Claude Code enabling coordinated multi-agent orchestration. The feature's power is exemplified by Jarred Sumner's rewrite of Bun from Zig to Rust—750,000 lines of code in just six days. This advancement suggests AI-assisted development workflows may fundamentally accelerate software engineering timelines, moving complex refactors from months to days.
