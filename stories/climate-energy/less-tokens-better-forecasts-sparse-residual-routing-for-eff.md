Sparse-Reslim is a parameter-free routing module routing only 25% of spatial tokens through expensive transformer blocks in weather forecasting while maintaining full grid resolution. Routed tokens contribute residual deltas while unselected tokens preserve pre-routing values exactly, eliminating grid dropout. Tested across ERA5 resolutions and deterministic and diffusion model families.
