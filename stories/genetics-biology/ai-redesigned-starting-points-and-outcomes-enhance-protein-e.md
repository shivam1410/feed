Researchers developed a workflow using artificial intelligence to redesign protein scaffolds before directed evolution, creating enzymes with superior properties compared to those evolved from natural wild-type proteins. This hybrid approach combines computational protein design with evolutionary optimization, demonstrating that AI-optimized starting points overcome limitations of natural evolution. The method accelerates development of engineered proteins with improved functions for biotechnology applications.
