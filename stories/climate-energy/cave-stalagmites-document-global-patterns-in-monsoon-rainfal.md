Cave stalagmites reveal a 450,000-year record of monsoon rain. A stalagmite record from the Amazon Basin, combined with existing Asian records, shows the global monsoon system swinging regularly. It strengthens in one hemisphere while weakening in the other. During warm, warming climates, monsoons in both hemispheres failed for thousands of years.
