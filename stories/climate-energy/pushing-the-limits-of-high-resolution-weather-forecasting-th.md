BaguanHR enables 0.1° global weather forecasting by synthesizing high-resolution training data from coarse ERA5 reanalysis via variable-wise super-resolution. On synthetic-plus-real datasets, it outperforms both ML methods and IFS-HRES across 85%+ of lead times within 72 hours. Data scaling follows power-law: doubling data reduces RMSE by 4.6% for 72-hour and 4.9% for 120-hour forecasts. Results confirm that high-resolution ML forecasting is primarily data-constrained rather than model-constrained, with super-resolution providing a simple general solution to unlock decades of coarse-resolution reanalysis.
