Climate forecasters predict an exceptionally large El Niño event will occur by a "mind-blowing margin," potentially the largest on record. When combined with ongoing global warming trends, this climate pattern is expected to push global temperatures in 2027 to unprecedented heights, breaking existing records. El Niño naturally warms the planet by redistributing ocean-atmosphere heat, and when coupled with anthropogenic climate change, the combined effect produces compounding temperature increases. This convergence signals heightened climate risks for 2027, including potential impacts on weather patterns, agriculture, and ecosystems worldwide. The forecast underscores the intensifying interplay between natural climate variability and human-caused warming.
