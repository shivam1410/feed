GOOSE enables the systematic design and testing of thousands of distinct disordered protein region sequences to reveal sequence-to-function relationships. Disordered proteins, which lack fixed three-dimensional structures, play important roles in cell signaling and regulation. By exploring sequence space through design and testing, researchers identify which amino acid patterns produce specific functions. This approach accelerates functional prediction and protein engineering by establishing clear connections between sequence composition and biological activity, advancing synthetic biology and therapeutic protein development.
