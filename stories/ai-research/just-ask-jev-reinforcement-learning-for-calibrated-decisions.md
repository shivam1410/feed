Researchers built Jev, a model trained with reinforcement learning that answers many questions about one input with calibrated probability confidence in a single call. Tested on ten AI safety failures across 44 benchmarks and five models, it achieved median AUROC of 0.886 zero-shot and beat supervised baselines. This helps detect when deployed AI systems fail safety tests.
