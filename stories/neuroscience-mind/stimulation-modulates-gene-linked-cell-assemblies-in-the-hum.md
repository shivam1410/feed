Electrical stimulation of human cortex reorganizes neural cell assemblies and induces cell-type-specific gene regulatory programs. This framework linking stimulation parameters to circuit-level and transcriptional responses provides a foundation for designing targeted neuromodulatory therapies tailored to specific brain circuits and cellular mechanisms.
