Researchers discovered that language models can literally hold two thoughts at once. When you feed in text from two different sources combined together, the model outputs a superposition of what it would say for each one separately. They call this Superposition Linearity. Through lightweight fine-tuning, they restored this ability and created a decoding trick that extracts two coherent completions in a single pass. This reveals fundamental architecture properties transformers use.
