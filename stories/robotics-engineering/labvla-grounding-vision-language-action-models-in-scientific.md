LabVLA is a vision-language-action model trained to automate complex laboratory tasks through advanced visual understanding and robotic control. It employs a two-stage training approach combining action token pretraining with flow matching techniques. The model leverages both simulated data generation and robot-specific learning to achieve superior performance on lab automation benchmarks. Laboratory automation demands precise understanding of visual scenes, identification of objects and procedures, and appropriate action selection in context. LabVLA addresses these challenges through its specialized architecture that integrates vision, language understanding, and action planning. By effectively bridging these three modalities, the approach enables robots to understand and execute scientific laboratory procedures with improved accuracy and reliability compared to previous methods.
