Inflammation triggers DNA interactions between different chromosomes that produce chimeric mRNAs—hybrid molecular sequences encoding functional proteins relevant to immune response. Researchers discovered that interchromosomal DNA proximity facilitates this chimeric RNA formation during inflammation, revealing a previously unknown mechanism by which the immune system responds to inflammatory signals through creation of novel functional hybrid proteins.
