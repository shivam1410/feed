Proteomic analysis of bone fragments and teeth from Southwest China identifies multiple Denisovan remains revealing details about evolution and physical characteristics. Ancient protein analysis determined evolutionary relationships and phenotypic features of these extinct hominins. The findings fill significant geographical gaps in documented Denisovan distribution, extending their known range across broader Asian territory. This work demonstrates how ancient protein analysis provides insights into human evolutionary history beyond what DNA alone reveals.
