Scientists developed a sophisticated robotic, cell-free platform enabling rapid prototyping and testing of redesigned genetic codes with reassigned codons and non-standard amino acids. This breakthrough technology allows systematic exploration of novel genetic systems without modifying the genomes of living organisms. The platform accelerates synthetic biology research and dramatically expands possibilities for creating proteins with modified genetic codes and incorporating novel amino acids.
