Researchers discovered that type 2 inflammation trains specialized lung immune cells to protect against lethal flu through tissue repair rather than viral killing. These trained macrophages limit tissue damage without improving viral clearance. This discovery explains how prior immune exposure builds disease tolerance and has implications for vaccine design and understanding why some people survive severe infections.
