People systematically learn new game rules through adaptive, rational exploration rather than random trial-and-error. Large-scale behavioral studies tracked players learning novel games and found they sample strategies intelligently, adjusting based on outcomes and inferred game structure. A computational model called Intuitive Gamer captures this process: players rapidly form mental models of how games work and test predictions. The model explains observed learning curves across diverse games. Findings suggest human reasoning about complex systems is structured and systematic, with implications for designing more flexible AI systems.
