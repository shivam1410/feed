Perovskite solar modules treated with chemically stable lead carboxylate passivators achieved high efficiency and reliability, outperforming commercial silicon modules in yield. Non-uniform passivator layers had previously limited module durability and performance. The new surface treatment provides consistent protection against defects and surface damage, addressing a key barrier to commercializing perovskite solar technology. This advancement enables more efficient, reliable, and cost-effective solar power generation.
