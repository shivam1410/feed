Morris is a Northern Gannet, the only known specimen of this Atlantic species in the entire Pacific Ocean. First appeared in Farallon Islands off San Francisco 14 years ago, now permanently inhabits Pillar Point Harbor near Half Moon Bay. Distinctive white plumage with yellow head makes Morris easily identifiable from the commercial pier. Represents unusual wildlife distribution and serves as local celebrity attraction.
