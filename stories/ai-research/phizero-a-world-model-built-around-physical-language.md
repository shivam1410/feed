Physical language—a discrete, abstract representation of world-state transitions—enables explicit reasoning about dynamics from video. PhiZero learns this abstraction through self-supervised training on in-the-wild video, then uses reason-then-render paradigm: first infers future evolution as sequence of physical-language tokens, then renders transitions into video. Approach improves physical coherence benchmarks and enables fine-grained action-conditioned simulation and zero-shot motion transfer unavailable to pixel-direct models.
