ArogyaSutra is a multi-agent framework for medical reasoning in Indic languages, addressing the critical gap in AI systems for low-resource language communities. The framework builds on the ArogyaBodha dataset integrating diverse medical knowledge sources, using actor-critic multi-agent reasoning to improve diagnostic accuracy. By targeting Indic languages—spoken by over a billion people globally—the work extends AI-enabled medical assistance to underserved populations. The multi-agent approach improves both accuracy and interpretability in medical decision-support systems where errors have serious health consequences.
