Explorative Modeling introduces a new approach to generative AI by exploring multiple candidate outputs and training on the best, rather than factoring generation procedure. Testing across images, video, and language showed performance gains scaling from 7% to 36% with data scale and 13% to 23% with model size. The method achieved 4.1× computational efficiency, 6.2× sample efficiency, and 47% parameter efficiency improvements, positioning exploration as a scalable third axis.
