Two archaeal strains have now been brought into culture, meaning they can be grown in the lab. They belong to a lineage closely related to the ancestor of eukaryotic cells, the complex cells with membrane-bound organelles. Their simple internal organization, elaborate surface structures, microbial partnerships and protein-dependent lifestyle offer clues. Those clues point to how eukaryotic cells arose.
