High-energy electron scattering experiments on three different nuclei reveal that short-range pairing of nucleons—strong, brief attractions between protons and neutrons at very small distances—depends far more heavily on specific quantum orbitals occupied by nucleons than theoretical models predicted. This finding indicates nuclear shell structure plays a more critical role in nucleon pairing than previously understood, requiring refinement of nuclear structure theory.
