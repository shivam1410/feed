Large and giant DNA viruses that infect unicellular eukaryotes are abundant in the environment but have rarely been found in the gut microbiome. The discovery of such viruses that prevail in the digestive system of humans and animals exposes a long-overlooked facet of the gut microbiome that clarifies the origin of poxviruses.
