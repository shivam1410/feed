Two-agent system bootstraps its own training: an explorer generates practice tasks while a solver learns from failures, no human guidance needed. Success rates jumped 15.9 points and pass@5 improved 2.2x over baseline. Matters because agents stuck in new environments repeat the same mistakes unless they can build and remember their own lessons.
