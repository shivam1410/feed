A benchmark study evaluating world models for robot policy assessment reveals that long-horizon rollout consistency and robot-specific controllability matter more than short-term visual realism. This finding reframes priorities for simulator development, suggesting resources should focus on behavioral accuracy over photorealism rather than visually accurate robot simulators.
