Xaira Therapeutics demonstrated that information-rich data resolves scaling plateaus in AI-driven drug discovery. A model trained on a single small dataset hit a wall at 1.5 billion parameters—test loss flatlining while training loss continued dropping, indicating data limitation, not compute. With approximately 30x more information-rich data, the 3.1-billion-parameter model resumed normal scaling behavior. Xaira estimates infrastructure investment at tens of millions. It matters because this reframes AI drug development: scaling parameters and compute alone cannot overcome data poverty; success requires substantial capital in data collection, experiments, and infrastructure.
