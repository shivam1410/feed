Scientists can extract information about millisecond-timescale protein dynamics by analyzing what is missing in nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) spectra. This novel analytical approach provides insights into protein movement and flexibility that standard spectroscopic analysis might overlook, advancing structural biology.
