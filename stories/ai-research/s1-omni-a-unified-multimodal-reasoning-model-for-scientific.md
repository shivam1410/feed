S1-Omni consolidates fragmented scientific AI capabilities into one model for understanding, prediction, and generation. It maps diverse scientific objects—molecular structures (CIF, SMILES), protein sequences, spectra, and images—into a shared representation space while incorporating scientific laws and expert knowledge. The model was trained on S1-Omni-Corpus covering 200 scientific tasks with millions of reasoning samples and evaluated on over 60 benchmarks. It outperforms GPT-5.5 and Gemini-3.1-Pro on most tests and supports applications from property prediction to protein structure and image generation. This matters because it replaces domain-specific models with one coherent system that can jointly model heterogeneous scientific data and knowledge.
