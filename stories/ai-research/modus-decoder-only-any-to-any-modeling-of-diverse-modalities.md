MODUS introduces a decoder-only architecture for any-to-any multimodal modeling, treating all modalities symmetrically without modality-specific heads or task pipelines. A single model can predict any modality from any input combination, supporting chained generation and cross-modal self-verification. The architecture enables flexible compositional reasoning and outperforms specialist and multitask baselines across diverse benchmarks with minimal architectural constraints. This unified approach simplifies deployment and extends to scientific domains like ecology and astronomy. Open-sourced implementation enables broad adoption beyond traditional vision-language applications.
