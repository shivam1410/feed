Researchers identified molecular glue degraders targeting the HuR RNA-binding protein as a therapeutic approach for BRAF-mutant colorectal cancers. Molecular glue degraders are small molecules that recruit proteins to degradation machinery. HuR regulates gene expression in cancer cells. BRAF mutations occur in a subset of colorectal cancers with limited treatment options. By degrading HuR, these compounds disrupt cancer cells' ability to regulate survival genes. Molecular glue degraders represent a newer drug development strategy gaining prominence in oncology. This work expands treatment options for BRAF-mutant colorectal cancer.
