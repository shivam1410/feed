A mild chemical process upcycles polyvinyl chloride waste into polyalphaolefin lubricants with tunable properties, offering an economical plastic recycling route while improving carbon circularity metrics. The process creates commercially valuable synthetic lubricants from discarded plastic materials. This development demonstrates sustainable chemistry for waste valorization. This matters because it provides financial incentive for plastic waste collection and reuse, making circular economy principles economically viable at commercial scale while reducing plastic waste in landfills and oceans.
