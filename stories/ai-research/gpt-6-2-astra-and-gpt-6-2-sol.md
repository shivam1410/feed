OpenAI's GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol are now in the API as gpt-6.2-astra and gpt-6.2-sol. Astra costs $8 per million input tokens and $40 for output, matching Claude Fable 5.2. Its cached input price fell to $0.40 per million, down from $1. Sol costs $2 and $10, half the price of GPT-5.6 Sol. In the author's three.js mech animation test, neither model did a good job. The cheaper cached input is good news for long agent sessions.
