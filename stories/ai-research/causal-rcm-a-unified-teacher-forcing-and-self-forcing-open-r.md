Researchers present Causal-rCM, a framework extending diffusion distillation techniques to real-time video generation through causal autoregressive training paradigms. The approach achieves state-of-the-art performance in streaming video generation while maintaining fast model convergence, enabling interactive world modeling capabilities. By combining autoregressive diffusion models with causal attention mechanisms, the framework processes video sequences in real-time without requiring full future information. This advancement improves video synthesis capabilities for applications requiring interactive or streaming generation, with implications for virtual environments, physics simulation systems, and dynamic interactive content creation.
