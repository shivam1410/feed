StationPDE improves multi-station weather forecasting by constructing terrain-aware continuous fields from discrete observations. The model decomposes physical evolution into surface wind transport and upper-air inference, combined with a data-driven diffusion branch. On Weather2K and MeteoNet benchmarks, it reduces mean squared error by 9.6% compared to state-of-the-art baselines, achieving more accurate predictions than purely statistical or physics-only approaches.
