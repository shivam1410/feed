Scientists created a spatial map showing where different proteins appear throughout healthy human tissues and various cancer types. This detailed proteome atlas reveals tissue-specific protein distributions, advancing understanding of normal development and cancer biology. By identifying unique protein patterns in different cancers, the map helps researchers discover new therapeutic targets. The comprehensive survey shows how proteins are organized across the human body in space, illuminating which proteins increase or decrease in different tissue contexts. These insights aid identification of cancer-specific proteins suitable as drug targets or diagnostic markers, establishing a foundation for personalized cancer treatment strategies.
