The world's most-prescribed antidepressants, SSRIs, are attracting increasing scrutiny about how they actually work. Scientists and clinicians question whether these drugs are as effective as believed and whether they're harder to discontinue than comparable substances. The debate highlights persistent gaps in understanding a medication class used by millions globally.
