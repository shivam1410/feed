Cryo-electron microscopy of the rice photosystem II–light harvesting complex II supercomplex was determined in its native membrane environment rather than in purified form. This in situ approach preserves physiologically relevant protein and lipid components typically lost during standard biochemical purification. The intact architecture reveals how photosynthetic antenna complexes and reaction centers naturally organize and function together in living systems. This information enables better understanding of energy transfer and photosynthetic efficiency under real cellular conditions, informing efforts to optimize photosynthesis.
