MACTS-EM uses teams of specialized agents, pattern detection, anomaly detection, causal inference, uncertainty quantification, to forecast time series across financial, climate, energy, and health domains. They share emergent memory that transfers patterns between domains. Evaluation showed 8-12% better accuracy, 22-27% better zero-shot transfer to new domains, and 16-21% better resilience during market shifts. This matters because forecasting real phenomena requires multiple skills working together, not a single generic model.
