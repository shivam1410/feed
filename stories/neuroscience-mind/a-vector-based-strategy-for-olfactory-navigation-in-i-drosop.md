Virtual reality experiments in fruit flies reveal memory-based mechanisms for navigating odour plumes. Flies use a vector-based strategy, following the boundary of odour plumes and tracking toward smell sources rather than simply orienting upwind. This research illuminates how insects locate food and mates using learned spatial memory and boundary-tracking behaviours, with insights applicable to understanding animal cognition and biologically-inspired navigation systems.
