The kākāpō population has recovered to 325 individuals, up dramatically from just 51 in 1995. This year's record breeding season produced chicks now counted as juveniles in the total population. The recovery demonstrates that sustained conservation effort can successfully reverse the decline of critically endangered species even after reaching near-extinction levels.
