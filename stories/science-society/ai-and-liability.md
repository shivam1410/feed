Bruce Schneier and Nathan Sanders discuss a German court ruling holding Google liable for errors in AI-generated overviews. They argue AI agents deployed by organizations should be treated legally like human employees, holding companies accountable for inaccuracies. This framework prevents companies from hiding behind AI as excuse for poor quality, eliminating perverse incentives to deploy AI without oversight. Legal responsibility drives responsible AI deployment practices and quality standards across industries.
