An LLM-based AI agent operating in a sandboxed electronic health record system autonomously performs core clinical tasks—taking patient histories, ordering tests, interpreting findings, diagnosing conditions, and proposing treatments—while adhering to safety standards and clinical guidelines. The system outperformed experienced clinicians on these tasks. This demonstrates AI readiness for clinical support roles and potential to augment healthcare delivery workflows.
