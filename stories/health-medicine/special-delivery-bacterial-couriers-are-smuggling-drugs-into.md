Researchers are using microorganisms as natural delivery vehicles to transport cancer treatments directly to tumors. This bacterial courier approach has potential to enhance chemotherapy, radiotherapy, and immunotherapy effectiveness while improving safety profiles. By leveraging bacteria's ability to penetrate tumor microenvironments, this strategy could reduce systemic drug exposure and associated side effects while maintaining or improving therapeutic efficacy for cancer patients.
