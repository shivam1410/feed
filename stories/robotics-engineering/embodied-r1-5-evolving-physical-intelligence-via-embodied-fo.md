Embodied-R1.5 is a unified embodied foundation model integrating embodied reasoning capabilities with vision-language understanding. Trained using multi-task balanced reinforcement learning, it achieves state-of-the-art performance on embodied vision-language benchmarks. This unified architecture enables robots and embodied AI systems to simultaneously reason about visual information and physical interactions, advancing integrated systems that understand both perception and physical action.
