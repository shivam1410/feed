EBench is a comprehensive simulation benchmark designed to evaluate generalist mobile manipulation policies across diverse tasks and environmental conditions. The benchmark systematically assesses state-of-the-art robotic models, revealing distinct capability profiles and generalization patterns among different approaches. This comprehensive evaluation framework is crucial for understanding where current robot manipulation systems excel and where they struggle, enabling researchers to identify gaps in generalization, transferability, and robustness. By providing standardized evaluation across multiple dimensions and task types, EBench helps guide development of more capable and reliable mobile manipulation systems for real-world applications requiring dexterous handling across varied scenarios.
