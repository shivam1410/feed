Stem cells may treat the immune system. Scientists are moving toward clinical trials using stem cells for autoimmune disease, where the body attacks itself. Early results look promising. The challenge is preventing rejection without crippling immunity. This matters because autoimmune conditions affect millions and current treatments broadly suppress immunity instead of fixing the root.
