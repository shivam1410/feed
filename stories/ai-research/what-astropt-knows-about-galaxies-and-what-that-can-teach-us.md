Analysis of AstroPT, a galaxy-image transformer model, reveals that linear probes recover real physical structure from learned representations. Concepts emerge during training in a fixed, difficulty-based sequence, suggesting transformers learn in an organized, interpretable way similar to human cognition. This illuminates how large language models and vision transformers acquire and organize knowledge. Understanding these patterns helps build more interpretable AI and clarifies what models learn versus memorize.
