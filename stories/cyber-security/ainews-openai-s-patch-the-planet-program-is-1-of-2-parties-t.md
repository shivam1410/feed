Two teams independently found serious vulnerabilities in core PHP, called PHP2Shell. One finder was Ryan Austin of the security firm Calif, working in OpenAI's Patch the Planet project using GPT 5.6 Sol. The other was Wiz, now part of Google Cloud, using Gemini 4 Argon, GPT-5.6 Sol and its own tool, Wiz Atlas. The newsletter says the bugs affect 70% of the web. The response was coordinated over two months. That matters because the author expects a year of similar finds, and sees that as healthy.
