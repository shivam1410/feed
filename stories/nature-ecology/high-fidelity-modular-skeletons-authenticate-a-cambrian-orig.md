Exceptional early Cambrian fossils of two bryozoan species preserve detailed soft-tissue anatomy and skeletal microstructure, pushing the evolutionary origin of bryozoans deeper into the Cambrian period. The fossils of Protomelission gatehousei and Dayingomelission hexaclitia definitively confirm these organisms as bryozoans through their preserved skeletal organization. Bryozoans are colonial marine animals that build elaborate calcium carbonate skeletons. This discovery extends the known fossil record of bryozoans to earlier times, providing evidence of a longer evolutionary history.
