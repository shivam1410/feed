ComplianceGate enforces compliance by design through a trained encoder classifier that routes queries to appropriately sized models before any LLM computation. Queries containing personally identifiable information route to local endpoints immediately, making data residency violations structurally impossible. On 600 test queries, the system achieved 39% median latency reduction, 33-52% cost savings, and 122-200 tokens/second throughput versus 50-64 baseline. The classifier achieved 99.2% accuracy, enabling cost-efficient and compliant regulated AI deployment.
