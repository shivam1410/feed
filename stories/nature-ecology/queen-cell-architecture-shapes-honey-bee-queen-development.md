Worker honeybees actively engineer a specialized physical and chemical environment—the queen cell—that is essential for developing new queens. This construction behavior creates the precise conditions needed for queen development to proceed successfully. The finding demonstrates that worker bee behavior actively shapes their colony's future by constructing specialized niches. This reveals how collective building behavior creates developmental environments, with implications for understanding how social structures influence individual development in eusocial insects.
