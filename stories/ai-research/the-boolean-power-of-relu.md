arXiv:2608.12617v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We prove that, on finite simple undirected graphs equipped with a single Boolean node feature, the Boolean queries expressible in $\Sigma$-MPLang, for any collection $\Sigma$ of eventually constant activation functions and with arbitrary real coefficients, form a strict subclass of the Boolean queries expressible in ReLU-MPLang. We thereby settle a recently posed open problem: whether ReLU-MPLang is more powerful than trReLU-MPLang when it comes to Boolean queries. In particular, this implies that ReLU-GNNs are strictly more expressive than {TrReLU,id}-GNNs with respect to Boolean queries on Boolean-featured graphs.
