DeepMind has created AlphaGenome Atlas, a database that predicts the effects of every possible single nucleotide variant in the human genome. This comprehensive resource maps genetic variation across all three billion base pairs, enabling researchers to systematically understand which DNA changes affect protein function and disease risk. The tool could accelerate drug discovery and personalized medicine by providing a complete functional map of human genetic variation.
