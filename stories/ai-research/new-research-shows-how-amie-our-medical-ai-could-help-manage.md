AMIE, DeepMind's conversational AI system, matches primary care physician performance in managing complex chronic diseases. The system engages patients naturally, synthesizing clinical information to guide treatment decisions comparable to specialist physicians. This parity with human clinicians suggests AI can augment healthcare workflows, handling routine disease management and freeing physicians for complex cases. The finding opens possibilities for scaling primary care access in resource-limited regions where physician shortages constrain healthcare delivery.
