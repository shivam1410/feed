Researchers completed sequencing of over 2,000 centromeres from humans across diverse populations, revealing structural variations and elevated mutation rates in these chromosomal regions. Centromeres are repetitive, structurally complex regions critical for chromosome segregation. Understanding centromere variation illuminates human genetic diversity and evolutionary history.
