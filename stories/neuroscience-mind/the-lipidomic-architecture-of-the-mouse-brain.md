Scientists created a detailed chemistry map of the mouse brain. They identified more than 500 distinct biochemical territories, each one mirroring specific cell types and their patterns of connectivity. The atlas captured how these chemistry zones shift during pregnancy and revealed hidden diversity within brain cell types. This shows how the brain's molecular architecture directly sculpts neural organization and supports development.
