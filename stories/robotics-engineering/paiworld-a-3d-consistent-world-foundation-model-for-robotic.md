PAIWorld is a world foundation model for robotic manipulation that enhances diffusion-transformer architectures with geometric awareness and cross-view attention mechanisms. These additions improve multi-view three-dimensional consistency, enabling the model to maintain accurate spatial understanding across different camera perspectives. Better 3D consistency directly improves prediction accuracy for manipulation tasks.
