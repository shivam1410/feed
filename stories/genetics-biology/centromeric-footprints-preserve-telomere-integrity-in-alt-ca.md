Researchers identified that centromeric DNA repeat insertions and CENP-A chromatin assembly act as genomic signatures protecting telomere integrity in ALT (alternative lengthening of telomeres) cancer cells. These findings reveal how certain cancers maintain telomeres without telomerase. Understanding these protective mechanisms is important because ALT activation occurs in multiple cancer types, and identifying these structures could inform new therapeutic strategies targeting cancer cell immortality and survival.
