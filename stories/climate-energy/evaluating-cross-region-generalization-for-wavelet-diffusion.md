Wavelet diffusion models downscale low-resolution precipitation to kilometer scale across six US regions with different weather patterns. A model trained only on Oklahoma remained competitive elsewhere, while an all-region model performed best overall but with uneven gains across precipitation intensities. Better at detecting localized high-reflectivity structures.
