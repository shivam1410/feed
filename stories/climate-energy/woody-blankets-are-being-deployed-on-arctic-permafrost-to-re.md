A startup is testing whether woody blankets can stabilize crumbling Arctic permafrost and keep carbon locked underground. The logic is straightforward: stop the thaw, stop the greenhouse gas emissions. This matters fundamentally because permafrost contains vast amounts of stored carbon that warms the planet as it thaws.
