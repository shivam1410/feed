Researchers designed an insulator-free topological photonic waveguide with four inequivalent photonic valley half-semimetals arranged in parallel, stacked closely together. This architecture achieves robust, unidirectional light transport with 100% spatial efficiency—light travels through all available channels without loss. This multi-lane photonic highway design represents an advance in controlling light propagation, with potential applications in optical communication and computing systems.
