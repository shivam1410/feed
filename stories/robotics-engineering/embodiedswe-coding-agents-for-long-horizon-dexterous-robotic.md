EmbodiedSWE tests whether coding agents can solve long-horizon robot tasks and turn their solutions into training data for learning policies. Created a benchmark spanning contact-rich manipulation and deformable objects. Generated trajectories improved VLA performance, and an agent-trained simulation policy succeeded on real robot.
