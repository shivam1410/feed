Microsoft announced two new text language models: MAI-Thinking-1, a reasoning model with one trillion total parameters but only 35 billion active, available to select partners; and MAI-Code-1-Flash with 137 billion total and 5 billion active, rolling out to GitHub Copilot users. The company claims MAI-Thinking-1 outperforms Sonnet 4.6 in blind evaluations despite its smaller size, demonstrating that parameter efficiency is possible in today's expensive AI landscape. Both models were trained on commercially licensed, enterprise-grade data without relying on distilled knowledge from third-party models—rare in the industry.
