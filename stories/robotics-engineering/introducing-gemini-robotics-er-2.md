Google unveiled Gemini Robotics ER 2, marking a major step forward in robotic AI. The system demonstrates dramatic improvements in three critical areas: understanding video feed from robot cameras, coordinating complex tool use and object manipulation, and orchestrating seamless collaboration between multiple robots. These advances could enable robots to handle far more complex, real-world tasks including manufacturing, construction, hazardous exploration, and disaster response.
