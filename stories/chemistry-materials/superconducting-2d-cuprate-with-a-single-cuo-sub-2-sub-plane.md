Researchers fabricated a high-quality pristine monolayer cuprate superconductor with a single CuO2 plane and identified two quantum critical phenomena at the transition between Mott insulating and superconducting phases. This ultrathin limit reveals fundamental quantum properties previously inaccessible in bulk materials, advancing understanding of high-temperature superconductivity mechanisms at the atomic scale.
