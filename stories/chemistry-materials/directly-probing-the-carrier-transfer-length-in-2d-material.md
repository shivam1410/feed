Cross-sectional scanning tunneling microscopy revealed bismuth contacts create a 2-nanometer carrier transfer length in monolayer MoS2 transistors. This measurement defines scaling limits for metal contacts in sub-10-nanometer 2D electronic devices. The findings establish physical boundaries for transistor miniaturization, informing design of next-generation computing hardware as devices approach atomic-scale dimensions and conventional silicon scaling reaches fundamental limits.
