ResearchClawBench evaluates whether AI systems can independently conduct scientific research end-to-end across 40 tasks from 10 domains, grounded in real published papers with expert-curated success criteria. Results show severe limitations: the strongest agent (Claude Code) averaged only 21.5/100, with top LLMs averaging 26.5/100. Failures concentrate in experimental protocol mismatches, evidence gathering gaps, and incomplete scientific reasoning, indicating autonomous research remains unreliable.
