Researchers introduced Large Discovery Models for open-ended search across chemistry, biology, and programming. The architecture combines a generative model proposing candidates with a Bayesian non-parametric surrogate estimating reward while quantifying uncertainty. This uncertainty-aware approach allows intelligent exploration of design spaces, identifying where additional information is most valuable. By integrating generative modeling with probabilistic reward estimation, the framework enables efficient discovery across domains as diverse as novel molecules, protein designs, and program synthesis.
