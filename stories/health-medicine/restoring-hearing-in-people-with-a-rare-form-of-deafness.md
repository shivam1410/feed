Scientists achieved the first gene therapy for inherited deafness caused by a specific genetic mutation. After decades of painstaking research, they succeeded in fixing the faulty gene responsible for profound hearing loss. This matters because it opens treatment possibilities for people with this form of genetic deafness and demonstrates gene therapy can work on sensory organs.
