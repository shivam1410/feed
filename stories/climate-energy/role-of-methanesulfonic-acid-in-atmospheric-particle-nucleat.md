Research examines the role of methanesulfonic acid (MSA), a sulfur-containing compound, in the nucleation and growth of atmospheric particles or aerosols. These particles form from gas-phase molecules in the atmosphere and subsequently grow. Methanesulfonic acid appears to play a significant role in this process. Understanding atmospheric particle formation mechanisms is important for climate science, as aerosols influence cloud formation, precipitation, and the planet's radiative balance. MSA's contribution to aerosol nucleation and growth has implications for air quality modeling and climate prediction.
