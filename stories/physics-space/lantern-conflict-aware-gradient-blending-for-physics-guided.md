Lantern applies physics-guided diffusion to calorimeter shower simulation by encoding soft per-sample constraints as two auxiliary losses: a variance-stabilized voxel residual grounded in counting statistics and a graph Laplacian over detector geometry. The Correlation Frobenius Distance (CFD) metric evaluates correlation fidelity across layers and voxels. GradBlend combines auxiliary physics losses with denoising by anchoring step magnitude to the denoising gradient while steering direction toward physics correctness, improving structural fidelity in high-energy physics simulation.
