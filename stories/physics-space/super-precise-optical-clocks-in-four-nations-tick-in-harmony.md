Seven precision optical clocks distributed across Europe have been synchronized using signals transmitted through fiber-optic cables, demonstrating unprecedented agreement between the devices. This test represents a major step forward in establishing reliable, linked timekeeping systems across vast distances. Optical clocks are among the most precise instruments ever built, losing or gaining just one second over billions of years. The successful synchronization validates technologies essential for future applications in GPS navigation, telecommunications, and fundamental physics research. The findings confirm that ultra-precise timekeeping can be coordinated across continents.
