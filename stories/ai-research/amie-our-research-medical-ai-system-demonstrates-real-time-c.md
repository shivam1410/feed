In a first-of-its-kind study, AMIE demonstrated its ability to engage with patients in simulated clinical settings, answering medical questions and providing consultation feedback through video interactions. This system represents a significant step toward AI-assisted clinical consultation. AMIE's real-time video capabilities could potentially extend healthcare access and support physicians in patient consultations.
