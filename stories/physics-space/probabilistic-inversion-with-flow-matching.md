Flow Matching, a generative AI technique, is adapted for probabilistic inversion in seismic full-waveform inversion and other geophysical applications. The approach is evaluated on a simple 2D velocity model to demonstrate core features and the OpenFWI benchmark dataset to demonstrate capabilities on complex seismic velocity models. This work bridges generative modeling theory with inverse problem frameworks, showing that modern generative techniques efficiently sample posterior distributions for uncertainty quantification in geophysical imaging.
