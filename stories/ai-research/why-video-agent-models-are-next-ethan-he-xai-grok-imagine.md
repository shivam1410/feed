Ethan He (xAI Grok Imagine architect) argues video models derive intelligence primarily from LLMs rather than video data alone. He predicts the next frontier is video agent systems that plan, generate, edit, critique, and iterate across full creative tasks. This parallels how AI coding evolved from one-shot output models to multi-turn reasoning agents. As video model performance improves and costs decrease, advancement likely involves orchestration systems rather than isolated generation improvements.
