Forecasters predict an unprecedented El Niño for 2027, described as the largest on record by a 'mind-blowing margin.' This massive climate pattern will combine with global warming to push global temperatures to new heights in coming years. El Niño emerges from temperature anomalies in the Pacific Ocean and influences weather patterns globally. The convergence with anthropogenic warming will amplify climate impacts. Such extreme events carry major consequences for agriculture, water resources, ecosystems, and human societies worldwide. The magnitude makes 2027 a critical year for climate monitoring.
