OmniVideo-100K introduces an automated system for audio-visual question answering combining entity-anchored video scripting with clue-guided question-answer generation. Rather than manual annotation, the system automatically extracts key entities from videos and generates questions with supporting evidence chains that guide reasoning across audio and visual modalities. This approach improves cross-modal reasoning—the ability to combine information from different input types—while maintaining temporal consistency critical for video understanding. The structured generation process produces more reliable reasoning chains than unstructured question-answer pairs.
