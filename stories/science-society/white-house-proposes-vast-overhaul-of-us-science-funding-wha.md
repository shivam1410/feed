The Trump administration has proposed a major structural overhaul of how federal science funding is allocated and governed throughout the United States. The proposal aims to improve transparency and clarity in federal funding decision-making processes and mechanisms. However, critics have expressed significant concern that the proposed regulatory changes could inadvertently inject political considerations and influence into scientific research selection and funding allocation decisions. Why it matters: How federal science funding is distributed fundamentally determines research priorities and directly affects which scientific questions get studied and pursued, making funding governance structure and decision-making criteria absolutely critical for maintaining scientific integrity, independence, and public trust in the objectivity of federally-supported research.
