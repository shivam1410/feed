An Iron Age female skeleton reveals sophisticated ancient practices: her bones were deliberately carved into functional tools. After death, her brain was removed, yet remains were carefully reassembled for burial, suggesting complex mortuary rituals balancing practical utilization of skeletal material with reverential reintegration of the deceased.
