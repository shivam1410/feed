Analysis reveals systematic replacement of human-made components with model-generated alternatives in the AI pipeline: judges (reward models), rubrics (learned metrics), data (synthetic), and environments (simulations). The pattern started with InstructGPT's reward model in 2022 and continues expanding. Each synthetic version is approximately 10% worse but 100× cheaper and 10,000× faster than human-produced equivalents. This represents a fundamental paradigm shift in how machine intelligence is constructed—from humans creating every component to increasingly ambitious synthetic systems automating the pipeline while maintaining acceptable quality thresholds.
