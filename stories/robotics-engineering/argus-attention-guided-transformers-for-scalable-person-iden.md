ARGUS passively identifies people from Wi-Fi Channel State Information without attached devices or prescribed motion. Statistical maps (statgrams) compress CSI into compact representations; a lightweight decoder-only Transformer processes coarse patches and aggregates segment-level logits. On 154 subjects with physical-segment split: 78.88% ± 1.62% Top-1 accuracy on 6-second windows, 84.85% ± 1.31% after 60-second aggregation, and 99.26% Top-5. Using 4.4× fewer FLOPs than raw-CSI baselines while preserving full accuracy with only half the patches, it offers a device-free biometric alternative.
