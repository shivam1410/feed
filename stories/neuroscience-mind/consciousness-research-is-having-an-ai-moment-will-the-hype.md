Increased public focus on artificial intelligence sentience is drawing substantially more attention to consciousness research as a scientific discipline, though some consciousness researchers question whether this visibility ultimately helps their field advance. The surge in AI-consciousness discussions raises the scientific profile and public interest significantly. However, researchers worry this attention may conflate speculative AI debate with rigorous experimental consciousness science, potentially muddying research priorities and public understanding. The field must navigate heightened visibility while maintaining scientific rigor and focusing on phenomena researchers can actually measure. This tension reflects broader questions about how emerging technology shapes scientific agendas.
