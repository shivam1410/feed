OpenAI used an unreleased model to produce a resolution to the Navier–Stokes existence and smoothness problem, one of seven $1 million Millennium Prize Problems unsolved since 2000. The achievement is overshadowed by accusations that OpenAI may have accessed prior work from Anthropic researchers (Levent Alpöge and Tristan Buckmaster) who achieved a breakthrough on August 15th using Claude and GPT models. Timing questions arose about when OpenAI began their effort and whether they accessed the researchers' sessions. This raises questions about IP, collaboration ethics, and competitive dynamics.
