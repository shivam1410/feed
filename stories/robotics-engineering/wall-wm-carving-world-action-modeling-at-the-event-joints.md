WALL-WM reframes video-action learning by treating semantic events as fundamental units rather than fixed-duration action chunks. Traditional methods divide videos into arbitrary time segments; this approach identifies meaningful moments as learning boundaries, better capturing how humans understand action. This event-based structure aligns with actual semantic boundaries in video, enabling more flexible training at natural action scales rather than forced uniform lengths. Results show improved scalability and performance in vision-language-action tasks. The advance matters for robotics and embodied AI, where understanding when actions begin and end is critical for task execution.
