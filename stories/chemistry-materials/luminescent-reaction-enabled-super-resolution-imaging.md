A chemistry-based super-resolution imaging framework enables 3D, laser-free, highly sensitive and long-term imaging of live cells. The luminescent-reaction-enabled approach overcomes the spatiotemporal resolution limitations associated with conventional reaction-based imaging methods. By eliminating the need for intense laser illumination, the method significantly reduces phototoxicity that typically damages living cells during extended imaging sessions. This advance allows researchers to perform sustained, high-resolution imaging studies of cellular dynamics in living systems without the temporal and spatial constraints of conventional fluorescence microscopy techniques. The laser-free approach enables continuous observation of cellular processes over extended periods, opening new experimental possibilities for dynamic cell biology research.
