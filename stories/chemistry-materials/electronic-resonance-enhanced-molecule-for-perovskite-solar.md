Scientists have engineered a molecule with enhanced electronic resonance properties designed to improve perovskite solar cell performance. Electronic resonance allows charge carriers to move more efficiently through the material, increasing energy conversion. The approach optimizes light absorption and charge transport in perovskite crystals, addressing conventional limitations. This advancement enhances perovskite material properties directly, contributing to more efficient and practical commercial solar applications.
