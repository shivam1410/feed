DrugGen-2 generates small molecules conditioned on disease ontology and target protein sequences through fine-tuning GPT-2 with supervised learning and GRPO reinforcement learning. The model achieves superior molecular diversity and binding affinity compared to baseline models, integrating disease context directly into generation to create more therapeutically relevant candidates.
