Deserts worldwide are experiencing unprecedented flash flooding, a dramatic shift from historical arid weather patterns. This phenomenon suggests climate systems are destabilizing in unexpected ways. Desert ecosystems and infrastructure evolved for drought conditions are unprepared for major water events. The pattern threatens both environmental stability and threatens water management in regions historically defined by scarcity.
