Suspected China-linked hackers used autonomous AI agents to find a new flaw in MONAI Label, an open-source medical imaging server. Amazon Threat Intelligence says the attackers then gained root access to a university server. The flaw lets someone with no account write files outside the server's storage folder. It affects MONAI Label 0.8.5 and earlier in the default setup. Why it matters: AI agents are now finding real flaws in live systems.
