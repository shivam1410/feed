Antineutrinos—subatomic particles produced during weapons-grade plutonium manufacturing—can be detected using existing technology. This detection capability could identify clandestine nuclear weapons production programs by monitoring the distinctive antineutrino signatures accompanying plutonium enrichment, offering a potential verification mechanism for nonproliferation efforts.
