Zero-WAM enables robots to perform novel tasks by learning from in-context human video examples without prior training on those specific tasks. The system uses a causal video-action model conditioned on human demonstrations, supported by an automatically generated training dataset and a future-chunk prediction objective. This approach enables significant task generalization for robotic manipulation in open-ended scenarios.
