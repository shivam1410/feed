Researchers identified potential predictive biomarkers distinguishing which non-small cell lung cancer patients will benefit from perioperative nivolumab therapy, using CheckMate 77T trial participant data. Nivolumab is an immune checkpoint inhibitor activating anti-cancer immune responses, but only some patients respond robustly. Predictive biomarkers enable personalized medicine, allowing clinicians to select patients most likely to experience therapeutic benefit while avoiding unnecessary therapy in non-responders. Identifying these markers in clinical trial settings strengthens their utility for treatment planning, advancing precision immunotherapy in early-stage lung cancer.
