MA-VLA enables multi-arm robots to collaborate by assigning atomic actions to individual robotic arms and using training-time permutations of arm assignments to learn coordination patterns. This approach allows the model to generalize to unseen coordination strategies without explicit programming, advancing compositional generalization in multi-agent robot systems.
