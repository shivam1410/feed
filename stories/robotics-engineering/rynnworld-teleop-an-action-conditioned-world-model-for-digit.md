RynnWorld-Teleop replaces physical robot interaction with generative world models that simulate robotic environments. Instead of collecting data from real robots, the system creates diverse synthetic training data through digital teleoperation of simulated robots. These generative models enable zero-shot Sim2Real transfer, where policies trained entirely on simulation work effectively on real robots without fine-tuning. The approach dramatically improves sample efficiency by reducing reliance on expensive real-world data collection. By leveraging generative models to create varied training scenarios, the method accelerates robot learning while reducing physical hardware wear and operational costs, enabling more practical and scalable robot training pipelines.
