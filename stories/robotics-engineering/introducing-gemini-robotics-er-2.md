Google introduced Gemini Robotics ER 2, representing a significant advance in robotic capabilities across three key areas: video understanding (processing visual information), tool orchestration (coordinating actions and resources), and multi-robot collaboration (enabling teams to work together effectively). These improvements enable robots to handle more complex real-world tasks and coordinate in groups, expanding potential applications in manufacturing, logistics, and other industries.
