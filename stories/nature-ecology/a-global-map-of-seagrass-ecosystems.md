Scientists have created the first comprehensive global map of seagrass meadows by combining satellite imagery with machine learning algorithms. Seagrass ecosystems are vital for marine biodiversity, coastal carbon sequestration, and natural coastal protection from storms, yet their global extent and condition have remained poorly understood. This mapping breakthrough enables researchers and policymakers to identify critical seagrass habitats, monitor ecosystem health, and prioritize conservation efforts. The data-driven approach demonstrates how satellite remote sensing and AI can unlock large-scale understanding of fragile marine ecosystems, directly supporting conservation strategies.
