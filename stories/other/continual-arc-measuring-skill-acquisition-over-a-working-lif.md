Continual-ARC, a lifelong stream of ARC-AGI tasks, compares memory- and weight-based continual learning under frozen-weight agent harnesses, revealing that harness persistence rarely improves memorization and most harness methods underperform simple caching, while weight-based learning shows better generalization.
