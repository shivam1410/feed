A small trial demonstrates that a modified virus can instruct immune cells to eliminate disease-causing targets in multiple sclerosis. The engineered viral approach reprograms immune function to specifically attack MS-related cells rather than broadly suppressing immunity. This represents a novel therapeutic strategy differing from traditional MS treatments, potentially offering more targeted disease control.
