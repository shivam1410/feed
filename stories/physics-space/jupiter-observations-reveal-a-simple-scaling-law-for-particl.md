Observations of Jupiter's supersonic shocks reveal that transient plasma structures—temporary disturbances in charged gas—likely govern particle acceleration in space. The simple scaling law discovered suggests that shock characteristics universally determine how energetically particles get accelerated. This finding explains a fundamental mechanism driving cosmic ray production and particle energization throughout the universe.
