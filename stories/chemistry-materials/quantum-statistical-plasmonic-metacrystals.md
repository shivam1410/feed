Novel quantum statistical plasmonic metacrystals selectively control which light passes through based on quantum properties alone. These materials combine plasmonic effects with metamaterial engineering and exhibit bands of allowed and forbidden photon states determined by light's quantum coherence—whether photons are in quantum superposition. This selectivity based purely on quantum statistics is unprecedented; traditional materials respond to wavelength and intensity. Applications could include quantum-enhanced imaging, quantum communication systems, and photonic quantum processors. The discovery enables quantum-engineered optical devices.
