Scientists designed bitopic kinase inhibitors that simultaneously bind to two different sites on ABL1 and EGFR kinases with greatly enhanced precision and selectivity. The new ABL1 version works effectively against cancer cells carrying resistance mutations that typically escape standard drugs, while causing less damaging side effects to healthy tissue. This innovative two-site binding strategy could help overcome major drug resistance problems in cancer therapy.
