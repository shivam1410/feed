Pharmaceutical engineers are redesigning medications to remain active in the body for months or even years after a single dose, potentially transforming disease management globally. This persistence has profound implications for low- and middle-income countries where access to frequent medical care is limited. Patients receiving one injection that treats a condition for an entire year would require far fewer clinic visits and reduced logistics burden. The approach applies across infectious diseases, chronic conditions, and preventive care. This development addresses both clinical and economic barriers to effective treatment in resource-limited settings.
