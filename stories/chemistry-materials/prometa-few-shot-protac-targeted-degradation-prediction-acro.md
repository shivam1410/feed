ProMeta reformulates PROTAC degradation prediction as few-shot meta-learning, using prototype-based graph neural networks. On the CRBN-to-VHL benchmark, it achieves AUROC 0.883 with minimal support samples, improving performance by 19.9% over prior approaches. This matters because most E3 ligases lack training data; few-shot generalization expands the chemical design space for targeted degrader drugs beyond well-studied cases.
