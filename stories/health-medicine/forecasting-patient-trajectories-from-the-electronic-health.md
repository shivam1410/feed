A world model for electronic health records predicts how patient histories unfold under clinical actions. It keeps patient states separate from the actions taken, then learns how those states change. On binary classification and time-to-event tasks, it performs competitively with much larger EHR foundation models, using a 30x smaller parameter footprint. Performance depends on the task. Changing the action sequence also changes the generated trajectories. It matters because it can simulate what happens under different actions.
