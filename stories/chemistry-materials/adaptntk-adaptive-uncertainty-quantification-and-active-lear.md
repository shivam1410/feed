AdaptNTK introduces a single-model uncertainty-quantification method for machine-learning interatomic potentials using regularized Mahalanobis distance in neural tangent kernel space. The approach achieves 2.6× speedup per active-learning cycle while maintaining correlation with force errors (Spearman 0.68) comparable to ensemble methods. This enables faster, more efficient molecular dynamics simulations with quantum accuracy.
