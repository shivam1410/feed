OpenAI unveiled Jalapeño, a custom inference chip significantly outperforming NVIDIA's GB200/GB300 systems. Benchmarks show 1.5–1.9× more work per watt at peak throughput and 1.7–3.6× lower end-to-end latency on real model workloads. For highly interactive applications, the chip delivers 2.1–4.1× higher performance. Rated at 700W, Jalapeño stayed at or below 550W during testing. Deployment into OpenAI's infrastructure begins by year-end, with Gen 2 in development and Gen 3 underway. The shift in the inference stack prioritizes performance-per-watt over raw compute, reshaping AI infrastructure priorities.
