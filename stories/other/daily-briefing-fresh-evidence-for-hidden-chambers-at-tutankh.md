New scans of Tutankhamun's tomb provide evidence supporting the presence of previously unexplored hidden chambers that may contain the remains of Egyptian queen Nefertiti. This finding builds on earlier speculation about sealed chambers within the famous tomb. Discovery of Nefertiti's remains would be a major archaeological breakthrough, potentially revealing new information about this significant figure in ancient Egyptian history and royal lineage.
