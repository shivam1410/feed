Active learning reduces expert annotation burden in biodiversity monitoring but creates a practical tension: non-random AL labels are unsuitable for model validation or calibration. A research synthesis across acoustic and camera-trap modalities reveals most published studies use pre-labelled benchmarks; real deployments remain rare. Existing work concentrates on birds and cetaceans while bats, insects, amphibians, and fish are severely underrepresented.
