Systematic exploration of unified multimodal pretraining reveals four core insights into language-vision interaction. Knowledge flows asymmetrically across modalities with distinct patterns. Data complexity determines synergy versus competition; shared attention plus modality-specific layers promote synergy and generalize across visual tokenizers. Early unification outperforms late alignment or sequential training, uncovering vision laziness where delayed integration causes over-reliance on language priors. Efficient pretraining recipes achieve strong generative performance using 5% of standard compute budgets, providing actionable design principles for foundation models.
