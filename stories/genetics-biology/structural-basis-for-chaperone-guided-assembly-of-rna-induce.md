Structural studies of the AGO maturation complex reveal precisely how molecular chaperone proteins and RNA duplexes orchestrate assembly of the RNA-induced silencing complex (RISC), a fundamental cellular gene regulation system. These three-dimensional structural insights explain the mechanistic details of how cells construct and mature this essential regulatory machinery.
