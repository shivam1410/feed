DeepMind released EmbeddingGemma 2, a new open-source AI model that converts text and images into digital representations for machine learning. It's lightweight and designed to run directly on personal devices without sending any data to cloud servers. This opens new possibilities for privacy-focused AI applications where sensitive information stays completely on your device while you still get full machine learning processing benefits.
