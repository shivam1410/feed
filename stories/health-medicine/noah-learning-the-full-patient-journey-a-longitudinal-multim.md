NOAH is a generative transformer that models complete patient journeys using multimodal clinical data with continuous time dynamics and stochastic latent states. It enables forecasting, zero-shot classification, and counterfactual simulation across diverse datasets. This matters because a single unified model spanning multiple clinical tasks could improve diagnosis, prognosis, and treatment planning while revealing how interventions propagate through patient outcomes.
