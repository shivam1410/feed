SyncWorld is an action-conditioned world model that learns environment-specific visual dynamics through brief calibration episodes, enabling zero-shot simulation in unseen robotic settings without retraining. The model maps actions to visual changes and supports test-time policy improvement. This matters because robots could work in novel environments immediately, reducing deployment friction and enabling faster adaptation to new tasks.
