Researchers found that spreading lime on farmland actually pulls carbon out of the air. Looking at century-scale records in the Mississippi River Basin, they discovered agricultural liming acted as a substantial net carbon sink, offsetting acidic pollution from human activity. This shows farming practices themselves could play a meaningful role in fighting climate change and deserve more attention in climate solutions.
