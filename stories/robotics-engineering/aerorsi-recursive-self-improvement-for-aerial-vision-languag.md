A drone that follows spoken directions can learn from its own crashes. The trick is that a failing policy judges its fixes by flying on from them. Over three rounds, a 2B-parameter model's success rate rose from 46.4% to 67.5%. Collisions were roughly halved. That beat the previous best by 10.6 points, with no extra human labels. The code and models are on GitHub.
