Google Cloud is deploying AlphaEvolve widely to optimize algorithms for computationally hard problems across customer applications. The system finds efficient algorithms for tasks including microchip design, logistics routing, and medical research. By automating algorithm discovery, AlphaEvolve reduces the manual engineering effort required to solve complex optimization problems in industry and research.
