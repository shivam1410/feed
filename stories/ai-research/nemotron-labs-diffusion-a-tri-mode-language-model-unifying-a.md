Nemotron-Labs-Diffusion is a tri-mode language model combining autoregressive, diffusion, and self-speculation decoding methods. The architecture switches between generation modes based on task requirements, allowing flexible inference strategies. By integrating diffusion-based decoding alongside traditional autoregressive generation and self-speculation, the model achieves superior throughput and efficiency compared to single-mode alternatives. This multi-mode approach enables different workloads to use the decoding strategy best suited to their latency and efficiency constraints. The design achieves practical advantages for deployment scenarios with varying requirements, improving both generation speed and resource utilization while maintaining quality across different inference patterns and use cases.
