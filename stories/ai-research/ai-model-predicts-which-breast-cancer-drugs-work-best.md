An artificial intelligence model trained on millions of protein measurements predicts drug effectiveness for triple-negative breast cancer in individual patients. The system analyzes protein patterns in tissue samples to forecast which anticancer drugs work best for each tumor. This personalized medicine approach guides treatment selection based on a tumor's molecular signature. Accurate drug-response prediction reduces ineffective treatments and improves outcomes by directing patients toward therapies most likely to succeed.
