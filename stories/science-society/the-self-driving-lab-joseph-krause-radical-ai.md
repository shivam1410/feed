Radical AI is accelerating materials discovery at 10x traditional pace by tackling why materials science exceeds biology in complexity: success depends on manufacturing processes, microstructure variables, and supply-chain factors rather than molecular design alone. Founder Joseph Krause argues this acceleration matters urgently for aerospace, computing, and defense. Unlike biological molecules predictable via token sequences, material success requires end-to-end system validation at scale. This makes reproducibility, manufacturing disclosure, and comprehensive process understanding critical—limitations that defeated earlier materials prediction attempts.
