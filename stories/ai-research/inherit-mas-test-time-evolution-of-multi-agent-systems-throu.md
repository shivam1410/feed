Inherit-MAS improves multi-agent workflows by learning from failures. A judge scores each execution and diagnoses problems; workflow inheritance drops unhelpful agents and fixes broken steps. Execution inheritance reuses prior results when context matches, avoiding redundant computation. On two benchmarks with GPT-4o-mini, it achieved 55.4% and 49.7% accuracy, beating evolving-agent competitors. This matters because multi-agent systems are hard to design perfectly upfront; evolutionary improvement through feedback is more practical.
