AI has suddenly gotten good at hard math. In May, an internal OpenAI model found a counterexample to Erdős's unit distance conjecture, open since 1946. A month later, OpenAI published a proof of the Cycle Double Cover problem. Anthropic published a formalization of Fermat's Last Theorem. The author models difficulty as the shortest chain of insights a solver needs. The big question is which problems models will crack next. Knowing that would show where to point AI.
