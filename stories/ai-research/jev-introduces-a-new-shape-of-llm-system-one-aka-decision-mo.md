Jev represents a new model category called System One models or decision models, which outputs floating-point confidence scores instead of text. It charges only $0.042 per million input tokens, cheaper than even budget models, and doesn't charge for output. Because outputs are structured and verification is implicit, it achieves massive speed and eliminates hallucination, making it ideal for classification tasks at scale.
