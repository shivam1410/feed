Teaching AI to improve machine learning engineering requires executable environments and structured learning. Frontis-MA1 (35 billion parameters) trains via OpenMLE using four program-evolution operators—Draft, Improve, Debug, Crossover—composed into long-horizon evolution search. On MLE-Bench Lite with 12-hour per-task budget on single RTX 4090 GPU, the model improves Medal Average from 39.39% to 60.61% over baseline, reaching 71.21% with extended search. This approaches frontier model performance while compute-constrained. Both trained model and OpenMLE framework transfer to held-out benchmarks, suggesting the learning approach generalizes beyond training domain.
