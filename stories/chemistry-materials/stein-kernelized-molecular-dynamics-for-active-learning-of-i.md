Stein Kernelized Molecular Dynamics (SKMD) uses interacting particle dynamics to acquire informative training configurations for machine learning interatomic potentials. By preserving Boltzmann distribution while exploring diverse configurations, SKMD achieves higher model accuracy in fewer training iterations. An adaptive stopping criterion selects non-redundant training data, demonstrated on Müller-Brown potential and alanine dipeptide.
