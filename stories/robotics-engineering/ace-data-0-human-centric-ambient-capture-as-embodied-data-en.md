Multimodal embodied dataset captures 150 hours, 17 million frames, 75,000 episodes across 200 household tasks from 50 participants in two home environments. ACE-Data-0 records synchronized egocentric/exocentric video, full-body and hand motion, object geometry and trajectories, audio, and tactile signals as unified stream. Hierarchical benchmark progresses from signals to scene components. Dataset preserves natural behavior by issuing goal-level rather than step-by-step instructions, addressing fundamental data bottleneck in embodied AI.
