Personalized gene therapy cures severe epilepsy. Researchers successfully treated two boys with intractable epilepsy using personalized gene therapy that inactivated one copy of an epilepsy-causing gene. One child achieved independent walking for the first time. Seizures were dramatically relieved in both. This breakthrough demonstrates gene therapy's transformative potential for treating severe neurological conditions resistant to conventional medicine. The success offers hope for similar applications in other genetic disorders.
