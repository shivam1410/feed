Researchers using improved laboratory fractionation strategies discovered manikomycin, a novel antibiotic from Streptomyces rimosus with a chemical scaffold previously unseen in known drugs. The compound targets the E-site of the bacterial large ribosomal subunit—a site distinct from where conventional antibiotics bind—blocking bacterial protein synthesis through a unique mechanism. This discovery exemplifies how refined fractionation techniques can systematically identify antibiotics with unprecedented structures and modes of action, offering new strategies to combat antibiotic-resistant infections.
