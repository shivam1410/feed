Coding agents excel at modifying existing codebases but struggle building programs from scratch—frontier models resolve fewer than 1% of ProgramBench tasks. MindForge automates conversion of open-source tools into source-free training environments exposing only compiled binaries and documentation. Fine-tuning Qwen 27 billion parameters on synthesis trajectories from these environments increases ProgramBench pass rate from 37.98% to 49.51%, matching frontier model performance. Fine-tuned model generalizes robustly across seven unseen benchmarks spanning repository generation, translation, bug fixing, and feature implementation, with absolute gains of 31 percentage points or more. Synthetic training approach scales whole-lifecycle program synthesis for smaller models.
