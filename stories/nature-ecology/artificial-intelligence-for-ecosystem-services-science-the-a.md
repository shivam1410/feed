AI4ESS framework applies artificial intelligence to ecosystem services science through community collaboration. Ecosystem services encompass natural processes like pollination, water purification, and soil formation that provide ecological and economic benefits to humanity. The community-centered framework structures how AI can enhance measurement, modeling, and management of these services at scale. This collaborative approach matters because it incorporates diverse stakeholder perspectives — environmental scientists, policymakers, and resource managers — ensuring AI tools align with real-world conservation and environmental management needs.
