MRPO, a reinforcement learning approach, improves clinical image reasoning by addressing cascading failures through step-wise process rewards. Instead of penalizing only final answers, rewarding correct intermediate reasoning steps prevents early errors from propagating, yielding superior performance on medical multimodal reasoning tasks.
