Researchers tested whether the timing between bat calls helps AI identify species. Transformer model PaSST achieved 71% accuracy with natural call timing and 70% with artificial spacing, showing timing helps marginally. Simpler model EfficientNet jumped from 47% to 57% accuracy when timing was removed, revealing it over-relied on spacing. Transformers extract real biological patterns while simpler models exploit temporal artifacts, showing architecture matters for learning true features.
