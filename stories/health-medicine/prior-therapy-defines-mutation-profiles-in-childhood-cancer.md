Chemotherapy causes dramatic genetic changes in childhood cancers that relapse. Platinum-based drugs triple therapy-specific mutational signatures and double overall mutation burden in relapsed tumours. This therapy-induced mutagenesis demonstrates how treatment itself drives rapid tumour evolution, with practical implications: understanding these chemotherapy-generated mutations could guide development of safer, de-escalated treatment strategies that achieve disease control while reducing genetic damage to cancer cells.
