A new benchmark tests whether AI research agents can accurately predict what happens when you change experiment settings. Agents inspect code, select limited measurements, and predict scores for every configuration. When a Gaussian process fitted observations, its ability to recover true effect magnitudes improved from 63.2 to 69.8 percent. This matters because research agents need to understand which component changes actually matter to make better design decisions.
