Researchers created a universal cell embedding foundation model by training on 36 million cells from hundreds of experiments spanning dozens of tissues across eight species. The model learns to capture fundamental patterns of cellular organization and biological variation. This comprehensive foundation establishes a shared reference for understanding cell biology across diverse experimental contexts.
