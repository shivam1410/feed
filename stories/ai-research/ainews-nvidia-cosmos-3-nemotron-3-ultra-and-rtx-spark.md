NVIDIA released Cosmos 3, an open-source unified model combining language, image, video, audio, and action in a Mixture-of-Transformers architecture with an autoregressive reasoner paired with a diffusion generator. Base Nano spans 16B parameters (8B reasoner, 8B generator), while Super reaches 64B (32B each). Super finetunes for Text-to-Image and Image-to-Video now achieve state-of-the-art performance among open-weight models. Simultaneously, NVIDIA announced Nemotron 3 Ultra, a 550B parameter open-weight model representing the strongest U.S. open alternative, and previewed RTX Spark, a 1-petaflop personal supercomputer. This open-source initiative democratizes advanced physical AI research, significantly lowering barriers for independent developers and research institutions operating beyond proprietary vendor ecosystems.
