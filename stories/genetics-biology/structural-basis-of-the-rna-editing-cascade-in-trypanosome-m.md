Cryo-electron microscopy has revealed the three-dimensional architecture of the Trypanosoma brucei mitochondrial editosome, a protein assembly that performs unusual mRNA editing. The structure shows how this complex orchestrates precise insertion and deletion of uridine nucleotides in messenger RNA, a process essential for producing functional proteins in trypanosome mitochondria. This structural insight clarifies mechanisms of RNA editing, a process unique to these parasites.
