A world model's physical understanding can be transferred to vision-language-action robot policies via representation alignment, without deploying the world model itself at test time. A 0.8B student policy trained with frozen world-model supervision reaches 97.9% on LIBERO and improves from 48.2% to 50.5% on real humanoid manipulation, running in 32ms with 1.86GB memory on consumer hardware. The representation gain generalizes across model scale, architecture, and teacher changes, indicating a robust physical prior that survives on both single and bimanual real robots.
