DRAM memory prices jumped 500% in twelve months, with 128GB DDR5 kits now ten times costlier than historical lows. Hyperscaler buyers locked in nearly all global DRAM production for 2027 through advance deposits. Memory is worth over half as much per kilogram as solid gold. This reverses Moore's Law specifically for memory. Hyperscalers' advance commitments reflect explosive AI infrastructure demand. The shortage threatens to constrain AI scaling and raises marginal costs for training, inference, and retrieval systems across the industry.
