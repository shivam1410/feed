Generating 3D scenes from video typically produces implicit representations difficult to render or edit downstream. FLAT adapts video diffusion models to decode explicit surface primitives directly from latent space rather than implicit features. This approach enables high-quality 3D scene generation with improved geometric accuracy and real-time rendering capabilities. Explicit surface representations make 3D content more practical for applications requiring geometry manipulation, editing, or asset extraction from video.
