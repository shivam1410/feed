TransA-MERFISH combines anterograde trans-neuronal tracing and spatial transcriptomics and reveals distinct retinal on–off direction-selective ganglion cell-dependent and independent motion-selective pathways in superior colliculus, establishing a sensitive cell-type-resolved framework for wiring-diagram reconstruction in early visual processing.
