A new two-step image generation model achieves quality comparable to eight-step systems through teacher-student distillation combined with distribution-aligned adversarial learning and end-to-end optimization. Reducing generation from eight to two steps significantly decreases latency and deployment costs. The research demonstrates that careful distillation techniques can compress generation pipelines without sacrificing visual fidelity. This matters because image generation speed directly impacts user experience and operational efficiency. Faster generation without quality loss opens new practical applications for generative models in real-time systems.
