The ArogyaSutra framework and ArogyaBodha dataset enhance medical AI reasoning in Indic languages and low-resource settings using actor-critic multi-agent reasoning. By integrating diverse medical data types, the system generates diagnoses and recommendations in underserved linguistic communities. This work addresses a massive gap: most medical AI serves high-resource languages and regions, leaving billions without accessible healthcare intelligence. ArogyaSutra extends AI-assisted medical reasoning to populations typically excluded from AI advancement, handling multiple languages and data source complexity.
