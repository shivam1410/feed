Magic-W0 is a foundation model that teaches robots to predict how the world changes when they take specific actions. It models the current 3D state of the environment, the 3D motion that an action causes, and what happens as the future outcome. Trained on human manipulation videos, robot demonstrations, and simulation data, it combines world prediction with action control for better robot learning.
