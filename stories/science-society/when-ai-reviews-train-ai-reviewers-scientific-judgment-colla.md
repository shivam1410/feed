Study reveals that training LLMs on synthetic reviews generated by earlier AI models causes "scientific-judgment collapse"—rating distributions narrow and semantic diversity decreases across papers. The research introduces TrustReviewer, an open-source system that mitigates this through curated training data and test-time activation steering to preserve judgment diversity without additional expert annotation.
