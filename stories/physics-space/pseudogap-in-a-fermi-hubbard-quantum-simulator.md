A cold-atom quantum simulator experimentally revealed the pseudogap metallic state predicted by the Hubbard model for strongly correlated electrons. By mapping the phase diagram of this exotic state, the work advances understanding of doped Mott insulators and their connection to high-temperature superconductivity.
