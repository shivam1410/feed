COrigami is a computational system that generates origami crease patterns from natural language descriptions through AI-driven optimization and aesthetic evaluation. The system bridges the gap between human creative intent (expressed in text) and mathematical constraints that govern flat-foldable origami—which crease patterns actually fold correctly without overlap or gaps. Users describe desired origami properties, and the system searches the space of geometrically valid designs while evaluating aesthetic qualities. This matters because it demonstrates human-AI collaboration in a domain combining art, mathematics, and engineering. Rather than replacing human designers, the tool extends human creativity by handling mathematical complexity, enabling faster iteration and exploration of designs that might be difficult to discover manually.
