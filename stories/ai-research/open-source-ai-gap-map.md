Current AI, a non-profit founded in February 2025 with $400 million committed funding, launched Gap Map v0.1, a comprehensive index of the open source AI ecosystem. The catalog details 421 products: 266 software tools and libraries, 85 models, 50 datasets, and 20 hardware projects from 228 organizations across 14 categories and 3 stack layers. An additional 24,400 uncategorized artifacts form the long tail. The dataset—1,184 YAML files with notebooks and schemas—is released under MIT license on GitHub, enabling community exploration and contribution.
