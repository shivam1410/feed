Observations reveal a gas-enshrouded and gas-reddened supermassive black hole at cosmic dawn, illuminating how early black holes may have grown rapidly while obscured by gas and dust. These dust-shrouded systems appear as mysterious "little red dots" in early-universe surveys. The discovery explains how early supermassive black holes accumulated mass rapidly, helping reconcile observed objects with theoretical formation timescales.
