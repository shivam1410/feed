Modern misinformation combines text, images, and multiple languages, requiring sophisticated detection mechanisms. ReMMD presents a comprehensive multimodal framework handling complex multilingual content with multiple images and diverse verification approaches. The system achieves superior misinformation detection performance while reducing computational costs compared to existing methods. Better multimodal detection combats coordinated disinformation campaigns exploiting media diversity and language barriers in international contexts.
