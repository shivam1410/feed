A new chemistry-based super-resolution imaging framework enables 3D, laser-free, highly sensitive, ultralong-term imaging of live cells. This approach overcomes spatiotemporal resolution limitations of previous reaction-based imaging methods. Eliminating lasers reduces phototoxicity, enabling extended observation without cellular damage. The technique combines improved resolution with high sensitivity and biocompatibility, advancing cellular microscopy capabilities for dynamic biological studies.
