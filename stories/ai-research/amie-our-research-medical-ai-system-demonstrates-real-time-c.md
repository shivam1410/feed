Google introduced AMIE, a medical AI system conducting real-time clinical video consultations in simulated clinical settings. The system demonstrated ability to engage in clinical dialogue and provide medical guidance through video interaction, representing an early step toward AI-assisted telemedicine and remote clinical support. AMIE could potentially expand access to medical expertise in resource-limited settings. However, evaluation in realistic clinical environments with real patients and clinicians remains necessary before clinical deployment. The work demonstrates progress in conversational medical AI.
