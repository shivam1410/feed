Two mathematicians claimed proofs of the Riemann Hypothesis within hours of each other. Levent Alpöge, of Anthropic, released a Claude-driven proof as a note with Lean code, about 1.3GB, or roughly 250,000 pages of equivalent text. Elliot Glazer confirms the formal statement is the generalized version and puts the chance of a Lean bug well under 1%. Alessandro Masullo then released a 940-page, all-human work in progress he has been building for 12 years. Both AI-era claims will take years to verify.
