HypoEvolve uses a generational genetic algorithm to coordinate specialized large language model agents for scientific hypothesis discovery and evaluation. Separate agents integrate mechanistic arguments, reconsider assumptions, and assess evidence and testability. Rather than treating collaboration implicitly, the framework makes it explicit through successive updates to a hypothesis population. Applied to drug repurposing, the system validates proposed mechanistic explanations against external biological evidence from DepMap and Open Targets databases, linking explanations to target-level biological claims assessed experimentally. This approach isolates collaboration effects on hypothesis quality from individual agent capability, enabling direct testing of how multi-agent orchestration affects scientific reasoning outcomes.
