AIUC, founded by Rune Kvist, announced a 40 million dollar Series A to build insurance and standards for autonomous AI agents. They created AIUC-1, a standard for stress-testing agents against jailbreaks, hallucinations, and data leaks. The startup argues that trust and liability will become the actual limiting factor for AI adoption, not model capability.
