Your cells gradually lose the regulatory instructions that define their identity and function as you age. This molecular grammar was carefully written and established during your development but slowly erodes over the years. Aging appears to be progressive forgetting of the cellular program, much like losing the instruction manual that tells your cells what to do, how to behave, and how to maintain themselves properly.
