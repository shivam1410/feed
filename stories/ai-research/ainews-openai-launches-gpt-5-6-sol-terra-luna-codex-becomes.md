OpenAI released GPT-5.6 in three sizes—Sol, Terra, and Luna—with a new ultra effort level coordinating four parallel agents. Terra achieves just-above-Fable-5 performance while Luna outperforms Opus 4.8, each using roughly one-third the time, half the output tokens, and approximately one-quarter the cost. The model achieves state-of-the-art on Terminal-Bench 2.1 and DeepSWE.
