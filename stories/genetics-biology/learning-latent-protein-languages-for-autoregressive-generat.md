New discrete alphabets for protein tokens significantly improve transformer generation quality. Protein Latent Language maps sequences to a 4,096-state alphabet; Structure Latent Language tokenizes backbone coordinates with auxiliary supervision. Under matched training, the sequence model reduces bad samples by 54 percent, while structure models cut perplexity by 34 percent and run approximately 1,000 times faster than MSA-based AlphaFold2. Learned latent languages unlock transformer power for biology.
