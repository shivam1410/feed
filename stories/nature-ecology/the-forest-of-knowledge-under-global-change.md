The Amazon rainforest's biocultural heritage—comprising plant species, plant services, and indigenous cultures—faces significant threats from climate change. This knowledge system represents centuries of ecological understanding and sustainable resource management practices accumulated by native peoples. The loss threatens both unique biodiversity insights and irreplaceable cultural traditions.
