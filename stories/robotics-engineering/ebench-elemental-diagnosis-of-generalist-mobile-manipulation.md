EBench is a comprehensive simulation benchmark designed to systematically evaluate generalist mobile manipulation policies. The benchmark assesses state-of-the-art robotic models across diverse tasks and multiple dimensions, revealing both distinct capability profiles and generalization patterns. Understanding how policies generalize across different task combinations is critical for deploying mobile manipulation robots in real-world environments where they encounter diverse scenarios. By identifying which policies generalize effectively across novel task combinations and which struggle, EBench provides researchers with actionable insights for developing more robust and adaptable robotic systems. This framework helps inform improvements in policy design and training methodologies.
