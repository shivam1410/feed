Following the merger of two black holes, astronomers detected gravitational waves carrying direct signatures of the remnant black hole's event horizon. This landmark observation opens a new channel to test black hole physics by directly measuring frame-dragging effects—relativistic distortions of spacetime near spinning black holes—within the ergosphere. The detection enables measurement of horizon surface gravity, a fundamental black hole property. These capabilities test general relativity predictions in extreme gravity regimes previously inaccessible, advancing understanding of black hole physics and spacetime behavior.
