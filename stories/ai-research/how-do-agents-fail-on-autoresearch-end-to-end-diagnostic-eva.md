Researchers evaluated autonomous AI agents on 100 real-world frontier research tasks spanning the complete scientific workflow. Evaluation revealed a critical weakness: agents consistently failed to engage in metacognitive self-correction—the ability to recognize and fix their own errors during research. This systematic failure across diverse scenarios prompted creation of a new benchmark and taxonomy of agent failure modes. The findings highlight the gap between current AI capabilities and human-like scientific reasoning, particularly in error detection and self-improvement.
