EgoPhys automatically generates digital twins of deformable objects from first-person RGB video, avoiding per-spring optimization costs. The system uses learned generalizable physics priors and compact neural codebooks to efficiently predict dense spring stiffness fields across complex objects like fabrics and materials. This approach eliminates traditionally expensive optimization for each individual spring. The method could significantly advance physics simulation, robotics control, AR/VR applications, and embodied AI systems requiring accurate deformable object dynamics from egocentric viewpoints, enabling real-time understanding of object interactions without manual calibration.
