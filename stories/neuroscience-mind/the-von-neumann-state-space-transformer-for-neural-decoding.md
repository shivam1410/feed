New Von-Neumann State-Space Transformer (VN-SST) improves neural decoding from limited data. Rather than applying identical operators across tokens, this architecture uses a low-rank instruction bank where a slow latent state acts as an instruction pointer—mirroring how brain dynamics may route computation. The memory-augmented approach achieves far greater data efficiency than standard Transformers across three motor-cortex benchmarks while maintaining small parameter budgets. Performance improvements are largest on the scarcest datasets, validating the efficiency gains. The model jointly predicts neural spikes and decodes behavior, offering practical advantages for brain-computer interfaces.
