Adaptive physics-informed neural networks solve the Blasius boundary-layer equation with high accuracy using gradient-norm-based loss weighting, residual-based collocation, and sequential optimization. The representative model predicts f''(0)=0.3320762918 versus benchmark 0.332057336215, an absolute error of 1.896×10⁻⁵ and wall-shear error 21.5× smaller than prior PINN work, showing that combined adaptive training achieves strong performance on classical PDEs.
