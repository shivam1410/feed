Legora's CTO explains how to build legal agents when the company cannot see their work. The data belongs to law firms' clients, so the agent's traces cannot be read. Instead, three engineers and three lawyers build test environments modeled on customers' work, similar to reinforcement learning worlds. One citation task went from right 70% of the time to 99% after one release, and the team changed nothing. The CTO estimates models already handle about 60% of legal work well. Agents can draft Word documents and review 10,000 contracts in one table.
