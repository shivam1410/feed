Ruby predicts how tightly small molecules bind their drug targets. It uses intermediate representations from the Pearl structural model, so it needs no explicit binding pose for each compound. It posted the highest early enrichment across seven public hit-identification targets. It also led on four public benchmarks. In zero-shot tests across eight assays, it beat other methods.
