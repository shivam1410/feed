arXiv:2608.06951v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Hanneke, Moran, and Waknine \cite{HannekeMoranWaknine2024} asked how the agnostic PAC learning curve of the direct sum $C^r$ depends on the single-instance learning curve $\epsagn(n\mid C)$ and on $r$. We show that the single-instance learning rate does not determine the direct-sum rate. Let $\F$ be the class of the two constant binary functions and let $\G$ consist of the zero function and the identity function. Both classes have agnostic learning curve of order $n^{-1/2}$.
