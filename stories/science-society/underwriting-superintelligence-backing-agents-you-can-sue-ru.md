AIUC, founded by Anthropic's first product hire Rune Kvist, announced a $40M Series A backing AIUC-1, an agent standard tied to insurance underwriting. Companies like Cursor, Harvey, Lovable, and ElevenLabs increasingly face liability and responsibility questions as autonomous systems deploy. AIUC stress-tests agents for jailbreaks, hallucinations, and data leaks, positioning standards and insurance as critical infrastructure for responsible AI adoption, potentially making trust the binding constraint rather than capability.
