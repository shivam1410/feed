LibriBrain100 is a large-scale MEG (magnetoencephalography) dataset containing 100 hours of brain recordings paired with speech, designed for neural speech decoding research. The dataset improves decoding accuracy through extensive within-subject recordings and multi-subject supervised fine-tuning of pre-trained models. Large-scale brain-to-speech datasets are scarce; this resource enables researchers to train and validate decoding algorithms that could advance brain-computer interfaces for communication.
