Older female mice treated with an anti-obesity medication—typically used for weight loss and type 2 diabetes—survived longer and maintained better overall health than untreated controls. The medication enabled extended lifespan and improved healthspan in aging animals. This finding matters because it suggests these drugs may have anti-aging properties beyond weight-loss effects, potentially slowing age-related disease and decline. If validated in humans, it could represent a novel therapeutic approach to aging, warranting investigation into longevity mechanisms beyond weight loss.
