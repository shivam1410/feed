TC-Next is a multimodal deep learning model that forecasts tropical cyclone track and intensity 6 to 24 hours ahead by combining foundation model atmospheric forecasts with satellite infrared imagery. Trained only on GraphCast forecasts over the Western Pacific, the model substantially reduces track error by 15 to 44 percent and intensity error by a factor of 3 to 6 compared to conventional rule-based systems like TempestExtremes. The model generalizes zero-shot to other weather prediction systems including Pangu-Weather and IFS HRES without additional retraining. On real-world 2025 Western Pacific season data, TC-Next achieves lower intensity error and matches or exceeds specialized trackers, with ablation studies confirming multimodal approach benefits.
