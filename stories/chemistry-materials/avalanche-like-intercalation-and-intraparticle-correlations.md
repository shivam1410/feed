Using operando optical microscopy, researchers observed avalanche-like lithium deintercalation processes—cascading events—as graphite's dilute stages empty and intercalation as they fill. These observations show how local disorder controls phase-transition dynamics and ion transport in lithium-ion battery electrodes. Understanding these mechanisms at the microscale provides insight into improving battery design and performance. The avalanche behavior explains observed phenomena during charge-discharge cycles and could guide development of next-generation batteries.
