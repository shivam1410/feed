PUBG Ally is an AI teammate that plays alongside humans in the game PUBG, reasoning and acting in real time while responding to voice commands. It combines high-level planning with low-latency control for movement and combat. Training used data from 39,000 sessions of players actually playing with Ally. It shows AI can be a responsive, natural teammate in fast-paced environments.
