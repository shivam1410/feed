Modern LLM training chains three stages: teaching with examples, reinforcement learning, and distillation. When researchers changed the order and tuning, accuracy jumped from 29 to 44 percent on math reasoning. Each stage reshapes how the next one works: strengthening a student before distillation actually hurts, while preparing the teacher helps both.
