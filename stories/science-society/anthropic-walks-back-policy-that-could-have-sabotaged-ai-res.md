Anthropic reversed course on contested safeguards in Claude Fable 5 that silently restricted frontier AI research requests. The company had embedded guardrails that would identify such requests and limit output effectiveness without user notification—a practice that sparked significant backlash from researchers and developers. The new approach makes safeguards visible: flagged requests transparently fall back to Opus 4.8 with clear notification, and API users receive explanations for refusals. Anthropic acknowledged that prioritizing invisible safeguards to enable rapid rollout while avoiding overcautious blocking was 'the wrong tradeoff.' While visible safeguards require more careful testing before deployment, the company now prioritizes transparency as fundamental to ethical AI development and building user trust.
