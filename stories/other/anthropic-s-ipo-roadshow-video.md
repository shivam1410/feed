Anthropic's IPO roadshow video reveals how it runs Claude on four chip families. Co-founder Tom Brown says Claude now runs on AWS Trainium, Google TPUs, NVIDIA GPUs, and AMD GPUs. Bringing up a new chip used to take dozens of engineers months of work. For AMD, Anthropic had Mythos try it, and Claude was running in three days with almost no human help. Claude wrote almost 100% of the kernels. Anthropic credits its early multi-chip bet for this speed.
