Analysis of US patent records and bibliometric databases reveals scientific research with military or dual-use applications receives substantially more citations than purely civilian-focused research. Researchers tracked this pattern across scientific literature, demonstrating that papers with potential military relevance attract greater attention from the scientific community. This correlation suggests scientific impact correlates with potential military applications, raising important questions about research prioritization, funding allocation, and the relationship between applied and fundamental science.
