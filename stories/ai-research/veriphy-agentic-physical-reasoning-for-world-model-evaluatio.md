VeriPhy verifies AI-generated video by converting prompts into physical constraints, then analyzing generated content against those constraints using expert models. The system tracks evidence and assigns three-valued verdicts (true, false, unknown), making verification auditable. This approach enables systematic fact-checking of generated video by ensuring compliance with known physical laws and logical consistency.
