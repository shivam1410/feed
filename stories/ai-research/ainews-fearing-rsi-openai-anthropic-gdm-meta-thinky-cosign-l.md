Over 1,000 employees from frontier AI companies—OpenAI, Anthropic, GDM, Meta, and Thinky, notably excluding X.ai—cosigned a statement requesting government intervention to pace AI development. Signers warn that leading AI companies could be close to automating AI research itself, risking capability development accelerating beyond human understanding or control. They argue each company faces intense competitive pressure to accelerate unilaterally, and the world lacks technical and governance tools to deliberately pace frontier progress. The statement requests U.S. government support for an international effort to develop such tools. This represents a significant shift from 2023's pause proposals—now insiders from labs themselves advocate for measured development.
