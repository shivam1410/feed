Climate downscaling models trained on historical data fail on future climate projections due to temporal distribution shift. Researchers combined supervised HR reconstruction with domain alignment between historical and future distributions, testing over the Continental US. The domain-adaptive approach outperformed statistical and deep-learning baselines, with largest gains in topographically complex regions and improved extreme temperature forecasts.
