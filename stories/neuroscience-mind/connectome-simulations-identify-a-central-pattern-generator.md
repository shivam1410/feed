Researchers built computer simulations of a fly's nervous system from connectome maps. They found a three-neuron circuit organized to produce the leg rhythms used in walking. This kind of network is called a central pattern generator. The results come from simulations, so they still need testing in real flies. It matters because it points to a small circuit that may set the walking rhythm.
