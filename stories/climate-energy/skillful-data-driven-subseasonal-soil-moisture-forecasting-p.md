Vision Transformer predicts root-zone soil moisture better than operational systems by forecasting in physical units rather than anomalies. Residual learning proved essential. Model detects dry anomalies reliably but flash drought onset, defined by rapid multi-week intensification, remains hard across all systems. Matters because early drought warning needs both prediction skill and honest acknowledgment of what remains unsolved.
