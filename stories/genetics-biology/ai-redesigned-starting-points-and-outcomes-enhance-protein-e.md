A newly developed artificial intelligence-driven workflow for protein engineering employs computationally redesigned protein variants as optimized starting points for directed enzyme evolution, yielding enzymes with significantly superior catalytic properties compared to those evolved from natural wild-type proteins. By computationally optimizing initial protein scaffolds, researchers effectively circumvent evolutionary constraints that normally limit the functional potential of natural starting materials. The AI-evolved enzymes demonstrate improved catalytic efficiency or substrate selectivity far exceeding what conventional natural evolution typically produces within comparable timeframes. This approach demonstrates artificial intelligence's transformative potential to dramatically accelerate protein engineering and expand the functional landscape of engineered biomolecules. Practical applications span pharmaceuticals, biofuel production, and industrial biotechnology, where custom-designed enzymes command substantial economic and scientific value.
