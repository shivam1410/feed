An electrochemical cell containing molten hydroxide electrolyte with an integrated palladium membrane actively extracts hydrogen gas during chemical reactions. This hydrogen extraction shifts equilibrium, increasing conversion efficiency for ammonia and methylcyclohexane synthesis while producing purified hydrogen fuel ready for immediate use. The electrochemical approach combines product generation with targeted hydrogen extraction, improving both reaction yield and fuel quality. This demonstrates how coupling extraction with synthesis enhances efficiency in chemical processes.
