Lila Sciences is building an automated AI-guided lab combining robotics, lab equipment, and vision-language models to run continuous experiments 24/7. The facility has generated over 10 trillion experimentally validated scientific reasoning tokens. Operating vision-language models to control legacy systems and deploying autonomous floating plates for sample management, Lila aims to create scientific superintelligence integrated with wet-lab workflows. The approach targets biology, chemistry, drug discovery, and materials science simultaneously, betting that scale of experimental data produces general scientific reasoners.
