Shanghai AI Lab developed InternW0, a physical world model trained on 7,200 hours of robot and egocentric video that predicts visual dynamics and generates robot control actions. It handles contact-rich manipulation and long-horizon tasks, succeeding at real-world 15-stage chemical synthesis and precision pipetting. This advances AI systems that understand and act in the physical world.
