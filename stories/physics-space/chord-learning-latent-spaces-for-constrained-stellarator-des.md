CHORD is a conditional variational autoencoder for designing stellarators, a type of fusion energy device. It penalizes predicted constraint violations along the paths it interpolates between designs. When blending distinct geometry families, up to 81 percent of midpoints were simulator-feasible. An unregularized model reached 50 percent, and direct interpolation of boundary parameters reached only 28 percent. Ablations show both ingredients are needed for that gain.
