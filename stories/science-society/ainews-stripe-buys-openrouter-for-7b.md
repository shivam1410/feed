Stripe acquired OpenRouter for $7B, representing 50× revenue multiple on $140M annualized revenue. OpenRouter routes queries across models for 8M developers, processing 250 trillion tokens monthly (up from 50T in February). Annualized infrastructure costs are ~$40M (28.5% of revenue), yielding 70% gross profit margins competitive with top-tier public software firms. The 5×-in-six-months growth and broad developer base justify high valuation as AI infrastructure consolidates around model-routing platforms.
