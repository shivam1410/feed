A simple organic catalyst directly copolymerizes carbon dioxide with bicycloalkanes to create high-performance polyester plastics that can be completely depolymerized and recycled in a closed loop. The process converts waste CO₂ into useful polymers and allows end-of-life plastic to become feedstock for new material, addressing both carbon emissions and plastic waste through circular manufacturing.
