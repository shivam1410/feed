Electric vehicles have prevented approximately 260,000 premature deaths in China by reducing air pollution from fossil-fuel combustion. Fewer gasoline and diesel vehicles on roads have significantly decreased certain major air pollutants. However, the transition to EVs has had limited impact on some other air pollutants, indicating that vehicle electrification alone is insufficient for comprehensive air-quality improvement. Additional interventions such as industrial emission controls are necessary.
