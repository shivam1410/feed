RynnBrain 1.1 is a family of embodied foundation models spanning 2B to 122B parameters trained on unified spatio-temporal and physically grounded data for robot perception, spatial reasoning, localization, and planning. New features include contact-point prediction and native 3D grounding. The 122B variant outperforms all evaluated proprietary and open-source competitors on multiple embodied cognition benchmarks. Real-robot experiments on three different robot platforms show policies initialized with RynnBrain outperform competing VLAs in success rates and task performance.
