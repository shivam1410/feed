Genetic sequences from Chilean mummies provide the first direct genomic evidence that smallpox was introduced to the Americas from Europe during early colonization. Viral DNA analysis confirms the disease came from the Old World rather than emerging independently. This molecular evidence establishes a definitive historical link between European contact and the devastating epidemics that followed. The finding reshapes understanding of colonial-era disease transmission and clarifies how smallpox became the first pandemic connecting continents. This genomic approach demonstrates how ancient viral sequences can resolve long-debated historical questions about disease origins and early human contact.
