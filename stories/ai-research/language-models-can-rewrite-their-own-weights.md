A 27B language model rewrote its own weights and got better, with no gradients or training. Its own edit made it 6.6 points more accurate on 490 problems it never saw. Its accuracy kept rising over its first eight sessions. It also repaired a corrupted copy of itself, lifting six image benchmarks from 7 percent to 70 percent. That is 83 percent of the undamaged model's accuracy. The gain stays in the weights and adds no cost per answer.
