Life and Earth have co-evolved over four billion years in a continuous interaction that has shaped both the biosphere and the physical world. Organisms have fundamentally transformed Earth's atmosphere, oceans, and geology, while the planet's changing conditions have driven biological evolution. This deep co-dependence reveals that life doesn't simply adapt to environmental conditions—it actively reshapes them. Understanding this reciprocal relationship provides crucial lessons for managing Earth's future amid climate change, showing how interconnected living systems and planetary processes truly are.
