Deep learning automates sea-ice classification, but operational ice charts vary—different agencies label the same conditions with different stage codes. The paper quantifies annotation uncertainty from multi-annotator disagreement and model uncertainty separately. Models trained on multiple independent annotations perform better where charts diverge. This matters because accurate ice-type forecasting improves navigation safety and climate monitoring.
