Researchers developed an in vivo model enabling recovery of human T cells directly from solid tumors, allowing genome-wide CRISPR screening within the tumor microenvironment. This approach identifies genetic targets capable of enhancing CAR T cell antitumor activity. Traditional CAR T development relies on laboratory optimization, which may not reflect the complex tumor microenvironment. Performing functional genomics directly in tumors identifies targets more likely to improve real-world therapeutic efficacy. This represents major progress toward genome-informed CAR T engineering and accelerates development of more potent, personalized cancer immunotherapies.
