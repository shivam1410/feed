DeepMind launched AlphaGenome Atlas, a comprehensive database predicting the effects of every possible genetic variant in the human genome. They've mapped millions of DNA changes and their consequences. This helps researchers identify disease-causing mutations, understand individual genetic risk, discover new drug targets, and improve genetic counseling, serving as a searchable guide to human variation.
