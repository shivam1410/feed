HiFi-UMI demonstrates that raising robot-free data fidelity can eliminate the need for real-robot post-training. The portable system achieves 3mm workspace-local end-effector accuracy using head-mounted stereo-inertial SLAM, native inter-gripper pose measurement, and two wide-angle cameras per hand covering ~200 degrees. Policies post-trained solely on HiFi-UMI demonstrations match in-domain teleoperation baselines across three backbones with success-rate differences of -2.5 to +3.1 percentage points. The strongest policy reached 85% on a precision insertion task despite the teleoperation baseline being collected in the evaluation scene.
