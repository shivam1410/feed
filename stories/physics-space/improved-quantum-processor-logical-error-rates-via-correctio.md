Researchers demonstrated substantial improvements in quantum error correction using trapped-ion quantum processors, reducing logical error rates by 11 to 800 times compared to baseline physical circuits. The work combines quantum error-correcting codes with error detection and post-selection techniques. These improvements represent critical progress toward practical, fault-tolerant quantum computing where errors are automatically detected and corrected. The wide range of improvements across different circuit types indicates the robustness of the approach and suggests a clear path for scaling quantum computers to tackle real computational problems.
