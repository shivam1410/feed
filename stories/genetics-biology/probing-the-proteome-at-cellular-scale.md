Rapid technological advances now enable researchers to identify thousands of proteins within individual cells, revealing molecular mechanisms underlying biological development and disease. Single-cell proteomics provides unprecedented resolution compared to traditional bulk tissue analysis, exposing how protein expression varies between individual cells and changes during development or disease. This cellular-scale resolution illuminates fundamental developmental biology, explains how identical genes produce different cell types, and identifies disease mechanisms at the molecular level. By mapping protein diversity in individual cells, researchers can understand hidden complexity that bulk measurements would obscure, potentially identifying new therapeutic targets and explaining disease pathology.
