Transformer models can predict viscoplastic flows, but they fail in specific regimes. The authors tested RheOFormer on steady Bingham channel flow, a simple setup. Errors appeared in plug-dominated flows and outside the velocity ranges seen in training. Logarithmic velocity inputs and a gradient-aware loss substantially improved accuracy where the baseline failed. Adding a small amount of training data also reduced extrapolation errors.
