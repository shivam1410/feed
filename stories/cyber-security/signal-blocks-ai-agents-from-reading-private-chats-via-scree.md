Signal will restrict screenshots by default to stop AI agents from reading private chats. The change applies on Android and iOS. Signal admits the protection is 'incomplete'. On iOS it covers agentic systems built into iOS 27. On Android it works only where Signal can detect an AI assistant trying to read a chat. It matters because even a privacy-focused app has gaps against AI assistants.
