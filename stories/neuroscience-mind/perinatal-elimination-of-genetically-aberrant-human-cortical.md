Many human brain cells carry genetic errors before birth, and most are largely cleared out around the time of birth. Researchers sequenced single neurons to find this. Widespread genomic abnormalities were common before birth but largely gone afterward. That points to a quality control process running during development. It matters because it suggests the developing brain removes its own damaged cells.
