Simon Willison used Claude Opus 5.5 to build a Bluesky reply bot detector, leveraging the platform's still-open API. The tool identifies bots by detecting replies posted within seconds of other posts, accounts that never post original content, and automated question-mark spam. It's a small but practical demonstration of how open APIs and capable AI models can address real social media problems.
