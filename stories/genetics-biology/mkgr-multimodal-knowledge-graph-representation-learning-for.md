MKGR addresses cold-start protein-protein interaction (PPI) prediction—where candidate proteins lack observed training interactions—through multimodal representation learning. The framework combines region-aware protein sequence encoding with four biomedical knowledge graphs: protein-drug, protein-disease, protein-miRNA, and protein-lncRNA associations. A sequence branch extracts contextual representations from structurally informed regions, while graph attention encoders learn protein embeddings from sparse associations. Bridge reconstruction regularizes graph learning by recovering shared protein-entity associations, and pair-level gating adaptively integrates sequence and graph evidence. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate consistent outperformance across multiple metrics (ACC, F1, AUC, AUPR, MCC) in novel-old and novel-novel cold-start scenarios.
