Whole-brain cellular imaging in zebrafish identified a hierarchical thalamus-brainstem attractor network encoding recent behavioral history. This network biases future decisions: animals that recently succeeded tend to repeat similar choices. The finding reveals neural mechanisms underlying history-dependent decision-making and behavioral bias.
