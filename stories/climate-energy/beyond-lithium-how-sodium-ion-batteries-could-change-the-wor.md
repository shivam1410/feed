Sodium-ion batteries are entering mass production as a potentially transformative alternative to lithium-ion technology. Researchers and manufacturers argue sodium-based systems could offer significant cost advantages and improved safety compared to lithium batteries in electric vehicles and energy storage applications. Sodium is more abundant and geographically distributed than lithium. Large-scale deployment could make energy storage more affordable and accessible. This addresses supply chain vulnerabilities with lithium. As lithium supply remains constrained and costs elevated, sodium-ion technology offers a viable pathway to scale battery production and reduce energy storage costs globally.
