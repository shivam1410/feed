Researchers developed a bioorthogonal click chemistry technique to link therapeutic antibodies directly to drug molecules in living tumors. The in vivo conjugation approach enhanced anti-tumor activity against heterogeneous and treatment-resistant cancers. The method combines antibodies with antibody-drug conjugates through molecular ligation, improving therapeutic effectiveness where standard treatments fail. This advance represents progress in overcoming drug resistance through innovative chemical coupling strategies applied directly within tumor tissue.
