Artificial intelligence discussions about sentience are elevating scientific attention to consciousness research. However, some researchers question whether AI hype benefits the field, concerned that speculative AI consciousness may distract from rigorous human consciousness studies or mislead discourse through sensationalism. This tension reveals how external attention shapes research priorities, potentially diverting resources from foundational questions.
