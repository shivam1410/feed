Researchers asked what really drives scores on the WM-811K wafer map dataset. After cleaning, they kept 25,513 labeled maps across eight classes. The original maps reached a Macro-F1 of 0.8563. Keeping only the layout dropped it to 0.3829. Randomizing failed-die locations within distance rings gave 0.7439. So spatial detail carries much of the signal that the model learns.
