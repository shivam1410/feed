Physics-informed deep learning predicts lithium-ion battery state of health from partial discharge curves across arbitrary voltage ranges, achieving mean absolute percentage error below 4%. The method combines data-driven learning with physically motivated degradation dynamics and introduces real-time degradation trend estimation detecting aging transitions without prior knowledge or historical data. The approach enables state-of-health estimation from heterogeneous operating conditions, overcoming limitations of methods requiring fixed discharge protocols.
