Late-life treatment with a GLP-1 drug—a medication typically used for weight loss and diabetes—extended lifespan in female mice from one inbred laboratory strain. This longevity increase appeared to exceed effects expected from calorie restriction alone, suggesting GLP-1 drugs may directly slow aging processes beyond their metabolic effects. This matters because if validated in other studies, it could reveal a new mechanism for how these increasingly popular drugs might slow age-related decline in humans, motivating research into GLP-1 effects on aging independent of weight loss.
