Researchers built CyberArena, a benchmark where AI agents fight each other in live attack-and-defense rounds. Several agents compete at once, over multiple rounds, in environments that keep changing. Scores come from a dynamical scoring model rather than a fixed checklist. It matters because it tests agents against an active opponent, not just a fixed task.
