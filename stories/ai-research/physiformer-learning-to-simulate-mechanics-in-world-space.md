PhysiFormer uses coordinate-space diffusion to generate physically-plausible 3D object motions without relying on explicit physics equations or hard-coded inductive biases. The model learns to reason about multiple interacting objects and generalizes to complex materials and geometries unseen during training. This matters because accurate physics simulation is computationally expensive and critical for robotics, game development, and digital twins; learning physics implicitly from data could significantly accelerate design workflows.
