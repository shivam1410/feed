RoboQuest tests whether agents can actively explore and inspect the world to gather task-relevant information, not just execute learned skills. Ten manipulation tasks require search, inspection, and interactive testing. The best agent succeeds only 23% of the time, but oddly, isolated execution tests show agents can do most required actions. Analysis reveals the real problem: agents stop exploring too early and miss critical information.
