Railway, founded in 2020, has grown to 3 million users despite a May 19 GCP outage that exposed workload discoverability still being tied to a single cloud provider. The 35-person team adds roughly 100,000 signups weekly. Their value proposition: zero-activation deployment—push code, get a URL, iterate. They've raised $124 million and operate bare metal data centers with 70% margins and three-month payback periods, with hardware appreciating as RAM prices climbed. This demonstrates how simplified deployment infrastructure scales in the AI era when shipping speed matters.
