Lila Sciences operates an automated laboratory—a dark warehouse of robotic equipment, vision-language models, and experimental apparatus—designed to generate scientifically validated training data at massive scale. The lab runs floating automated plates on tracks, uses vision-language models to control legacy systems, and operates 24/7 conducting new experiments. Lila has generated over 10 trillion experimentally-validated reasoning tokens—training data grounded in physical reality rather than synthetic sources. The lab simultaneously pursues discovery across biology, chemistry, drug discovery, and materials science. Lila's thesis follows the bitter lesson: produce data at scale and emergent general reasoning follows. Positions AI-in-the-loop labs as potentially self-improving scientific engines, where experiments generate data that improves models that design better experiments.
