John Platt has an Oscar, two named asteroids, and textbook algorithms you know from sklearn. He discussed Google's Empirical Research Assistance (ERA), which automates solving scientific problems that can be written as score functions. You define the objective, ERA finds the code that maximizes it. The hard part is formulating the score; finding the solution becomes automated search.
