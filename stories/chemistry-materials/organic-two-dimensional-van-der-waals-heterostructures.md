Researchers developed a method for creating two-dimensional van der Waals heterostructures by sequentially assembling 2D polymers on a water surface with layer-by-layer stacking. The technique produces both lattice-matched and deliberately lattice-mismatched structures with precisely defined registry, stacking sequence, and thickness. This breakthrough enables engineering of complex layered materials with customized properties unavailable in single-layer materials, opening possibilities for novel semiconductor, optical, and electronic devices.
