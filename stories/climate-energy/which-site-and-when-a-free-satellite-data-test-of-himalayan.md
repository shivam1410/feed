Free satellite data successfully predicts glacial lake outburst and landslide risk in Nepal using two signals: radar interferometry detecting moraine dam deformation and satellite weather data identifying high-stress periods. Testing 589 documented outburst events against unfailed sites, the model achieved ROC scores of 0.73 for large moraine-dammed bursts, 0.83 for landslides, and 0.82 for smaller glacier-pond floods. Terrain features alone showed poor discrimination (0.76, 0.71, 0.54) when matched against comparable nearby sites using spatial cross-validation. The approach provides cost-effective disaster prediction for vulnerable mountain communities using publicly available satellite data.
