Google introduced AMIE, a research medical AI system capable of conducting real-time clinical video consultations. In a first-of-its-kind study, AMIE demonstrated this capability in simulated clinical settings, interacting with patients through video. This proof-of-concept could eventually support healthcare access and telemedicine, though current work represents controlled environment testing rather than real clinical deployment.
