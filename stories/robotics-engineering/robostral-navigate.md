Robostral Navigate, an 8B vision-language model, navigates using only monocular RGB camera feeds, the most common sensor across robots. Trained on 2.4 million simulated trajectories across 350,000 environments, it predicts waypoints by pointing to targets in current frames, operating in image space rather than robot-specific coordinates. A prefix-caching training recipe reduced tokens 22x, compressing training from months to days. The approach generalizes across wheeled, legged, and aerial robots without recalibration.
