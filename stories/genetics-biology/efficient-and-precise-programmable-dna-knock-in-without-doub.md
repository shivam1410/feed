KNIT (nickase-targeting) editing inserts DNA fragments exceeding 10 kilobases through single-nick edits at multiple genomic locations and cell types with high efficiency and minimal off-target effects. This approach avoids double-strand breaks, reducing unwanted mutations. The technique enables precise, large-scale DNA insertions crucial for correcting genetic disorders and engineering cells, advancing programmable DNA editing without cellular damage from conventional break-repair methods.
