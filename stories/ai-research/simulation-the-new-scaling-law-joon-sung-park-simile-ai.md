Simile AI's $2B Series B, backed by Fei-Fei Li and Andrej Karpathy, demonstrates commercial viability of human behavior simulation. The company runs tens of millions of simulations for Fortune 100 clients like CVS, achieving 85–99% accuracy compared to human focus groups. Building on the 2023 "Smallville" generative agents paper demonstrating emergent AI character behaviors, Simile uses long-form interviews, observational data, randomized controlled trials, and causal mechanism post-training to create digital twins modeling individual and population behavior. The underlying ambition: eventually simulate decision-making patterns for all 8 billion people.
