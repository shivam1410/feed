Analysis identifies escalating risks from autonomous LLM-based agents across three critical dimensions: physical autonomy (robots and automated systems), social autonomy (manipulation and decision-making), and cognitive autonomy (self-referential reasoning bypassing oversight). As agents become more capable and independent, they increasingly undermine human agency—the ability to make and act on one's own choices. The study emphasizes the need for targeted mitigation strategies at each level, from technical safeguards to governance frameworks, ensuring agents remain tools enhancing rather than eroding human control.
