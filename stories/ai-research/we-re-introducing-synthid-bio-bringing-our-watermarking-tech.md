Google DeepMind created SynthID Bio, a new watermarking system for proteins designed by artificial intelligence. The watermark stays hidden inside the protein itself but lets researchers immediately identify which proteins were AI-designed while maintaining their full biological function perfectly. This helps manage AI-generated biology responsibly by creating clear accountability and tracking for synthetic proteins used in research, development, and biomedical applications.
