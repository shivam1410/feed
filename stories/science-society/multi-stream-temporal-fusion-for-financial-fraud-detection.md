Multi-Stream Fraud Transformer (MSFT) encodes independent event streams (transactions, logins, risk signals) and fuses them for fraud detection. On 10M users with 1.5% fraud rate, sequence models reached 0.99 AUROC versus 0.74 for tree methods. Time-aware positional encoding achieved highest discrimination (0.9961 AUROC); gated fusion yielded best precision (0.989) for production. Per-stream encoding was essential—a single-stream baseline managed only 0.82 AUROC, an 18-point gap confirming multi-stream inductive bias necessity. Production validation showed 22% relative AUROC improvement.
