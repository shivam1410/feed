Researchers developed pangenome reference methods that improve genetic variant discovery in the Icelandic population by reducing bias from single-reference genomes and enabling better identification of variants in genomic regions previously difficult to sequence or map. The new Icelandic pangenome revealed numerous novel genetic variants, including pathogenic alleles that cause disease. This approach constructs population-specific haplotypes and maps population-scale reads more accurately than traditional methods. The findings advance precision medicine by providing population-specific genetic references that capture human diversity better than standard single-genome approaches, accelerating discovery of disease mutations.
