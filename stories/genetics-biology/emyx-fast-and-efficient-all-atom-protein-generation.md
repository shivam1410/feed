Emyx is a 140-million-parameter conditional flow matching model for all-atom protein generation that outperforms much larger systems in enzyme design. On the AME enzyme design benchmark, Emyx beats both Proteina-Complexa and RFdiffusion3, achieving higher success rates under strict evaluation requiring correct global fold recovery and catalytic geometry accuracy. Remarkably, Emyx trained in 682 GPU-hours—roughly four times less than RFdiffusion3. The model achieves efficiency by concentrating capacity in standard transformer blocks, replacing heavy embedding stacks with lightweight conditional representations.
