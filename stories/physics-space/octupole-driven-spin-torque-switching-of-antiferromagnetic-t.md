Researchers describe a magnetic tunnel junction built entirely from antiferromagnets. Such junctions can show a large TMR, a strong magnetic readout. They can also be switched by a torque driven by octupole magnetism. The text says this could enable nanoscale magnetic memory and, potentially, room-temperature terahertz devices.
