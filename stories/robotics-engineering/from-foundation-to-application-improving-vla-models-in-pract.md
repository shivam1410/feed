LingBot-VLA 2.0 significantly enhances generalization of vision-language-action robot models across diverse tasks and robot embodiments. The model incorporates expanded data preprocessing and training on multiple distinct robot configurations, substantially improving transfer between different hardware platforms and physical form factors. It extends the action space to include whole-body degrees of freedom, enabling execution of more complex manipulation tasks beyond prior arm-only approaches. The architecture integrates predictive dynamics modeling using video representations and depth estimation, improving temporal reasoning about future states during extended manipulation sequences. These cumulative enhancements enable more capable general-purpose robot policies that perform robustly across varied tasks, different embodiments, and complex manipulation scenarios.
