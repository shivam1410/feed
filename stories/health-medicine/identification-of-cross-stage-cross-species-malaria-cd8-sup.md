Immunopeptidomics identified malaria parasite antigens preserved across multiple Plasmodium species and expressed throughout the parasite's life cycle. These cross-stage, cross-species T cell targets represent conserved epitopes suitable for vaccine development. Targeting these invariant antigens present during multiple transmission stages and across diverse malaria species could generate broad immunity against variant strains. The discovery addresses a central vaccine challenge: identifying immunological targets that remain constant despite parasite diversity and life-cycle transitions.
