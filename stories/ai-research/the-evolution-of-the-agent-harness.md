Around Christmas 2025, agents began reliably working—the result of models crossing capability thresholds while harness tooling matured simultaneously. As models absorb harness capabilities into weights, engineers delete the absorbed pieces, gradually leaving only a harness for human attention rather than model control. This co-evolution of model and harness explains recent capability jumps and predicts future agent progress.
