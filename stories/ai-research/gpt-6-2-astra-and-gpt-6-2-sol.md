OpenAI made GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol available in the API as gpt-6.2-astra and gpt-6.2-sol. Astra costs $8 per million input tokens and $40 for output, which is 80% of the old Astra price. Cached input falls to $0.40, down from $1. Sol stays at $2 and $10, half the price of GPT-5.6 Sol. On an animation test, both models did a weaker job than Claude Fable 5.2 and Gemini 4 Carbon. Why it matters: cheaper cached input helps long agent sessions.
