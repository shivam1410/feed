The dorsolateral prefrontal cortex follows a non-linear, cell-type-specific aging pattern: dynamic transcriptional changes during development, relative molecular stability in midlife, then selective gene reactivation in late adulthood. Different cell types age at different rates and trajectories. This reveals aging is not uniform across brain regions or cell populations and suggests specific cellular pathways reactivate late in life.
