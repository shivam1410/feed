Using liquid-cell transmission electron microscopy, researchers visualized competitive interfacial reactions inside high-energy lithium-sulfur batteries under realistic operating conditions. They observed concentration-driven phase segregation within high-concentration layers at the electrode-electrolyte interface when electrolyte supply is limited. These observations reveal how different chemical phases compete and segregate at boundaries during battery operation. Understanding interfacial mechanisms is critical for improving lithium-sulfur battery performance, which promises much higher energy density than conventional lithium-ion batteries.
