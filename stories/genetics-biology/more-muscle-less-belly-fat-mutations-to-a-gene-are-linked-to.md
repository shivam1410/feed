Analysis of one million diverse genomes identified genetic variants associated with lower risk of diabetes, heart disease, and other cardiometabolic conditions, linked to increased muscle mass and reduced abdominal fat. These findings reveal genetic mechanisms underlying metabolic health and suggest that the relationship between body composition and cardiometabolic disease has genetic underpinnings.
