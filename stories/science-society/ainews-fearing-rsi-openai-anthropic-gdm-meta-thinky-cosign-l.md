Over 1,000 employees from frontier AI labs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta, others—excluding X.ai) signed a statement requesting U.S. government support for international mechanisms to deliberately pace frontier AI development. The signatories argue that advances in AI research automation risk rapidly accelerating capability development beyond human control, and that companies face intense competitive pressure to accelerate unilaterally rather than slow. They request government help developing technical and governance tools to allow time for risk mitigation and oversight strengthening. This represents significant industry consensus on coordinated development pacing.
