Leash Bio is turning a messy lab method into clean data for AI. Their method is DNA-encoded libraries (DELs), which measure how proteins and small molecules interact at very high throughput. The team found a way to get clean, reproducible results from it. Years of collected data could support tens of thousands of lab-in-the-loop simulations. That led to Minos, an AI playground where agents can practice verifiable scientific reasoning. Agents need that kind of checkable data to learn science through reinforcement learning.
