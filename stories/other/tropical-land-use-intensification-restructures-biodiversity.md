Tropical land-use intensification drives substantial biodiversity loss and reorganizes community composition, with aboveground assemblages showing an increase in nestedness in monocultures compared with forests, underscoring the need for landscape-scale, multi-taxon conservation that integrates agricultural and natural habitats.
