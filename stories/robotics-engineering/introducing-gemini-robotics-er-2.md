DeepMind introduced Gemini Robotics ER 2, representing significant advances in robotic capability. The system demonstrates improved video understanding—interpreting visual scenes and action sequences—enhanced tool orchestration enabling robots to manipulate multiple instruments, and better multi-robot collaboration for coordinated action. Video understanding lets robots perceive complex environments. Tool orchestration enables multi-step procedures requiring sequential actions. Multi-robot collaboration enables complex tasks impossible for single robots. These capabilities represent a step change—significant advances beyond previous performance. Progress in these areas is essential for robots to handle real-world applications from manufacturing to service work and deployment.
