AI-assisted math is moving quickly. Two papers claimed proofs that VP is not VNP, an algebraic cousin of P versus NP. Another paper claimed OpenAI's matrix multiplication exponent omega is exactly 9/4. A further paper claimed matching bounds for the finite Fourier transform. Yupan Liu showed two messages suffice for quantum interactive proofs, and Aaronson says Liu used AI. It matters because AI-assisted proofs now appear faster than people can read them.
