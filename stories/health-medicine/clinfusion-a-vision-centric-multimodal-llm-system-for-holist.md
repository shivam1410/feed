ClinFusion, a vision-centric multimodal LLM, processes heterogeneous 2D and 3D medical images via a Cascade Spatial-Aware Locality Fusion encoder. It achieves state-of-the-art performance on medical imaging benchmarks, outperforming open-source models on 20 of 24 tasks and leading proprietary systems on 13 of 16 benchmarks. A vision-grounded evaluation framework (MedIF-Bench, region-of-interest-grounded assessment) ensures alignment with radiologists' clinical workflows and factualness-driven evaluation, addressing deployment challenges in medical imaging.
