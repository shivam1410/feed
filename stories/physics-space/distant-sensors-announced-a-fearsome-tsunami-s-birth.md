Seismic sensors detected and characterized the underwater collapse of a volcanic structure that generated a destructive tsunami. By analyzing earthquake data from distant monitoring stations, researchers reconstructed the precise eruption mechanism and traced the sequence leading from volcanic collapse to tsunami generation. This analysis demonstrates how far-field sensor networks pinpoint catastrophic geological events and their triggers, providing critical data for understanding tsunami formation from submarine volcanoes. Such knowledge improves coastal hazard prediction.
