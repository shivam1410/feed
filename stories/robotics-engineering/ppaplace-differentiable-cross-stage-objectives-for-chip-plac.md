Chip placement optimization shows a near-zero correlation between standard half-perimeter wirelength optimization and actual post-route timing performance, explaining why AI placers degrade performance versus baselines. PPAPlace instead directly predicts post-route power-performance-area metrics using a dual-stream neural network combining graph attention and spatial convolution, trained on post-global-routing labels. Predicted timing gradients flow end-to-end to optimize macro and cell placement.
