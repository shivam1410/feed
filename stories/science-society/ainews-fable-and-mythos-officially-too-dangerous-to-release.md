Anthropic revoked Fable and Mythos for all customers globally following a US government directive citing national cybersecurity risk from a potential jailbreak, just three days after their initial release. Anthropic disputed the decision, stating it received only "verbal evidence of a potential narrow, non-universal jailbreak" and considered it a misunderstanding. This marks an unprecedented precedent: the first global model revocation affecting all customers regardless of jurisdiction, distinct from prior government-specific restrictions. The event has reignited open-source AI advocacy debates around model sovereignty and government overreach, making this consequential regardless of the actual technical legitimacy of the jailbreak claim.
