NanoEQL, a white-box neural network, reveals nanocrystal size determination as a linear equation of three interpretable scalars: nanocrystallization capability (-Zp), growth capability (Zrea), and external input potential (-Zops). Eight mathematical operators replace standard activation functions, with temperature-gated attention pooling encoding concentration-driven and reactivity-driven synthesis mechanisms. This approach advances rational nanocrystal design and establishes a paradigm for deciphering chemical reaction mechanisms through interpretable machine learning.
