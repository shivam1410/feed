DeepMind introduced a cognitive framework to measure progress toward artificial general intelligence (AGI), establishing concrete benchmarks rather than relying on informal assessments. The framework outlines cognitive capabilities required for AGI and measurement approaches. Complementing this, DeepMind launched a Kaggle hackathon to crowdsource development of evaluation metrics and datasets. Establishing measurable, community-vetted benchmarks helps the research community objectively track progress rather than relying on subjective claims as organizations pursue AGI-level systems.
