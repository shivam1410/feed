India's cyber emergency team, CERT-In, has warned about AI-assisted attacks. Threat actors are targeting the country's information and communication technology assets. The attacks have caused unauthorized financial transactions at banks, financial services firms, and tech service providers. Those payments were never approved, so the losses are real and already happening.
