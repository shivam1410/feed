Meta Probabilistic Pooling GNN (MPP-GNN) achieves state-of-the-art Alzheimer's classification by discovering subject-specific brain modules rather than assuming preset connectivity patterns. The hierarchical adaptive partitioning algorithm uses discovered modules as explicit priors for edge refinement. Testing on two public datasets achieved highest AUC versus established baselines. Results demonstrate significant alignment with canonical Yeo atlas functional networks and reveal network-level dedifferentiation patterns in AD, addressing inter-subject variability overlooked by previous graph neural network approaches.
