An enzymatic platform has been developed to synthesize thiophosphate molecular motifs without expensive reagents, creating a foundation for enzymatic cascades. This advance lowers barriers to incorporating thiophosphate-containing molecules—important scaffolds in drug development—by replacing costly chemical synthesis with sustainable enzyme-based methods.
