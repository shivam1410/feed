Concept-Wrapper Network, an explainable AI system, was deployed on real autonomous vehicles to make decision-making transparent to drivers. The concept-based explanation approach helped drivers better understand and predict vehicle behavior in various scenarios. When humans grasp how autonomous systems make decisions and what features they consider, they develop more accurate mental models and greater trust. This work addresses a critical barrier to autonomous vehicle adoption: building sufficient human understanding and confidence in autonomous systems for widespread acceptance.
