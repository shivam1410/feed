Immunotherapy using engineered CAR T cells targeting GPC3 protein demonstrated enhanced efficacy in advanced hepatocellular carcinoma patients using a dominant-negative TGFβ receptor II ('armoured' cells). The cells showed improved anti-tumor activity and tolerable safety profiles. Previous treatments had failed in these patients. The engineering strategy counteracts tumor immunosuppression mechanisms. CAR T cell therapy represents a powerful cancer treatment approach. This advancement improves CAR T cell performance against a challenging cancer type. The approach of armoring CAR T cells could potentially be applied to other cancer types and difficult-to-treat malignancies.
