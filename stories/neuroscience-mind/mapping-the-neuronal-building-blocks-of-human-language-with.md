Scientists used wide-scale brain recordings to identify neurons that encode fundamental language components: grammatical relationships between words, parts of speech, and higher-order syntactic structure of phrases and sentences. This mapping reveals where language building blocks are neurally represented. Understanding how the brain encodes grammar and sentence structure provides insights applicable to both neuroscience and artificial language systems design.
