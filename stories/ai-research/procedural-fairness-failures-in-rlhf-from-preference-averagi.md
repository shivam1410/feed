Standard RLHF aggregates heterogeneous human preferences into a single reward model, causing majority preference groups to dominate learning while minority preferences are systematically under-represented. Preference-Aware RLHF (PA-RLHF) separates optimization across different preference modes during reward learning. In controlled experiments, PA-RLHF improved overall alignment accuracy from 46.9% to 67.9% and reduced fairness gap from 15.9 to 9.6 percentage points. Results demonstrate procedural fairness failures arise from structural design choices in standard RLHF.
