Anthropic showed off dynamic workflows, called flows, at AI Engineer NYC. Flows are a new kind of multiagent orchestration built for large, ambitious jobs. Presenters CJ Avilla and Harrison Stall, both members of technical staff, framed it with history. They pointed to Ken Thompson building the original Unix and to Linux moving from one maintainer to layers of trusted maintainers. Their lesson is that agents need the same organization that big human projects did. The feature is brand new.
