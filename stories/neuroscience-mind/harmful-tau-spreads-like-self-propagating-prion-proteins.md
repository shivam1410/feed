Tau proteins spread disease like a self-replicating error in the brain. When tau misfolds into an abnormal shape, it acts as a template that forces other healthy tau proteins to misfold in the same way, starting a damaging cascade. Structural studies confirm this prion-like behavior explains disease progression. Stopping this spread could be key to treating Alzheimer's and other neurodegenerative conditions that destroy brain tissue.
