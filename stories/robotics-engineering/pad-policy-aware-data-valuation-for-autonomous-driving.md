PAD scores each driving demonstration by how much it helps a specific planner. More data doesn't always help, since one demo can help one planner and hurt another. With DiffusionDrive, PAD beat real-only training by 3.5 points on NavTest and 5.0 on NavHard. It worked across diffusion, regression, and flow-matching planners. It matters because teams can pick data that actually improves a driving policy.
