Analysis of 25 years of satellite data reveals Earth maintains a persistent east-west albedo symmetry divided at 27° E longitude. Clear-sky albedo, cloud radiative effect and open-ocean fraction all exhibit triple symmetry around this meridian, suggesting fundamental atmospheric and oceanographic organization. This geometric pattern reflects deep climate system structure. Understanding such symmetries refines climate models and energy balance calculations critical for predicting how climate responds to changing conditions.
