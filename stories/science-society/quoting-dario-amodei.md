Dario Amodei attributes public distrust of AI to decades-long institutional trust deficits rather than warnings about AI risks from industry leaders. He argues that glossy marketing campaigns promoting AI benefits lack credibility, noting that claims about AI curing cancer have become clichés perceived as deceptive by the public. Instead, Amodei contends that actual, tangible delivery of benefits to society—not messaging strategies or marketing narratives—will rebuild trust in the technology and companies. He acknowledges that Anthropic and other AI companies deserve legitimate criticism for failing to fulfill major promises to benefit the world.
