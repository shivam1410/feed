High-throughput screening systematically mapped mutations in the STING signaling protein to understand immune function. Results reveal molecular principles controlling STING activity across diverse immune contexts. STING activation drives antiviral and anti-cancer responses, so understanding how mutations tune its function could guide development of immunotherapies targeting infectious diseases and cancer.
