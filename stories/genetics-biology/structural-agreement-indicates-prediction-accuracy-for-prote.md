Confidence scores are a weak guide for protein-ligand predictions. Across seven models, picking by confidence barely beats choosing at random. About three-quarters of structures are either always right or always wrong. The team's CORE Score measures how tightly predictions agree and how well they are supported. It reaches a ROC AUC of 0.866 for top-1 success. The standard confidence score, ipTM, reaches 0.757. CORE needs no experimental ligand pose.
