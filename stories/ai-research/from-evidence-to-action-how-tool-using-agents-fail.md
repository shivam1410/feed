Benchmark of 656 cases shows tool-using agents fail not just from missing information but from how they use evidence. Agents act with incomplete investigation or before required evidence exists. Once evidence is gathered, single actions usually work but multi-action workflows expose unmet prerequisites. Matters because correct outcomes hide broken reasoning chains that will fail in new situations.
