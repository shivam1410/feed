Physics-informed conditional normalizing flows solve orbit determination in cislunar space (Earth-Moon region) from limited angle measurements over short observation periods. The method estimates probability distributions of spacecraft initial states, capturing multimodal uncertainty. Normalizing flows trained on perturbed observations from Near Rectilinear Halo Orbits generate statistically consistent state hypotheses that serve as high-quality warm starts for classical optimization algorithms, improving convergence in space situational awareness applications.
