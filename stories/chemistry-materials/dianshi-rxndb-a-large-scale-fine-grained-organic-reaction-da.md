DianShi-RxnDB is an automated platform that extracts and structures millions of organic reactions from patent databases. The system normalizes this data for AI-driven chemistry research. This matters because it democratizes access to vast chemical knowledge previously locked in unstructured patents, enabling researchers and AI agents to discover new synthesis routes and design novel molecules at scale.
