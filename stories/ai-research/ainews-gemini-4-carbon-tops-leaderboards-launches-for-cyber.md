Google DeepMind launched Gemini 4 Carbon, which the newsletter says now tops independent leaderboards. Its claimed cyber results are 88.2% on SEC-bench Pro for finding vulnerabilities, 54.8% on ExploitGym for exploitation, and 71.0% on CWE-bench for patching. For now, access is limited to trusted cyber defenders through the Fairwind Program. Pricing is unclear: one figure is $2 and $10 per million input and output tokens, while Artificial Analysis lists $4 and $20 with a launch discount.
