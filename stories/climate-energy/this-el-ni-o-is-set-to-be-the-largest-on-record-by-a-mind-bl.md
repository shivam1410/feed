Forecasters predict an approaching El Niño will reach record magnitude by a 'mind-blowing margin,' surpassing all previously observed El Niño events. Combined with ongoing global warming, these effects will drive global temperatures in 2027 to unprecedented levels. This exceptional climate pattern raises concerns about extreme weather, agricultural impacts, and climate tipping points, potentially making 2027 the warmest year on record.
