They identified two types of unstated agent questions: horizontal proactivity finds missing information already relevant to the task, while vertical proactivity pursues needs that only emerge from earlier findings. Their Q&D method trains smarter questioning, outperforming a model fifteen times larger on multi-step questions while asking fewer, and completing more customer-service tasks.
