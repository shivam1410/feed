Scientists used interpretability techniques to find which neurons in a brain-reading speech decoder help decode imagined words using knowledge from spoken words. Small groups of neurons carry this cross-modal information, concentrated in later layers where condition-specific early neurons begin to overlap. Vocalized speech provides the strongest signal for decoding covert speech.
