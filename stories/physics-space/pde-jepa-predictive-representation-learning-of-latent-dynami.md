Researchers built PDE-JEPA, an AI system that predicts how physical systems evolve by learning from trajectories rather than just reconstructing snapshots. The approach combines masked prediction, geometry alignment with actual physics, and parameter-aware dynamics. On nine benchmarks, it beat existing methods by 33.4% on standard tests and 51.4% when extrapolating to unseen conditions. This matters because accurate physical models are essential for climate simulations, engineering design, and understanding complex systems.
