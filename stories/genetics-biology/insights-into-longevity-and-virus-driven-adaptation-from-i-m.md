Comparative genomic analysis of Myotis bat species identified distinctive evolutionary adaptations in both virus-interacting proteins and longevity-associated biological pathways, revealing fundamental connections between these two hallmarks of bat biology. These specialized adaptations explain why bats tolerate viruses better than other mammals while achieving exceptional lifespans relative to body size. The findings illuminate how viral adaptation and extended longevity represent interconnected evolutionary traits.
