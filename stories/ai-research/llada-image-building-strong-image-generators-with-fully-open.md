LLaDA-Image unifies a 6-billion-parameter diffusion transformer with frozen vision-language modules to generate photorealistic images with precise editing. Trained with image-only pretraining and the Muon optimizer, it's distilled into a fast 2-4 step variant achieving state-of-the-art open-source results. This matters because it demonstrates that competitive image quality is achievable with fully transparent training recipes, potentially democratizing high-quality generative capabilities beyond proprietary systems.
