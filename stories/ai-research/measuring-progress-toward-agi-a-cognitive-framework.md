Google DeepMind has proposed a cognitive framework for evaluating progress toward artificial general intelligence, addressing a critical need for standardized measurement of AGI capabilities. The framework provides structured methodology for assessing AI systems across cognitive dimensions and general-purpose performance benchmarks. Concurrently, DeepMind launched a Kaggle hackathon to crowdsource development of AGI capability benchmarks, engaging the broader AI research community in defining measurable milestones and evaluation standards. This approach promotes transparency in AI progress assessment and establishes shared evaluation standards as AI systems approach greater general-purpose capability levels.
