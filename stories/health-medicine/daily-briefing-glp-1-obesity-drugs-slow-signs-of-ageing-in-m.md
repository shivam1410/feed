Mice treated with GLP-1 obesity drugs demonstrated slower aging progression and superior cognitive performance compared to mice on calorie-restricted diets alone. Satellite imagery also revealed early warning signs of glacier collapse near the Nepal-Tibet border, and researchers documented reduced speech frequency in people. These diverse findings suggest GLP-1 medications may have broader physiological effects extending well beyond obesity treatment.
