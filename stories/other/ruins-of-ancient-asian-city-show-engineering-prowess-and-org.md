The 5,000-year-old city of Liangzhu reveals sophisticated engineering and organization of ancient peoples. Built in challenging waterlogged environments, inhabitants constructed elaborate mounds, dams, and city walls demonstrating advanced hydraulic engineering and urban planning. The structures required coordinated labor organization for large-scale construction. This site provides compelling archaeological evidence that complex civilizations with coordinated infrastructure existed earlier and across more geographically diverse locations than previously documented.
