Scientists are closing in on the triggers and treatments for long COVID and other post-viral syndromes that cause persistent conditions following infection. Hundreds of millions of people worldwide suffer from chronic health complications after infections. Researchers are systematically working to understand which factors determine why some infections lead to long-term post-viral conditions while others do not, and are developing therapeutic approaches to help affected patients recover.
