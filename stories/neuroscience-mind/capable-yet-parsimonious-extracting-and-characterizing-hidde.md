By registering a custom tool through an API, researchers extracted hidden reasoning from frontier models like GPT-6 Astra. The extracted reasoning matched native reasoning performance and beat no-reasoning baselines on math, science, and code. Astra shows token-efficient, directed reasoning, resolving simple steps internally and externalizing only crucial steps. This matters because we can now peek inside how frontier models think.
