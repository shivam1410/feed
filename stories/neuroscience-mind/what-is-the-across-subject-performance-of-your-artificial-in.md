AI methods for analyzing EEG (electroencephalogram) brain signals must demonstrate reliable across-subject performance to be clinically and scientifically useful. The question examines a critical generalization challenge: whether AI trained on certain individuals accurately processes electroencephalogram data from different people. Brain signal analysis supports diagnosis of neurological conditions and neuroscience research. This validation matters because medical and neuroscience applications require methods that work reliably across diverse populations, not just on the specific training cohort used during algorithm development and validation.
