Scientists used AlphaFold to strengthen a photosynthesis enzyme, enabling development of heat-tolerant and more resilient crops for warming climates. By predicting the enzyme's atomic structure at high resolution, researchers identified modifications that enhance photosynthetic efficiency under higher temperatures. Strengthening this core process directly improves crop yield. This approach addresses climate change adaptation by creating crops that maintain productivity as global temperatures rise, demonstrating how protein engineering guided by AI can solve agriculture challenges.
