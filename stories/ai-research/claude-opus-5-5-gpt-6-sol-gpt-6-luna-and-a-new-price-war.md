Anthropic and OpenAI released new models on the same day, triggering a fierce price war. GPT-6 Luna costs half as much as its GPT-5.6 predecessor—just $0.10 per million input tokens versus $0.20. GPT-6 Sol similarly halved in price to $2/$10. These represent the cheapest mainstream models available ($0.10/$0.50 for Luna's input/output). Meanwhile, Claude Opus 5.5 remains at $4/$20 and Grok 4.7 at $2/$6. The significance lies in dramatically lower barriers to building AI applications—especially important because GPT-5.6 pricing faces a 25% increase in November, making the newer, cheaper models far more attractive for cost-conscious developers building production systems.
