OpenAI's internal model (speculated to be GPT-5.6) disproved a mathematical conjecture standing for 80 years—the planar unit distance problem, posed by Paul Erdős in 1946. The model solved the problem in under 32 hours for less than $1,000 using general-purpose reasoning rather than specialized math systems, producing 125 pages of output. Notably, the model discovered a disproof rather than a proof, which would have been even more impressive. This differs from previous achievements because those used domain-specific systems; this result came from an off-the-shelf reasoning model, suggesting extended reasoning may generalize beyond mathematics.
