Scientists created a detailed map of how genetic risk for brain disorders affects different cell types in the prefrontal cortex using single-nucleus transcriptomics data collected across diverse human populations. They discovered previously hidden connections between specific genes and brain disease traits within particular cell types. This reveals which neurons are most vulnerable to genetic risk factors, directly pointing toward more targeted and effective treatments for brain disease.
