In mixed-halide perovskite solar cells, bromide and iodide domains crystallize at different rates, creating structural defects that limit performance. Researchers solved this by adding a specialized molecule that selectively binds bromide-bound lead, synchronizing crystallization rates between domains. This approach produced uniform films with improved operational stability and increased efficiency in perovskite-organic tandem solar cells, demonstrating how precise chemical control enhances solar cell performance.
