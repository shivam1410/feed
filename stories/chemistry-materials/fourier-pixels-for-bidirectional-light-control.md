Miniaturized diffractive optical elements enable pixels that fully control the amplitude, phase, and polarization of light wavefronts. Based on Fourier-optics principles, these multifunctional pixels can both generate and sense complex light patterns. This matters for photonic applications ranging from advanced displays to sensing systems, enabling new possibilities in optical computing and communication.
