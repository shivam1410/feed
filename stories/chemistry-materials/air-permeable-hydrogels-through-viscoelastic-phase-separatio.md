Silica aerogel beads embedded in high-water-content hydrogels create non-collapsible, air-rich networks that allow oxygen to permeate through the material. The new hydrogel achieves a tenfold increase in oxygen permeability compared to standard hydrogels. This advancement could improve oxygen delivery in medical applications like wound healing and tissue engineering.
