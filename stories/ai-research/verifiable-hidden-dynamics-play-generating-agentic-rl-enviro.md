They built a system that generates diverse reinforcement learning environments from solved math models, then uses them to train agents. Generated 3,300 environments at a few cents each and improved Qwen3.6-35B's agentic score from 0.204 to 0.815. The gains transferred to external benchmarks for planning and e-commerce. Matters because training diverse environments at scale is a bottleneck for agent development.
