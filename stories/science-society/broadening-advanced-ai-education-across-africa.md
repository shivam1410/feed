Google DeepMind, Google.org, and Google Skills jointly launched a free, high-impact AI curriculum to train researchers and educators across Africa. The program addresses the shortage of AI expertise in an underrepresented region. Expanding AI talent pools builds local capacity for innovation and ensures diverse perspectives in AI development.
