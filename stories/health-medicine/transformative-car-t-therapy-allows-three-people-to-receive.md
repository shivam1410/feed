CAR-T cell therapy enables kidney transplants for previously ineligible patients. Three people with highly sensitized immune systems—who normally reject donated organs and face limited survival options—successfully received kidney transplants using modified CAR-T cells that suppress rejection responses. This breakthrough could expand transplant access to thousands of immune-resistant patients.
