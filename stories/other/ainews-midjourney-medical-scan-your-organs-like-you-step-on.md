Midjourney announced a medical imaging device using 358,000 ultrasonic elements arranged in a ring configuration to create full-body ultrasound scans without radiation exposure. CEO David Holz positioned it as the first fundamentally new whole-body imaging modality in 50 years, comparing performance to MRI, X-ray, or CT approaches. The system comprises 8,960 transducers per chip with 40 systems in a 70 cm diameter ring. While observers compared the launch to iPhone and Tesla debuts in ambition, significant questions persist about clinical readiness and diagnostic utility.
