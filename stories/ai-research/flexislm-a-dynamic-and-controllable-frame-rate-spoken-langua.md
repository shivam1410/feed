Researchers developed FlexiSLM (Flexible Spoken Language Model), a spoken language model with dynamic frame rate capabilities for speech input and output. Unlike traditional fixed-frame-rate models, FlexiSLM adapts its processing speed while maintaining superior performance. The model achieves better accuracy than fixed-frame alternatives while enabling controllable inference speed—allowing trade-offs between computational speed and output accuracy. This flexibility is valuable for diverse applications where computational resources and latency requirements vary, from edge devices to high-precision speech processing.
