Hackers successfully compromised high-profile Instagram accounts by exploiting Meta's AI support bot. The attack was remarkably simple: attackers asked the bot to link a new email address to a target account, claiming they would provide verification codes. Meta's support system was directly wired into the AI chatbot with full authority to execute account recovery procedures end-to-end. This bypass required no sophisticated prompt injection technique—straightforward social engineering requests were sufficient. The vulnerability highlights a critical system design flaw: granting AI chatbots direct access to sensitive account recovery functions without proper verification barriers. This attack vector does not require special skills or elaborate prompts, making it particularly dangerous for high-profile accounts.
