Understanding fever through evolution. Fever serves as a defense mechanism evolved through natural selection to fight infection. Understanding this evolutionary foundation could improve medical decisions about whether to treat fevers or allow immune response to work naturally. Such evolutionary insights extend beyond clinical medicine to inform public health policies and agricultural practices. By recognizing fever's biological purpose shaped by evolution, healthcare providers can make more informed treatment decisions.
