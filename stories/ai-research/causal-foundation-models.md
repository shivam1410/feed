Causal Foundation Models apply the foundation model paradigm to causal inference, enabling pretrained networks to estimate causal quantities—such as average treatment effects—on new datasets via in-context learning without fine-tuning. This shifts from requiring custom pipelines for each problem. This matters because it generalizes causal inference across epidemiology, economics, and policy evaluation, potentially accelerating research requiring causal estimation.
