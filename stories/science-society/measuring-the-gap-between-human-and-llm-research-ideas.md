Large language models generate research ideas that cluster narrowly around specific opportunity patterns, while human researchers explore significantly broader and more diverse opportunity spaces. Distributional analysis reveals systematic divergence in creative exploration between models and humans, suggesting LLMs may reinforce existing directions rather than discovering fundamentally novel areas.
