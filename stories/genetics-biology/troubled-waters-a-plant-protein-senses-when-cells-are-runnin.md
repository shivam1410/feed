The plant protein SAM8 senses cellular water availability through phase separation, a process that depends on hydration levels. When water availability changes, SAM8 undergoes phase separation, which then regulates gene expression in response. This mechanism reveals how cells sense and respond to water status at the molecular level. The discovery is important because water availability fundamentally affects plant physiology and survival, informing research on drought response and plant adaptation.
