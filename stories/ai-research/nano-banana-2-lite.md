Google released Nano Banana 2 Lite (Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite), marketed as 'the fastest and cheapest Gemini image model, engineered for velocity and scale.' The model targets high-volume, cost-sensitive image generation. Testing with a where's Waldo style prompt requesting a raccoon holding a ham radio produced visually appealing results that improved over earlier Nano Banana models. However, the generated image misspelled 'Forest Festival' in two different ways, illustrating remaining quality trade-offs. The model prioritizes throughput and cost over perfect accuracy.
