Deep-foam photolithography employs light-controlled polymer foaming to fabricate high-resolution three-dimensional microstructures with multiple integrated properties. The process enables precise creation of structures with tunable optical properties, wetting characteristics, and fluid-handling capabilities. This biomimetic manufacturing platform combines high resolution with functional versatility, enabling simultaneous engineering of multiple material properties in single structures. The approach opens possibilities for advanced manufacturing applications across microelectronics, biomedical devices, and other fields requiring sophisticated microstructures.
