Google introduced AMIE, a medical AI system designed for real-time clinical video consultations. In a first-of-its-kind study, AMIE demonstrated the ability to engage in simulated clinical conversations via video. The system aims to provide clinical decision support or patient consultation in real-time settings. This matters because AI-assisted consultation could extend care access to underserved regions, reduce physician workload during routine consultations, and provide consistent clinical reasoning support.
