AIDE^2 implements recursive self-improvement: an AI research agent autonomously modifies its own code, benchmarks changes, and retains improvements. Over an 8-day run, it discovered seven successive improvements including new search policies and context-management mechanisms. The evolved agent matched human-engineered production agents across four held-out benchmarks spanning machine learning, algorithm engineering, and weather forecasting.
