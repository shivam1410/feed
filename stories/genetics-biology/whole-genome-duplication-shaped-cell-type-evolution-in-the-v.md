Comparative single-cell RNA analysis across five vertebrate species—humans, mice, lizards, lampreys, and amphioxus—demonstrates that ancient whole-genome duplication events and resulting gene duplicates (ohnologues) were essential drivers of cell-type diversification during early vertebrate brain evolution, illuminating how genetic innovation fueled nervous system complexity.
