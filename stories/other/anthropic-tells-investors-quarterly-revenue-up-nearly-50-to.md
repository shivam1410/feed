Anthropic told investors its third-quarter revenue reached $16.8bn. Per the FT, people with knowledge of the matter say that is up nearly 50% on the prior quarter. Q2 was $11.5bn, and Q1 was about $4.8bn. In Q3, the company earned $1.70 in revenue for every $1 spent on computing power. Adjusted operating margins, excluding stock-based compensation, were 11%. Anthropic also said distillation made up 5 to 7% of revenue, and it cut that back materially. The numbers come ahead of an IPO.
