Cross-ecosystem analyses spanning marine, lake, stream, and soil systems show that food web complexity—not just biodiversity alone—explains how species richness affects ecosystem functioning. The study measured multi-trophic relationships and their connection to ecosystem processes like productivity and nutrient cycling. This reveals the mechanistic link between biodiversity and ecosystem services.
