Rhetorical framing—how research papers are written—significantly influences AI scientific reviewer scores in predictable ways. Bias varies by reviewer identity, score range, and evaluation strictness, but is not driven by rewriting complexity; identical content receives different scores based on presentation alone. This matters because AI-assisted peer review increasingly manages scientific submissions. If rhetorical framing systematically biases AI reviewers more than humans, it could reward presentation over scientific merit, disadvantaging authors who communicate differently. This undermines the objectivity and fairness of automated scientific evaluation systems.
