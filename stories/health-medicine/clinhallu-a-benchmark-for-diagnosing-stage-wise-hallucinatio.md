ClinHallu is a new benchmark specifically designed to diagnose hallucinations in medical multimodal large language models through stage-wise reasoning analysis. The benchmark identifies where in the reasoning pipeline—input processing, intermediate reasoning, or output generation—hallucinations occur, using trace-supervised fine-tuning to mitigate errors. Medical AI systems are critical for patient safety, making hallucination detection essential. Diagnosing the stage at which errors occur enables targeted fixes rather than blanket retraining. This work advances medical AI safety by providing systematic methods to identify and correct errors in complex multimodal reasoning tasks.
