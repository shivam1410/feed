CorrFlow uses flow matching to generate spatial transcriptomics from histology images while preserving gene-gene interactions. An annealed masked flow matching strategy progressively masks gene subsets to enforce conditional modeling, and a gene graph regularization scheme incorporates protein interaction data from STRING. The approach captures biological co-expression patterns better than per-gene marginal estimation alone.
