GE-Act 2.0 is a world-action model enabling robots to perform manipulation tasks without task-specific training. Built with a control-oriented autoencoder, single-step visual planner, and inverse dynamics model, it uses knowledge-aligned selective optimization. The system demonstrates zero-shot generalization across diverse skills and environmental conditions.
