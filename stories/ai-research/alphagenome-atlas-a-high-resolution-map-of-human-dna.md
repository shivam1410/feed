DeepMind created a database that predicts what happens when any single letter of your genetic code changes. The AlphaGenome Atlas covers every possible single nucleotide variant across the human genome. This matters because it could help doctors identify which genetic variations cause disease, which provide protection, and which are completely harmless. Having a comprehensive map of all variants could revolutionize how medicine becomes personalized to individual genetics.
