Twisted graphene bilayers exhibit exotic quantum states called high-Chern insulators in both integer and fractional forms. Researchers stacked Bernal bilayer graphene with rhombohedral tetralayer graphene to create a moiré superlattice where misaligned layers interfere. In this structure, electrons exhibit topological properties quantified by Chern numbers. Both integer-Chern and fractional-Chern states appeared, indicating electrons behave collectively as if carrying fractional charge. These observations advance topological physics and provide a platform for exploring exotic quantum phenomena and testing theoretical predictions about topological matter.
