Endocannabinoids—molecules naturally produced in the brain—regulate motivated behavior through retrograde signaling in a thalamostriatal circuit connecting thalamus to striatum. During reward seeking, dynamic endocannabinoid release provides retrograde neuromodulation that controls behavioral engagement and persistence. This mechanism represents a key system for translating motivation and reward into action. Understanding endocannabinoid control of reward circuits has implications for treating motivation deficits in depression and other psychiatric conditions where behavioral engagement is impaired.
