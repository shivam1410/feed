MathForm improves automated mathematical formalization by combining knowledge retrieval and verification-guided refinement. The system retrieves relevant theorems from Mathlib and iteratively refines outputs using verification feedback, ensuring formalized statements remain mathematically valid. This approach produced a large verified dataset and trained an effective 8-billion-parameter model. By integrating external knowledge retrieval with automated verification, MathForm overcomes autoformalization's core challenge: ensuring translated statements preserve mathematical correctness. This advancement could accelerate formal verification in mathematics and computing by automating tedious conversion from informal proofs to machine-checkable logic.
