Researchers built a quantum light state with up to 500 photons. They used quantum confocal microscopy to prepare it in a controlled way. The photon count spread drops by 21.5 dB. That let them sense tiny displacements 19.2 dB better than the standard quantum limit, a record. It is a big step for precision sensing.
