Researchers built Jev, a system that makes service decisions much faster than language models. Jev reduces decision latency by 22.7 to 64.5 percent relative to the fastest LLM and costs 59.7 to 80.9 percent less per decision on four-field contracts. It maintains 91 to 95 percent accuracy on real requests where LLMs fall below 10 percent. Time-sensitive services benefit from faster, cheaper decisions for edge computing.
