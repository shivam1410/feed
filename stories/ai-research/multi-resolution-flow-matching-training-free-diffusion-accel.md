MrFlow, a novel training-free approach, accelerates text-to-image diffusion generation by up to 25x through multi-resolution flow matching. The method combines low-resolution image generation with pixel-space super-resolution and strategic noise injection, enabling dramatically faster image synthesis without requiring model retraining or runtime modifications. By processing images at multiple resolution scales simultaneously, the approach generates visually coherent results in fewer steps. This acceleration makes high-quality text-to-image generation practical for resource-constrained settings and interactive applications where generation speed is critical. The training-free nature means existing diffusion models can benefit immediately without expensive retraining, democratizing access to fast image generation.
