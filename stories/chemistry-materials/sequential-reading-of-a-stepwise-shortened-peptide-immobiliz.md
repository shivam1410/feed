A nanopore-based sequencing method reads peptide sequences at single-amino-acid resolution by enzymatically shortening the peptide's N terminus one residue at a time while repeatedly measuring it with a nanopore sensor. This chop and measure approach directly decodes amino acid sequences without mass spectrometry or other indirect methods. The technique enables single-molecule peptide analysis with minimal sample requirements. This innovation could enable rapid protein sequencing, characterization, and structure determination. The nanopore-enzyme approach represents a new modality for biomolecule analysis with potential applications in clinical diagnostics and protein research.
