Researchers discovered a previously unrecognized function for the Rb protein, a critical cell-cycle regulator, in breast cancer signaling pathways. The protein's inhibition of cell division can enhance signaling mediated by the estrogen receptor, the major driver of hormone-responsive breast cancer. This mechanistic insight helps explain why certain cancer treatments blocking cell-cycle progression show efficacy against estrogen receptor-driven cancers. Understanding Rb's dual role in cell-cycle control and growth-factor signaling could inform development of better-targeted therapies, improving treatment outcomes for breast cancer patients.
