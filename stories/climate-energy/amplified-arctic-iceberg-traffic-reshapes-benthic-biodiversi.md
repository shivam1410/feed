Accelerated Arctic glacier disintegration and increasingly dynamic sea ice patterns are delivering more icebergs to the deep ocean floor. These icebergs carry rock and sediment that reshape benthic habitats and ecosystems on the seafloor when they melt. The findings show that glacial melting impacts extend far beyond coastal areas, fundamentally affecting deep-ocean biodiversity. As Arctic warming intensifies, iceberg-delivered dropstones are becoming an increasingly important ecological force in remote marine environments, demonstrating how cryospheric changes propagate throughout global ocean systems.
