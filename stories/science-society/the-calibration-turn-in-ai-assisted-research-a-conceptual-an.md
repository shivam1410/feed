This framework establishes that scientific credibility in AI-assisted research requires licensing claims to evidence rigor, not merely cautious wording. The work identifies five research operators—hypothesis generation, model-mediated derivation, external validation, belief update, and claim calibration—and distinguishes linguistic from evidence-licensed semantics. It defines the claim-evidence gap and epistemic debt, proposing minimal structural reconstruction as calibration. These principles ensure AI systems assert only those claims that supporting evidence warrants, establishing scientific standards for AI research pipelines.
