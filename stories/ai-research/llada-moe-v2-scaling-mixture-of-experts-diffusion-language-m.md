LLaDA MoE v2 characterizes scaling laws for mixture-of-experts diffusion language models, showing how optimization, compute allocation, and architecture differ from autoregressive models. The 30B-A3B model trained on 23.5T tokens approaches competitors using only 65 percent of their pretraining tokens. After supervised fine-tuning, it outperforms baselines on reasoning and coding benchmarks, establishing design principles for scaling future diffusion language models efficiently.
