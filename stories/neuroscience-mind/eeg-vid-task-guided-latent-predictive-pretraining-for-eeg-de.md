EEG-VID improves brain-signal decoding by predicting future EEG patterns from recent history, then fine-tuning for specific tasks. The method improves accuracy in 41 of 42 benchmark comparisons, with gains up to 16.22 percentage points, and enables assistive target selection reaching 40.24% accuracy versus 25% chance baseline. This approach generalizes across subjects and recording sessions, making brain-computer interfaces more practical and robust for clinical or assistive applications.
