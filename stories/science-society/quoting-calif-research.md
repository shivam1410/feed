Researchers working with AI developed the first zero-click WeChat worm—victims don't need to answer calls or interact with phones; remote code execution succeeds silently. The team found the vulnerability and wrote the exploit in two days; the worm took one additional week. Traditionally this required a larger team and months. This matters because AI is dramatically compressing the time-to-exploit for sophisticated mobile vulnerabilities, raising the threat surface.
