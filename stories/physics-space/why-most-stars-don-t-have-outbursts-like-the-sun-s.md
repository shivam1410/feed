Most stars lack the dramatic outbursts our Sun produces, and strong magnetic fields explain why. A powerful magnetic field disrupts a star's ability to generate coronal mass ejections, the fountains of charged matter ejected from stellar atmospheres. Understanding what prevents these outbursts in other stars explains differences in stellar activity and has implications for how stellar behavior affects planets and habitability in orbiting systems.
