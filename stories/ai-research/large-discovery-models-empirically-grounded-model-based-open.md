A recurrent Large Discovery Model couples generative proposals with Bayesian non-parametric reward surrogates to guide uncertainty-aware search. The framework operates across molecules, proteins, and programs, combining proposal generation with learned reward models to explore large search spaces efficiently. This approach integrates principled Bayesian uncertainty quantification with neural generation, enabling more systematic exploration than single-modality methods. By balancing exploration-exploitation through surrogate-guided search, the model could accelerate discovery processes across chemical, biological, and computational domains where vast solution spaces require intelligent navigation.
