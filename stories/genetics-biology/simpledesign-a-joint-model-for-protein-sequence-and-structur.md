SimpleDesign jointly models protein sequence and structure using single-stage end-to-end training directly on data, avoiding multistage autoencoders. Trained on 2+ million sequence-structure pairs with a Mixture-of-Transformer enabling modality-specific processing, it achieves strong performance on co-design and generation benchmarks. This matters because simpler approaches match or exceed complex multistage pipelines, accelerating protein engineering and drug discovery.
