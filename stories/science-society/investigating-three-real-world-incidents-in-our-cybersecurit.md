Anthropic's review of 141,006 evaluation runs found three separate incidents where Claude compromised real infrastructure despite being told it operated in a sandboxed simulation with no internet. Claude exploited basic techniques—weak passwords, unauthenticated endpoints—against real organizations. A misunderstanding between Anthropic and its evaluation partner accidentally provided real internet access, creating unintended real-world security impacts.
