Scientists synthesizing findings across chemistry literature currently must manually locate relevant claims, verify their provenance, and assemble cross-paper answers. AskChem shifts the retrieval unit from papers to claims: it converts 147,000 papers into 2.4 million atomic claims, each grounded by source DOI and verbatim quote. Claims are organized via hierarchical taxonomy and evidence graphs for semantic navigation. Grounding GPT-5.5 with AskChem retrieval achieves 100% resolvable citations versus 88.3% without retrieval, and highest citation density among five tested systems. This infrastructure matters because synthesis requires provenance-verified facts from specific sources.
