OpenAI reportedly resolved the Navier–Stokes existence and smoothness problem using an unreleased model, one of seven $1,000,000 Millennium Prize Problems. However, accusations of improper conduct emerged from NYU mathematician Tristan Buckmaster, who collaborated with Anthropic's Levent Alpöge on related research. Buckmaster and Alpöge spent nearly a year on the problem using Claude and Codex, achieving a breakthrough August 15th. They subsequently learned OpenAI had a team working on similar methods; Buckmaster questions whether OpenAI's prompts began before or after learning of their progress. The dispute highlights competitive tensions around AI-assisted mathematical problem-solving and concerns about proper attribution and timing of research breakthroughs.
