PRECEDE is an LLM-orchestrated system for redesigning pharmaceutical compounds to mitigate specified side effects while preserving therapeutic activity. Rather than isolated molecular generation, it frames drug redesign as evidence-grounded reasoning over drug–side-effect associations, biomedical knowledge graphs, and historical precedents of safety-driven optimization. The system incorporates explicit policies and human-review checkpoints at each stage, ensuring all hypotheses remain auditable, falsifiable, and bounded by prior pharmacological knowledge. This approach positions AI-assisted drug discovery as a transparent, human-supervised workflow where reasoning stays grounded in established pharmacology, fundamentally distinguishing it from opaque black-box processes that lack full traceability.
