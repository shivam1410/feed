Scientists created air-permeable hydrogels using viscoelastic phase separation to embed silica aerogel beads into high-water-content networks. The approach produces non-collapsible, air-rich structures achieving a tenfold increase in oxygen permeability compared to standard hydrogels. The enhanced gas transport through water-rich networks represents a significant functional improvement.
