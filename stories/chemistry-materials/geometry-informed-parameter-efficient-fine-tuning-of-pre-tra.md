BBBP-GeoPEFT applies parameter-efficient fine-tuning to pre-trained molecular GNNs for blood-brain barrier permeability prediction by incorporating 3D geometry. The framework constructs distance-based graphs at multiple cutoffs and uses lightweight auxiliary encoders with cutoff attention and gated residual connections to capture spatial atom and second-order interactions. Addresses class imbalance and molecular structure sensitivity in CNS drug screening. Compared to full fine-tuning and existing PEFT methods limited to 2D graphs, this geometry-informed approach enables efficient model adaptation.
