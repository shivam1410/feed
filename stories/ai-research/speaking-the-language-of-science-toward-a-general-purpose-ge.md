Researchers developed a unified generative language model that encodes diverse scientific objects and spatial interactions as token sequences, enabling autoregressive next-token prediction across multiple scientific domains simultaneously. The model achieves strong performance by using a single tokenization scheme for chemistry, physics, biology, and other fields without domain-specific modifications. This unified approach reduces the need for specialized architectures and could accelerate scientific discovery by enabling knowledge transfer across disciplines. The work demonstrates that language-based representations can effectively capture and generate complex scientific phenomena across otherwise disparate fields, potentially democratizing access to scientific modeling.
