Scientists engineered probiotics with a synthetic glucose-responsive circuit that transiently colonize the gut and secrete therapeutic factors when blood glucose rises. Tested successfully in mice and primates, this approach enables safe, oral, autonomous sense-and-respond diabetes control without external monitoring. By engineering bacteria to detect metabolic signals and respond with therapeutic molecules, researchers created a living drug system.
