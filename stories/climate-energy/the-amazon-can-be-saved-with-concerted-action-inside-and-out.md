Fresh evidence from climate modeling shows the Amazon rainforest can withstand global warming if worldwide deforestation stops. As deforestation rates have begun falling, new data suggests the forest possesses sufficient resilience to survive projected warming scenarios provided human cutting ceases. The rainforest's capacity to survive depends critically on preventing further loss and maintaining forest area. This matters because it demonstrates the Amazon's fate depends not solely on global climate trajectories but critically on human action to end deforestation. It offers hope that reversing deforestation trends could successfully preserve this crucial ecosystem, which plays an essential role in global climate regulation and biodiversity.
