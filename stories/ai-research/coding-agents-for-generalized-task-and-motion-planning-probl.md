Researchers trained AI coding agents (Claude Opus and GPT models) to solve robot task-and-motion planning by writing programs that generalize across environments. They tested 980 generated programs across 28 simulated environments and 98,000 total evaluation episodes. The agents achieved 56-95% success rates, outperforming hand-engineered planners. This shows AI can automate engineering work that previously required human expertise.
