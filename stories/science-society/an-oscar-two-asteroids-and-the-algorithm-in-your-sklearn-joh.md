Google's Empirical Research Assistance automates hard science by turning problems into optimization tasks. Once you write a score function measuring success, AI finds code that maximizes it. Platt's team has tackled climate change and other huge problems this way, treating diverse scientific challenges as versions of the same optimization problem.
