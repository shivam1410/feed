Researchers introduced MatMMExtract, a novel pipeline that extracts structured data from scientific papers in materials science. The system processes complex scientific figures by automatically decomposing them into individual panels and generating structured annotations using large language models. This creates a comprehensive dataset enabling vision-language models to learn from scientific imagery and text. By transforming scientific literature into machine-readable training data, MatMMExtract provides infrastructure for training AI systems that understand specialized scientific visual and textual information, accelerating materials science research.
