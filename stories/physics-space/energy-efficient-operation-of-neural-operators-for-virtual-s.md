Neural operators reconstructing physical fields from observations can cut energy by reusing computation across requests. At higher rates (40 per second), sharing computation reduces operating energy by 20% to 22.5% versus independent processing. The insight connects request frequency and execution lifetime to energy efficiency gains in field reconstruction with models like DeepONet and FNO.
