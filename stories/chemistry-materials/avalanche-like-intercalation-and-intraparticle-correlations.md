Real-time operando optical microscopy reveals that lithium ions move through graphite battery electrodes in avalanche-like bursts rather than smoothly during intercalation and deintercalation cycles. Local disorder within the electrode material governs these phase-transition dynamics and ion-transport kinetics. Understanding these mechanisms is crucial for optimizing lithium-ion battery performance. The findings show how nanoscale structural disorder affects ion transport behavior, enabling better electrode material design for faster charging, improved cycle life, and enhanced battery performance. This work bridges materials science and electrochemistry to improve battery technology.
