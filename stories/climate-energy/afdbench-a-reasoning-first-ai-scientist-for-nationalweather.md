AFDBench is the first benchmark for meteorological reasoning, with 7,732 expert Area Forecast Discussions from 13 NWS offices paired with weather data. Baseline LLMs show poor professional style (Style-Align ~0.33) and moderate input fidelity (Input-Grounding ~0.88). Group Relative Policy Optimization with domain rewards nearly doubles Style-Align to 0.619 and improves Input-Grounding to 0.940 on unseen offices, showing RL trains models to write professionally and faithfully interpret forecast data.
