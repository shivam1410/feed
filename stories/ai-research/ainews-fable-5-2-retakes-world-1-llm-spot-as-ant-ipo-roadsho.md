Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2 takes back the number one spot from Gemini 4 Carbon. Its per-token price fell 20%, to $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens. But it uses more tokens. On SemiAnalysis's GDPx test, cost per task rose from $67.16 to $88.15, even as it took first place at 16.4%. Anthropic reports that Fable at High effort matched Opus 5.5 at Max for about half the cost per task.
