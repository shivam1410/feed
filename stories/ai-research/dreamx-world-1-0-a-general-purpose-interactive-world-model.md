DreamX-World 1.0 is a general-purpose interactive world model that generates realistic video sequences from text or image prompts. The system produces extended-duration video content while maintaining consistent scene structure and enabling fine-grained user control over camera viewpoint, movement, and scene interactions. The model employs specialized neural encoding methods and novel optimization techniques to synthesize realistic long-horizon video with smooth transitions and physics-aware scene behavior. This significantly advances capabilities for immersive interactive video generation, addressing persistent technical challenges in temporal consistency, scene persistence, and user-directed viewpoint control.
