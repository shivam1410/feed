Adenine base editing, a precise genome-rewriting technique, offers researchers an innovative tool to investigate how genes function during early human embryo development. By selectively targeting adenine nucleotides in DNA, this approach enables controlled genetic modifications to understand specific gene contributions to embryonic development. The method's high precision allows researchers to study effects of specific genetic changes without broad unintended disruption, advancing understanding of human development at critical early stages.
