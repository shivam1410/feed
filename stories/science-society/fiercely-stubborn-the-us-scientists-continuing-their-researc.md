Scientists in the US are refusing to give up despite funding cuts. Researchers in climate change, diversity, transgender health and international aid are turning to lawsuits, private donors and new topics to keep their projects going. Each group found its own workaround. Some have simply switched to alternative subjects. It matters because cuts can quietly change which questions get studied.
