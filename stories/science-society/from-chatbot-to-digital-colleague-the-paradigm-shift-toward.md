Large language models are undergoing a fundamental paradigm shift from conversational chatbots to integrated autonomous AI colleagues capable of persistent work within organizational environments. These evolved systems feature enhanced reasoning capabilities, longer context windows, and persistent memory of prior work sessions, enabling them to function as ongoing collaborators rather than one-off question-answering tools. This transition reflects increasing integration of AI into professional workflows where continuity and autonomy matter. The shift has implications for workplace productivity, AI interface design, and the nature of human-AI collaboration.
