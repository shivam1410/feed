Renal cell carcinomas containing tertiary lymphoid structures (TLSs) harbour specialised exhausted CD8+ T cells that recognize tumour-specific antigens. These T cells retain stem-like progenitor features with reduced terminal exhaustion. This unique composition suggests TLSs preserve functional anti-tumour immunity that may be therapeutically exploitable, offering insight into how local immune structures within tumours enhance immunotherapy response.
