A recent disaster in Nepal demonstrates the dangers of compound climate risks, where multiple climate hazards interact and amplify each other. Traditional planning addressing individual climate threats separately misses how they combine to create cascading impacts. Adequate climate adaptation and disaster preparedness must account for interconnected environmental stressors, particularly in mountainous regions where geography intensifies multiple hazards simultaneously.
