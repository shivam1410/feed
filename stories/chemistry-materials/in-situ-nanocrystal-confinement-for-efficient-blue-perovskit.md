Researchers achieved highly efficient blue perovskite light-emitting diodes with an external quantum efficiency of 21.8%—a significant milestone for this emerging technology. The breakthrough used in situ polymerization-driven nanocrystal confinement, a technique that constrains nanocrystals within the perovskite matrix during polymerization. This nanostructure engineering optimizes charge carrier dynamics and light emission efficiency. The achievement represents major progress toward practical perovskite LEDs for displays and lighting applications. Blue LEDs are particularly challenging but essential for full-color displays. The method demonstrates how precise control of nanostructure can enable high-performance optoelectronic devices.
