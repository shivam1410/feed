SELECTBENCH is a new benchmark that checks whether generative drug design models avoid binding the wrong proteins. It has three tasks: literature case studies, pharmaceutical safety screening panels, and large-scale structure-based sets. Nine representative models were tested. Even models trained for selectivity showed only modest off-target discrimination, and inference-time guidance gave limited gains. It matters because off-target effects contribute to clinical attrition. The benchmark is open and extensible.
