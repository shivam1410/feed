NatureBench evaluates whether AI coding agents can replicate breakthroughs from 90 scientific tasks spanning Nature-family publications. The benchmark reveals current agents excel at methodological translation—implementing known algorithms—but struggle with genuine scientific innovation requiring novel approaches. Results show agents cannot yet discover new solutions, only execute existing ones with computational implementation. This gap highlights fundamental limitations in current AI for scientific discovery and identifies key capabilities needed for agentic science.
