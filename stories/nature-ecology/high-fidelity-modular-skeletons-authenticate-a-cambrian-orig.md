Exceptional early Cambrian fossils—Protomelission gatehousei and Dayingomelission hexaclitia—preserve soft-tissue anatomy and skeletal details confirming these organisms as bryozoans. Their discovery pushes back the evolutionary origin of bryozoans deeper into the Cambrian period than previously understood. These exquisitely preserved specimens reveal microstructural details impossible to see in younger fossils. The findings refine our understanding of early animal evolution and the Cambrian explosion.
