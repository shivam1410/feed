Jensen Huang has stepped down as NVIDIA's CEO, president, and board member after 33 years. Colette Kress, the CFO since 2013, keeps the CFO job and becomes interim CEO. The exit follows an NYT report of an affair with an accused Chinese spy, then news of a second affair. The newsletter notes the link is debatable and says the board presumably pushed for it. Next week's earnings call will likely be heavily attended.
