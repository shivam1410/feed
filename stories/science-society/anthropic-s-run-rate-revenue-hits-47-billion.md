Anthropic's run-rate revenue—an annualized projection of current monthly revenue—has reached $47 billion, announced in a Series H funding round. This represents extraordinary growth from $9 billion at end of 2025, $14 billion in February 2026, and $30 billion in April 2026. The company has grown its run-rate revenue over 10x annually for three consecutive years. CEO commentary and industry analysis suggest Anthropic may be the fastest-scaling company by organic revenue in any industry historically. The metric, while not GAAP accounting, reflects accelerating enterprise adoption and customer demand for AI services.
