LingSante MedGuard is a safety checker for medical AI answers. It merges a guardrail teacher with six specialist experts into one model. At 4B, 9B and 27B sizes, it reached mean benchmark F1 scores of 82.3%, 82.4% and 87.4%. The 4B model also reached 73.9% mean accuracy on MedHallu and MedHALT-NOTA with retrieval enabled. Medical AI needs this kind of check before it advises patients.
