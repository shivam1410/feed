Researchers developed an optical technique using mid-infrared hyperbolic polaritons to measure nanoscale deformations between material layers at unprecedented picometre-scale precision. This method creates a bridge between nanomechanics and photonics, combining optical measurement capabilities with mechanical property analysis in ways previously impossible. The technique was experimentally validated to examine how thin films and layered materials deform under stress. Such measurements are crucial for understanding mechanical properties of modern nanomaterials, semiconductors, and advanced materials used in nanotechnology applications requiring detailed knowledge of internal structural deformations and interface behavior.
