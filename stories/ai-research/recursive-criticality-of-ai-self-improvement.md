A recursive reproduction number theoretical framework determines whether AI research and development feedback loops become self-amplifying based on research productivity, feedback strength, and rising difficulty. The model shows how increases in research output drive improvements in AI capabilities, which in turn enable more efficient research, potentially creating exponential growth. However, increasing problem difficulty can dampen this effect. This analysis provides theoretical grounding for understanding AI development dynamics and whether current trends might lead to accelerating improvement cycles. The framework helps structure thinking about long-term AI trajectories and conditions under which self-reinforcing advancement becomes possible.
