Researchers identified TM184C, a previously understudied G-protein-coupled receptor-like protein that regulates autophagy and intercellular exchange. This discovery is significant because the human proteome remains largely unexplored, and understanding proteins like TM184C that control fundamental cellular processes—autophagy and material exchange between cells—reveals new biology relevant to cellular health and potentially disease mechanisms. The protein's dual role in both autophagy regulation and intercellular connectivity suggests it may be a focal point for cellular communication networks. This work underscores how mining the dark proteome for ancient, conserved proteins can uncover regulatory systems hidden in plain sight.
