Genetic variants affecting extracellular matrix molecule production appear crucial for skeletal adaptations distinguishing humans from other great apes. These gene regulatory changes enabled unique anatomical features including bipedal locomotion adaptations and enhanced manual dexterity. Understanding these variants illuminates the genetic basis of human skeletal evolution and may inform research into developmental biology and bone-related diseases.
