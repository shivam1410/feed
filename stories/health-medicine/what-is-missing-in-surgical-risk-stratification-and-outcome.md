A scoping review of 190 studies reveals major methodological fragmentation in machine learning for surgical outcome prediction using EHR data. Most rely on single-center private datasets with limited modalities. Key preprocessing steps (missing data, feature selection, class imbalance) are often incompletely reported. Conventional ML models predominate while deep learning and multimodal approaches remain uncommon. Only ~33% incorporate explainability methods. Benchmark datasets and standardized evaluation protocols are absent, preventing reproducibility and cross-study comparisons essential for clinical decision-making.
