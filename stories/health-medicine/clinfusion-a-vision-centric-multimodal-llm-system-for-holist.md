ClinFusion is a vision-centric multimodal language model for medical imaging. It features a Cascade Spatial-Aware Locality Fusion operator unifying heterogeneous 2D and 3D medical image understanding within a fused encoder. A vision-grounded evaluation framework includes MedIF-Bench for instruction-following and region-of-interest-grounded methods for clinically aligned report evaluation. ClinFusion achieves state-of-the-art on 20 of 24 multimodal medical benchmarks including visual question answering, report generation, and instruction-following, outperforming open-source medical models (Hulu-Med, Lingshu) and competing with GPT-5.2 and Gemini-3-Flash on 13 of 16 benchmarks. The architecture addresses the unique challenge of integrating diverse medical imaging modalities for diagnostic and research applications.
