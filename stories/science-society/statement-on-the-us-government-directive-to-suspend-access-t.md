The U.S. government issued an export control directive suspending all access to Fable 5 and Mythos 5 for foreign nationals, citing national security concerns. The order required immediate disablement of both models for all customers, while other Anthropic models remain available. The government's concern stems from a potential jailbreak method discovered for Fable 5—specifically, asking the model to read a codebase and identify flaws. Upon review, Anthropic found the demonstrated vulnerabilities were minor and relatively simple, with other publicly available models capable of discovering them without specialized bypass techniques. The directive provides no detailed explanation, marking the first export control targeting frontier AI models.
