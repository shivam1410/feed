Researchers can now track molecules at buried interfaces, the layers hidden beneath a surface. They combine machine learning with optical modeling of polarized resonant soft X-ray scattering. The result is depth-resolved readings of molecular orientation and packing, even at sub-monolayer coverage. Why it matters: hidden molecular layouts become measurable.
