A method reconstructs hidden 3D physical fields like coronal temperature from 2D images, revealing a hidden problem: images can look perfect while the 3D field underneath is wrong. Cross-seed instability flags where fields are unreliable without needing ground truth. On solar tomography, limited observations can overfit the images while failing on the actual 3D field. This matters because unseen fields are hard to validate after deployment.
