Jev, a new 'System One' model, handles decisions, classification, and routing—not reasoning—at >100× faster speed and >200× lower cost than frontier LLMs. TypeSafe trained it via RLCD (calibrated decisions), eliminating hallucination and enabling parallel sampling. The model complements slower System Two reasoning LLMs and launches as a complement to chat-tuned autoregressive models that dominated AI development.
