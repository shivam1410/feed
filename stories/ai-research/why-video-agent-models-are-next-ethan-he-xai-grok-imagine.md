The next video generation frontier is "video agent" models that plan, generate, edit, critique, and iterate across creative tasks—mirroring how coding agents evolved from single outputs to multi-turn reasoning and debugging. Ethan He, builder of Grok Imagine at xAI, argues video models derive intelligence primarily from underlying LLMs, not video training alone. As base capabilities strengthen, bottlenecks shift from single-output performance to orchestration and multi-turn iteration.
