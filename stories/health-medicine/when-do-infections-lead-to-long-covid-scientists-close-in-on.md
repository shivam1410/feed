Hundreds of millions of people worldwide experience long-term health conditions following infections—a phenomenon termed long COVID or post-viral syndromes. Researchers are actively investigating what determines whether an infection leads to chronic complications, identifying potential biological triggers and developing treatments. Understanding the mechanisms causing these persistent conditions is crucial for helping affected populations recover. The scale of the problem—affecting hundreds of millions—makes this research a major health priority, as current treatment options remain limited.
