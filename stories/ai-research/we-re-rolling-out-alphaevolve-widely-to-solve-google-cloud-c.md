Google DeepMind is expanding AlphaEvolve, an AI system that discovers efficient algorithms across diverse domains. The tool helps solve optimization problems in microchip design, logistics routing, and medical research — tasks traditionally requiring extensive human expertise. AlphaEvolve is now available to Google Cloud customers to tackle their most computationally challenging problems. The system represents a practical application of AI to algorithm discovery, reducing the manual search space exploration engineers must perform.
