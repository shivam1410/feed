A small clinical trial demonstrates that a modified virus can reprogram the immune system to attack and eliminate cells causing multiple sclerosis. Rather than suppressing immunity broadly, this approach harnesses the immune system's own capabilities for targeted therapeutic effect. The modified virus acts as a teaching tool, instructing immune cells to recognize and destroy disease-causing tissue while preserving healthy cells. This represents a novel immunotherapy strategy for MS, a debilitating neurological disease affecting millions. If results hold in larger trials, the approach could transform MS treatment.
