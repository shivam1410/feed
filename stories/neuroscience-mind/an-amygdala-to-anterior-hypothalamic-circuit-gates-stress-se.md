Neuroscientists discovered a brain circuit connecting the amygdala to the anterior hypothalamus that controls how sensitive you are to stress. This circuit acts as a gate for negative emotions and threat responses. Understanding this pathway matters because it identifies a concrete target for developing new treatments for anxiety disorders and stress-related mental health conditions.
