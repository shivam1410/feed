High-resolution spatial transcriptome analysis of a stage-6 human embryo reveals that blood-cell formation begins before gastrulation, originating from the hypoblast rather than epiblast as previously assumed. The study maps three distinct developmental paths from epiblast toward amnion, primitive streak, and axial mesoderm. This is significant because it clarifies the earliest steps of human hematopoiesis and challenges existing models of embryonic germ-layer specification.
