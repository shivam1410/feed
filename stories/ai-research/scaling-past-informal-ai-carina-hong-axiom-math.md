Axiom, a seven-month-old startup, solved all 12 Putnam mathematics problems—8 within the official time limit, surpassing typical human competitors and the previous AI benchmark (DeepSeek). This demonstrates elite mathematical reasoning capability. However, CEO Carina Hong positions math mastery as necessary but insufficient for AGI progress: coding breakthroughs push AI toward super-intelligence in specific domains, yet gaps remain. "Verified AI"—AI systems that explain and validate their reasoning—represents the actual bottleneck limiting progress toward general intelligence.
