A Google DeepMind scientist explains how AI weather prediction works for cyclones and other major storms. Machine learning models rapidly process vast amounts of atmospheric data to spot patterns that traditional forecasting completely misses, giving communities hours or even days of extra warning before major storms hit. More warning time directly saves lives through better evacuations and preparation.
