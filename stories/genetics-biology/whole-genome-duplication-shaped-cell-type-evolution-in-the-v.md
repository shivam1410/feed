Comparative analysis of brain gene expression across humans, mice, lizards, lampreys, and amphioxus shows whole-genome duplication drove vertebrate brain cell-type diversification. Duplicated genes (ohnologues) diverged to generate specialized cell types and greater neuronal complexity. This mechanism explains why vertebrate brains possess greater cellular diversity than invertebrate nervous systems.
