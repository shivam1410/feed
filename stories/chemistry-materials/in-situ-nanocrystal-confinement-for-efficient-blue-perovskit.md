Researchers achieved efficient blue perovskite light-emitting diodes with external quantum efficiency of 21.8% using in situ polymerization-driven nanocrystal confinement, which precisely controls crystal growth during synthesis to optimize light emission. This efficiency level represents significant progress toward practical perovskite-based technologies for displays and general lighting applications. Perovskites offer potential advantages including tunable emission colors, solution processability, and lower manufacturing costs than traditional LED materials.
