Shieldstral is a 3-billion-parameter safety classifier that matches or outperforms models nearly 7 times larger on text safety benchmarks while achieving state-of-the-art performance on multimodal safety classification. The model formulates content moderation as binary question-answering, unifying diverse safety taxonomies under one training framework. Training consolidated approximately 54.1 million curated and generated samples into a single dataset. This efficient approach enables policy-adaptive safety classification across heterogeneous datasets, reducing computational overhead for production deployment. Parameter-efficient designs match or exceed larger competitors.
