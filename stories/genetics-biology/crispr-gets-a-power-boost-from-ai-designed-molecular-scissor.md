Scientists designed novel CRISPR enzymes using artificial intelligence that do not exist in nature and perform genome editing more efficiently than naturally evolved versions. This computational approach to enzyme engineering showcases how machine learning can create functional biological proteins that surpass those shaped through natural selection and evolution over millions of years. The advancement could enable more targeted and effective genetic therapies with fewer off-target effects on the genome. This success demonstrates the potential of AI-driven synthetic biology.
