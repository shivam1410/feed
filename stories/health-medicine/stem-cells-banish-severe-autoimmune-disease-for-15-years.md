Stem cell therapy has delivered a sustained remission in two patients with severe autoimmune disease—a condition damaging the spinal cord and optic nerve. Both experienced relief lasting 15 years, marking the first recipients of this treatment. This extended remission demonstrates stem cell transplantation can control severe autoimmune conditions where traditional treatments fail. The long-term durability is significant because it suggests durable disease control rather than temporary symptom management, offering hope for patients with otherwise debilitating spinal and vision complications.
