A startup company plans to deploy an unprecedented array of 50,000 mirror-bearing satellites in orbit to reflect sunlight and boost renewable energy generation on Earth. However, astronomers strongly oppose the project, warning that such a massive satellite constellation would severely compromise ground-based and space telescopes, hampering astronomical observations for decades. The conflict illustrates growing tensions between innovative clean energy solutions and the protection of scientific research capabilities. Both sides acknowledge legitimate concerns about advancing clean energy while preserving astronomical discovery.
