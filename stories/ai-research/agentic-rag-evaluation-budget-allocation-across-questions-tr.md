When evaluating retrieval-augmented agents, you must decide whether to test more questions, more search trajectories, or multiple reads per question. Testing on HotpotQA and MuSiQue showed that at 34M tokens, testing more questions cuts error by 33% versus more reads. Temperature zero reduces answer disagreement from 14.3% to 3.4%, suggesting simpler can be better. These findings help researchers spend compute efficiently when benchmarking these systems.
