RAIBO2, an energy-efficient quadruped robot, completed a full marathon in 4 hours, 19 minutes and 52 seconds on a single battery charge, achieving a cost of transport of 0.25—significantly outperforming the human benchmark of 0.37. The achievement demonstrates that robots can exceed human locomotor efficiency in sustained long-distance travel. This highlights advances in robotics energy management and mechanical design, with applications in long-range autonomous exploration and transportation systems.
