For long-horizon agent tasks, humans need help spotting which decisions matter and finding supporting evidence. The Evidence-Grounded Behavior Graph groups evidence into behaviors and organizes their relationships, helping monitors identify consequential decisions and verify their implications. Across eight models, this approach improved decision identification compared with direct access to raw context.
