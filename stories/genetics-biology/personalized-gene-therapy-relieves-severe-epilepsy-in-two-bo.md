Personalized gene therapy has successfully treated severe epilepsy in two boys by selectively disabling one copy of each child's disease-causing gene. Beyond seizure control, one boy gained the ability to walk independently for the first time, demonstrating broader neurological improvement. The case demonstrates how tailored genetic interventions can address severe, treatment-resistant epilepsy previously unresponsive to conventional therapies.
