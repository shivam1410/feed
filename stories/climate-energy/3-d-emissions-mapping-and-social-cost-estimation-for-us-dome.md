Researchers reconstructed flight paths for West Coast airports using neural networks trained on radar data, then calculated pollution and health costs. They found nitrogen oxides drive 99.7 percent of air quality damage costs even though they're under 0.4 percent of emissions by weight. This flips conventional thinking about which pollutants harm health most.
