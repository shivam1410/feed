Despite AI's rapid advancement, evidence doesn't support predictions of mass software engineer unemployment from AI adoption. In March 2025, when New York introduced a new AI disclosure requirement for WARN Act filings, 160+ companies filed layoff notices; notably, not a single company checked the AI checkbox. Quantitative research analyzing how engineers spend time shows meetings and debugging consume substantial hours, suggesting coding itself isn't the primary bottleneck. Qualitative analysis of engineers' own accounts reveals three robust bottlenecks resisting automation: deciding what to build, estimating implementation impact, and managing stakeholder expectations.
