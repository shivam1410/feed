New Mongolian fossils reclassify Cretaceous zhelestids not as early placental mammal relatives but as dental variants within Zalambdalestoidea, possessing distinctive molars alongside characteristic zalambdalestid skull and limb traits. The reclassification revises understanding of Cretaceous mammalian diversity and clarifies evolutionary relationships within mammalian lineages during the age of dinosaurs.
