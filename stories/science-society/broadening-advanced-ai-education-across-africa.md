Google DeepMind, Google.org, and Google Skills are partnering to launch a free, high-impact curriculum designed to empower AI researchers and educators across Africa. This initiative aims to democratize access to advanced artificial intelligence education and build local expertise in a region currently underrepresented in the global AI research community. By providing structured, professional-grade AI training resources at no cost, the program addresses the critical shortage of AI talent in Africa and creates pathways for the next generation of African technologists to contribute to global AI advancement.
