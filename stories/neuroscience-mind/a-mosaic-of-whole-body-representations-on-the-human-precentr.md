Researchers created comprehensive single-neuron resolution map of human motor cortex, the brain region controlling voluntary movement. This atlas reveals spatial organization and connectivity of individual neurons involved in motor planning and execution across cortex surface. Mapping identifies how motor commands for different body parts—limbs, face, trunk—organize spatially. High-resolution reference map advances understanding of brain movement encoding and control, providing foundation for studying motor area reorganization after injury or in neurological disease and developing better brain-computer interfaces.
