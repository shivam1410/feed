Research demonstrates that agent safety should be enforced at runtime through preventive controls and verifiable evidence, rather than relying solely on training-time alignment methods. Runtime safety mechanisms provide active protection against misuse and failure modes. Verifiable evidence provides assurance of safety properties. This matters because it shifts safety from assumptions about learned behavior to guarantees through system design.
