LLM-based agents learning from their own experience risk reinforcing errors through self-confirmation bias. The EDV framework uses three collaborative stages with multiple heterogeneous agents to address this: execute tasks, distill findings, and verify results. This distributed verification prevents single-agent confirmation loops where agents reinforce their own mistakes. More reliable agent experiences through external verification improve downstream task performance and reduce error propagation in agentic systems.
