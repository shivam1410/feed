Agentic Chain-of-Thought Steering (ACTS) formulates reasoning control as a Markov decision process, enabling large language models to reason more efficiently while maintaining control. The method reduces token consumption—the primary cost driver for deployed systems—while preserving reasoning quality. By treating reasoning steps as decisions to optimize rather than fixed procedures, ACTS allows models to skip unnecessary steps and focus computational effort on problem-solving. This optimization is valuable for deployed systems where token costs directly impact operational expenses and response time.
