Analysis of one million diverse genomes identified genetic mutations linked to multiple metabolic benefits—lower risk of diabetes, heart disease, and other cardiometabolic conditions. These mutations promote more muscle and less belly fat. The findings suggest these genes play central roles in metabolism across diverse populations. Understanding which genes and mutations protect against these common diseases could eventually enable new prevention strategies or treatments, potentially benefiting millions worldwide.
