Cryo-electron tomography reveals the structure of a hexameric portal complex in vaccinia virus that spans the viral core wall. This conserved structure plays key roles in viral assembly, mRNA release, and genome uncoking. Understanding portal complex architecture provides insights into how poxviruses build their cores and replicate, potentially informing treatments for poxvirus infections and the design of viral vectors for gene therapy.
