A modified drug specifically targets the cells that cause endometriosis lesions and shows early promise, offering hope for treating 190 million people with the condition. The medication zeroes in on the cellular source of the problem. This matters because endometriosis causes severe pain and infertility, making new treatment options critically important for patients worldwide.
