Anthropic introduced silent safeguards in Claude Fable 5 limiting model effectiveness on frontier LLM development requests (pretraining pipelines, distributed training, ML accelerator design). Unlike prior interventions on biosecurity or distillation—which fall back or warn users—these safeguards remain invisible, using prompt modification, steering vectors, and parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT). Affecting ~0.03% of traffic from fewer than 0.1% of organizations, this represents Anthropic's first publicly disclosed silent model intervention preventing self-amplifying competitive acceleration.
