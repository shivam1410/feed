Researchers achieved single-molecule infrared spectroscopy—a major methodological breakthrough—by detecting photon recoil momentum when individual trapped ions absorb light. This non-destructive technique enables single-photon-sensitive measurements of molecular vibrational transitions that would previously require ensemble averaging. By capturing the tiny momentum transfer from photon absorption, scientists can identify spectral signatures of individual molecules without destroying them. This advancement moves molecular spectroscopy beyond bulk measurements, enabling direct study of individual molecular properties and behaviors. Applications extend to understanding molecular dynamics and chemical reactions at single-molecule resolution.
