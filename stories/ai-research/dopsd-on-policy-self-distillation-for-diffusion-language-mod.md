On-policy self-distillation improves reasoning in diffusion language models by leveraging internal denoising trajectories. The method enhances performance on mathematical reasoning and code generation tasks. This addresses a key limitation where diffusion-based LLMs struggle during post-training refinement, opening pathways for more capable reasoning in alternative generative architectures.
