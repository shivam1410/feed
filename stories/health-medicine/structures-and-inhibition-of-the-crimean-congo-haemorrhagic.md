Scientists have determined the three-dimensional structures of the Crimean-Congo hemorrhagic fever virus polymerase enzyme responsible for viral RNA synthesis. These structural details reveal the molecular basis of how the virus replicates its genetic material. The research identifies druggable sites vulnerable to pharmaceutical intervention, applicable to both traditional nucleoside-based antivirals and newer non-nucleoside inhibitors. This structural knowledge provides foundation for developing treatments against CCHFV, a severe viral hemorrhagic fever with high mortality.
