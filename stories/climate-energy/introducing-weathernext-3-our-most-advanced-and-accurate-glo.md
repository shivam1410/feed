WeatherNext 3 is Google DeepMind's latest and most advanced global weather prediction model, now integrated across Google's ecosystem including Search, Gemini, Maps, Google Maps Platform, and Cloud infrastructure. This integration represents a significant step in making AI-driven weather forecasting accessible to billions worldwide. By deploying the model across multiple consumer and enterprise platforms, Google aims to democratize accurate weather predictions. The unified integration enables real-time, precise weather information delivery at scale, supporting both end users seeking local forecasts and businesses requiring reliable weather data for operations and planning.
