Four major AI-for-pharma tools deals were announced at January's JPM conference, marking a pivotal industry shift. Chai Discovery, a 2-year-old startup valued at $4 billion, was central to announcements. Historically, pharma AI companies built internal drug pipelines to prove tool efficacy; proving worth required specific validated targets before scaling. The shift to direct pharma tool adoption reflects tools now being trustworthy enough for drug design teams to adopt across portfolios without internal pipeline development.
