One pretrained video model can learn actions from video without any labels. The method, called O2LaF, repurposes a shared video diffusion transformer to learn both inverse and forward latent action dynamics from unlabeled videos. It reaches state-of-the-art results on few-shot action probing and zero-shot action transfer. Action labels are scarce, so learning from raw video is a practical win.
