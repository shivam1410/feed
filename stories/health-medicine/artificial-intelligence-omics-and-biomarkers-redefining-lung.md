This research explores how artificial intelligence and omics—comprehensive analysis of biological molecules like genes and proteins—can improve early detection of lung cancer through biomarkers. AI identifies patterns in complex biological data that predict disease. Early detection dramatically improves survival rates. Integrating AI with omics-derived biomarkers could enable earlier diagnosis and transform outcomes for lung cancer patients.
