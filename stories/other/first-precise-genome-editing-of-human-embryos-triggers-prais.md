Researchers achieved the first precise genome editing in human embryos using a 'base editing' technique. While this demonstrates technical capability and has garnered praise, the method remains far from clinical readiness and therapeutic deployment. Critics express concern that premature commercialization efforts may be spurred before comprehensive safety evaluations, efficacy verification, and ethical implications are adequately resolved. The technology requires substantial additional development before human applications.
