The Great Barrier Reef Microbial Genomes Database compiles comprehensive genomic sequences from prokaryotic, viral, and eukaryotic microorganisms inhabiting reef seawater environments. This genomics resource systematically catalogs the vast microbial diversity living within and supporting one of Earth's most biodiverse and ecologically significant marine ecosystems. The database enables researchers to investigate how distinct microbial communities structure reef ecosystem functions, mediate critical nutrient cycling processes, and respond dynamically to environmental stressors including warming and acidification. Understanding microbial contributions to reef ecosystem health and resilience is essential for predicting ecosystem stability under climate change and informing evidence-based conservation strategies. The publicly accessible genomic resource accelerates scientific discovery while supporting management and conservation research initiatives for reef ecosystems globally, facilitating collaborative work across institutions.
