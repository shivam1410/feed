Physicists using the JUNO facility aim to determine which type of neutrino carries the highest mass, solving a fundamental mystery in particle physics: neutrino mass ordering. There are three neutrino types, and physicists know they have mass but do not know their mass ordering. Determining this ordering would constrain theoretical models of particle physics and help solve outstanding puzzles in fundamental physics. This measurement may reveal physics beyond the standard model.
