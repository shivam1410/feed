Anthropic revoked access to Fable and Mythos across all customers worldwide after just three days due to a US government directive citing cybersecurity risk from possible jailbreaks. The company disputes the severity, believing the government has only verbal evidence of a potential narrow, non-universal jailbreak rather than a universal vulnerability. This marks a significant precedent for 'model sovereignty'—direct government authority to force offline models affecting global users, not just US vendors. The revocation triggered debate among open-source AI advocates. This matters because it establishes a new regulatory model: frontier AI capability can be pulled offline abruptly for national security reasons, with limited transparency and disputed technical justification. The precedent may influence how labs approach model releases and government relationships moving forward.
