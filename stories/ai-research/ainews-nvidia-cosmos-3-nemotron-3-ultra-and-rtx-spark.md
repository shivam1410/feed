NVIDIA released Cosmos 3, an omnimodal world model unifying language, image, video, audio, and action in a Mixture-of-Transformers architecture. Models include Nano (16B total) and Super (64B total), with Super variants for Text2Image and Image2Video becoming new state-of-the-art open-weight models. NVIDIA also announced Nemotron 3 Ultra, a 550B open-weight LLM with 55B active parameters, and previewed the RTX Spark 1 petaflop personal supercomputer.
