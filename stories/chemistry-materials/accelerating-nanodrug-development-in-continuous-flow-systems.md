Shape-constrained predictive modeling accurately predicts nanoparticle size and dispersity across varying lipid concentrations, flow rates, and mixing ratios in pharmaceutical applications, reducing reliance on iterative experimental screening. Informed by controlled microfluidic data and expert knowledge, the framework enables rational process development for nanomedicine manufacturing. This addresses the nanotherapeutic development bottleneck by replacing extensive empirical optimization with computational prediction.
