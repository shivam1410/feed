Google DeepMind's conversational AI system, called AMIE, matched primary care physicians in managing complex diseases. Published in Nature, the research shows the system handled difficult patient cases as skillfully as experienced doctors with years of clinical training. If AMIE can assist real physicians in clinics, it could expand quality healthcare access in under-served regions facing severe doctor shortages.
