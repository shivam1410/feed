Early people settled the desert where water and good stone for tools were both found, not water alone. The team surveyed the Rub' al Khali, the Empty Quarter of Arabia, and modelled the wider Arabian Peninsula to test this. Water alone did not predict settlement. Stone suitable for tools had to be nearby too. It reshapes how we read where early people could live in the desert.
