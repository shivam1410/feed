CRISPR and other genetic technologies offer dual benefits for agriculture under climate pressure, according to this comprehensive review. The analysis highlights how untapped CRISPR-Cas techniques can combat hidden hunger—nutritional deficiencies affecting millions globally—while simultaneously improving crop resilience to environmental stress. By combining these genetic approaches strategically, researchers can address two interconnected agricultural challenges: securing food's nutritional value amid changing climates and strengthening farming's capacity to withstand extreme weather. This integrated use of genetic technologies represents a pathway to more nutritious, climate-adapted crops that reduce malnutrition while sustaining agricultural productivity long-term.
