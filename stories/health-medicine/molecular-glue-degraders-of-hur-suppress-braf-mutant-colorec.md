Molecular glue degraders represent a novel therapeutic approach targeting HuR, an RNA-binding protein implicated in BRAF-mutant colorectal cancers. Unlike traditional drugs that block protein function, these degraders eliminate HuR entirely, potentially offering treatment for specific colorectal cancer variants driven by BRAF mutations. This degradation-based mechanism could address an important gap in precision oncology for cancers currently difficult to target with existing approaches.
