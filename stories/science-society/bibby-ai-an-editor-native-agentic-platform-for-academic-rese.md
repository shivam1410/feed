Bibby AI is an editor-native platform that consolidates the entire academic writing workflow into a unified Research-Write-Publish pipeline. The platform integrates literature management, citation insertion, and manuscript formatting tools directly within a document editor interface, streamlining tasks typically scattered across multiple disparate applications and services. By operating on document syntax representations, Bibby deploys AI agents to automate research discovery, writing assistance, and publication formatting throughout the academic workflow. The unified environment reduces context-switching between tools and enables more efficient academic processes, from initial literature review and note-taking through manuscript drafting, comprehensive citation management, and final submission and publication formatting. This consolidation significantly accelerates academic productivity.
