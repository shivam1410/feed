Scientists demonstrated macroscopic quantum coherence in two-component exciton Bose-Einstein condensates within layered semiconductor structures. The system exhibits distinct spin-valley polarized phases and undergoes quantum phase transitions under magnetic fields, remaining stable up to approximately 1.8 K. This represents achievement of exciton condensation, a collective quantum state where numerous particles behave coherently as one. The work validates theoretical predictions and demonstrates controlled manipulation of exotic quantum phases. Such systems could enable new quantum technologies for computing and sensing. The findings deepen understanding of many-body quantum phenomena.
