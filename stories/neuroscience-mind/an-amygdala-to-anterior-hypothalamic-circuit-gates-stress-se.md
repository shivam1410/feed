Researchers discovered a specific brain circuit connecting your amygdala to the hypothalamus that controls how stressed you feel in response to threats. This pathway acts as a gate that determines your sensitivity to stressful events and fear responses. Finding it points toward new treatments for anxiety disorders and could reveal other hidden neural circuits that influence mental health and emotional responses more broadly.
