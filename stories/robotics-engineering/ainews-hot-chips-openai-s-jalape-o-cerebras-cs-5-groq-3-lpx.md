OpenAI unveiled Jalapeño, a custom inference chip with substantial performance advantages over NVIDIA's GB200/GB300 systems. Benchmarks demonstrate 1.5–1.9× greater work per watt at peak throughput and 1.7–3.6× lower end-to-end latency on real workloads. Interactive applications achieve 2.1–4.1× higher performance. The 700-watt-rated chip operated at or below 550 watts during testing. Deployment begins by year-end, with Gen 2 in advanced development and Gen 3 underway. This shift toward specialized inference silicon reflects the inference inflection point in AI economics, where custom silicon enables power efficiency and latency critical for real-time API services and interactive AI assistants.
