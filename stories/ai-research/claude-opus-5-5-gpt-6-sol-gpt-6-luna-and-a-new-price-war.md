OpenAI and Anthropic released new AI models in a price war that's reshaping the market. GPT-6 Luna costs half what its predecessor did, just ten cents per million input tokens and fifty cents per million output tokens, making it among the cheapest models available. Claude Opus 5.5 remains significantly pricier at four dollars and twenty dollars. This fierce competition is forcing providers to dramatically cut prices to win over developers building applications.
