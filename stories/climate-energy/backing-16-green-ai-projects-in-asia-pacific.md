DeepMind is backing 16 organizations across Asia-Pacific to use frontier AI for solving environmental challenges. The projects span climate science, agriculture, and biodiversity protection. By putting advanced AI directly in the hands of scientists and conservationists working on the ground, DeepMind is shifting powerful technology away from tech companies and toward protecting ecosystems and farming that feed millions of people.
