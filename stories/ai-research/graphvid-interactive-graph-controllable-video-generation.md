GraphVid replaces tedious pixel-level trajectory drawing with graph-based semantic control for multi-object video generation. The model accepts structured interaction graphs specifying object relationships rather than requiring users to manually track each object's path. GraphVid-Bench provides a new large-scale dataset with relational annotations. Compared to prior motion-control methods, GraphVid reduces FID by 39.9% and FVD by 37.6%, improves PSNR from 9.87 to 15.98, SSIM from 0.38 to 0.61, while using substantially less data and fewer parameters. Structured semantic interfaces for video control offer more precise multi-subject manipulation while remaining practical for complex, occluded scenes where trajectory drawing becomes ambiguous.
