Scientists say that personalized medicine requires understanding and mapping exposomics, which is a comprehensive lifelong assessment of a person's environmental exposures. These varied exposures shape how diseases develop and progress differently in each individual. Understanding individual environmental histories could finally make treatments truly personalized and tailored to each person rather than using one-size-fits-all approaches, since environment clearly influences health in complex, individual ways.
