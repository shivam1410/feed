Researchers identified manikomycin, a new antibiotic from bacterium Streptomyces rimosus, using improved fractionation strategies to separate bioactive compounds. This depsipeptide targets the E-site of the bacterial ribosome's large subunit—a previously undiscovered mechanism of action. Discovery of novel antibiotic scaffolds with new mechanisms is urgent as bacteria develop resistance. The ability to identify naturally occurring antibiotics with unseen structures and targets represents a critical strategy for combating antimicrobial resistance.
