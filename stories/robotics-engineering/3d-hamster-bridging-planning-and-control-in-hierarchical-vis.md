3D HAMSTER enhances robotic manipulation by combining vision-language models with depth encoding to generate metrically accurate 3D trajectories for point cloud-based control. The framework bridges high-level semantic planning from vision-language models with low-level robotic control. By integrating spatial awareness from depth information with semantic understanding, it generates specific geometric guidance for robot manipulation tasks. The approach translates what language models understand about intended actions into precise 3D coordinates that control policies can execute. This integration of planning and control enables more accurate and reliable robot manipulation in complex scenarios, addressing a fundamental challenge where semantic understanding must translate to precise physical execution.
