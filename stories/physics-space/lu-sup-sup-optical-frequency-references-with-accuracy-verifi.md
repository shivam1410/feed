Two independent 176Lu+ optical clocks achieved fractional frequency uncertainties near 10−19 and agreed with each other at 5.7 × 10−19, representing the most precise timekeeping ever verified. These clocks would gain or lose one second every 15 billion years. This breakthrough enables fundamental physics tests and next-generation positioning and timing systems.
