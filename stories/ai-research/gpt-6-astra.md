OpenAI launched GPT-6 Astra, priced identically to Claude Fable at $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens, positioning it as its primary competitor in the highly competitive AI model market. Astra demonstrates strong benchmark performance across multiple domains: achieving 99.9% on the March-released ARC-AGI 3 benchmark, 100% on ExploitBench security tasks, and 42.4% on ExploitGym exploit discovery. The model excels at long context retrieval, achieving perfect 100% accuracy at 256K-512K token lengths and 96.3% accuracy at 512K-1M tokens. The rollout begins with limited organizations, progressively expanding to ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise users.
