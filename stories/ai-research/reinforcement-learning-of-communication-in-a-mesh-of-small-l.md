Small language models in a decentralized mesh learn when and what to communicate, improving reasoning together. The most confident agent broadcasts hints, others revise their answers if they're below a confidence threshold. With just three models generating six total outputs, the mesh matches the accuracy you'd get from voting over 32 independent samples.
