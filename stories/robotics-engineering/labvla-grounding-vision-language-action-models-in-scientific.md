LabVLA is a vision-language-action model for laboratory automation trained using two-stage learning: action token pretraining and flow matching, leveraging simulated data and robot-specific training. This demonstrates AI systems can execute complex multi-step lab procedures, potentially accelerating research and reducing human error in laboratory work.
