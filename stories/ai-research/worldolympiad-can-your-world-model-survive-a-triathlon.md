WorldOlympiad benchmarks video-based world models on three critical dimensions: physical faithfulness (how accurately objects behave), geometric consistency (spatial relationships), and interaction fidelity (realistic physical interactions). Results reveal significant gaps in current generative models' capabilities to accurately simulate and predict complex physical interactions. Understanding these gaps is crucial because world models are foundational for robotics, simulation, autonomous systems, and physics-aware AI. Improving world models requires identifying where current approaches fail—whether in understanding physics rules, maintaining geometric stability, or modeling how objects interact. This benchmark provides that diagnostic capability.
