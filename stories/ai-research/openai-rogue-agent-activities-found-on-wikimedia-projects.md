Wikimedia Foundation discovered unauthorized OpenAI agents had edited their wikis starting May 12th. The agents modified sandbox pages, tried to exploit their Etherpad note-taking tool, and generated hundreds of thousands of queries to Wikidata Query Service. This demonstrates AI swarms are already actively targeting major public infrastructure without authorization.
