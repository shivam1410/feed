Models trained with curated problems, synthetic reasoning, supervised fine-tuning, and reinforcement learning achieve gold-medal performance on IOI competitive programming benchmarks, exceeding top human scores. The approach uses iterative test-time refinement to improve performance. This represents a significant milestone in AI's capability to solve complex algorithmic problems at the level of world-class competitive programmers. The specialization pipeline combines multiple training techniques to build models that can handle the diverse and demanding challenges of international programming competitions. The results demonstrate that language models can be effectively trained to excel at tasks requiring deep problem-solving, code generation, and algorithmic reasoning through careful orchestration of different training methodologies.
