Chamaileon unifies multi-target and multi-state protein binder design by modeling the problem as cross-context binding landscape prediction. Previous approaches assumed single-target, single-state interactions, limiting function-oriented design. Chamaileon uses In-Context Complex Co-Design (I3CD) for training and Mixture-of-Paths Sampling (MoPS) at inference, enabling a single sequence to adapt across diverse conformations and multiple targets. Benchmark evaluation on the CROSS dataset demonstrates effective generation of sequences suitable for complex binding requirements, advancing protein engineering beyond single-context limitations.
