A sparser way to learn how incompressible fluids flow. The team built on their earlier kernel method, which scaled poorly. The new version uses local interpolants that stay divergence free and run in parallel on a GPU. Across ten fluid benchmarks, sparse models were chosen nearly everywhere. For forced isotropic turbulence, affine terms cut the error by more than tenfold. The sparse models were at least as accurate as the earlier dense method. It matters because fast, accurate flow models can replace slow solvers.
