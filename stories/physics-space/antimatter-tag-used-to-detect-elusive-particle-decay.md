Physics researchers have revived a long-underexplored experimental test of particle-physics theory by using antimatter as a detection tag. The method leverages quantum entanglement to infer the existence of particles that cannot be directly observed. This innovative approach resurrects a promising avenue for testing fundamental physics principles that had been neglected for years.
