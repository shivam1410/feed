Google DeepMind, Google.org, and Google Skills are launching a collaborative free curriculum to train AI researchers and educators across Africa. The program represents a coordinated effort to democratize advanced AI education in a continent historically lacking access to cutting-edge training and development resources. This initiative matters because developing local AI talent in Africa enables region-specific innovation, ensures diverse perspectives shape AI development, creates economic opportunities for underrepresented communities, and builds sustainable capacity independent of external expertise.
