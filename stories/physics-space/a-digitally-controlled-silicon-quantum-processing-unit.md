Silicon quantum processor executes high-fidelity multiqubit circuits with control signals generated by a cryogenic digital controller and transmitted through superconducting cables. This integrated approach, combining digital electronics with quantum devices in one cold environment, reduces noise and improves circuit fidelity for practical quantum computing.
