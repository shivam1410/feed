OpenAI unveiled Jalapeño, its custom inference chip, claiming significant efficiency gains over NVIDIA systems on real workloads. The chip delivered 1.5–1.9× more work per watt at peak throughput, 1.7–3.6× lower end-to-end latency, and 2.1–4.1× higher performance for interactive workloads. Rated at 700W but stayed at or below 550W during testing. OpenAI plans infrastructure deployment by year-end with Gen 2 already in development.
