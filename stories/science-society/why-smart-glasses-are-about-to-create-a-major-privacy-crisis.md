Cheap, discreet AI wearables are spreading into everyday life and risk normalizing non-consensual recording in public and private spaces. Smart glasses are becoming small and affordable enough that anyone can secretly film people around them without being noticed. As they proliferate, recording becomes background noise. This matters because it undermines the privacy expectations we've built around everyday life and shared spaces.
