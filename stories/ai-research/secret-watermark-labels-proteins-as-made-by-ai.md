Scientists developed a hidden digital watermark that marks proteins designed by AI tools like AlphaFold. The marker gets embedded in the protein's structure itself, allowing identification of artificial designs. The catch: the watermark can be erased, so it may not work as a permanent safeguard for reliably tracking which proteins were created by artificial intelligence versus evolved naturally.
