Plastics with embedded enzymes degrade readily in the sea. The enzymes break down ester bonds in these compostable polyesters. Biodegradation is rapid and complete, even in environments with few microbes. The method works at marine sites from coastal seawater to the deep sea. It could help tackle global plastic pollution.
