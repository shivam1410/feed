Google DeepMind used agentic AI to formally verify key cryptographic guarantees of the Signal protocol. The proof-of-concept took four weeks. It independently validates cryptography that protects messages on Signal, Google Messages, and WhatsApp. The work also demonstrates a new AI framework called AlphaProof Code. Schneier calls it a good thing.
