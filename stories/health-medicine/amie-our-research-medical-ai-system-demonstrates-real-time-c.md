Google introduced AMIE, a conversational medical AI system designed for conducting real-time clinical video consultations. In the first-of-its-kind research study, AMIE successfully demonstrated it could conduct full clinical consultations in simulated medical scenarios with appropriate bedside manner. This breakthrough suggests AI could extend medical expertise to remote and under-resourced areas where qualified doctors are scarce, potentially improving healthcare access for millions of people worldwide.
