Three practical tutorials teach numerical computation and machine learning for mathematicians. Topics include exterior calculus via flux formulation, neural networks on elliptic curves and boundary problems, and rigorous evaluation of ML results. The tutorials distinguish numerical agreement from predictive accuracy from structural guarantees from rigorous bounds. Accompanying notebooks let readers reproduce all examples.
