Researchers discovered that transformer embeddings encode a structured, navigable geometry aligned with a consciousness spectrum—ranging from reactive, self-focused patterns to integrative, coherent ones—inspired by contemplative and philosophical traditions. Sentences cluster into locally coherent regions forming a stable manifold, with intermediate states forming a transition corridor. Both greedy and utility-guided trajectories consistently navigate upward through this spectrum, suggesting the geometry is an intrinsic property. This reveals language models may implicitly represent structured consciousness-like states without explicit training.
