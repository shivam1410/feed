A platform for synthesizing C-glycoside molecules has been developed using glycosyl sulfonyl hydrazides as readily accessible free-radical precursors. The strategy enables targeted formation of carbon–carbon bonds across carbohydrate scaffolds with high specificity for the desired three-dimensional arrangement of atoms. By leveraging free-radical chemistry, researchers can selectively attach carbon-containing groups to specific positions on sugar molecules. C-glycosides are highly desirable in pharmaceutical development but notoriously difficult to synthesize. This new approach makes the synthesis more accessible and controllable, enabling creation of complex molecules useful in drug development and materials science.
