Pixel-space video prediction leaves world dynamics implicit, but PhiZero learns physical language—compact discrete representation of world-state transitions—from unlabeled videos via self-supervision. It adopts reason-then-render: first infers future evolution as physical-language sequence, then renders transitions as video. This explicit intermediate representation mirrors how humans abstract predictive structure from visual experience and organize it symbolically for reasoning. Validation across generation and understanding benchmarks confirms physically coherent world modeling. Demonstrated capabilities include realistic and interactive world simulation, fine-grained action-conditioned prediction, and zero-shot motion transfer.
