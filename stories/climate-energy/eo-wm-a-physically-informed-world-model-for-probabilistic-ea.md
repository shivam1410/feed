EO-WM is a video diffusion transformer designed to forecast Earth's land surface conditions using multispectral satellite imagery. The model incorporates physics-informed conditioning frameworks—constraints based on actual meteorological and environmental laws—to better capture weather-driven uncertainties affecting land-surface dynamics like soil moisture, vegetation, and temperature. By anchoring predictions to physical principles rather than pure data pattern-matching, the approach improves probabilistic forecasting. This matters because accurate land-surface forecasting supports climate research, agricultural planning, drought prediction, and environmental monitoring. Physics-informed constraints make predictions more interpretable and reliable, especially for rare weather events or changing climate conditions where historical patterns may be less reliable.
