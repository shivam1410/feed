Mining operations trigger extensive tropical forest deforestation across sub-Saharan Africa, with energy transition minerals—particularly cobalt—driving the largest losses. This reveals a fundamental climate paradox: minerals essential for clean energy transition require destructive extraction that removes carbon-storing forests. Cobalt mining for batteries creates immediate environmental damage while supposedly contributing to climate solutions. The finding highlights critical environmental tradeoffs in transitioning away from fossil fuels and emphasizes need for extraction strategies that minimize deforestation.
