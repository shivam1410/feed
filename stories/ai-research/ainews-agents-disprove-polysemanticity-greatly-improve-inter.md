Goodfire's Nick Cammarata has an essay called Neural Networks Are Already Clean. The working theory is that many known cases of polysemanticity fall to further agentic analysis. Polysemanticity and superposition create tangled knots of weights that resist human interpretation. The newsletter calls it a rare interpretability and alignment story. It matters because if agents can untangle the knots, interpreting models gets easier.
