TypeSafe AI released Jev, a new AI model that outputs numbers instead of text. It takes text or data as input and returns confidence scores, yes/no answers, or numeric ratings. Priced at just $0.042 per million input tokens with free output, it's cheaper than OpenAI's GPT-5 Nano. This makes structured decision-making like categorizing content or scoring customers both faster and cheaper.
