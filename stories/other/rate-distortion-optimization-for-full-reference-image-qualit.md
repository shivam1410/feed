They figured out how to use better image quality metrics like MS-SSIM in video compression, which normally can't work because these metrics don't decompose by blocks. They approximate the metrics using mathematics from the video source. The result: 14 to 37 percent bitrate savings with only ten to 30 percent more encoding work. This matters because videos can now optimize for what humans actually see.
