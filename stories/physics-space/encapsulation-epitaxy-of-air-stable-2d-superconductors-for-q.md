Scientists demonstrated "encapsulation epitaxy"—a technique growing air-stable, two-dimensional superconducting films at 2D–3D material interfaces. Previously, 2D superconductors degraded in air, limiting applications. This breakthrough enables fabrication of stable superconducting quantum circuits using air-tolerant films.
