Xaira Therapeutics demonstrates information-rich data unlocks AI scaling where parameters and compute alone cannot. The X-Cell model predicting gene-expression changes hit a 3.1B parameter scaling plateau with limited data—test loss flatlined while training loss continued climbing. Collecting ~30x more information-rich data broke the plateau, resuming scaling. This required substantial investment in data collection infrastructure (tens of millions) plus compute and research. For cellular modeling requiring comprehensive biological information, data quality and information density proved more limiting than model size. Chief AI Scientist Bo Wang and Chief Discovery Officer Ci Chu made this strategy central to Xaira's approach.
