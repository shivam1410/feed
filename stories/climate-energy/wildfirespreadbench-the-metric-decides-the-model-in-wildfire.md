Benchmarking six architectures on wildfire spread prediction reveals that model rankings differ drastically by metric. The highest average-precision model flags 4–5× the area that actually burned and ranked fifth on F1 and IoU. Across models, three distinct profiles emerge: over-predicting, balanced, and under-predicting, which AP alone cannot distinguish. Input expansion changes AP by 0.03 but architecture varies predictions by 0.24–0.37.
