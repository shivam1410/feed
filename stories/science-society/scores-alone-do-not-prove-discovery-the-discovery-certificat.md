The Discovery Certification Protocol establishes rigorous standards for auditing and validating claims made by AI research agents through executable recovery tests that independently verify discoveries, controlled audit conditions that vary verification approaches, and deterministic verification procedures with proven finite-sample recovery bounds. Rather than trusting reported metrics or agent claims, the protocol demands independent reproducibility and systematic testing under varied controlled conditions. This methodology directly addresses reproducibility concerns in AI-assisted research where agents perform complex analyses, generate novel hypotheses, or solve open problems.
