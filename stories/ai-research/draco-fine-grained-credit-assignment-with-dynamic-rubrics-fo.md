DRACO improves reinforcement learning for long-horizon agents by automatically generating task-specific rubrics and translating trajectory-level rewards into per-step advantages. Unlike prior methods, it works without requiring separate verification models. This dynamic scoring approach enables agents to learn more effectively from sparse rewards in complex, multi-step tasks where credit assignment remains challenging.
