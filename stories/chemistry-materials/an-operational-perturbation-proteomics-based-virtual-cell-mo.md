ProteinTalks, a virtual cell model built from temporal protein-abundance data in systematically perturbed breast cancer cells, serves as an operational tool for drug discovery. By measuring how protein levels change across multiple perturbations, researchers created a computational representation of cellular biochemistry. This model enables rapid testing of drug effects, candidate identification, and mechanism prediction without time-consuming laboratory assays. Virtual cell models accelerate drug development by simulating cellular responses and helping prioritize promising compounds early in the discovery pipeline.
