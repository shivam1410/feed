A newly discovered dinosaur fossil from China with feathery limbs shows that multiple lineages of dinosaurs independently evolved flight. This adds evidence that the path to flight wasn't unique to the lineage leading to modern birds. This matters because it reveals that flight evolution was a powerful strategy that different lineages discovered separately.
