Researchers fabricated a pristine monolayer cuprate superconductor with a single CuO₂ atomic plane—the theoretical minimum. Using high-quality samples, they identified two quantum critical phenomena emerging between the Mott insulating phase and the superconducting phase. These quantum critical points represent fundamental phase transitions at the heart of cuprate superconductivity. The monolayer system enables study of quantum phenomena at ultimate dimensional limits.
