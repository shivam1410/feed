Observations revealed an unusual gas-enshrouded, optically reddened supermassive black hole at cosmic dawn—when the early universe was young. The gas-rich, dusty environment suggests rapid black hole growth under conditions allowing faster accretion than standard models predict. This system may explain mysterious 'little red dots' observed in deep surveys and illuminates how early supermassive black holes achieved their observed masses.
