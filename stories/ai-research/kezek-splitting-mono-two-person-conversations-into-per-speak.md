Kezek separates a mono two-speaker conversation into per-speaker tracks while preserving timing and short replies, using a phase-insensitive speaker-fingerprint-guided two-voice activity model followed by a conditioned time-domain separator, and it improves short-reply retention, leakage, channel-swap, and runtime metrics over an open baseline.
