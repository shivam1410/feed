The Skaling law improves neural scaling-law predictions by coupling model size and training data through a single interaction exponent, rather than treating them independently. This generalized functional form reduces mean absolute percentage error by 1.5-3x in both interpolation and extrapolation regimes. Paired with sparse grid strategies, it achieves full-grid extrapolation using approximately 10x less compute than uniform sweeps. The law enables reliable performance prediction from small-scale experiments, improving compute-budget allocation for large-model training.
