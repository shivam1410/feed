Weight training may help protect muscle while people take weight-loss drugs. The finding is aimed at middle-aged and older adults. Resistance exercise could help them keep their muscle mass and strength during treatment. Muscle loss is a worry with these drugs, so this matters for anyone starting treatment. The article is short, so it does not give trial numbers or describe how much muscle was kept.
