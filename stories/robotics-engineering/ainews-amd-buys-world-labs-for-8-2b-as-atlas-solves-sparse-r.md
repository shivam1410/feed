AMD bought World Labs for 8.2 billion dollars for their spatial intelligence technology. Their Atlas model predicts what a scene looks like from a new camera angle, like an LLM predicts the next word. It solves sparse reconstruction, a long-standing computer vision problem, unlocking applications from robotics simulation to real estate scanning.
