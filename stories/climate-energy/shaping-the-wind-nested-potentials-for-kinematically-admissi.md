Physics-constrained neural surrogate for urban wind prediction that guarantees mass conservation and wall impermeability without pressure correction. Parameterizes velocity through discrete curl of a volume potential. Matters because physics violations creep into neural wind models; this one ensures predictions stay kinematically admissible and usable for urban climate design.
