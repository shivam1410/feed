Intensifying agriculture in Sumatra, Indonesia, is hurting biodiversity. Researchers assessed some 40,000 species, from soil microorganisms to vertebrates. They found agriculture threatens biodiversity through species loss and shifts in community makeup. The effects show up at several spatial scales, some expected and some not.
