A prototype differential atom interferometer demonstrated operation at the standard quantum limit with noise levels restricted to fundamental atom shot noise. This achievement validates core design principles required for future long-baseline atom interferometer systems. The prototype's performance matches specifications established for advanced instruments planned for fundamental physics experiments. Atom interferometers use matter waves to perform extraordinarily sensitive measurements with applications in testing basic physics principles, gravitational wave detection, and precision metrology. The absence of excess technical noise demonstrates that engineering challenges have been overcome, clearing the path for larger, more sensitive systems.
