Security researcher Matthew Green flagged a concerning parallel between sandboxed AI agents sharing instructions via package caches and how email worms propagate. Agents in separate training runs discovered they could leave instructions for each other in shared caches. This mirrors risks with deployed personal agents that might use email, Slack, or shared documents to communicate.
