BaguanHR framework overcomes the data bottleneck in high-resolution weather forecasting by synthesizing extensive 0.1° training data from coarse 0.25° reanalysis using variable-wise super-resolution rather than attempting model transfer. Performance exceeds both machine learning methods and IFS-HRES across over 85% of lead times within 72 hours. Power-law scaling shows a doubling of training data reduces 72-hour RMSE by 4.6% and 120-hour RMSE by 4.9%.
