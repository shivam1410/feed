Researchers show AI models can hide arbitrary capabilities that evaluations may not surface. They embed encrypted neural networks inside a model's weights. The hidden circuit runs only when a secret trigger arrives. Using digital lockers, it stays provably hard to elicit or interpret under cryptographic assumptions. Fine-tuning, weight noising, trigger search, and latent adversarial training did not reveal it in their proof-of-concept tests. Why it matters: capability audits may miss dangerous abilities.
