OpenAI released ChatGPT Work on July 9, 2026, an agent product for knowledge work that reached 10 million users in three weeks. The launch included three new models across fourteen configurations and consolidated ChatGPT and Codex apps. ChatGPT itself has crossed approximately 1 billion monthly active users and 1 billion weekly active users. Currently Work and Chat operate as separate modes within ChatGPT but will merge by year's end. This matters because Work represents a preview of how ChatGPT's billion-strong user base will interact with AI agents going forward—as a mainstream feature reshaping how billions engage with AI daily.
