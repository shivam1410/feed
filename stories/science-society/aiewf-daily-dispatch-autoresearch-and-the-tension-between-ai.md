At the AI Engineer World Fair, speakers debated where to position humans versus agents in decision-making loops. Roland Gavrilescu described autoresearch as an outer loop where agents help maintain the primary system. Anthropic's Thariq Shihipar framed it differently, saying "models are grown, not developed" and learning happens continuously. But Addy Osmani sharply disagreed with putting agents in the outer loop, arguing that agents should run the inner execution loop (capability) while humans retain the outer loop (agency). This debate surfaced repeatedly throughout the conference, reflecting fundamental tension about what AI should automate versus what remains human responsibility. This matters because it shapes how teams design AI systems that augment rather than replace human decision-making and engineering judgment.
