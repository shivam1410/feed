HiFi-UMI presents a portable high-fidelity teleoperation data-collection system achieving 3-millimeter workspace-local end-effector accuracy without external tracking infrastructure. Policies trained solely on HiFi-UMI demonstrations deploy directly on real robots without post-training on costly real-robot data, matching teleoperation performance across three architecture families. Success rates reached 85% on precision insertion tasks despite using zero real-robot training data. The system features head-mounted stereo-inertial SLAM, native relative pose tracking, and synchronized dual wide-angle cameras. High-fidelity human demonstration data alone suffices for deployment.
