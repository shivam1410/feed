Puma predation on deer populations significantly reduces vehicle-wildlife collisions on roads in areas where the big cats are present. Where pumas naturally control deer populations, vehicles are markedly less likely to strike deer on roads, resulting in fewer accidents. This demonstrates an unexpected human safety benefit from maintaining apex predator populations. The ecological role of pumas in controlling prey species generates positive spillover effects for human drivers and road safety, extending beyond purely environmental concerns.
