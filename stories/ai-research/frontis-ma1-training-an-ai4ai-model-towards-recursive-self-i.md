AI system achieves recursive self-improvement in machine learning engineering through meta-evolution. Frontis-MA1 (35B) trained on execution-grounded operators (Draft, Improve, Debug, Crossover) improves Medal Average from 39.39% to 60.61% on MLE-Bench Lite, reaching 71.21% with enhanced search under 12-hour single-GPU budget. Performance exceeds GPT-5.5+Codex and approaches frontier models. Components transfer to held-out benchmarks, indicating learned procedures generalize across domains.
