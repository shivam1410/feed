AREX alternates between research and verification loops: an inner loop gathers evidence and constructs provisional answers; an outer loop audits answers constraint-by-constraint and launches targeted follow-up research. An autonomous context-update tool compresses interaction history into compact states preserving verified evidence. Models range from 4B to 122B MoE parameters. Through mid-training and reinforcement learning, AREX learns to systematically refine answers by leveraging the discovery-verification asymmetry inherent in deep research problems.
