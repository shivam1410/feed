Google developed AMIE, a medical AI system capable of conducting real-time clinical video consultations in simulated settings. This represents a first-of-its-kind study demonstrating AI moving beyond diagnostic support into direct patient interaction. The system processes clinical scenarios in real-time, enabling live responses to unfolding situations. While tested in simulated rather than actual clinical environments, AMIE's capabilities suggest AI could expand access to consultation services. This advancement indicates AI systems are becoming capable of increasingly complex, interactive healthcare roles, though clinical validation would be necessary before real-world deployment.
