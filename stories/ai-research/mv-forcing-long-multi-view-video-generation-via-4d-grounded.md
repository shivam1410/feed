A video diffusion framework generates long, multi-view consistent videos through temporal and view-wise autoregression. The method combines 4D geometric bridging with spatio-temporal distillation techniques to maintain consistency across viewpoints over extended sequences. This enables coherent long-form multi-perspective video synthesis, valuable for content creation, virtual environments, and computer vision training data generation.
