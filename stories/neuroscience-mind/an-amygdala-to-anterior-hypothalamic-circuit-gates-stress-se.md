Researchers mapped a brain circuit from the amygdala to the anterior hypothalamus that controls stress sensitivity. This pathway determines how negatively the brain responds to threatening events and emotional stressors. Understanding this circuit opens new approaches for treating anxiety and stress disorders by targeting how the brain processes fear and perceives threatening situations.
