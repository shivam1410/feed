Researchers engineered probiotic bacteria containing synthetic genetic circuits that sense glucose levels and colonize the gut transiently. These glucose-responsive organisms secrete therapeutic compounds specifically when blood glucose rises. Successful tests in mice and primates demonstrated the bacteria can safely modulate blood sugar through oral administration alone. This sense-and-respond approach represents a novel living therapeutic that activates only when needed, minimizing potential side effects from continuous drug exposure. The engineered microbes essentially create a biological closed-loop control system, providing insulin-independent diabetes management that could reduce medication burdens and improve quality of life for patients.
