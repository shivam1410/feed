SolarWM provides an open framework and unified training recipe for developing interactive video world models across diverse datasets and model backbones. The system enables long-horizon real-time video rollouts, allowing researchers to simulate extended sequences of future events. By standardizing the training process and releasing open data, SolarWM democratizes access to world model development tools. The framework's ability to work with different data sources and generator architectures makes it flexible and adaptable to various applications. This accessible platform accelerates research in embodied AI and video understanding by providing reliable, scalable infrastructure rather than requiring each team to build solutions from scratch.
