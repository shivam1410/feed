Qwen-Drive-1.0 is a vision-language foundation model for autonomous driving that unifies three critical capabilities: 3D perception, visual question answering, and motion planning through shared learned representations. The model uses staged training to progressively build competency across interconnected tasks. By unifying functions in a single model with shared representations, the system can leverage learning across domains and reduce need for separately trained specialist models. This foundation model approach to autonomous driving differs from traditional pipelines handling perception, prediction, and planning as separate stages. The work represents an initial step toward more integrated and efficient end-to-end autonomous driving systems.
