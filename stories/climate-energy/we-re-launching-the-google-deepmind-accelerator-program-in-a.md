Google DeepMind is launching an Accelerator program in Asia-Pacific to address environmental risks in a region vulnerable to climate change despite driving global economic growth. Green technologies are gaining momentum across the region. The program aims to support climate solutions in climate-vulnerable economies. This matters because it directs advanced AI capabilities toward environmental challenges in economically important but climate-vulnerable regions.
