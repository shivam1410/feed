Fixed teams of AI agents learn to organize themselves during problem-solving: who speaks when, how to share information, when to challenge ideas. Trained on just fifteen and twenty-five problems, they reach 66.7 percent accuracy on math, versus 48.8 for the best individual. On AIME 2026, they beat the optimal router by 13.4 points. This matters because teamwork emerges from shared experience.
