Grounded Action Models (GAMs) represent a new approach to robot foundation models using explicit 3D spatial grounding. On RoboTwin 2.0, GAMs achieved 55.3% success across 50 tasks, including 47.6% under scene randomization. On LIBERO-PRO, GAMs reached 61% average success across 16 perturbation settings, substantially outperforming prior methods. By explicitly modeling which objects matter and their precise locations, GAMs improve manipulation performance even when objects are relocated or newly designated.
