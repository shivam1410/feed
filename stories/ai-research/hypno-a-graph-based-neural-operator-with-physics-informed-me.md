arXiv:2607.20541v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We introduce HypNO, a graph-based neural operator for scalar hyperbolic conservation laws. HypNO operates directly on a space-time graph of finite-volume cells and uses adjacency-factored, physics-informed message passing to respect upwinding and entropy admissibility near shocks. We benchmark the architecture on the Lighthill-Whitham-Richards (LWR) and Aw-Rascle-Zhang (ARZ) traffic-flow models, a stress test for operator-learning methods because of their simultaneous global transport and shock formation. HypNO predicts solution snapshots accurately across a range of initial conditions while capturing the shocks and discontinuities of the solution.
