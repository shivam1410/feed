Scientists created EEGAgentBench to evaluate LLM agents on brain signal analysis. It spans six applications with signal lengths from 2 seconds to nearly 23 hours, requiring agents to select tools, accumulate evidence, and build workflows. They tested 29 frontier LLMs from 15 model families, revealing how LLMs handle reasoning over extended medical data.
