DySCo is a framework for downscaling global climate model outputs to high spatial resolution while preserving dynamical consistency with coarse-grained inputs. Using data-driven nudging, it creates dynamically paired training trajectories without intrusive GCM modifications. The approach corrects statistical biases from unresolved processes while ensuring rare extreme events and causal relationships remain traceable to actual atmospheric dynamics. This matters for infrastructure design and disaster forecasting: high-resolution projections maintaining physical consistency enable more credible storyline-based risk assessment than methods optimizing statistics alone.
