AlphaGenome Atlas catalogs how every possible single-nucleotide variant in the human genome affects health, disease risk, and biological traits. This comprehensive database encompasses millions of individual genetic changes across the entire genome. It serves as a powerful reference tool for understanding your complete genetic landscape and predicting disease risk from your DNA sequence at an unprecedented scale and accuracy that was impossible before.
