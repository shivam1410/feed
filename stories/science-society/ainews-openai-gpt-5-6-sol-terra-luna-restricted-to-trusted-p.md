OpenAI announced GPT-5.6, a new three-tier model family, with Sol positioned as the flagship frontier model, Terra as mid-range, and Luna for fast inference. The release is limited to trusted partners rather than public availability, with broader access planned in coming weeks. In evaluations, Sol outperforms Mythos on certain coding agent tasks but deliberately does not cross OpenAI's 'Cyber Critical' threshold—it identified exploitation primitives and bugs in browsers but did not autonomously produce functional end-to-end exploits under test conditions. This matters because it illustrates how frontier AI labs are balancing capability advancement with safety controls, using staged releases and explicit safety thresholds to manage deployment risk as models grow more powerful.
