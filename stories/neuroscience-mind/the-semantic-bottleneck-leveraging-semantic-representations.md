Non-invasive brain-to-text decoding breakthrough: Researchers mapped magnetoencephalography (MEG) brain responses directly to semantic embeddings, enabling sentence-level text reconstruction without requiring explicit word-level alignment. By leveraging semantic (meaning-based) representations instead of linguistic units, the approach reduces calibration complexity. This advances practical neurotechnology applications, including brain-to-text communication for paralyzed patients, with potential for faster deployment across subjects due to reduced per-user training requirements.
