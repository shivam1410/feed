Google DeepMind just shipped Gemini 4 Neon, its fourth Gemini 4 model in about seven weeks. It costs $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens, with a 1M-token context window. It also offers four effort levels. The newsletter says Neon is cheaper and faster than Carbon, yet it beats Carbon on some measures. It's rolling out to the paid Gemini API and to Pro and Ultra subscribers. For builders, that's a fast, cheap option with a large context window.
