Three kidney transplant recipients with highly sensitized immune systems—which typically reject donated organs—successfully received transplants using CAR-T immunotherapy. This "transformative" approach represents a breakthrough for patients previously considered unsuitable candidates. Highly sensitized individuals face severe shortages of compatible donors and high rejection risks. This success expands transplant access for a medically challenging population.
