Computer scientists developed a smarter way for AI agents to communicate by sending only necessary information instead of complete data. Their method, CacheBack, discards 75% of unnecessary information while improving task accuracy by 14.7 percentage points and reducing response time by 3.2 times. This makes AI teams more efficient for multi-agent problem solving.
