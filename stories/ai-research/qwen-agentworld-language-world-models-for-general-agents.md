General-purpose agents struggle without domain-specific simulators for training in diverse environments. Qwen-AgentWorld uses language-based world models to simulate diverse environments across multiple domains without requiring domain-specific implementation code. This approach enables agents to train on simulated tasks improving downstream real-world performance. Language-based simulation scales across domains, reduces engineering overhead for agent development, and accelerates creation of generally capable agents.
