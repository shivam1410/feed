NASA tested a new protective vest on an uncrewed spacecraft that reduced radiation exposure by fifty percent. The garment showed effectiveness in shielding astronauts against the worst impacts of solar storms, a major radiation hazard for deep space missions. Results suggest the technology could significantly improve crew safety during extended missions beyond Earth's protective magnetic field, addressing a critical concern for human space exploration.
