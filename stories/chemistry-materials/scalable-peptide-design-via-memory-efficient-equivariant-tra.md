MEET (Memory Efficient Equivariant Transformer) combines E(3) equivariance with memory-efficient attention for scalable peptide design. The architecture maintains coupled invariant and equivariant feature streams while reformulating geometric computation to scale linearly with atom count. Integrated into a VAE and latent diffusion pipeline, MEET improves binding affinity, physical validity, and sample diversity over existing peptide design methods. Testing on large AFDB-derived datasets confirms the approach supports systematic scaling—enabling better peptides through combined model and data scale-up.
