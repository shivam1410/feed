Researchers have eliminated the lock-in phenomenon that has limited ring laser gyroscope precision for decades, using spontaneous symmetry breaking in a helium-neon laser. Lock-in—where friction causes tiny rotations to be unmeasurable—has been the fundamental barrier to accurate near-zero rotation sensing. By exploiting symmetry-breaking physics, the new approach enables precise measurements without requiring external components, a major advantage for miniaturization and system integration. High-precision gyroscopes are essential for navigation, aerospace applications, and scientific instrumentation. This breakthrough removes a long-standing technical barrier, promising more compact, accurate, and practical gyroscopic sensors for diverse applications demanding rotation measurement.
