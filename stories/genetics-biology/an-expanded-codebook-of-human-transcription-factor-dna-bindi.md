Researchers performed a panel of complementary assays analyzing multiple aspects of DNA sequence specificity to catalog transcription factor binding patterns. They identified more than 100 new binding motifs—specific DNA sequences recognized by transcription factors that control gene expression. These motifs are essential for understanding genetic regulation. The expanded codebook provides crucial reference data for characterizing previously uncharacterized human transcription factors. This advances fundamental understanding of how transcription factors control genes and has applications in studying diseases where transcription factor dysfunction plays a role.
