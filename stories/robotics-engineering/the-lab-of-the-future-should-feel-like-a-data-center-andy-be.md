Lila Sciences is building a fully automated AI-guided robotic laboratory operating 24/7, envisioning science labs as massive token generators for training general scientific reasoning. The facility uses robotics with vision-language models for control and has generated over 10 trillion experimentally-validated scientific reasoning tokens. The ambitious platform simultaneously tackles biology, chemistry, drug discovery, and materials science. Their thesis follows the "bitter lesson": scale experimental data production through automation to train a general reasoner capable of solving any scientific problem.
