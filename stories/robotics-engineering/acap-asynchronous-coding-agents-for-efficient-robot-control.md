ACAP runs one robot as a team of persistent coding agents. Each agent controls a group of parts, such as an arm with its gripper. A hyper-agent assigns tasks and coordinates dependencies. Across 72 real-world rollouts, it cut task-balanced time cost by 28.2% against a single-agent Direct Policy. It also beat Fixed Delegation on all eight Collaborative settings.
