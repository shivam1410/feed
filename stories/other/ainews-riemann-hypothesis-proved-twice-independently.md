Two mathematicians claim independent proofs of the Riemann Hypothesis, one of math's most famous open problems. Levent Alpöge of Anthropic used Claude and released Lean code of about 1.3GB instead of a paper. Elliot Glazer puts the chance of a Lean bug well under 1%. Alessandro Masullo released a 940-page human proof he worked on for 12 years. Experts say verification will take years. One proof is probably correct but hard to read, the other readable but probably flawed.
