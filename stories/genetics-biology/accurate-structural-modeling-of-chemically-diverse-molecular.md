Vilya-2, a diffusion transformer, extends all-atom structural prediction from individual molecules to their interactions with protein targets, enabling transfer learning across molecular types. It recovers 59.1% of peptide interfaces to sub-2-angstrom backbone accuracy, far exceeding co-folding models even when provided the bound receptor. It generalizes to novel small-molecule docking and macrocycles several-fold larger than training examples, and serves as a foundation model fine-tuned for compound enrichment in drug discovery workflows.
