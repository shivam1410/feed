High-precision neodymium isotope analysis of lavas from the submarine Fani Maoré volcano in the Comoros archipelago detected mantle material from Earth's Hadean period, over 4 billion years ago. This discovery reveals ancient, compositionally distinct mantle rock remains intact in the deep Earth and surfaces through volcanic activity. The finding provides a geological window into Earth's primordial composition and deep internal structure.
