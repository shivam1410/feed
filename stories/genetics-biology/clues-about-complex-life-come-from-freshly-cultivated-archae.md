Researchers have brought two archaeal strains into culture in the lab. They belong to a lineage closely related to the ancestor of eukaryotic cells, the complex cells with membrane-bound organelles. The strains have simple internal organization, elaborate surface structures, microbial partnerships, and a protein-dependent lifestyle. These traits offer clues about how complex cells arose.
