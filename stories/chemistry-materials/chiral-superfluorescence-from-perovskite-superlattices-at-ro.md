Chiral perovskite superlattices—specially engineered crystalline materials—emit circularly polarized light with preferential handedness at room temperature through a quantum phenomenon called superfluorescence. Remarkably, the intensity and polarization of this emission can be precisely controlled using weak magnetic fields applied to the material. This discovery combines materials science with quantum optics, enabling potential applications in quantum information processing and specialized optical technologies requiring chiral light sources with tunable properties. The room-temperature operation makes this particularly promising for practical applications requiring stable chiral light generation.
