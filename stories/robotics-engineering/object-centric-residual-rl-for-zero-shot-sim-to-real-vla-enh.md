An object-centric residual reinforcement learning framework enhances real-world vision-language-action (VLA) robot models by training corrective policies in simulation that transfer zero-shot to the physical world. Despite the simulation-to-reality gap, the approach improves robustness for complex tasks. This is significant because most robotics research struggles with sim-to-real transfer; this method offers a practical path to more reliable autonomous manipulation.
