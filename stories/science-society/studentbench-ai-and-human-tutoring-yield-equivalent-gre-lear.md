StudentBench evaluated 2,383 students and found AI tutoring achieved statistically equivalent GRE learning gains to expert human tutoring (p=.015), with AI surpassing humans in five of seven subject domains. One AI tutor delivered equivalent learning at 918 times lower cost ($0.0052 versus $4.81 per percentage-point gain). Faster AI responses correlated with higher student engagement.
