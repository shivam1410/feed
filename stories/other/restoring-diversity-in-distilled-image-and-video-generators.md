Distilled few-step diffusion models exhibit diversity collapse because activation variance is concentrated into fewer directions in an intermediate block at the initial denoising step, and ReDiverse restores sample diversity by reintroducing teacher variance directions through adapter fine-tuning or training-free activation steering while preserving generation quality.
