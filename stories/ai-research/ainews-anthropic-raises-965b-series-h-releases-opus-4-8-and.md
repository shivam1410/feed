Anthropic reported $47 billion annual revenue run-rate and raised $65 billion in Series H funding at a $900 billion pre-money valuation, temporarily surpassing OpenAI on several headline metrics outside compute and non-coding benchmarks. The company released Opus 4.8, which resolved issues found in Opus 4.7 and achieved state-of-the-art performance on economically relevant benchmarks. Most significantly, Anthropic introduced "ultracode" (dynamic workflows) in Claude Code, enabling massively parallel agent orchestration—demonstrating its power when engineer Jarred Sumner rewrote Bun (750k LOC) from Zig to Rust in 6 days.
