Researchers have demonstrated base editing, a genome-editing technique capable of making precise alterations to human embryos. The work has generated both praise for its technical precision and significant alarm about ethical and safety implications. Experts emphasize the technique remains far from clinical readiness and requires extensive further study. Critics worry that media attention and investor interest will trigger a premature commercialization rush before sufficient evidence of safety and efficacy is established.
