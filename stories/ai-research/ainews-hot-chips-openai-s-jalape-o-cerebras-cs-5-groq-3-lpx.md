OpenAI's Jalapeño inference chip delivers 1.5–1.9× more work per watt and 1.7–3.6× lower end-to-end latency than NVIDIA GB200/GB300 systems on real workloads, with 2.1–4.1× higher performance for interactive use cases. The chip is rated at 700W but ran at or below 550W in testing. Deployment into OpenAI's infrastructure begins by year-end, with Gen 2 already deep in development. This represents a major shift in the inference stack toward custom hardware for efficiency and latency-critical applications.
