OpenAI's internal model solved the planar unit distance problem, an 80-year-old mathematical conjecture posed by Erdős in 1946, spending less than $1,000 and under 32 hours of computation. The general-purpose reasoner (reportedly GPT 5.6) produced 125 pages of output containing a disproof—technically not a proof, but rather a new construction family surpassing square-grid-based solutions. Critically, this result came from a general-purpose language model rather than a domain-specific mathematics system, suggesting extended reasoning capabilities may generalize beyond mathematics to novel research domains. The achievement demonstrates that scaling general-purpose models with extended reasoning enables discovery in unsolved mathematical problems, potentially opening new frontiers for AI-assisted research.
