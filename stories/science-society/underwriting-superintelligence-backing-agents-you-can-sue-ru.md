AIUC raised 40 million dollars to build insurance and standards for AI agents. Their thesis: trust, not capability, is the constraint blocking adoption. AIUC-1 is the emerging standard, backed by real insurance, with stress-testing for jailbreaks, hallucinations, and data leaks. Companies like Cursor and Harvey confront liability when autonomous systems fail. Matters because it addresses the actual problem holding back deployment: who pays when AI breaks things.
