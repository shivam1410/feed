Foundation model trained on 36 million cells from hundreds of experiments, spanning dozens of tissues and eight species, learns to capture fundamental patterns of cell organization and variation. This universal embedding represents principles applicable across tissue types and diverse organisms. By training on such massive, heterogeneous data, the model discovers what is common about how cells vary and organize themselves. This creates a reusable foundation applicable to future cell biology research. Such comprehensive cell understanding could accelerate discoveries about cellular function and help researchers identify how cells behave differently in health versus disease states.
