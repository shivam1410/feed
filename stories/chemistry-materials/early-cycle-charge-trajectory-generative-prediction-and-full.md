Iron-chromium redox flow batteries are promising for long-duration grid storage due to abundant, inexpensive active materials, but predicting degradation is challenging because multiple coupled processes gradually reshape charge voltage/current behavior. Researchers developed FlowBD-E1, a machine learning framework predicting complete charge trajectories over an industrial battery's 289-cycle lifetime using only the first 9 cycles. The architecture combines multi-scale convolution, lifecycle Transformers, and age-aware decoders. Testing showed remarkable accuracy: 0.731% error predicting voltage/current trajectories and <1% error estimating state-of-health across the entire lifecycle.
