Researchers created a "virtual biotech" comprising up to 37,000 AI agents organized under a "chief scientist" to discover new drugs. This system successfully identified a promising lung-cancer treatment, demonstrating that large-scale, hierarchically-organized AI can accelerate pharmaceutical development. The approach represents a shift toward AI-driven drug discovery, potentially reducing the time and cost required to bring new treatments from lab to patients.
