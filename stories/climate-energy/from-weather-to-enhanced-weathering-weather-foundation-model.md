Weather models can help measure carbon removal from Enhanced Rock Weathering. Researchers tested five weather foundation models on 5,192 sequences from 264 fields in Brazil and the US. The best, TerraNova-ERW, ranks measurements correctly in fields it never saw. With two samples per field, it recovers over 60% of the highest-removal fields. It can also cut sampling by 28 to 98% on the densest fields. Predicting a new field with no samples is not yet feasible. Cheaper measurement matters because today's method needs dense soil sampling and lab analysis.
