A Google DeepMind scientist explains how AI weather models predict cyclones faster and much earlier than traditional meteorology methods. AI systems can warn communities sooner and give them significantly more time to evacuate or prepare for dangerous storms. This lifesaving capability comes from training machine learning systems on vast amounts of historical weather data, satellite imagery, and global weather patterns.
