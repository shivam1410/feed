Scientists created comprehensive spatially resolved map of human proteome showing where each protein is located in healthy tissues and various cancers. This atlas integrates protein expression with tissue architecture, revealing cell type-specific signatures and differences between normal and malignant tissue. Spatial map generates insights into developmental biology—how proteins guide tissue formation—and oncology—which proteins distinguish cancers and serve as therapeutic targets. Resource accelerates cancer biomarker discovery by revealing druggable proteins differentially expressed in tumors versus healthy tissue.
