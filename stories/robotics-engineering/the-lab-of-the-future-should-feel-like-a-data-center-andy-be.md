Lila Sciences operates an automated laboratory with AI-guided robotics and lab equipment generating scientific data 24/7, treating the lab as an infinite token generator. The facility features floating robotic plates on automated tracks, vision-language model-controlled Windows boxes, and equipment running continuous experiments across biology, chemistry, drug discovery, and materials science.
