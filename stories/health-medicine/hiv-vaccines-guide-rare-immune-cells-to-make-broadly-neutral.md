Three studies in non-human primates demonstrate that strategically designed vaccines can prime immune B cells to produce broadly neutralizing antibodies against HIV. Broadly neutralizing antibodies are rare and valuable because they can block infection from multiple HIV variants, making them the gold standard for vaccine development. This research shows that vaccines can be engineered to trigger production of these powerful antibodies, offering genuine hope that an effective HIV vaccine may be achievable.
