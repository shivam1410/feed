New fossil evidence suggests juvenile Tyrannosaurus rex were effective predators from early in life. Fossils indicate baby T. rex possessed killing capabilities, contradicting prior assumptions about their development. These discoveries help researchers reconstruct the developmental pathways and predatory prowess of these apex dinosaurs. The findings fill important gaps in understanding T. rex ontogeny and behavior during their earliest life stages.
