A new chemistry-based super-resolution imaging framework enables 3D visualization of live cells without laser illumination, offering high sensitivity and extended temporal imaging windows. By replacing laser scanning and fluorescent dyes with luminescent chemical reactions, the method overcomes previous spatiotemporal resolution limitations. The laser-free approach reduces phototoxicity, enabling longer observation periods while achieving superior resolution compared to prior reaction-based imaging.
