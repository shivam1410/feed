Smartphones can passively measure heart rate by detecting tiny skin-color changes that occur during regular device use, enabling continuous health monitoring without specialized hardware. This passive approach provides early warning capability for health issues as heart rate patterns shift. The research also examined Richard Feynman's solution to the 'restaurant dilemma' mathematical problem. This technology leverages existing smartphone cameras and sensors to enable personal health surveillance during normal daily activities, making medical-grade monitoring accessible to all.
