Novel enzymatic pathways produce previously unknown polyene compounds, with unusual enzymes capable of adding additional sugars to polyene scaffolds. These modified polyenes can be synthesized through efficient fermentation and show promise as safer, more effective antifungal treatments, expanding options for next-generation antifungal therapeutics with improved bioactivity, specificity, and reduced systemic toxicity.
