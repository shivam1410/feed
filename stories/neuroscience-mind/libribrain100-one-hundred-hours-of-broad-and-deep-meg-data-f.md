LibriBrain100, a large-scale MEG dataset, provides 100+ hours of high-quality recordings for neural speech decoding. The depth-first design includes 80 hours from a single subject—8× more within-subject data than previous datasets. Researchers also collected 40 minutes each from 32 additional subjects, demonstrating broad and deep coverage. Using an existing decoding model, the dataset achieved state-of-the-art word-classification performance. Pre-training on deep single-subject data followed by finetuning on multi-subject data substantially compensates for limited per-subject data in real-world scenarios, validating scalable approaches to brain-to-text interfaces.
