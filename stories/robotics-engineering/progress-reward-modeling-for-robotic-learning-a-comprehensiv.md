Progress reward modeling provides feedback during task execution beyond terminal success signals, yet the literature lacks a shared framework due to inconsistent observations, goal specifications, outputs, supervision sources, and evaluation protocols. This survey organizes the field into three steps: problem interface (what information is received and produced), methods (how signals are constructed), and data/benchmarks (how supervision is obtained and validated). Unified organization enables meaningful comparison of progress estimation and reward generation approaches across robotic learning research.
