Ebola preparedness must go beyond human health to include ecosystem management, beginning before symptoms appear in people. The approach recognizes that outbreaks originate in animal populations and environmental conditions before reaching humans. By starting prevention at the ecosystem level, public health systems can potentially stop outbreaks at their source rather than responding after cases emerge. Why it matters: understanding ecological factors underlying disease transmission is critical to preventing future epidemics and enabling early detection before human spillover occurs.
