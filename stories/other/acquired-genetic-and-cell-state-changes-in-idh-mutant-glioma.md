Longitudinal analyses combining transcriptomics, chromatin mapping and genomics reveal detailed mechanisms driving IDH-mutant glioma progression across two distinct tumor types. Research demonstrates disease advancement results from interdependent genetic, epigenetic and microenvironmental factors working in concert. No single molecular change alone explains tumor progression—instead, multiple biological layers integrate dynamically to drive evolution. This detailed mechanistic understanding enables identification of new treatment strategies and prognostic markers for brain tumors.
