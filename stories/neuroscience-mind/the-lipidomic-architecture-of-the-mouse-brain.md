A new atlas maps the mouse brain through its fatty acid composition, revealing over 500 distinct biochemical regions where specific lipids create functional territories. These lipid neighborhoods align with brain cell types and neural wiring patterns, and they're remodeled during pregnancy. The discovery shows that brain organization depends not just on cells but fundamentally on lipids too.
