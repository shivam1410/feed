Logit-Contribution Scoring (LOCOS) identifies specific attention heads in large language models responsible for synthesizing non-literal meaning from context. By measuring how these heads contribute to answer predictions, LOCOS outperforms existing methods on information retrieval benchmarks and reveals how LLMs compose complex reasoning.
