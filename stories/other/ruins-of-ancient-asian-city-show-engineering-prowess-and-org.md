The 5,000-year-old city of Liangzhu in ancient China demonstrates sophisticated engineering adapted to challenging environmental conditions. Built in a waterlogged region prone to flooding, the city featured an integrated system of mounds, dams, and fortified walls. These structures showcase advanced hydraulic engineering, careful urban planning, and organizational capability required to coordinate construction and water management in difficult terrain. The ruins reveal ancient Asian civilizations possessed remarkable technical expertise 5,000 years ago.
