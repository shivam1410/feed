Sickle-cell disease causes stem cells throughout the body to age prematurely, impairing their regenerative function. Researchers demonstrated that a combination of drugs can restore stem-cell function and reverse this accelerated aging in mouse models of the blood disorder. This finding suggests a therapeutic pathway by targeting the mechanism of premature stem-cell aging, offering hope for improved treatments.
