Google DeepMind proposed a cognitive framework for evaluating progress toward artificial general intelligence (AGI)—AI systems matching or exceeding human capability across diverse domains. The framework establishes criteria and benchmarks for measuring AGI advancement. DeepMind launched a Kaggle hackathon to build capability benchmarks, crowdsourcing expertise to develop rigorous tests. AGI measurement is challenging because no consensus exists on what capabilities define AGI or how to rigorously test them. A standardized cognitive framework addresses this by providing shared metrics. Such frameworks are essential for the AI community to track genuine progress and communicate credibly about AGI timelines.
