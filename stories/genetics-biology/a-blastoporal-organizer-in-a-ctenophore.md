Studies of the comb jelly Mnemiopsis leidyi and sea anemone Nematostella vectensis identified a conserved core signaling pathway present in both organisms. This signaling pathway appears to be a key evolutionary innovation enabling the transition from unicellular organisms to multicellular animals. Understanding this ancient genetic mechanism illuminates how the earliest animals organized into complex body structures hundreds of millions of years ago. The pathway orchestrates fundamental developmental processes allowing individual cells to communicate and coordinate to form specialized tissues and organs. This discovery connects molecular genetics to major evolutionary transitions in animal evolution.
