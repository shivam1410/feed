Researchers used machine-learning algorithms to screen chemical libraries and identify new antibiotic compounds effective against drug-resistant strains. This computational approach demonstrates the potential of AI-assisted drug discovery to accelerate antibiotic development as traditional approaches struggle to keep pace with growing resistance worldwide.
