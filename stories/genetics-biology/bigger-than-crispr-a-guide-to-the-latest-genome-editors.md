Researchers developed gene editors that go beyond CRISPR, potentially replacing entire genes and even engineering complex cellular circuits inside living cells. The big challenge is getting these tools safely into cells without triggering unwanted immune responses or off-target cuts. Cracking the delivery problem could make these editors more powerful than CRISPR itself.
