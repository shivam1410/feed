Theoretical analysis proves that Hypergraph Neural Networks with 3-body message passing are universal approximators for potential energy surfaces. Multi-layer completeness theory justifies the widespread practice of stacking message-passing layers with per-layer cutoffs smaller than actual interaction ranges, providing rigorous mathematical foundation for practical architectures like DPA3 and CHGNet used in molecular simulation.
