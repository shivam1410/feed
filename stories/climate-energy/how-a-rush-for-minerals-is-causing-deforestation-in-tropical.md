An analysis of mining-driven deforestation between 2001 and 2020 in sub-Saharan Africa near active mines documents the extent of habitat loss from mineral extraction. This data could help inform conservation strategies and identify regions most vulnerable to mining impacts. Understanding the spatial relationship between mining operations and deforestation is important for policymakers working to balance economic development with environmental protection in resource-rich regions, particularly biodiversity hotspots.
