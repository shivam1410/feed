Anthropic has revoked access to Fable and Mythos models for all customers worldwide following a US government directive citing national cybersecurity risks. The models were suspended just three days after their release, affecting not only US government employees and vendors but all global customers—an unprecedented move. The government claimed the models posed jailbreak risks, though Anthropic disputes this characterization, stating the government provided only verbal evidence of a narrow, non-universal jailbreak. This incident sets an important precedent for government intervention in AI model releases globally. The suspension highlights tensions between national security concerns and open AI development.
