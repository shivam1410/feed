Indie developer Popcar says AI is now decompiling games at scale. The results cut both ways. Old games are being ported to new hardware, often in a browser using WebAssembly, and game preservation projects are booming. But anyone could reuse other software this way. Companies could copy competitors, and paid apps could be open-sourced against the owner's will. Popcar argues that people could then own the software they bought, whatever the company thinks. Why it matters: it could keep old games playable as servers shut down.
