In-Context World Modeling (ICWM) enables robot policies to infer system variables—such as mass, friction, and dynamics—from their own interactions with novel environments, then adapt to new configurations without parameter retraining. By framing system identification as an in-context adaptation problem, robots learn general-purpose models that adjust on-the-fly. This matters because robots frequently encounter new environments; in-context adaptation could eliminate expensive retraining and enable rapid deployment across diverse real-world settings.
