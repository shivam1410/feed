Wave power controls where mangroves grow along open coastlines worldwide. After analyzing 20 years of wave data, researchers proved that wave energy determines whether mangrove forests thrive or fail in coastal regions. This shows ocean dynamics matter as much as climate in shaping coastal ecosystems and reveals an unexpected primary driver of forest distribution patterns.
