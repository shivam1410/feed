The largest iron hoard ever found—one tonne, enough to forge roughly 500 swords—reveals Iron Age societies engaged in vast trade networks. This cache provides material evidence of sophisticated metallurgical commerce and resource distribution across ancient populations. The discovery fundamentally expands understanding of how Iron Age civilizations accumulated and moved valuable metals.
