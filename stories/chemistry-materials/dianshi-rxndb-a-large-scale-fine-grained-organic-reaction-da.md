DianShi-RxnDB is an automated platform that extracts millions of structured organic reactions from patents and normalizes them into a single queryable database. The fully automated pipeline handles extraction, standardization, and validation—tasks traditionally requiring manual chemist review. By converting unstructured patent text into machine-readable reaction data at scale, the platform enables AI agents and researchers to identify patterns, predict outcomes, and discover new chemistry faster. This removes a major bottleneck: chemistry AI systems previously lacked sufficient structured training data.
