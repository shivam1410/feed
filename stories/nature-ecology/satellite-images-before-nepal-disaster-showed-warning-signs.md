Satellite imagery detected accelerated movement of a glacier-rock mass complex just days before it collapsed in Nepal, triggering a deadly flash flood. The images revealed warning signs visible from space before the disaster struck. This finding suggests satellite monitoring could provide crucial early warnings for glacial hazards in remote mountain regions where ground-based monitoring is difficult. Early detection systems using satellite data could potentially save lives by enabling evacuations and preparations before catastrophic failures occur.
