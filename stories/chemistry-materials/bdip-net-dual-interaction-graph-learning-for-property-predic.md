BDIP-Net, a graph neural network, efficiently predicts properties of stacked bilayer materials. The framework uses MatterSim-D3 for DFT-quality structure generation from monolayer building blocks at reduced computational cost, then BDIP-Net models intra- and inter-layer interactions through interaction-specific potentials and adaptive fusion. Evaluated on homobilayers, heterobilayers, and twisted systems, it consistently outperforms existing graph neural networks and potential-based approaches. The model explicitly distinguishes different interaction types during property prediction, addressing prior limitations of existing machine-learning models for bilayer materials.
