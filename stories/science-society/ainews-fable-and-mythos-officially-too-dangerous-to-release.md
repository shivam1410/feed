Anthropic revoked Fable and Mythos—released just three days prior—for all customers worldwide on US government directive citing cybersecurity risk and alleged vulnerability to jailbreaking. Anthropic disputes the severity, claiming the government provided only verbal evidence of a "narrow, non-universal jailbreak" rather than documented proof. The revocation's scope extends globally to all users, not just US government systems, establishing a significant precedent for state control over model availability. This matters because it raises critical questions about model sovereignty, the legitimacy of safety claims used to restrict access, and governmental authority to demand sudden global suppression of commercial AI products without transparent evidence.
