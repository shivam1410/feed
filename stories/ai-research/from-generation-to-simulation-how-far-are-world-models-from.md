Generative world models lag behind traditional simulators despite recent progress. Researchers evaluated eight core capabilities and identified significant gaps in physical guarantees, state feedback, and long-horizon stability, though interaction and controllability show promise. Understanding these limitations helps guide improvements toward simulation-grade reliability.
