Researchers achieved absorption spectroscopy of thorium-229 nuclear excited states using thorium-doped calcium fluoride crystals, establishing a practical pathway toward building highly stable, efficient solid-state optical nuclear clocks. These clocks offer superior stability compared to conventional atomic clocks, with applications in fundamental physics, timekeeping, and precision measurements. The demonstrated technique shows feasibility for realizing this long-sought advancement in nuclear clock technology.
