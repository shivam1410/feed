Two-dimensional hexagonal boron nitride (hBN) radio-frequency switches were co-integrated onto gallium nitride (GaN) microchips to create programmable millimeter-wave integrated circuits for 6G technology. The hybrid approach leverages hBN's switching properties to advance next-generation wireless hardware. This demonstrates how layered materials can enhance semiconductor devices for future telecommunications.
