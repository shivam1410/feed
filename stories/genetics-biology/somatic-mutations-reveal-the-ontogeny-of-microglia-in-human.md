Somatic mutations in microglia—immune cells in the brain—serve as a molecular record revealing how these cells develop and change across the human lifespan. By analyzing accumulated mutations, researchers can reconstruct the developmental history of microglia from early development through aging. Understanding microglia ontogeny is critical because these cells play central roles in brain homeostasis, neuroinflammation, and age-related cognitive decline. Mapping their development through somatic mutations could illuminate how immune dysfunction contributes to Alzheimer's disease and other neurodegenerative conditions. This approach offers new insights into brain aging and may guide therapeutic interventions.
