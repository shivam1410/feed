Kimi K3.1 Pro is live on Moonshot's API, but its weights are not out yet. Moonshot promises full weights by November 4, 2026. A Highspeed version runs 2 to 3 times faster at twice the price. Willison tested it with a pelican SVG prompt. Artificial Analysis scored it 55, the first time a soon-to-be open model scored this high against the frontier. It matters because the open-weight gap to closed frontier models is narrowing.
