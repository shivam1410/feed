Over 1,000 frontier AI lab employees—from substantively all labs except X.ai—cosigned a statement to the US government requesting international frameworks to deliberately pace frontier AI development. The signatories express genuine concern that AI research itself could soon become automated, potentially accelerating capability development beyond human understanding and control. While acknowledging AI's potential benefits, they identify competitive pressure as preventing any single company from unilaterally slowing progress. The letter requests government support building technical and governance tools for deliberate frontier-wide pacing. This marks a significant shift from earlier AI safety discourse: three years ago, industry leaders largely ignored similar calls for pauses. Today, from within leading labs, employees are explicitly requesting institutional mechanisms to manage capability acceleration risks.
