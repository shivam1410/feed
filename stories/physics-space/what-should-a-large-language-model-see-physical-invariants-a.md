Researchers show that translating raw spatiotemporal fields into physical invariants—domain-specific measurements a theorist would consult—dramatically improves LLM performance on PDE discovery. On simulated fields, data interpretation nearly tripled equation recovery accuracy compared to raw data input, with negligible computational cost and zero training required. This addresses a central challenge in molecular sciences: automating theory construction from vast modern datasets. By allowing language models to "read" data as domain experts do, the approach enables automated field theory construction at scale while coevolving with experimental capabilities.
