JoyAI-Echo-1.5 generates long-form video and interactive worlds by unifying multiple capabilities: cross-shot memory to maintain consistency across scenes, geometry-aware camera control to prevent visual artifacts, and rollout-aware training to anticipate future frames. The system maintains character identity and coherence over extended sequences. Long-form video generation typically suffers from drift, inconsistency, and character deformation; this architecture addresses those core challenges to enable coherent narrative generation.
