Researchers developed a hybrid cloud-edge system that brings specialist-level medical AI to rural areas with limited bandwidth and computing power. Lightweight edge models transform raw medical data into compact outputs while a cloud LLM synthesizes findings into clinical summaries. Tested on 20 multimodal clinical cases across cardiac, obstetric, and trauma scenarios, the system achieved 98-99% diagnostic tool recall and 92-96% precision with 25-35 second latency at 4-15x lower token cost than cloud-only approaches. The architecture dynamically selects relevant diagnostic tools based on patient context, improving both accessibility and efficiency in resource-constrained settings.
