Researchers developed a personalized gene therapy that successfully treated severe epilepsy in two boys. The treatment involves switching off one copy of a specific disease-causing gene. In at least one case, this therapy enabled the child to walk independently for the first time. Gene therapy represents a potential new approach for severe genetic epilepsy cases that resist conventional medications. The therapy's dual benefit of seizure control and mobility improvement demonstrates the potential impact of genetic treatments.
