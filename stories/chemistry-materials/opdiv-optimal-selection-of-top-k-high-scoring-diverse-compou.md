OPDiv solves the tradeoff between ranking and diversity when selecting compounds from virtual screening campaigns. It uses integer optimization to find the best subset under diversity constraints, testing fingerprint distance, shape, and electrostatic metrics. Provides fair benchmarking of pipeline diversity achievable.
