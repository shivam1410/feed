Stream4D improves autoregressive video diffusion by replacing static 3D critics with a dynamic 4D reconstruction reward and motion prior. Autoregressive video generation accumulates geometric drift and incoherent motion across frames; this method preserves spatial-temporal consistency by explicitly modeling motion dynamics and penalizing drift. The approach addresses a fundamental challenge in streaming video synthesis where frame-by-frame generation can diverge from global coherence.
