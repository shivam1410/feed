Two chemists won the Nobel Prize for understanding why life strongly favors one mirror-image form of molecules over the other. This asymmetry, called chirality, affects how drugs work and how all living things build their molecules. The mystery deepens because almost all biological molecules come in one specific handedness, yet science still doesn't fully explain why living systems show this preference.
