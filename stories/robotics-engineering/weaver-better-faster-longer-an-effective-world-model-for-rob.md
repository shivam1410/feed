WEAVER is a multi-view world model using flow-matching loss to achieve high-fidelity and consistent predictions for robotic manipulation. It demonstrates superior performance in policy evaluation, improvement, and test-time planning. Accurate world models enable robots to plan and adapt better in real-world tasks.
