Analysis of language models shows that distinct reasoning operations are geometrically separable in their hidden representations. Structure in these reasoning signatures emerges consistently across layers and is context-dependent. This mechanistic insight reveals that reasoning is not monolithic but composed of distinct, identifiable operations. Understanding the internal geometry of reasoning operations could improve interpretability and enable better control over model behavior.
