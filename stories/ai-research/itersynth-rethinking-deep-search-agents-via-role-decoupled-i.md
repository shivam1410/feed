They built a new way for AI agents to search by splitting roles: one plans what information is needed, another integrates evidence into growing summaries. Their 8-billion-parameter model scores 50.7, beating prior 8B models by 4.2 points. This works because separating planning from synthesis reduces noise and helps agents stay focused, even on very long searches.
