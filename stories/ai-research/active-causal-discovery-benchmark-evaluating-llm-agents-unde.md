The Active Causal Discovery Benchmark tests whether LLMs can figure out cause-and-effect relationships when they can only run a limited number of experiments. A traditional statistical algorithm (PC) beat Claude Sonnet 31.7 to 42.7 percent, and both struggled compared to an oracle. LLMs tend to over-commit to wrong causal claims, making causal reasoning a hard frontier task.
