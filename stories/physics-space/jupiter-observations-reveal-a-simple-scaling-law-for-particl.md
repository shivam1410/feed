Observations at Jupiter's bow shock reveal that supersonic plasma shocks generate and harbor transient electromagnetic structures that appear to be key governors of particle acceleration throughout space. These dynamic plasma formations modulate and control how energetic particles gain extreme relativistic velocities as they interact with and traverse cosmic shocks. The discovery suggests a universal mechanism governing particle acceleration. Why it matters: Understanding the physical mechanisms by which plasma shocks accelerate particles to extreme relativistic energies is fundamental for explaining cosmic ray generation throughout space, predicting space weather hazards near planets and spacecraft, and understanding high-energy astrophysical phenomena observed everywhere in the universe and beyond.
