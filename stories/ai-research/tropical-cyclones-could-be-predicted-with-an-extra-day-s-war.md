An artificial intelligence model can predict tropical cyclone track and intensity with high accuracy, potentially providing one additional day of advance warning compared to existing forecasting methods. This extra day of warning enables communities to better prepare, evacuate residents, and reduce casualties from storm impacts. AI-based tropical cyclone prediction demonstrates the potential for machine learning to improve hazard forecasting when trained on sufficient historical data and environmental observations. Responsible global dissemination of this forecasting capability could significantly protect vulnerable coastal populations in cyclone-prone regions, particularly if shared equitably worldwide.
