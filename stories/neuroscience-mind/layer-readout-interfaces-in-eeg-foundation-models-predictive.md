Intermediate layers of EEG foundation models often predict better than the final layer. The team tested five models on three tasks. Improving how the final layer is read out recovered much of the gap, but middle layers still helped. Layers picked on one dataset also worked on another, across five dataset pairs. That means you can skip a costly layer search on each new target.
