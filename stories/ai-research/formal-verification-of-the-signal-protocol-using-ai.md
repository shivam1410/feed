Google DeepMind used agentic AI to mathematically prove several key guarantees of the Signal protocol. This is called formal verification. The proof-of-concept took just four weeks. The cryptography protects messages on platforms like Signal, Google Messages and WhatsApp, so this is a second independent check on it. The work also showcases a new AI framework called AlphaProof Code.
