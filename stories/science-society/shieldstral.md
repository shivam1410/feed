Shieldstral is a 3-billion-parameter safety classifier matching or outperforming models nearly 7 times larger on text safety benchmarks and achieving state-of-the-art multimodal safety classification. The system formulates content moderation as binary question-answering, unifying diverse moderation tasks into a single yes/no problem. This formulation consolidates heterogeneous safety datasets with divergent taxonomies under one training framework. Researchers curated and generated approximately 54.1 million samples and created a fine-grained evaluation set to assess policy adaptability, demonstrating efficient safety classification at scale.
