MaxProof achieves competitive performance on high-level mathematical competitions through test-time scaling: orchestrating multiple proof-oriented capabilities and using population-level search with tournament selection. Rather than training larger models, the framework amplifies diverse proof strategies and selects winners through tournaments. This allows weaker component models to produce strong results when properly coordinated. The approach matters because formal mathematics is an AI frontier; scaling test-time compute through search and orchestration offers a path to higher performance without model scaling.
