Carbon nitride polymer crystals engineered with out-of-plane carrier migration via internal electric fields in facet-selective nanofilms increase apparent quantum efficiency for overall water splitting. The strategy uses polymeric carbon nitride to enhance charge separation and carrier flow. This improved photocatalytic performance advances practical solar-driven water splitting for hydrogen production.
