Huge black holes are not the answer to the mystery of dark matter. The logic is simple: if these theoretical objects were a source of dark matter, they would have left a larger imprint on cosmic radiation. Dark matter is the invisible force that pulls galaxies together. Ruling out this candidate narrows the search.
