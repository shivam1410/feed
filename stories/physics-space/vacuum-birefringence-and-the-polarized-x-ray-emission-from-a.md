Polarization observations of magnetar 1E 1547.0−5408 reveal that vacuum birefringence—a quantum electrodynamic effect where intense magnetic fields alter light propagation—measurably shapes X-ray emission in extreme cosmic environments. This marks a key observational confirmation of quantum mechanics predictions in the universe's most extreme magnetic fields, bridging laboratory theory with astrophysical phenomena.
