Pelican-Sim 1.0 is a general world model simulator for embodied intelligence that predicts future observations from visual context and robot actions, using unified action representations, action-visual injection, sparse mixture-of-experts, and efficient rollout generation to improve controllability and video quality across diverse embodiments and tasks.
