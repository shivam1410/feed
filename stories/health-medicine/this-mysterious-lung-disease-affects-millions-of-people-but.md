Researchers have discovered a promising drug treatment for pulmonary fibrosis, a mysterious progressive lung disease affecting millions of people worldwide. Tested in mice, the therapy activates a specific protein that halts disease progression rather than merely slowing it. This represents a significant therapeutic advance for a devastating condition that gradually destroys lung tissue and severely impairs breathing, for which effective treatments remain limited. The discovery offers hope to patients with this debilitating illness and could lead to clinical trials in humans, potentially providing the first disease-modifying treatment option.
