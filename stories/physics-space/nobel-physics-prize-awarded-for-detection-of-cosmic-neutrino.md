Francis Halzen won the Physics Nobel for building the IceCube neutrino observatory buried deep in ice at the South Pole. The detector has captured cosmic neutrinos and unlocked mysteries of the universe. This matters because neutrinos barely interact with normal matter, yet they carry crucial information from distant cosmic events like supernovae and black holes.
