Morris is the only known Northern Gannet in the entire Pacific Ocean. This Atlantic seabird first appeared 14 years ago in the Farallon Islands off San Francisco's coast and now makes Pillar Point Harbor in Half Moon Bay its home. Easily recognized as the only white bird with a distinctive yellow head in the area, Morris regularly frequents the harbor near the commercial pier, typically seen among smaller black Brandt's cormorants. Morris has become a local celebrity and popular wildlife attraction—a remarkable example of a species far outside its typical Atlantic range, offering a unique viewing opportunity for coastal residents and visitors.
