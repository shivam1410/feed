Submarine dives discovered an ancient whale graveyard spanning millions of years, revealing complex deep-sea ecosystems colonizing whale remains on the seafloor. Research also yielded a secondary breakthrough: a novel technique for converting plant-based materials into synthetic nylon, demonstrating how deep-sea expeditions generate multiple unexpected scientific advances.
