Connectivity EEG features beat spectral features for spotting ADHD, with caveats. Seven feature families were compared across four attention-task cohorts of 123 to 140 participants each. In the strongest task, the best configuration averaged an AUROC of 0.773. Connectivity minus spectral features gained +0.2083 AUROC across six classifiers, with adjusted p = 0.0036. Sensitivity was 0.556. Eight configurations were selected after inspecting predictive results.
