Leash Bio is turning DNA-encoded library data into training material for AI agents doing science. DELs are a high-throughput way to measure how proteins and small molecules interact. The team found that its data could support tens of thousands of lab-in-the-loop simulations, which is what an agent needs for scientific RL. That work has matured into Minos, an AI playground for verifiable scientific reasoning. It matters because agents can check their reasoning against lab-derived data.
