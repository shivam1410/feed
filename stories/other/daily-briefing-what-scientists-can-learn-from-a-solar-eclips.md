Scientists will have a two-minute window during a solar eclipse to gather data on the sun's corona. Research across Chinese, Dutch, and Greek populations reveals that tickle sensitivity shows universal patterns—specific body areas are consistently perceived as most ticklish across cultures, with ticklishness strongest in rarely touched areas. Separately, a potential super El Niño event poses serious risks to the Amazon rainforest, with scientists concerned that combined drought and fire could trigger irreversible ecosystem collapse.
