Researchers combined therapeutic antibodies with drug-carrying molecules using click chemistry—a powerful chemical coupling technique—to create enhanced cancer treatments that overcome tumor drug resistance. This modular approach enables flexible assembly of antibody-drug combinations directly within living tissue, allowing more precise targeting of heterogeneous and drug-resistant tumors. The method addresses a major limitation of conventional cancer therapies by improving drug delivery specificity and potency. This advancement demonstrates how chemical engineering techniques can enhance immunotherapy effectiveness.
