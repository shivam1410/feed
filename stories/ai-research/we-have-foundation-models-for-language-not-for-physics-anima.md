Caltech's Anima Anandkumar developed FourCastNet, a weather prediction model competitive with decades-old physics-based simulations despite domain skepticism. The model now runs on consumer GPUs, democratizing weather forecasting. The work reveals physics AI differs fundamentally from language models—these systems are chaotic, multi-scale, and data-scarce, resisting standard scaling approaches.
