ARGUS is an LLM pipeline that audits climate policy studies for methodological flaws. On a benchmark with planted flaws, ARGUS detected 73 percent versus 18 percent for keyword search. On 26 real economics papers, it abstained on 40 percent of assessments due to insufficient evidence. It provides evidence-linked risk reports for expert review. This matters because methodological rigor in policy research affects billions of dollars in decisions.
