BRACE forecasts which groups of training attempts will show mixed rewards before any budget is spent on them. Mixed rewards mean some attempts succeed and others fail, which gives useful learning signal. It combines past task evidence with a reward model. At three Qwen3-VL scales, it beat the strongest baseline peaks with fewer new rollouts. It matters because it can cut the cost of training tool-using agents.
