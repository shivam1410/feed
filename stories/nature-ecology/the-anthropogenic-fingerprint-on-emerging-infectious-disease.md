Infectious disease outbreak risks peak in landscapes where human settlements, livestock farms, and fragmented natural ecosystems coexist together. Healthcare inequality limits outbreak detection globally. These ecological hotspots reveal where zoonotic pathogens jump from wildlife and livestock to human populations most readily. This matters because identifying high-risk landscapes helps predict where emerging infectious diseases are likely to originate and spread.
