IceCube, the neutrino experiment, just won a Nobel prize. Building it was a big risk, and the piece argues that risk paid off. To keep that kind of success going, scientists and funders need to stay bold. The same logic applies to other astroparticle physics projects. It matters because it is a case for backing ambitious science even when the outcome is uncertain.
