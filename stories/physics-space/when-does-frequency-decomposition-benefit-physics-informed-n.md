Researchers introduce DBSG-PINN, a dual-branch spectrally-gated architecture that separates low and high-frequency components for solving partial differential equations. Testing on five 1D benchmarks shows frequency decomposition reduces relative L2 error by up to 59.2% on spectrally complex multimodal wave problems. The benefit scales with solution complexity—largest on multi-scale benchmarks, negligible on smooth PDEs—suggesting targeted spectral decomposition addresses where network spectral bias most constrains approximation.
