NASA's Juno spacecraft observed relativistic electron acceleration at Jupiter's bow shock, the boundary where the solar wind collides with Jupiter's magnetosphere. The observations established a universal scaling law linking bow shock size to maximum cosmic ray energy. This fundamental relationship may apply broadly across astrophysical systems with similar shock structures, revealing universal principles that govern cosmic ray acceleration throughout the universe.
