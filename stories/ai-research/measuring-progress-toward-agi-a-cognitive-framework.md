Google DeepMind developed a cognitive framework for evaluating progress toward artificial general intelligence and launched a Kaggle hackathon to build capability benchmarks. Rather than relying on single metrics, the framework assesses multiple dimensions of AI cognition and reasoning relevant to AGI. The hackathon invites researchers to develop rigorous benchmarks measuring AI capabilities. This addresses a critical gap: standardized, meaningful measures of progress that help researchers understand remaining capability gaps and guide development priorities.
