Scientists developed a process to convert lignin—a complex biopolymer found in plants—into a chemical precursor for nylon production in high yields. Lignin represents an underutilized component of plant biomass currently treated as waste in paper and biofuel industries. This breakthrough offers a fresh strategy for valorizing this abundant plant material into valuable industrial chemicals. The achievement demonstrates progress toward sustainable chemical manufacturing that uses renewable plant resources rather than relying entirely on petroleum-based feedstocks.
