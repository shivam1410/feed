Researchers extracted hidden reasoning from frontier models like GPT-6 Astra by registering a custom tool through the API. The extracted reasoning matched native chain-of-thought performance and beat no-reasoning baselines on math, science, and code. Astra shows token-efficient reasoning, externalizing only crucial steps and solving elementary ones internally. This matters because reasoning quality in large models stays opaque without extracting what's happening inside.
