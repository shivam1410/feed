Spontaneous symmetry breaking in a helium-neon laser has eliminated the lock-in effect that limits gyroscope sensitivity, enabling accurate rotation sensing near zero rotation rates without external components. Ring-laser gyroscopes are now smaller and more precise. This matters for applications requiring compact, ultra-sensitive rotation detection, such as navigation in space or autonomous systems.
