Improved laboratory fractionation strategies have identified a natural antibiotic with a previously unknown scaffold and mechanism of action. Manikomycin, isolated from the soil bacterium Streptomyces rimosus, functions by binding the E-site of the bacterial ribosome's large subunit—an unusual target compared to conventional antibiotics. This discovery demonstrates how refined screening methods can uncover antibiotics with novel mechanisms, expanding options for combating bacterial resistance.
