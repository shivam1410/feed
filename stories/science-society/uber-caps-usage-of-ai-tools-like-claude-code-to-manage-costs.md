Uber implemented a $1,500 monthly token spending cap per AI coding tool (Claude Code, Cursor) per employee, after exhausting its 2026 AI budget in four months. The cap applies per tool, not globally, allowing separate budgets for each platform. Assuming two tools per engineer, this translates to $36,000 annual AI spending cap, approximately 11% of the median Uber software engineer's $330,000 compensation package. The policy reflects rational cost management as token-consuming coding agents proved more popular than anticipated.
