BaguanHR improves high-resolution weather forecasting by synthesizing training data through variable-wise super-resolution, overcoming the limitations of directly transferring coarse-resolution models. The approach demonstrates strong scaling benefits as more data is used. High-resolution weather prediction has been constrained by availability of fine-grained training data; this method generates synthetic data that bridges the gap, allowing models to generalize better to real-world high-resolution forecasting tasks.
