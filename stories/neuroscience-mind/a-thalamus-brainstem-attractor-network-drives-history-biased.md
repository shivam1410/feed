Whole-brain, cellular-resolution imaging in zebrafish reveals a hierarchical thalamus–brainstem attractor network that encodes recent behavioral history and drives decision-making bias. This circuit-level mechanism shows how the brain implements persistent internal representations influencing future choices. Understanding this network provides mechanistic insight into history-dependent decision-making across vertebrate species with implications for neuroscience and behavior research.
