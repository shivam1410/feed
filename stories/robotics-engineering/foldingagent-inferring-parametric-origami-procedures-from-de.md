FoldingAgent uses a vision-language model with specialized tools to convert origami demonstration videos into executable parametric folding programs via sequential reasoning. The system validates inferred procedures through physical verification. This matters because it bridges visual demonstrations to executable code, opening possibilities for learning complex procedural knowledge from video tutorials in domains beyond origami.
