RECTOR is a self-supervised learning framework for analyzing brain dynamics in EEG and sEEG signals using masked region-channel-temporal modeling with adaptive functional partitioning. Rather than relying on fixed anatomical regions, the method learns evolving functional brain regions through hierarchical self-attention. The framework achieves state-of-the-art results in emotion recognition and task-engagement classification while demonstrating robustness to missing channels and cross-montage generalization. These properties enable large-scale pre-training on heterogeneous brain recordings, providing interpretable insights for clinical diagnosis.
