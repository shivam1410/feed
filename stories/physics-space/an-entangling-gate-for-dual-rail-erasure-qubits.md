Researchers demonstrated a fast, low-error entangling gate for dual-rail cavity erasure qubits that preserves a strong error hierarchy essential for fault-tolerant quantum computing. This quantum gate operates with substantially improved error rates compared to previous approaches. The advance represents progress toward scalable quantum computers. This matters because quantum error correction is essential for practical, large-scale quantum computing, and this development brings the field closer to reliably operating quantum systems with thousands of qubits.
