Black Forest Labs released FLUX 3, a multimodal flow model architecture surpassing prior generation image and video synthesis systems. FLUX 3 outperforms Seedance 2.0, Gemini Omni, and Grok Imagine across benchmarks, representing a leap in generation quality and consistency. The release includes specialized variants: FLUX 3 Video for temporal coherence, and FLUX-mimic, a video-action robotics model enabling physical systems to learn from visual demonstrations. Flow models—diffusion's mathematical alternative—show promise for generation tasks requiring long-range coherence. FLUX 3's multimodal capabilities span text-to-image, image-to-image, and video synthesis with native vision understanding. This release exemplifies accelerating progress in multimodal generation.
