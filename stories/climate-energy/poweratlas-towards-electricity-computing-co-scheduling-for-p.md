PowerAtlas is an LLM-agent framework for coordinated electricity-computing scheduling that integrates historical data, domain knowledge, and physical constraints to satisfy grid operational rules and computing task SLAs. Built with a provincial power utility in China, the system validates its decision loop on real data-center data. Researchers constructed ECBench, a benchmark of 2,000 scheduling instances with oracle-optimal solutions. Experiments across eleven LLMs demonstrate effectiveness under realistic operating conditions with consistent feasibility and cost gains.
