Diogo Almeida, the InstructGPT paper co-author, launched Jev, a new model category that classifies, routes, and scores instead of generating text. His launch video exceeded 40 million views. Instead of general-purpose chat LLMs, Jev makes fast, calibrated decisions. This represents a philosophical shift: API models don't all need to do everything, some should excel at specific jobs in production.
