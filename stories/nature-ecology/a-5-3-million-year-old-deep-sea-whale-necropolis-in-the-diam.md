A 5.3-million-year-old whale necropolis in the Diamantina Zone of the southeastern Indian Ocean contains an enormous accumulation of whale skeletal remains. This ancient site harbored specialized long-term deep-sea ecosystems and provides an extensive fossil record revealing whale evolutionary history, deep-ocean biodiversity patterns, and mechanisms supporting specialized communities through geological time.
