Mitochondrial fission factor (MFF) was identified as a selective regulator of ferroptosis. The newly developed MFF-SPARK sensor enables high-content screening to identify compounds that sensitize ferroptosis and discover upstream regulatory mechanisms controlling this cell-death pathway.
