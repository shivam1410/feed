This study evaluates whether agents acknowledge uncertainty when evidence contradicts their prior knowledge. Higher accuracy did not equal more humility. Agents often detected conflicts early but failed to maintain or communicate uncertainty later. It reveals a gap: confident and wrong is worse than uncertain.
