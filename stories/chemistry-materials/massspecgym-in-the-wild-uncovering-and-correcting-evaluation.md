Reliable benchmarking is critical for machine learning in tandem mass spectrometry-based molecule discovery. Review of 26 papers reporting MassSpecGym results found issues in at least 17—over 65 percent. Three failure categories emerged: data leakage, shortcut learning exploiting spurious patterns, and implementation bugs. Authors quantified each failure's impact through extensive replication and released MassSpecGym v1.5 addressing identified problems. This audit demonstrates how subtle experimental design choices corrupt evaluation standards and mislead fields, emphasizing the importance of rigorous benchmark maintenance.
