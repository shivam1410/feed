TRAPS presents the first unified benchmark for pathway-informed cancer therapy response prediction, evaluating three biologically informed architectures (BINN, GraphPath, PATH) on five cancer cohorts with 2,622 patients using Reactome pathway scores. Models were trained jointly on three outcomes: targeted molecular therapy, radiation therapy, and six-month survival. Results showed task-dependent performance: PATH excelled at targeted therapy (AUROC 0.92 on prostate), BINN at survival, but no model succeeded at radiation therapy, revealing that clinical factors beyond gene expression drive those decisions.
