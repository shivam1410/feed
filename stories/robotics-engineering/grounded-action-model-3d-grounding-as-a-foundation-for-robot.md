Grounded Action Models (GAMs) build robot foundation models around explicit 3D spatial grounding instead of implicit learning from video or language. The model accepts language, point, or box prompts transformed into object-centric representations before predicting actions. On RoboTwin 2.0, GAM achieves 55.3% success across 50 tasks (baseline 52.0%) and 47.6% under scene randomization (baseline 30.4%). On LIBERO-PRO, GAM reaches state-of-the-art 61% average success, with largest gains when target positions change, demonstrating explicit grounding's robustness advantage.
