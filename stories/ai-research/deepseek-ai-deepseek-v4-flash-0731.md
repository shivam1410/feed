DeepSeek released V4-Flash-0731, a 304-billion-parameter model that ranks ahead of much larger competitors like MiniMax M3 (428 billion parameters) on benchmark evaluations. Pricing is exceptionally competitive at just $0.14 per million input tokens and $0.27 per million output tokens, making it currently the best value-per-intelligence ratio available. Output quality varies significantly with reasoning level settings: standard reasoning produces noticeably weaker outputs, but higher reasoning effort yields substantially stronger results. The model is available via OpenRouter and downloadable at 167 gigabytes from Hugging Face, offering accessible, economical inference for those seeking strong capability at reasonable cost.
