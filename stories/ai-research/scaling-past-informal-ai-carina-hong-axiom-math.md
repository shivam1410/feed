Seven-month-old startup Axiom Math solved all 12 Putnam examination problems, scoring 8/12 within the time limit and 12/12 total — surpassing top undergraduate performers (110/120) and closest comparable AI system DeepSeek (103/120). The Putnam exam, legendary for difficulty with median human scores of 0-1 points, demonstrates AI super-intelligence in mathematical problem-solving. However, CEO Carina Hong argues coding and math ability remain insufficient milestones toward AGI; significant gaps in informal reasoning bottleneck broader AI progress beyond elite academic competitions.
