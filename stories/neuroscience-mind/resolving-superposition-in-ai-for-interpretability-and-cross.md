Sparse autoencoders resolve superposition—forced compression of distinct concepts into low dimensions—in neural network representations of 100,000+ multiplexed Parkinson's disease neuron images. Sparse autoencoders recover interpretable features and restore representational geometry fidelity. Researchers adapted scRNA-seq analysis methods to the image domain, then used Gromov-Wasserstein optimal transport (GW-map) to align image representations with authentic RNA sequencing data without spatial transcriptomics reference, reconstructing hierarchical neuronal pathology pathways de novo.
