Forecasting leaderboard winners are often treated as deployment-ready, but they frequently fail when applied through real-world decision interfaces like alert thresholds or switching policies. Researchers introduce a fail-closed certification protocol that tests whether a forecast winner remains superior under deployment constraints. Testing on Traffic-Hourly data and 362 grid cells revealed that 155 apparent forecast/deployment winner inversions were blocked before certification—meaning the forecast leader was deployment-suboptimal. The protocol guards against claiming leaderboard success automatically transfers to actionable production decisions, distinguishing between predictive accuracy and utility under friction.
