Researchers adapted image-matching AI to identify individual animals from camera-trap photos without expensive keypoint annotations. They mined informative pairs with a pretrained matcher and fine-tuned it using only identity labels, improving accuracy over existing methods. This matters because wildlife monitoring depends on spotting individuals across thousands of photos, and now it works with less manual labeling.
