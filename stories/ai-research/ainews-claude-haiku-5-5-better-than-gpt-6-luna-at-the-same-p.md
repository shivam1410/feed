Anthropic has released Claude Haiku 5.5, its first Haiku update in about a year. Anthropic says it costs about 75% less to run than Haiku 4.5 on average. It is priced to match OpenAI's GPT-6 Luna. Anthropic positions it as a subagent that works alongside Opus 5.5 or Sonnet 5.5. It is available on the Claude Platform and in Claude Code. The same day, Anthropic also cut prices on Sonnet 5.5 and its subscription plans.
