Latent Space argues the AI pipeline has incrementally shifted from human-generated to model-generated components since 2022. Synthetic simulation—reward models, self-critique systems, and reinforcement learning environments—now dominates, producing results 10% worse in quality but 100× cheaper and 10,000× faster than human-produced equivalents. This trend suggests AI progress increasingly depends on simulating human judgment and evaluation at scale rather than collecting human data directly.
