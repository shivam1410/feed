They built a fast robot simulator using diffusion models that generate video frames in real-time without specifying exactly how many frames ahead. It hits 24 FPS after optimization and works across different robot types and camera setups. The simulator receives desired robot movements and autoregressively generates what the camera sees. This matters because testing robot policies becomes cheap and safe without needing real hardware.
