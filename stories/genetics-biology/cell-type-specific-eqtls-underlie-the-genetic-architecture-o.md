Cell-type-specific expression quantitative trait loci (eQTLs) drive much of the genetic architecture underlying complex diseases and traits. Single-cell RNA-sequencing reveals that variation in gene expression across different cell types—not just variation across populations—determines which genetic variants cause disease. This cell-type-specific gene regulation explains substantial heritability for complex traits, linking genetic variants to biological mechanisms through tissue-specific effects. Understanding which variants act in which cell types could accelerate drug development by identifying precise cellular targets for therapeutic intervention, improving specificity and efficacy.
