CoMedBench introduces the first comprehensive benchmark for evaluating synthetic medical data generators against consistent clinical standards. The benchmark spans 37 dataset-task pairs combining 20 static tabular and 17 temporal ICU time-series drawn from seven public sources: MIMIC-III, MIMIC-IV, eICU, UCI Machine Learning Repository, CDC BRFSS diabetes cohort, NHANES, and pycox survival datasets. By comparing models trained and tested on real versus synthetic data, researchers can now assess both statistical fidelity and task utility. This addresses a critical gap: determining when synthetic data preserves clinically meaningful signal needed for model development while protecting patient privacy.
