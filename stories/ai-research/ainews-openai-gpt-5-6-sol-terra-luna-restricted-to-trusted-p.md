OpenAI announced GPT-5.6 as a three-model family (Sol, Terra, Luna) with limited preview access restricted to trusted partners, rather than broad release. Sol, the flagship frontier model, beats Mythos on a subset of coding agent tasks but does not cross the "Cyber Critical" threshold under OpenAI's Preparedness Framework. In evaluations using Chromium and Firefox, Sol identified bugs and exploitation primitives but did not autonomously produce functional full-chain exploits under tested conditions. This measured release approach with documented safety evaluation demonstrates OpenAI's strategy of gradually expanding access while maintaining transparency about capability limitations relative to potential risks, particularly around autonomous cyberattack capabilities.
