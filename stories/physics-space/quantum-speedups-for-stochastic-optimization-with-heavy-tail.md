A novel quantum mean estimator achieves lower query complexity than optimal classical estimators for heavy-tailed multivariate random variables in low dimensions. Quantum normalized stochastic gradient descent (QNSGD) and quantum projected gradient descent (QPSGD) find stationary and optimal points with improved bounds over classical lower bounds, with dimension-dependent speedups and dimension-independent factors. Quantum lower bounds prove these estimators are optimal up to logarithmic factors, establishing quantum advantage for optimization under heavy-tailed noise.
