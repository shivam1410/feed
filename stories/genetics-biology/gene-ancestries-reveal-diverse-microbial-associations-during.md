Phylogenomic reconstruction of the last eukaryotic common ancestor reveals that horizontal gene transfer from diverse bacterial and viral lineages was crucial to eukaryote origins. The gene-ancestry analysis shows eukaryogenesis involved extensive genetic exchange with multiple microbial donors, reshaping understanding of how eukaryotes emerged from complex microbial partnerships.
