TabPFN achieved the highest threat score (0.637) predicting post-wildfire debris flows compared to 14 other models tested across western U.S. basin-scale observations. SHAP analysis reveals short-duration rainfall intensity and storm accumulation are primary drivers, while burn severity contributes less than expected. Synthetic data augmentation improved performance across most models by +0.041 mean threat score, offering practical solutions to address debris-flow prediction data scarcity.
