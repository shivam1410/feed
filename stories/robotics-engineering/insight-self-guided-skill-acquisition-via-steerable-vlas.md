InSight enables autonomous skill acquisition for vision-language-action models through fine-grained primitive-action level steerability and automated demonstration generation. Rather than requiring extensive manual human annotations for each new skill, the system guides itself to learn new capabilities by controlling lower-level action primitives and automatically generating training demonstrations. This self-directed learning approach reduces human supervision requirements while maintaining fine-grained control over skill development and learning process. The advancement enables robots and embodied AI systems to more efficiently acquire diverse skills and capabilities, reducing training overhead and supporting rapid adaptation to new tasks.
