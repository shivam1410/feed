Self-Organizing Agent Teams learn to work together through practice, developing reusable coordination strategies. After training on just 15 math problems and 25 graduate-level questions, teams achieved 66.7% accuracy on benchmarks versus 48.8% for their strongest member alone. The shared reasoning from multiple agents creates collective intelligence neither member could achieve independently, beating solo performance by 18 points.
