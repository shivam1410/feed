An AI tool improves weather forecasts up to a month ahead. It works in the difficult subseasonal range, 3 to 5 weeks out. The tool refines both physics-based and AI-based weather models. It forecasts precipitation, temperature and pressure. It also improves early warnings for extremes like heat, cold, rain and flooding. That gives communities more time to prepare.
