DianShi-RxnDB is an automated platform that extracts and normalizes structured organic chemical reactions from millions of patent documents into machine-readable formats suitable for AI research and chemistry applications. The fully automated pipeline processes unstructured patent text to create fine-grained reaction data including reactants, products, conditions, and outcomes. This large-scale structured dataset addresses a critical bottleneck in computational chemistry: access to diverse, high-quality reaction examples for machine learning. By democratizing access to vast chemical knowledge previously locked in patent literature, the platform enables accelerated drug discovery and materials science research.
