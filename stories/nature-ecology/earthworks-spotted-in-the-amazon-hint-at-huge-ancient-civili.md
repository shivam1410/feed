Archaeological surveys uncovered nearly 400 earthworks across the Amazon, indicating the Aquiry civilization was substantially larger and more complex than previously understood. Separately, fossils of the earliest known squid ancestors have been discovered, extending cephalopod origins further back. These discoveries challenge long-held assumptions about pre-Columbian Amazonian societies and early cephalopod evolution in their respective domains.
