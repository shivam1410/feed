Centenarians possess elevated levels of killer T cells, specialized immune cells that eliminate diseased cells, suggesting these cells may contribute mechanistically to extraordinary longevity and aging resistance. Research also identified the longest-lived brain organoids developed to date and discussed potential applications of mass genome screening programs for detecting genetic disease in newborns. These findings collectively illuminate cellular and genetic mechanisms underlying extreme aging and suggest early intervention strategies through genetic screening could prevent age-related diseases.
