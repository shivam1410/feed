Somatic mutations in microglia—immune cells resident in the brain—reveal how these cells develop and change during human aging. The findings illuminate cellular aging pathways in cells critical to brain health and increasingly implicated in neurodegenerative disease, potentially opening therapeutic avenues for understanding and intervening in brain aging.
