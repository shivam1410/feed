DeepMind is releasing AlphaGenome Atlas, a database of predictions. It estimates the effect of every possible single nucleotide variant in the human genome. A nucleotide variant is a change to one letter of DNA. The text gives no accuracy figures. It matters because it covers every possible single-letter change in one place.
