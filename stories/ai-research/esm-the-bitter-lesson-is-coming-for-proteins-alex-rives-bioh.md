BioHub released ESMFold 2, a protein prediction engine trained on millions of sequences using masked-token prediction, revealing that protein structure and function emerge predictably from scale—like language models. Achieved state-of-the-art performance on protein interactions and antibodies; inference scaling verified across cancer and immunology targets. Releasing atlas of 6.8 billion proteins and 1.1 billion predicted structures. Validates the "bitter lesson": simple, scalable approaches work across domains, not just language.
