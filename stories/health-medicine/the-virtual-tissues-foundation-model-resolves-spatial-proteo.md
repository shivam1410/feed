Virtual Tissues (VirTues) is a foundation model trained on spatial proteomics data—patterns of protein expression mapped across tissue locations. This model reconstructs missing protein markers, identifies and types individual cells, annotates tissue microenvironments, discovers spatial disease biomarkers, and stratifies patients across diverse tissue panels and datasets. Foundation models trained on large, diverse datasets can transfer learning to new, smaller datasets. VirTues enables systematic analysis of tissue organization across biological scales, supporting precision medicine.
