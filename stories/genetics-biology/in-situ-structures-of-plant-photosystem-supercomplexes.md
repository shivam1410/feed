Cryo-EM resolved the photosystem II–light harvesting complex II supercomplex from rice in its native cellular membrane, retaining physiologically relevant proteins and lipids lost during laboratory purification. The intact structure reveals the complete plant photosynthetic architecture and reveals how energy transfer operates in living cells. This advance clarifies fundamental mechanisms of photosynthesis as it functions in nature.
