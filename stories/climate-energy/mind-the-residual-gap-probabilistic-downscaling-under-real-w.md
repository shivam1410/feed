ReMatch addresses a fundamental mismatch in probabilistic downscaling: residual distributions during training differ from test-time requirements due to downscaling bias. Rather than treating errors as generic miscalibration, the method aligns training residuals toward test conditions using optimal transport in low-dimensional space. On synthetic benchmarks and real HRRR-ERA5 wind field data, ReMatch reduces under-dispersion, improves calibration metrics (SSR and CRPS), and outperforms standard approaches, enabling more reliable uncertainty estimates for weather and climate models.
