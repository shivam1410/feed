New materials science advances enable buildings to maintain comfortable temperatures as global warming increases external heat. These emerging materials leverage passive cooling principles—potentially radiative cooling, phase-change materials, or thermochromic coatings—to reduce indoor temperatures without energy-intensive cycles. As climate change intensifies heat waves, such innovations could provide energy-efficient alternatives or supplements to conventional air conditioning, reducing electricity demand and improving building resilience during extreme weather.
