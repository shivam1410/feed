Quantum oscillation measurements have mapped the g-wave altermagnetic order parameter in the compound CrSb, establishing it as a prototypical unconventional magnet. The measurements reveal momentum-dependent spin splitting—a property where electron spin behavior varies with momentum—characteristic of altermagnetic materials. This discovery helps classify and understand a new category of magnetic materials with unusual electronic properties. Altermagnets may have applications in spintronics and quantum technologies exploiting unconventional magnetic order.
