This theoretical work unifies disparate generative modeling approaches—flow-based, diffusion-based, variational, and adversarial models—as different evaluation principles applied to a single master action formulated as a path integral. Using the Martin-Siggia-Rose-Janssen-de Dominicis framework, the formulation separates free from interacting probability flows and enables diagrammatic perturbation theory. A one-loop correction to deterministic samplers reduces a 53% tree-level error to 1.6% on solvable and nonlinear drifts without stochastic sampling cost. The expansion shows how imperfect learned scores enter as insertions, yielding response-weighted score-matching objectives.
