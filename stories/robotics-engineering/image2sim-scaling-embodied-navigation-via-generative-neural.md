Image2Sim enables scalable embodied navigation training by generating high-fidelity interactive environments directly from RGB-D images. The approach uses decoupled 3D spatial anchoring to reconstruct accurate scene geometry and photorealistic rendering techniques to create navigable simulations from single image captures. By converting real-world image observations into interactive simulated environments, the method eliminates bottlenecks in manual environment design or expensive real-world data collection. This technique enables large-scale training of embodied navigation policies on diverse environments without the cost of building or simulating environments manually. Image2Sim accelerates development of robots that can navigate novel real-world spaces by enabling data-efficient training at scale.
