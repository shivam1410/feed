Simile AI, backed by $2B Series B from GreenOaks and Index Ventures (with Fei-Fei Li and Andrej Karpathy as backers), builds digital twins of human behavior combining long-form interviews, observational data, transaction logs, and RCTs. The company's foundation models reproduce human attitudes and decision-making 85–99% as accurately as actual focus groups for Fortune 100 clients like CVS. This extends generative-agent research (Smallville, 2023) to population-scale behavior modeling. Simulating human responses before launches could reduce reliance on traditional market research.
