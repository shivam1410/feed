Deep reinforcement learning controllers for spacecraft re-entry achieve performance comparable to traditional proportional-integral-derivative systems. RL shows poor out-of-distribution generalization initially; dynamics randomization during training enforces robustness within operational envelopes. Hybrid RL-PID controllers outperform traditional approaches on application metrics: better angle-of-attack tracking and robustness to variations in spacecraft mass, inertia tensor, and actuator bandwidth. This demonstrates learning-based control can handle extreme nonlinear dynamics while maintaining reliability under real-world variations.
