Simple lexical retrieval outperforms sophisticated methods as corpus scale increases. Controlled study across 28 nested corpus tiers (450-fold range) reveals File-System Agent leads at smallest scales but costs 39× more query tokens. BM25 overtakes agentic search around 10 million corpus tokens and maintains 20-point accuracy margin at full scale. Dense and graph-based retrieval remain less accurate. Lexical ranking before agentic reasoning outperforms agentic reasoning alone.
