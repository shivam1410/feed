Researchers created EgoTools, a benchmark with 100 hours of egocentric video and 1,000 QA pairs testing agents on tool use in real embodied tasks. Gemini-3.1-Pro achieves 66.9 percent overall accuracy but only 51.7 percent on perception and grounding. Current models struggle to anchor their understanding of tool use in visual evidence from real-world activities.
