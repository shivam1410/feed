NASA's Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope will detect approximately 40 times more distant planets than have been found to date. The upcoming mission applies advanced technology to exoplanet discovery, dramatically expanding our census of worlds beyond the solar system. This massive increase in detectable planets will accelerate our understanding of planetary system formation, composition, and the prevalence of potentially habitable worlds.
