AI Theorist autonomously discovers physical models by proposing hypotheses and testing them with first-principles calculations. Applied to quantum material α-RuCl₃, the system analyzed light-absorption data and independently identified distinct electron-hole bound states with different optical properties. This is the first AI system to autonomously develop a physical model explaining novel experimental observations in a quantum material. This accelerates materials science by automating the interpretation of experimental data.
