DeepMind released AlphaGenome Atlas, a comprehensive database that predicts the effects of every possible single-letter mutation in human DNA across the entire genome. The tool catalogs what happens for each genetic variant. This could transform personalized medicine by helping doctors understand which mutations cause disease, which are benign, and which might become drug targets.
