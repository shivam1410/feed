Anthropic released Claude Opus 5, described as a thoughtful, proactive model approaching Fable 5's frontier intelligence at half the price. It currently leads the Artificial Analysis leaderboard and is priced identically to Opus 4.8. One example highlights its proactivity: given only a raw pixel drawing of a machine part, Opus 5 wrote its own computer-vision pipeline to extract geometry before reconstructing a 3D model—showing capability to work around constraints.
