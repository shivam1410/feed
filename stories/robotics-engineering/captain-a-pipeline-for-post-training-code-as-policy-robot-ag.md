A team built CaPtain, a pipeline that teaches small open vision-language models to control robots by writing code. They trained the models in two stages, supervised fine-tuning first, then multi-turn reinforcement learning with a physical critic. The agents reach strong results without frontier models or vision-language-action backbones. That shows smaller open models can handle real robot manipulation. It matters because it makes this approach work without a giant model.
