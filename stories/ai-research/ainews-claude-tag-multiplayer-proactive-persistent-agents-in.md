Anthropic introduced Claude Tag, extending Claude across web, desktop, and Slack platforms in what constitutes a major UI/UX redesign. Tag enables multiplayer, proactive, persistent agents in Slack that can tag coworkers owning related code, wait days on git webhooks for blocking dependencies (achieving "stacked prompts"), summarize threads into actionable docs, and operate in ambient mode reviewing messages without explicit mentions. The system merges approximately 65% of product PRs through autonomous decision-making while following up across channels. This represents a significant evolution in async agent architecture, establishing new patterns for how AI integrates into team workflows and development processes with reduced friction.
