Companies are scrambling to reduce AI token spending amid escalating costs, with surprising findings about consumption patterns. Internal Accenture data reveals non-engineers—not software developers—drive the majority of token consumption through inefficient AI usage. Converting PDFs to markdown files emerged as a particularly egregious token consumer, with both conversion steps and format translation unnecessarily inflating token counts. The anecdote, surfaced in leaked Accenture meeting audio, highlights how organizations are beginning to recognize that unconstrained AI adoption by non-technical staff rapidly depletes budgets. As AI models become mainstream tools across business functions, awareness of consumption efficiency becomes critical: organizations discovering that their token spend reflects wasteful practices rather than valuable work, prompting cost-control initiatives.
