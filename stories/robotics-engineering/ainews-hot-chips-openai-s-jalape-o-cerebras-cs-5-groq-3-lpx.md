OpenAI revealed Jalapeño, a custom inference chip outperforming NVIDIA GB200/GB300 systems: 1.5–1.9× more work per watt, 1.7–3.6× lower latency, and 2.1–4.1× higher performance for interactive workloads, while rated 700W but consuming ≤550W in tests. Deployment into OpenAI's infrastructure begins year-end; Gen 2 is in advanced development with Gen 3 already underway. This shift toward performance-per-watt efficiency represents a fundamental change in AI inference strategy, signaling vertical integration across model and hardware layers.
