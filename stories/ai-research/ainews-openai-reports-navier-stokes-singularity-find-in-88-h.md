OpenAI reported solving a Navier-Stokes singularity problem in 88 hours using Astra-next, approximately 10,000 collaborative agents, and 130 billion tokens at a cost exceeding 40 million dollars. The effort employed multiagent reinforcement learning trained over the past year, with specialized agents deciding how to organize parallel test-time compute to tackle this notoriously difficult mathematical problem. If verified, the achievement would qualify as a contender for the second Millennium Prize in Mathematics. The effort has generated both technical interest and skepticism regarding the solution's validity and the surrounding claims.
