NVIDIA is acquiring HuggingFace for $13 billion, roughly 80× the company's $150 million annual revenue. This price more than doubles NVIDIA's initial $7 billion offer from January 2026, reflecting HuggingFace's dramatic growth: the company doubled its customer base within 2026. The acquisition follows other major AI infrastructure consolidations and signals confidence in the model hub market. HuggingFace's platform hosts open-source models and has become central to AI development workflows, making it a strategic asset for NVIDIA as it expands beyond chip manufacturing into software infrastructure.
