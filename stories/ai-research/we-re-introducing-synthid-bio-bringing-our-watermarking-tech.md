DeepMind developed SynthID Bio, a method to watermark AI-designed proteins while maintaining full biological function. The watermarks are woven into the protein sequence itself. This helps researchers identify and track AI-generated proteins, which increasingly matters as synthetic biology becomes more common and scientists need reliable ways to verify AI involvement and authenticate designed molecules.
