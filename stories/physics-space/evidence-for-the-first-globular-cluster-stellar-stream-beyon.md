Deep Hubble Space Telescope imaging of ultra-diffuse galaxy UGC 9050-Dw1 reveals the first globular cluster stellar stream detected beyond the Milky Way. This stream—tidal debris from a disrupting globular cluster—offers unprecedented opportunity to probe the galaxy's dark matter content through its gravitational effects on visible stars. Analysis and modeling illuminate the distribution of invisible matter. This discovery demonstrates a new method for mapping dark matter in external galaxies.
