IdeaAMBIG introduces a benchmark for evaluating how clearly research methods are specified for practical implementation. The study reveals that identifying missing implementation details is the primary challenge language models face when assessing research specifications. By systematizing evaluation of specification clarity, IdeaAMBIG helps researchers and AI systems recognize and fill gaps that hinder reproducibility and knowledge translation in scientific research.
