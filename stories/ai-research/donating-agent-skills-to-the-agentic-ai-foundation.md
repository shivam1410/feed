Anthropic just handed its Agent Skills standard to a neutral foundation. The Agentic AI Foundation sits under the Linux Foundation, and Anthropic co-founded it with Block and OpenAI last year. It already hosts the Model Context Protocol specification. The goal is to keep skills open, community-driven, and vendor-neutral. Why it matters: no single company controls the standard.
