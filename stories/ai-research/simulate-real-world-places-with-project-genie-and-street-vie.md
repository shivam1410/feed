Project Genie now integrates nearly 20 years of Google Street View imagery, enabling users to generate simulated worlds anchored in real-world locations. The approach combines generative AI's creative capabilities with geographic authenticity from documented street-level imagery. This hybrid method grounds synthetic environments in actual geography and photographic evidence.
