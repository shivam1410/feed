A novel evaporation technique for processing perovskite compounds overcomes long-standing manufacturing challenges that have limited scalable production of next-generation tandem solar cells. By enabling lower-temperature fabrication, the approach reduces thermal stress on underlying substrate materials and improves device stability and lifespan. Perovskite solar cells promise higher efficiency than conventional silicon panels, but manufacturing at scale has required high temperatures that damage components and limit architectural possibilities. This vaporization innovation moves perovskite technology closer to commercial viability and cost-competitiveness. The breakthrough addresses a critical bottleneck in deploying next-generation photovoltaic technology.
