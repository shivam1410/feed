A panel argues that AlphaFold was only the start. Pushmeet Kohli of Google DeepMind and Sal Candido of Biohub spoke about AI for biology. They say protein structure prediction is far from solved. Candido says protein language models may already hold scientific knowledge that has not been unlocked yet. They also argue that biology needs models that go beyond single proteins. Scaling compute and data alone won't solve biology, they say.
