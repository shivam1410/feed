Large-scale behavioral studies demonstrate that people approach novel games systematically using adaptive rational reasoning processes. A cognitive model called the 'Intuitive Gamer' captures this flexible learning strategy. People learn and adjust strategies based on observed outcomes, not random exploration. This systematic approach reflects how humans rapidly understand new rule systems. The model provides insights into human reasoning, learning, and adaptive strategy development. These findings have implications for designing more flexible artificial intelligence systems that can learn from and adapt to human behavior. Better understanding human adaptation could improve human-AI interaction and collaboration.
