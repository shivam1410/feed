Researchers have developed novel chemistry to transform discarded PVC plastic waste into high-grade motor lubricant, demonstrating effective waste valorization. Separately, scientists are using engineered yeast strains to improve production of cancer drugs more efficiently and sustainably. Both examples showcase how modern chemistry and biotechnology can convert waste materials or optimize manufacturing processes into economically valuable applications, demonstrating practical solutions to waste management while advancing pharmaceutical production capabilities and circular economy principles.
