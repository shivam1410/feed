Researchers developed a new lithium metal battery design incorporating a targeted ligand anti-solvent into an ether-based electrolyte. The resulting batteries achieve long cycle life, high energy density, and high capacity retention over extended use cycles. This represents a significant advancement over conventional lithium battery chemistry.
