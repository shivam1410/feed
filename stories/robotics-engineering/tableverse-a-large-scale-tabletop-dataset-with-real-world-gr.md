TableVerse develops an automated Real2Sim pipeline that reconstructs high-fidelity simulation-ready tabletop scenes directly from unstructured internet images rather than hallucinating layouts. The framework processes raw photos into physically accurate environments with correct metric scales, authentic topologies, and verified mechanical stability. An integrated trajectory generator synthesizes collision-free pick-and-place demonstrations. The resulting TableVerse-100K dataset contains 100,000 unique, physically consistent tabletop environments paired with interactive manipulation trajectories capturing diverse asset compositions and realistic spatial distributions. This scalable, high-fidelity foundation addresses the core bottleneck in robotic manipulation research: lack of large-scale, physically plausible training data grounded in real-world complexity.
