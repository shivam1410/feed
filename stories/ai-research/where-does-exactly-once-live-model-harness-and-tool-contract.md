Scientists studied where exactly-once guarantees should live in LLM agent systems: in the model, harness, or tool contract. They built LIMBO, a sandbox with six services and twelve fault modes, running 25,930 episodes across nine models. Frontier models duplicated writes 56-74% of the time when they could not verify outcomes. This matters because LLM agents need reliable systems to avoid double charges and failed work.
