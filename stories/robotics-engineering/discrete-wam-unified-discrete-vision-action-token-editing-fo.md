Discrete-WAM introduces a unified discrete latent vision-action policy for autonomous driving that enables compositional and counterfactual reasoning about driving decisions. Using aligned discrete tokens and a shared discrete diffusion framework, vehicles reason about causal relationships between visual observations and control actions. This capability supports safer decision-making by enabling systems to explore cause-effect chains and consider alternative actions before execution. The discrete token approach unifies traditionally separate vision processing and action synthesis pipelines, improving both generalization to new scenarios and interpretability of learned behaviors in safety-critical autonomous systems.
