Large language model pre-training requires optimally mixing diverse data sources, but fixed schedules waste computation on irrelevant data. The Holistic Data Scheduler uses reinforcement learning with multi-objective reward functions to dynamically mix training data during pre-training. This adaptive approach optimizes efficiency and final model performance simultaneously. Smarter data scheduling reduces pre-training costs and improves capabilities, making large-scale AI training more economical and efficient.
