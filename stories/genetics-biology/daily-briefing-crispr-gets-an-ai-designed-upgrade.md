Researchers used artificial intelligence to design novel CRISPR enzymes that have never been found in nature and that edit DNA more efficiently than naturally occurring versions. These computationally designed proteins demonstrate how AI can create functional biological molecules exceeding the capabilities of those shaped by millions of years of evolution. The breakthrough represents a major advance in synthetic biology and could accelerate development of more precise, more effective gene therapies for treating genetic diseases and other conditions.
