LongCounsel-8 provides 7,749 five-session counseling trajectories for depression tracking, grounded in empirical symptom patterns and real client profiles. Key finding: single-session depression scores do not guarantee trend identification—distinguishing improvement from worsening. This matters because clinical applications require tracking symptom progression, not snapshots, raising questions about evaluation metrics for mental health AI systems.
