Recursive Game Creator treats game development like a loop with Designer, Builder, Player, and Reviewer. The Player runs programmatic tests to collect gameplay data, while the Reviewer evaluates using trajectory metrics and visual checks to optimize for fun, not just technical correctness. It achieved 77.89 on GameCraft-Bench and 53.2% on GameASG-Bench with 34% improvement over baseline, plus users reported longer playtime and higher enjoyment.
