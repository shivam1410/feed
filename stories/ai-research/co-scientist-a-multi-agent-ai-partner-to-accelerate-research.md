Co-Scientist is a multi-agent AI system built on Gemini designed to collaborate with human researchers and accelerate scientific discovery. Rather than a passive tool, this system operates as an active partner, suggesting experiments, analyzing data, and identifying patterns across research domains. The multi-agent design enables diverse specialized capabilities working in concert across hypothesis generation, literature synthesis, experimental design, and result interpretation. By automating routine research tasks and providing intelligent suggestions, Co-Scientist frees scientists to focus on creative thinking and strategic decisions. This matters because scientific bottlenecks increasingly involve data synthesis and pattern recognition—areas where AI excels alongside human judgment.
