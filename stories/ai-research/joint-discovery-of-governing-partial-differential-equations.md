MCO-PDE discovers shared partial differential equations from multiple datasets using competitive optimization and neural surrogates. Tested on seven cases with just 50 observations per dataset, the system recovered canonical equations with high accuracy. It handles complex 2D and 3D geometries with irregular boundaries and heterogeneous coefficients, successfully extracting physical laws from real-world wave-tank experiments. By exploiting multiple data sources simultaneously and assessing dataset credibility dynamically, this enables automated scientific discovery when observations are sparse.
