Researchers discovered that chromosome arm-level aneuploidies in breast cancer selectively promote specific driver genes including PLGRKT, a newly identified oncogene. PLGRKT's tumor-promoting activity associates with highly stress-resistant mitochondria and enhanced detoxification of reactive oxygen species. This reveals how chromosomal imbalances enable cancer progression by selecting for genes enhancing cellular survival.
