An enzymatic platform enables efficient construction of thiophosphate molecular motifs—common structural features in drug candidates—without relying on expensive chemical reagents traditionally required. This enzyme-based approach reduces both cost and complexity in synthesizing thiophosphates, laying foundation for enzymatic cascades to produce thiophosphate-containing pharmaceuticals at scale. The strategy supports more sustainable drug development by replacing energy-intensive chemical synthesis with biological catalysis. For pharmaceutical companies, this could accelerate discovery and reduce production costs of thiophosphate-containing therapeutics. The platform demonstrates broader potential for using engineered enzymes to construct complex drug-like molecules efficiently.
