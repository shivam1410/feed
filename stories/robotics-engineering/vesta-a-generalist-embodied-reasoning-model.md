Vesta unifies embodied AI by integrating localization, spatial reasoning, navigation, and long-horizon planning into a single foundation model rather than separate specialized systems. Testing demonstrates it outperforms task-specific models on both benchmark evaluations and real-world robotic applications. This generalist approach suggests unified models can handle diverse embodied reasoning tasks more effectively than traditional task-specific architectures.
