A vaccination approach successfully induced broadly neutralizing antibodies against HIV-1 in primate subjects. This result represents progress toward a preventive HIV vaccine by demonstrating that immunization can generate antibodies capable of neutralizing diverse HIV strains. The finding advances vaccine development against one of the most persistent global health threats.
