AREX introduces Recursively Self-Improving (RSI) agents that alternate between research loops gathering evidence and self-improvement loops auditing constraints and launching targeted follow-up research. The framework learns an autonomous context-update tool compressing interaction history into compact improvement states. Available in 4B dense and 122B Mixture-of-Experts configurations trained via agentic mid-training and long-horizon RL, the approach emphasizes steps where decisive evidence arrives or research errors are corrected. This matters because verifying intermediate results enables efficient discovery of answers satisfying multiple constraints.
