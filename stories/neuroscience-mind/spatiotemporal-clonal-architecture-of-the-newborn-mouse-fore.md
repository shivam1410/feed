Clonal barcoding across the newborn mouse forebrain reveals developmental principles whereby glutamatergic neurons and astrocytes remain locally derived, whereas widely dispersed GABAergic (γ-aminobutyric acid-producing) lineages link neurons with oligodendrocyte precursor cells.
