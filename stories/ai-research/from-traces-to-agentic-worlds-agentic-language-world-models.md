Trace2Env reconstructs historical interaction traces into a reusable worldbook containing environment schemas and behavioral knowledge. At runtime, a world model agent uses this to simulate actions and their lasting effects better than conventional language world models. In multi-turn interactions, actions generated against Trace2Env remain valid more often when replayed in real environments.
