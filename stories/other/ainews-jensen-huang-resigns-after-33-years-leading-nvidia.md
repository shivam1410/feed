Jensen Huang has stepped down as NVIDIA's CEO, president, and board member after 33 years. Colette Kress, the CFO since 2013, takes over as interim CEO and keeps her CFO job. Reports of a board move followed an NYT report of an affair with an accused Chinese spy, and then a second reported affair. The newsletter presumes the board forced the exit, though it notes the link to the reports is debatable. The next earnings call is expected to be one of the most closely watched in NVIDIA's history.
