A hiring manager at Anthropic, Felix Reiseberg, has stopped giving coding interviews. Claude is now better than people at those tests, and strong builders succeed regardless of how they score. The advice for junior engineers is to pick business impact over backlog work. Choose a few radical ideas that could dramatically improve results. Build a working prototype over a weekend instead of waiting for requirements documents and buy-in. Focus on one good idea rather than cramming several.
