UniWind advances day-ahead wind power forecasting by integrating physics-informed constraints with data-driven modeling through physics-informed state routing. The Physical Prior Estimator constructs site-calibrated physical priors using monotonic warping and a shared physical power curve, with constraints ensuring predictions respect available generation capacity. A Latent State Encoder models operational states (shutdowns, curtailment), while a State-aware Power Corrector applies knowledge-guided supervised state routing and bounded expert correction. Testing on more than 20 real-world wind farm datasets demonstrates superior accuracy and robustness compared to purely physical or data-driven approaches. This hybrid method captures meteorological effects through physics while learning state-dependent deviations through data.
