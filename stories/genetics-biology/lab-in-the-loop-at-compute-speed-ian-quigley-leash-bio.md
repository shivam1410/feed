Leash Bio has turned DNA Encoded Libraries into clean, reliable data. These libraries measure how proteins and small molecules interact at very high throughput. Many users found them hard to work with. The team's new project, Minos, is an AI playground where agents can do verifiable science. Their existing data supports tens of thousands of lab-in-the-loop simulations, which suits training agents with reinforcement learning.
