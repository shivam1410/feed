Google just shipped Gemini 4 Neon, its fourth Gemini 4 model in about seven weeks. It is faster and cheaper than Carbon, and beats it on some measures. Pricing is $3 per million input tokens, $0.15 for cached input, and $15 for output. It has a 1M-token context window and four effort levels. It is live in the paid Gemini API and AI Studio, and Pro and Ultra subscribers can pick it in the Gemini app. Lower prices matter if you run lots of model calls.
