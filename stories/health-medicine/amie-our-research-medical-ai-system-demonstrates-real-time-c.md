Google introduces AMIE, a research medical AI system demonstrating advanced capabilities in real-time clinical video consultations within simulated settings. This represents early progress toward AI-assisted telehealth and distributed care delivery. AMIE engages in conversational dialogue during simulated video visits, handling patient history, symptom assessment, and clinical reasoning. The controlled simulated environment allows researchers to rigorously evaluate safety, accuracy, and clinical appropriateness without exposing patients to experimental systems. Results establish baseline comparisons for human-AI collaborative care models and inform design principles for future AI-assisted clinical consultation platforms.
