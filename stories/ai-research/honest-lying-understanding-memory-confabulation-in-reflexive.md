Agents relying on self-generated reflections can store confident but incorrect task interpretations, creating persistent errors even after environment resets. Researchers introduce Reflection Repetition Rate as a metric to quantify and detect this confabulation phenomenon. Understanding how agents generate false confidence in their beliefs helps identify and mitigate critical failure modes in reasoning systems before deployment in high-stakes domains like healthcare or finance.
