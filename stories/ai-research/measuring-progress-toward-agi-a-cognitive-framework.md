Google DeepMind proposes a cognitive framework for evaluating artificial general intelligence (AGI), moving beyond narrow task-specific benchmarks to measure fundamental AI capabilities. The team launched a Kaggle hackathon to crowdsource development of comprehensive AGI capability benchmarks. This framework addresses a critical gap in AI research: defining and measuring progress toward AGI in a principled, testable manner. Such standardized evaluation methods are essential as AI systems approach or exceed human-level performance across increasingly diverse domains. Clear metrics enable researchers to track advancement.
