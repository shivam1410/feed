Rather than pursuing artificial general intelligence designed to replace humans, researchers advocate building 'pro-worker' AI systems that amplify human expertise and expand opportunity. This perspective challenges the industry focus on AGI race narratives, proposing instead specialized tools that enhance human capability. The shift prioritizes human flourishing and socioeconomic outcomes alongside technical advancement, reframing AI development goals from automation and replacement toward collaborative human-AI systems.
