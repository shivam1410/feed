Google DeepMind proposed a cognitive framework for evaluating progress toward artificial general intelligence (AGI). The framework aims to provide structured benchmarks for assessing AGI capabilities. To crowdsource capability evaluation, DeepMind launched a Kaggle hackathon inviting researchers to build and contribute benchmarks. The initiative represents an effort to establish shared evaluation standards for AGI development.
