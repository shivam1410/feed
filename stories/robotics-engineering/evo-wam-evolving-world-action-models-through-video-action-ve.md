A framework that adapts robot models to new tasks by verifying their own generated video-action pairs, without real-world execution. On RoboTwin tasks, success improved from 26.9% to 68.0%, and on real-world composite tasks from 20% to 76.7%. This matters because robots learn new skills by checking if predictions match intended actions.
