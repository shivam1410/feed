Memory architectures in large language models have evolved from implicit computation byproducts to explicit, controllable mechanisms spanning transient attention, recurrent states, and lookup storage. Survey presents taxonomy along three orthogonal axes: representation (implicit versus explicit), update dynamics (offline versus online), and persistence (short-term versus long-term). Framework distinguishes computation-coupled memory from independently addressable storage, unifying fragmented research into cohesive foundation for memory-centric language model design.
