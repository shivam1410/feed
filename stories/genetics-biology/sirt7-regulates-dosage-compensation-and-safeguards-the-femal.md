The SIRT7 protein maintains female X chromosome integrity and regulates dosage compensation—the critical balance between X-linked genes and autosomal genes. In females carrying two X chromosomes, SIRT7's role as a dosage-compensation regulator illuminates how cells maintain proper genetic balance despite the complexity of dual X chromosome presence.
