Gemini Robotics ER 2 represents a significant technological advance in robotic systems, featuring substantially improved video understanding and interpretation capabilities. The platform enhances tool orchestration—coordinating and sequencing robotic actions and manipulators effectively—and enables seamless multi-robot collaboration where multiple autonomous systems work together toward shared objectives.
