A comprehensive review of AI/ML methods for early cognitive decline detection synthesizes neurophysiological signals (EEG), neuroimaging (MRI, PET), blood biomarkers, and digital markers. Deep models (CNNs, LSTMs, transformers) show strong accuracy, though many rely on small single-site datasets unlikely to survive external validation. Plasma p-tau217 achieved clinical utility with the first blood test cleared for Alzheimer's diagnosis in 2025. Anti-amyloid therapies (lecanemab, donanemab) are approved despite modest benefits. The review identifies early detection as critical but highlights validation gaps.
