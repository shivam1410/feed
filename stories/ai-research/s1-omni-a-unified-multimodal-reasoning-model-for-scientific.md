S1-Omni consolidates fragmented AI4S capabilities into a unified multimodal reasoning model handling heterogeneous scientific data—structures (CIF), molecules (SMILES), proteins, spectra, and scientific images. The system maps diverse inputs into shared representation space while incorporating scientific laws and expert knowledge. Trained on 200 scientific tasks with millions of reasoning samples, it evaluates on 60+ benchmarks, outperforming GPT-5.5 and Gemini-3.1-Pro on most, including molecular generation, structure prediction, and image editing.
