DeepMind developed AlphaGenome, an AI model forecasting effects of altering every DNA letter in the human genome—covering all nine billion possible mutations. This computational atlas systematically maps how genetic variations affect cellular function and disease risks without requiring expensive laboratory experiments. The model enables researchers to understand genetic consequences at unprecedented scale and identify mutations driving diseases. Such comprehensive insights accelerate disease understanding and drug development by revealing which genetic changes most significantly impact human health. The atlas could enable development of more targeted therapeutics, personalized medicine approaches based on individual genetics, and improved disease prevention and treatment strategies.
