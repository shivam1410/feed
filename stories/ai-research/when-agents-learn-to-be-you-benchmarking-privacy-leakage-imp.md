AntiSkillBench evaluates privacy and impersonation risks in persona-skill systems using 7,500 dialogue traces grounded in 50 behaviorally rich profiles across diverse scenarios. Experiments across three frontier agents reveal persona-skill risks persist across LLM backbones and distillation protocols, extending from explicit attributes to communication styles and personality traits. Four defense configurations tested show limited and distillation-dependent effectiveness, failing to generalize. Results highlight inadequacy of current privacy protections in personalized agent systems and the need for better defenses.
