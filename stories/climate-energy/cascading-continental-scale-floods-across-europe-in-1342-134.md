Medieval records document cascading continental-scale megafloods across Europe during 1342–1343, where extreme events occurred in rapid sequence rather than isolation. Current flood management assumes isolated incidents, leaving communities unprepared for compound disaster sequences. These historical patterns demonstrate urgent need to develop proactive, multievent flood management strategies accounting for cascading extreme weather and atmospheric conditions.
