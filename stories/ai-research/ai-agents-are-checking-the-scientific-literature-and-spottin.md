Artificial intelligence systems have demonstrated an unexpected capability: identifying errors in decades-old scientific papers and reference databases that humans overlooked. As papers age, errors can propagate through citations and reshape scientific understanding in downstream research. AI's systematic checking of scientific literature helps correct the cumulative knowledge base, improving research reliability across fields. This matters because foundational errors in widely-cited papers can mislead generations of scientists, distort scientific consensus, and misdirect research resources.
