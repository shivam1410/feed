Microorganisms are emerging as potential drug delivery vehicles that could revolutionize cancer treatment by smuggling chemotherapy, radiotherapy, and immunotherapy agents directly into tumors. This approach concentrates treatments at disease sites while minimizing damage to healthy tissue. The strategy exploits bacteria's natural ability to penetrate and survive within tumor environments, which conventional delivery methods struggle to reach. By leveraging these biological couriers, oncologists might reduce the severe side effects that limit current treatment dosing while improving therapeutic outcomes simultaneously. This microbial delivery platform represents a promising frontier in precision medicine for cancer care.
