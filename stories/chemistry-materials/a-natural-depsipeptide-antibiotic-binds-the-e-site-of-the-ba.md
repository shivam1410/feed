Scientists have identified manikomycin, a natural antibiotic featuring an unprecedented mechanism of action against bacteria. Isolated from the microorganism Streptomyces rimosus, this depsipeptide compound targets the E-site of bacterial ribosomes—a previously unexploited location on the bacterial protein-synthesis machinery. The discovery was enabled by improved fractionation strategies that systematically identify antibiotics with unique molecular scaffolds. This finding suggests new avenues for developing antibiotics against drug-resistant bacteria by targeting previously untapped ribosomal sites, potentially expanding the antimicrobial arsenal available to clinicians.
