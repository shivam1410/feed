Discovery of approximately 400 earthwork structures in the Amazon suggests the Aquiry civilization was larger and more complex than recognized, indicating sophisticated land use and organization. Separately, fossils of the earliest squid ancestor have been identified, expanding understanding of cephalopod evolution and the Amazon's paleontological importance.
