Marine bacteria have evolved cooperative strategies to digest complex algal carbohydrates called fucoidans, too chemically intricate for any single species to break down alone. Bacteria divide the task: some degrade the main molecular backbone while others remove side branches. This modular cooperation demonstrates how microbial communities function through metabolic specialization, illuminating nutrient cycling in oceans and revealing how bacterial partnerships sustain marine ecosystem productivity.
