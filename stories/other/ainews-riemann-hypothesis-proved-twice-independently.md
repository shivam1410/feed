Two mathematicians claimed independent proofs of the Riemann Hypothesis within hours of each other. Levent Alpöge of Anthropic posted a Claude-driven proof as about 1.3GB of Lean code, not a full paper. Elliot Glazer says the formal statement is GRH and puts the chance of a Lean bug well under 1%. Alessandro Masullo released a 940-page human-written work in progress, built over 12 years. The AI proof is probably correct but hard to read, while the human one is readable but probably technically incorrect.
