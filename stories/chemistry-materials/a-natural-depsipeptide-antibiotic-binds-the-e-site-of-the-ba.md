Scientists using improved fractionation strategies discovered manikomycin, a novel depsipeptide antibiotic from Streptomyces rimosus that targets the E-site of the bacterial large ribosomal subunit—a previously unseen mechanism of action. Unlike conventional antibiotics that inhibit normal translation, this molecule binds to a different ribosomal site. This discovery demonstrates that refined extraction methods can identify antibiotics with fundamentally different molecular scaffolds and mechanisms. The finding expands the therapeutic toolkit by revealing ribosomal vulnerabilities bacteria may not have evolved widespread resistance mechanisms against.
