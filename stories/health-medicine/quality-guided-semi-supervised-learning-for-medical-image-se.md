Researchers developed a framework for training AI systems to segment medical images using a mix of labeled and unlabeled data. The key innovation is a quality predictor—a separate AI component that judges which automatically-generated labels are reliable before using them to train the main segmentation system. Medical image segmentation is crucial for diagnosis and treatment planning, but creating fully labeled training data is expensive and time-consuming. By improving the quality of automatically-generated labels through the quality predictor, the framework achieves better segmentation performance while reducing manual labeling needs. This approach could accelerate AI diagnostic tool deployment in hospitals.
