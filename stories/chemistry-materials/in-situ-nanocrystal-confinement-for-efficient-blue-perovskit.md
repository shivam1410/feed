Blue perovskite light-emitting diodes achieved 21.8% external quantum efficiency using in situ polymerization-driven nanocrystal confinement. This synthesis approach physically constrains nanocrystals during formation, boosting light emission efficiency. The achievement advances perovskite LED development toward practical blue-light applications.
