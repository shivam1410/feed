Anthropic has released Claude Haiku 5.5, a fast, low-cost model. Up to 100,000 tokens, it costs $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens, matching OpenAI's GPT-6 Luna. Past 100,000 tokens, the price jumps fivefold to $0.50 and $2.50. The new tokenizer also uses about 1.25 times as many tokens as Haiku 4.5 for the same long prompt, which is a hidden price rise. Willison's take: under 100,000 tokens, Haiku matches Luna's price and reports higher benchmark scores, but above that, Luna is the better deal. Why it matters: the cheaper choice depends on prompt length.
