FedIoC enables detection of coordinated cyberattack campaigns without sharing sensitive threat data across organizations. Using federated learning, clients train on local data, folding threat indicators into gradient updates via contrastive learning—IoC-matched network flows form positive pairs. Campaign structure emerges in gradient geometry, allowing servers to cluster attacks by gradient similarity and recover global campaign patterns. This allows collaborative threat intelligence without exposing sensitive telemetry or intelligence across institutional boundaries.
