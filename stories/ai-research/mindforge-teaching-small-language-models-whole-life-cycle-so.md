Specialized training data dramatically improves small-model from-scratch program synthesis. MindForge converts open-source CLIs into source-free environments, exposing only compiled binaries and documentation. Fine-tuning Qwen3.6-27B on high-quality trajectories from GLM-5.2 teacher increases ProgramBench average test pass rate from 37.98% to 49.51%, matching much larger frontier models. Improvements transfer across seven unseen benchmarks spanning repository generation, translation, bug fixing, and cross-language resolution.
