GraphVid enables precise multi-object video control through interactive structured interaction graphs instead of pixel-level trajectories. The curated GraphVid-Bench dataset contains interaction-centric videos with relational annotations. Despite using substantially less training data and parameters than prior motion-control methods, GraphVid achieves 39.9% FID reduction versus Motion-I2V, 37.6% FVD reduction, and significant improvements in PSNR (9.87→15.98) and SSIM (0.38→0.61). This matters because semantic graph-based interfaces scale better than trajectory-drawing, handling complexity and occlusion more gracefully.
