LapidaryEngine enables fully conversational crystal generation, accepting free-form natural-language requests and supporting iterative refinement through dialogue—a major advance over existing systems that require structured chemical descriptions. The key innovation is a pivot representation, an intermediate form enabling bidirectional translation between text and crystal structures without paired training datasets. This allows the system to interpret user feedback and enable precise structural control during generation. LapidaryEngine handles diverse materials-design tasks: insulator discovery, stability optimization, compositional modification, and structural editing. This matters because intuitive conversational workflows align better with users' evolving and inherently ambiguous design goals, replacing rigid specification with dynamic collaborative refinement.
