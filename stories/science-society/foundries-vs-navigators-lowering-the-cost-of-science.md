In biotech, AI has made thinking work dramatically faster, but physical experiments still take days or weeks to verify. Adrian Sanborn distinguishes between foundries like Xaira and Endura that shrink measurement costs with next-generation sequencing, and navigators that speed knowledge work. The biotech industry has adapted by focusing on industrializing the measurement rather than just accelerating thinking.
