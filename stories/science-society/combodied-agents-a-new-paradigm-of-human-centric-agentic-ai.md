ComBodied Agents integrate digital and embodied tools in closed-loop systems that model individual human-state trajectories over time. The framework provides proportionate, consent-aware support personalized to each person's needs and preferences. Continuous modeling of human state enables adaptive assistance. This matters because it centers human autonomy and consent in agent design rather than imposing uniform assistance.
