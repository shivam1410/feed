Decentralized agent economies using auction-based competition and wealth accumulation enable emergent collective intelligence without centralized control. Instead of one monolithic AI system, autonomous agents trade resources, compete for tasks, and accumulate wealth based on performance. Through market interactions, agents self-organize problem-solving strategies that rival or exceed centrally-designed systems. Agents specialize in sub-tasks and exchange value, creating emergent division of labor. This approach outperforms monolithic systems on complex reasoning and optimization, suggesting distributed economics can drive intelligence emergence in future AI architectures.
