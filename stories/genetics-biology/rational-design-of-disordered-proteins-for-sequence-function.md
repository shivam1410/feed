GOOSE, a computational tool, enables rapid design and experimental testing of thousands of disordered protein sequences to systematically reveal how variations produce distinct functional outcomes. This accelerates understanding of intrinsically disordered proteins, which comprise approximately 30% of eukaryotic proteins and regulate numerous cellular processes.
