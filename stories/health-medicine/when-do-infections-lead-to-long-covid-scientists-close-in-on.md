Hundreds of millions of people develop long-term conditions following infections, called long COVID. Researchers are investigating the triggers that determine who develops post-viral syndromes and working toward treatments to help affected patients. This matters because long COVID represents a substantial public health burden affecting a large global population. Understanding what factors lead to prolonged symptoms after infection is essential for developing treatments that could improve outcomes for millions living with post-infection chronic conditions.
