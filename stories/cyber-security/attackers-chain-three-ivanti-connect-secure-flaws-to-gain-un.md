CISA added two Ivanti Connect Secure flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on Friday. The flaws are being actively exploited in the wild. One is CVE-2026-111357, rated 8.8 on CVSS, for missing authentication on a critical function. The original report says attackers chain three flaws to reach unauthenticated root access.
