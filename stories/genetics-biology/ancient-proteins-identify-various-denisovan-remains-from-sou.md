Proteomic analysis of bone fragments and teeth excavated from Southwest China identified multiple Denisovan remains, revealing details of their evolution and phenotype while expanding their documented geographic range. Ancient protein sequencing bypasses DNA degradation constraints, allowing researchers to identify individuals and infer evolutionary relationships across populations. This discovery fills a geographical gap in the Denisovan fossil record and provides insight into how this ancient human species adapted across Asian regions. The work exemplifies how modern protein analysis revives knowledge from fragmentary prehistoric remains.
