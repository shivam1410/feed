Google DeepMind proposes a cognitive framework for objectively evaluating progress toward artificial general intelligence, establishing measurable criteria and benchmarks for AGI assessment. The company launched a Kaggle hackathon to crowdsource development of additional capability benchmarks. This initiative aims to create standardized, rigorous methods for measuring AGI advancement and tracking incremental progress toward more general AI systems.
