Researchers found that harmful tau proteins in Alzheimer's disease spread through the brain like prions, essentially corrupting healthy proteins into damaged copies. Structural studies confirm the misfolded tau acts as a template, forcing other tau molecules into the same shape. This explains how diseases progress through the brain systematically, cell by cell, destroying neural tissue.
