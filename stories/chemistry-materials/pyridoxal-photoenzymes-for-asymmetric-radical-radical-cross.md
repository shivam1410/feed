Researchers have developed pyridoxal photoenzymes that perform asymmetric radical–radical cross-couplings, a chemical synthesis technique using vitamin B6-derived catalysts activated by light. This light-driven, enzyme-catalyzed method enables selective joining of molecular fragments with precise three-dimensional control. The approach advances synthetic chemistry for producing complex pharmaceuticals and materials while offering efficiency advantages over conventional chemical synthesis.
