Accelerating Arctic glacier melt and dynamic sea ice changes increase iceberg delivery to deep ocean floors, where dropstones reshape benthic habitats and biodiversity patterns. The discovery reveals climate-driven glacial changes cascade globally, extending climate impacts far beyond polar regions into remote deep-sea ecosystems previously thought insulated from surface changes.
