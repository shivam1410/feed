Researchers examine El Niño behavior in a thermally saturated world—that is, under conditions of extreme global warming. As Earth's temperature increases, the mechanisms and impacts of El Niño patterns may fundamentally change. Understanding how this critical ocean-climate phenomenon behaves when planetary warming reaches saturation is essential for predicting future climate disruptions and their global impacts on weather, agriculture, and coastal regions.
