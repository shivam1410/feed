An analysis of aquatic invertebrate species reveals their global production represents a substantial and nutrient-rich resource crucial for human health. The work establishes that aquatic invertebrates provide important nutritional contributions previously underrecognized in policy and conservation frameworks. The research provides a foundation for better recognizing these invertebrate nutrients and incorporating them into conservation management and policy efforts. This recognition could enhance both environmental stewardship and global food security by highlighting the nutritional value of aquatic invertebrate production.
