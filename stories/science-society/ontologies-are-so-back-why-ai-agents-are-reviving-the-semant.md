UC Berkeley professor Frank Coyle argues effective agentic systems need ontologies—structured semantic representations—to provide logical guardrails beyond LLM probabilistic reasoning. Ontologies describe data structure, classes, properties, and relationships within knowledge domains; Coyle defines them as data-as-graphs, tracing the concept to Aristotle. Neo4j CEO Emil Eifrem describes three complementary ontology types: business-facing (key organizational concepts), technical (metadata of data sources and assets), and execution trace ontologies. This revival reflects recognition that agent scaling requires structured knowledge frameworks.
