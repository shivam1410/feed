Gasdermin D, a protein, serves as a delivery mechanism for caspase inhibitors designed to suppress pyroptosis, a regulated form of cell death. This approach offers a mechanism-based strategy for controlling pathological cell death with potential therapeutic applications in diseases where pyroptosis contributes to tissue damage and inflammation.
