mRNA vaccines delivered in lipid nanoparticles represent a flexible vaccine platform whose immune response can be tuned by modifying individual vaccine components. Components include mRNA sequence, lipid formulation, delivery system, and immunological additives. By systematically adjusting these elements, researchers can customize immune responses for different pathogens. This flexibility is particularly valuable for challenging infectious diseases where fixed vaccine approaches prove inadequate. The review details mechanisms by which vaccine components shape immune activation and discusses optimization strategies for improved vaccines.
