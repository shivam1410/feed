Google DeepMind researchers used agentic AI to mathematically verify several key cryptographic guarantees of the Signal protocol. The proof-of-concept took just four weeks. It gives independent validation to the cryptography that protects communications on Signal, Google Messages, and WhatsApp. The work also used a new AI framework called AlphaProof Code. It matters because AI can help check high-stakes cryptography.
