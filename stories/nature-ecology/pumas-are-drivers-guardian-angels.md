Pumas reduce vehicle collisions with deer on roads in their territories. The presence and predation pressure from big cats reduces deer populations and alters their behavior near roadways, resulting in fewer vehicle-deer accidents in regions where pumas are active. This ecological mechanism demonstrates an unexpected benefit of predator conservation. This matters because it shows apex predators provide public safety benefits beyond conservation and environmental goals, improving outcomes for both wildlife and human drivers through reduced collision accidents.
