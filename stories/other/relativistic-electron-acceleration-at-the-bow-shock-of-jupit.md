NASA's Juno spacecraft observed relativistic electron acceleration at Jupiter's bow shock—the boundary where solar wind meets the planet's magnetosphere. These observations established a universal scaling law connecting shock size to maximum cosmic ray energy. This single governing principle unites planetary and cosmic ray physics under common rules. The discovery extends understanding of particle acceleration mechanisms throughout the universe, explaining how particles achieve extreme energies from nearby cosmic events to distant galaxies.
