TypeSafe launched Jev, a fast decision model that is over 100 times faster and 200 times cheaper than small frontier LLMs. It only handles classification, routing, and scoring, leaving reasoning to larger models. Trained with RLCD, a calibrated decision approach. The idea is to pair it with larger slow models rather than use one model for everything. Matters because it shows the future is specialized fast models plus slow reasoners.
