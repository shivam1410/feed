Cancer cells hide rogue DNA loops. Many tumors carry extrachromosomal DNA that fuels their growth and survives through a special repair mechanism at fragile TA-rich spots. When researchers blocked this repair protein, the rogue DNA fell apart. This matters because ecDNA-dependent cancers have a distinct vulnerability doctors could exploit therapeutically.
