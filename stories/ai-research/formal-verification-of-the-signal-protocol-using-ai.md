Google DeepMind used agentic AI to formally verify key cryptographic guarantees of the Signal protocol. The proof-of-concept took four weeks, using a new framework called AlphaProof Code. Formal verification means mathematically proving that a design works. The result independently validates the cryptography behind Signal, Google Messages, and WhatsApp. It also shows AI can do serious formal verification work.
