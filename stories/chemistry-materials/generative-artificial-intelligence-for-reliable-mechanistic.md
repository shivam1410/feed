A retrieval-augmented generation framework fine-tuned on 3,309 expert-verified QA pairs from 840 peer-reviewed papers predicts corrosion and explains underlying mechanisms. The system achieves 96.4% faithfulness and 98.8% context recall with token accuracy gains of 143–194% over baseline. A novel Reason Map framework detects reasoning errors by reconstructing evidence graphs, addressing the $4 trillion global cost of corrosion.
