Researchers developed APT (Action Expert Pretraining), a two-stage training method that addresses poor generalization in Vision-Language-Action models. The method first pretrains action experts using vision-action pairs, then integrates language conditioning in a second stage. This approach significantly improves out-of-distribution instruction generalization in robotic systems. The work matters because Vision-Language-Action models struggle to generalize to novel instructions in real-world settings. By decoupling action learning from language conditioning, APT enables robots to better understand and execute new instructions, advancing practical robot learning systems.
