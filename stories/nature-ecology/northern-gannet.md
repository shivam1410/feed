Morris is the only known Northern Gannet (Morus bassanus) in the entire Pacific Ocean. The bird first appeared in the Farallon Islands off San Francisco 14 years ago and established Pillar Point Harbor as its home, becoming a local celebrity. With distinctive white plumage and a yellow head, Morris is easily identifiable and regularly spotted near the commercial pier alongside smaller black Brandt's cormorants. This solitary seabird's presence in the Pacific—where Northern Gannets are unknown—offers researchers an unusual subject for studying seabird behavior, habitat preferences, and long-distance oceanic dispersal patterns across vast ocean basins.
