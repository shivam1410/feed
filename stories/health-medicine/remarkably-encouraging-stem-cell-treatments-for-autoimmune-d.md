Scientists are advancing stem-cell therapies for autoimmune diseases, transforming the body's versatile stem cells into viable treatments. A key challenge is managing immunosuppression—preventing the immune system from rejecting the treatment. Early progress has been described as 'remarkably encouraging,' suggesting these therapies may offer new options for conditions where current treatments are limited.
