This framework explores how artificial intelligence can enhance ecosystem services science—the study of benefits nature provides including pollination, water purification, and carbon storage. AI can analyze large environmental datasets, predict ecosystem responses, and optimize conservation decisions. Ecosystem services face threats from climate change and land use. AI-driven approaches could accelerate understanding of ecosystem dynamics and improve conservation effectiveness.
