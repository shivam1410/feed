DeepMind introduced Gemini Robotics ER 2, marking a significant advance in robotic capabilities. The system improves video understanding, tool orchestration—the coordination of multiple actions—and multi-robot collaboration. Better video understanding allows robots to perceive and respond to complex scenes; improved tool orchestration enables sequential task execution; multi-robot collaboration enables teams to solve problems larger than any single robot. This matters because these capabilities expand the range of real-world tasks robots can autonomously accomplish.
