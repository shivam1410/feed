The universe exhibits directional structure at gigaparsec scales, contradicting the standard assumption that it becomes statistically uniform at very large distances. Researchers using the Angular Distribution of Pairwise Distances method detected coherent anisotropic patterns in galaxy distributions. This finding is significant because isotropy is a cornerstone of modern cosmology; if confirmed, it reshapes our models of how matter is organized and how the cosmos evolves.
