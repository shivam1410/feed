DeepMind's AlphaGenome Atlas is a comprehensive database predicting the functional effects of every possible single nucleotide variant in the human genome. This tool maps how each of approximately three billion potential DNA sequence variations affects protein function and human biology. By providing genome-wide variant effect predictions, the database enables researchers to understand genetic contributions to disease susceptibility, interpret clinical genetic findings, and accelerate biomedical research. Access to such complete variant annotation represents a major resource for the scientific community, offering insights into the genetic basis of human traits and conditions at unprecedented scale.
