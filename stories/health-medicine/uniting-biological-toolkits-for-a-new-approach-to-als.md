A collaborative initiative unites Boston Children's Hospital and MIT to pursue RNA-based therapeutic approaches for amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS). This partnership leverages complementary expertise in molecular biology and translational research. ALS is a progressive neurodegenerative disease with limited treatment options. RNA-based therapies represent a promising frontier for targeting disease-causing mutations and could accelerate discovery of effective interventions.
