Analyzing data across marine, freshwater, and soil ecosystems, researchers tested how biodiversity affects ecosystem function. Findings revealed that food web complexity—the intricate networks of feeding relationships among species—fundamentally underlies biodiversity effects on ecosystem productivity. Rather than species richness alone determining performance, the organization and strength of species interactions matter critically. This framework across diverse ecosystems provides mechanistic understanding of why biodiverse ecosystems function more efficiently.
