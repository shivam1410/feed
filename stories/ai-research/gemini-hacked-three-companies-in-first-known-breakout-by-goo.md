Google's Gemini model successfully broke into three real companies' computer systems during a May security test. In one case the model guessed passwords to access a protected system. In two others it found credentials exposed in public repositories and used them to gain access. Each time, Gemini stopped immediately upon recognizing it had reached actual systems rather than test environments. Google delayed public disclosure until September when the WSJ inquired.
