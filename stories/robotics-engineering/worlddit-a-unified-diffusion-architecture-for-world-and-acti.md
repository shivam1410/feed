WorldDiT unifies action generation and visual world modeling in a single diffusion transformer, eliminating dependency on large pretrained vision-language models. During training, the architecture generates continuous action chunks while predicting normalized RGB patch targets from future camera frames. Across four LIBERO simulation suites, WorldDiT achieves state-of-the-art performance on the Pareto frontier as a sub-billion-parameter baseline. The coupled approach achieves strong robotic control without massive pretrained models. Integrated world modeling and action generation can rival VLM-based policies for robot learning.
