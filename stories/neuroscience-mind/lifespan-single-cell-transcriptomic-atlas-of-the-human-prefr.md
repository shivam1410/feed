Brain cells age on different schedules. Researchers mapped gene expression across a lifetime in the prefrontal cortex and found explosive changes during childhood, stability through midlife, then selective gene reactivation in old age. Different cell types follow different aging curves. This matters because it explains why cognitive abilities decline unevenly across life.
