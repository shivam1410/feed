ACORN (Active Continuous-Score Occupancy Modeling) optimizes expert review effort in machine-learning-assisted biodiversity surveys by strategically selecting samples for human verification that maximize downstream ecological inference, rather than maximizing classifier accuracy. Tested on camera-trap and bioacoustic datasets, ACORN recovers ecological conclusions close to fully human-labeled data while requiring substantially fewer expert reviews than non-targeted policies. This demonstrates that AI-assisted scientific workflows should optimize for the end-user's analytical goal, not the classifier's performance.
