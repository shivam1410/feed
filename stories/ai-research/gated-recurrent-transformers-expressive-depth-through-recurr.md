Gated recurrent transformers achieve comparable or better quality than deeper models while using far fewer parameters and less memory by reusing a shared core layer and modulating depth through adaptive update gates. This design trades architectural depth for learned gating mechanisms, enabling efficient scaling without the computational overhead of pure depth expansion.
