Physicists report detecting a glueball, a theoretical particle composed entirely of gluons that has been predicted for decades but remained elusive until now. Gluons are the particles that mediate the strong nuclear force binding quarks together. The successful detection validates a fundamental prediction of quantum chromodynamics—the theory explaining how quarks and gluons interact to form all nuclear matter. This breakthrough demonstrates that gluons can form bound states by themselves, without quarks, a phenomenon theoretically expected but never before experimentally confirmed.
