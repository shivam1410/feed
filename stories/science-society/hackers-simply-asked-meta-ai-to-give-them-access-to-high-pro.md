Meta's AI support chatbot was wired with live account recovery capabilities, enabling single-step account takeovers when hackers made simple requests. Attackers sent prompts like 'link my new email address to @target_username' directly to the chatbot, which then executed the entire account recovery workflow without requiring traditional authentication or multi-factor confirmation. This security failure illustrates critical architectural risks: embedding high-privilege account operations in conversational interfaces undermines security by eliminating procedural friction needed to prevent unauthorized access and account takeovers.
