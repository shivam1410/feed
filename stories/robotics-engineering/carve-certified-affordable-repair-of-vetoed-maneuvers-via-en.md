CARVE introduces a certification framework for repairing autonomous vehicle rule systems that fail in interactive driving scenarios. The system identifies cases where small adjustments could restore feasibility and provides runtime proofs for multi-agent repairs without requiring predictions of other drivers' compliance. Interactive driving reveals gaps where rigid rule systems break down when vehicles interact unpredictably. CARVE matters because autonomous vehicles must handle real-world complexity where other drivers behave unpredictably. By enabling runtime certification of repair strategies without predicting others' behavior, CARVE advances practical autonomous vehicle safety in complex, interactive traffic situations.
