SciReasoner is a multimodal scientific foundation model that enables interpretable structural reasoning across proteins, molecules, and crystals through a unified approach. The model discretizes structural elements from each domain into a shared vocabulary, enabling consistent reasoning across different scientific disciplines. By translating domain-specific structures into this unified representation language, SciReasoner improves prediction accuracy and enables transparent scientific inference with clear reasoning paths. The interdisciplinary architecture bridges chemistry, materials science, and structural biology, providing researchers with an interpretable foundation model for complex structure-property understanding and discovery. This approach demonstrates how unified representations can unlock cross-domain scientific insights.
