Single-molecule long-read sequencing of human sperm revealed that a substantial fraction of non-crossover gene conversions occur before meiosis, distinct from the well-characterized PRDM9-driven recombination. Variation exists across donors and events correlate with fragile sites. This finding uncovers an overlooked mechanism generating genetic diversity and explains previously unexplained patterns of human genetic variation.
