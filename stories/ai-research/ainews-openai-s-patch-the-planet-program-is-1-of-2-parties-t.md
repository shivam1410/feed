Two parties found major PHP vulnerabilities, both with AI help. Ryan Austin of the security firm Calif found one in OpenAI's Patch the Planet project, using GPT-5.6 Sol. Wiz, now part of Google Cloud, found another using Gemini 4 Argon, GPT-5.6 Sol and its own tool, Wiz Atlas. Disclosure was coordinated across the PHP ecosystem over about two months. The author expects at least a year of similar finds. It matters because critical code is getting far more scrutiny.
