Parameter count no longer defines model capability; data volume, compute allocation, and context matter equally, according to Professor Jie Tang. GLM-5.3's gains stem from reinforcement learning on long-horizon environments mimicking real engineering work. Chinchilla scaling assumptions (200–900 tokens per parameter) fail at inference scale: memorization improves with parameters, reasoning improves with post-training depth. This reframes how practitioners evaluate and optimize models.
