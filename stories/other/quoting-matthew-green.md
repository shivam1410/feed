Cryptographer Matthew Green contextualized Anthropic's recent AI cryptanalysis breakthrough within the historic transition from RSA/EC cryptography to post-quantum algorithms. Green argues this is an ideal moment for AI to develop strong cryptanalysis capabilities, potentially validating the hard mathematical problems underlying new standards or discovering real weaknesses. In the best case, the result strengthens confidence in post-quantum cryptography through robust academic vetting.
