The Nepal disaster demonstrates how compound climate risks—multiple interconnected hazards occurring together—can amplify each other beyond what single events alone would cause. Nepal's mountainous terrain intensifies such cascading effects. Scientists and policymakers emphasize that traditional risk management, focused on individual hazards, proves inadequate. Addressing these complex climate interactions is essential for protecting vulnerable populations.
