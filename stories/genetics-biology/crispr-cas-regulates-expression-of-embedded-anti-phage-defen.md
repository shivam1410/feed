Researchers discovered that CRISPR-Cas systems function as coordinated immune control networks in bacteria, transcriptionally regulating diverse antiviral defense mechanisms. The system uses CRISPR RNA-like guides to modulate expression of anti-phage defenses, balancing antiviral protection against fitness costs of maintaining these defenses. When bacterial cells detect viral threats, the system hyperactivates defenses to maximize protection. This layered immunity strategy reveals sophisticated bacterial immune coordination.
