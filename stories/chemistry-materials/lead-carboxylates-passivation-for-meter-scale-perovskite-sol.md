Lead carboxylates are employed as a passivation material for meter-scale perovskite solar modules. Passivation involves treating defect sites in semiconductor materials. The research demonstrates application of this chemical treatment approach to large-scale perovskite module manufacturing. Lead carboxylate passivation represents a materials engineering advance in scaling perovskite solar cell technology toward commercial deployment.
