Researchers used human-ape hybrid skeletal cells to study how regulatory DNA shaped human skeleton evolution. Massively parallel reporter assays revealed specific differences in cis-regulatory elements between species. These regulatory DNA variations explain how our skeletons became distinctly human. Understanding the genetic basis of skeletal differences matters because it shows how subtle changes in gene regulation drive evolution of human body structure.
