AI models are now solving famous open math problems. An internal OpenAI model found a counterexample to Erdős's unit distance conjecture, open since 1946. A month later OpenAI published a proof of the Cycle Double Cover problem, then solutions to ten more open problems by July. Anthropic published work on Fermat's Last Theorem and bounds on the Riemann hypothesis. The author wants to know what makes a problem easy for an LLM, since that shows where to aim it.
