After decades of research, scientists developed the first gene therapy that restores hearing in people born with deafness from a specific genetic mutation. The therapy inserts a healthy gene copy that lets the inner ear transmit sound signals to the brain. This breakthrough opens pathways for treating other genetic forms of hearing loss that currently have no effective treatment options available.
