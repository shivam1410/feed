Submarine expeditions to a newly discovered whale graveyard dating back millions of years reveal complex, specialized ecosystems thriving on decomposing whale remains in the deep sea. The site demonstrates how deep-ocean biodiversity depends on large-scale energy inputs from surface waters. Separately, researchers converted plant material into nylon through a novel chemical process, offering a sustainable alternative to conventional petroleum-based nylon production.
