An algorithm picks diverse compounds that score well, solving the real tradeoff in virtual screening: highest-scoring sets lack diversity, but diversity selection sacrifices good candidates. It uses integer optimization with fingerprint, shape, and electrostatic distance metrics. This matters because compounds you synthesize or purchase should be both promising and chemically different. Virtual screening should be scored on optimal diverse selection, not just ranking.
