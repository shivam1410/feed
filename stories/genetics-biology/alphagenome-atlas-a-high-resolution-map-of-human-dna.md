AlphaGenome Atlas is a comprehensive database that predicts the effects of every possible single nucleotide variant across the human genome. This resource maps the consequences of changing any single DNA building block at any genomic location, providing a systematic view of genetic variation impact. By cataloging predicted effects of all variants, researchers can more rapidly assess disease-causing mutations and understand evolutionary constraints. This database accelerates genetic medicine research by eliminating the need to study variants individually and enables population-level understanding of genetic architecture. It represents a major advance in genome science, making genetic knowledge more accessible to researchers worldwide.
