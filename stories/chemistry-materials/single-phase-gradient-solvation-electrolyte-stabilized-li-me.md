A new electrolyte formulation for lithium metal batteries achieves long cycle life, high energy density, and strong capacity retention using a targeted ligand anti-solvent in an ether-based electrolyte. This chemistry breakthrough improves battery performance, supporting wider adoption of lithium metal batteries in electric vehicles and energy storage where durability and power are critical.
