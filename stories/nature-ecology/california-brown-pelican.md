Pacifica Pier in San Mateo County, California, closed at the start of June due to a crack in its concrete walkway that made access unsafe. California brown pelicans have since entirely colonized the closed pier. This illustrates how wildlife species quickly exploit infrastructure when it becomes inaccessible to humans. The structure, originally built for public recreation, has transformed into an accidental refuge for these marine birds, demonstrating an unexpected ecological consequence of infrastructure failure.
