Anthropic reported a $47 billion revenue run-rate (up from $9 billion in December) and closed a Series H funding round at a $900 billion pre-money valuation, raising $65 billion including contributions from hyperscalers like Amazon. The company is now temporarily larger than OpenAI by headline metrics outside compute. Anthropic released Opus 4.8, addressing community concerns and achieving state-of-the-art performance on most economically relevant benchmarks. Most significantly, the company launched dynamic workflows (ultracode) in Claude Code, enabling massively parallel multi-agent orchestration. Developer Jarred Sumner used this feature to rewrite 750,000 lines of code in six days.
