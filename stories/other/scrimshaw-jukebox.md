Claude Opus 5.5 can compose game music. When asked to design a text format and build a playable music artifact inspired by Secret of Monkey Island, it produced surprisingly high-quality results. The capability mirrors recent breakthroughs in 3D graphics from text models. This raises the question of whether music composition is genuinely new or has existed in models longer.
