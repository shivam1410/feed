Six-month production analysis of autonomous language-model trading agents from two fleets reveals critical limitations: behavior driven by UI design rather than strategy, volatility-blind sizing, failure to capture price opportunities, and no directional edge. Frontier LLMs performed statistically identically to other families. Results suggest current LLMs are unsuited for autonomous trading.
