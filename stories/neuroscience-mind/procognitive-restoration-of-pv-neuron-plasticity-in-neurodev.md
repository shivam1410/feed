Expressing Meis2 in hippocampal parvalbumin-expressing interneurons of adult mice with neurodevelopmental disorder models restores experience-dependent neural plasticity, improves memory and network function, and suppresses seizures. This single-gene intervention in a specific interneuron population reverses multiple cognitive and neurological deficits, suggesting Meis2 and PV neuron plasticity represent promising therapeutic targets for neurodevelopmental disorders.
