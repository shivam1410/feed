Scientists developed a simple organic catalyst enabling direct copolymerization of carbon dioxide with bicycloalkanes to produce high-performance polyesters. Notably, these polyesters can be selectively depolymerized and recycled in closed-loop cycles, addressing plastic waste. The approach uses CO2 as a chemical feedstock rather than petroleum, reducing carbon footprint. This represents significant progress toward circular plastics that can be manufactured and recycled repeatedly without degradation.
