Engineered probiotics with synthetic glucose-responsive genetic circuits transiently colonize the gut and autonomously release therapeutic factors when blood glucose rises. Tested in both mice and primates, this living therapeutic provides non-invasive, oral control of diabetes-like metabolic disorders without requiring permanent gut colonization. The sense-and-respond approach demonstrates a new paradigm for autonomous biological therapeutics.
