Optical reasoning treats images as a primary reasoning medium for language and multimodal tasks, achieving superior token efficiency compared to traditional text-based approaches. By leveraging visual representations directly, the method reduces computational overhead while maintaining or improving performance on reasoning tasks. More efficient reasoning reduces computational costs and inference latency, making advanced AI capabilities more accessible, practical, and sustainable for real-time applications in production systems.
