Analysis of three major tropical forest regions reveals that relationships between aboveground biomass and climate factors vary significantly across regions, reflecting complex interactions between climate, topography, and soil properties. Understanding these multifactorial regional relationships is crucial for predicting how climate change will alter tropical forest carbon storage capacity, rather than applying universal climate-biomass rules.
