BRepCLIP enables natural language understanding of 3D CAD models through multimodal learning. The method aligns boundary representation geometry with language and image embeddings using contrastive pretraining, outperforming point-based approaches in retrieval and classification tasks. This advances CAD model search and automation for engineering design workflows.
