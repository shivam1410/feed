Molecular AI models learn from laboratory annotations, but these labels contain errors from experimental assays and weak annotation pipelines. MOLAR handles label noise in molecular property prediction by separating inference of true molecular properties from observation of recorded labels. The framework treats graph and text representations of molecules as independent evidence sources—each contributes to a distribution of the true property, which is then mapped to recorded labels through a probabilistic observation channel. This separates what the molecule truly is from what a possibly-corrupted label claims. Experiments on naturally noisy benchmarks show MOLAR outperforms baselines and provides interpretable diagnostics about label reliability and modality-specific evidence.
