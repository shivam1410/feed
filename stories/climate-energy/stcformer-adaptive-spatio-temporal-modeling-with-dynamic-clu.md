STCFormer is a weather forecasting model that dynamically groups weather stations based on how they evolve within each time window. Instead of using one fixed clustering across an observation window, it adjusts groupings constantly. Stations access local cluster information plus global regional summaries. It achieves lowest mean squared error on all eight forecasting tasks and ranks first or second in 47 of 48 comparisons.
