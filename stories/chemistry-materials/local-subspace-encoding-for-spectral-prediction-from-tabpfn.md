A new encoding called LVSE helps tabular AI models read near-infrared spectra. Across 61 numerical tasks, it cut prediction error by about 10 to 15 percent on average, depending on the checkpoint. Weighted F1 rose by 2.2 to 3.8 points across 76 classification datasets. Gains were small on a separate 35-task benchmark, so the authors don't claim a selector will reliably help. Still, it's a cheap feature worth testing alongside existing methods.
