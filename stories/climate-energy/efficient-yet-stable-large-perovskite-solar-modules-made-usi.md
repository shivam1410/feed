Researchers improved perovskite solar modules with a new surface treatment using stable lead carboxylate compounds applied uniformly across the panel. This approach replaces weaker protective molecular layers that wear unevenly. Large panels treated this way exceeded commercial silicon modules in efficiency and showed higher manufacturing yield. The breakthrough could make perovskite solar technology commercially viable.
