Fossil evidence and anatomical analysis suggest that spinosaurs possessed salt glands—specialized organs for regulating salt balance—indicating these massive prehistoric reptiles likely inhabited marine environments. This discovery fundamentally revises understanding of spinosaur ecology and habitat use.
