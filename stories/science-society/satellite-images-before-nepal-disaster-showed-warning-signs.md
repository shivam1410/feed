Satellite images revealed the accelerated movement of a glacier-rock mass just days before it collapsed and triggered a deadly flash flood in Nepal. The orbital analysis identified visible warning signs that preceded the disaster. This finding demonstrates that satellite monitoring can detect geological instability and precursor signals before catastrophic failure occurs. Such detection capability could substantially enhance disaster warning systems and community preparedness in mountainous regions vulnerable to glacier-triggered hazards, enabling faster response times and potentially saving lives.
