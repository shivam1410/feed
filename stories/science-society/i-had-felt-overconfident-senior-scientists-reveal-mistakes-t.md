Five senior scientists look back at their own mistakes. One of them has won a Nobel Prize. They talk about dead ends, bloopers, and moments when they were stubbornly certain. One admits to feeling overconfident. These moments shaped their science in ways they didn't expect. It's a candid look at the messy side of discovery, and a reminder that wrong turns are part of how science moves forward.
