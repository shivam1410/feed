Graph-PRefLexOR, a graph-native reasoning model trained with Group Relative Policy Optimization, advances scientific hypothesis generation in materials science through structured multi-phase reasoning. The approach progresses through mechanism exploration, graph construction, pattern extraction, and hypothesis synthesis stages, enabling systematic discovery of materials science concepts. Graph-native architecture provides inherent interpretability, allowing researchers to trace reasoning steps and understand how hypotheses emerge from underlying conceptual relationships. The method demonstrates enhanced semantic diversity and reasoning traceability compared to traditional approaches. By combining structured reasoning with graph-based representations, the system produces more trustworthy and explainable scientific hypotheses suitable for guiding experimental work.
