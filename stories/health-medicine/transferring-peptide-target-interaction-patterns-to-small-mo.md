Researchers built PIFT, a way to design small drug molecules that bind protein-protein interfaces. These surfaces are broad and shallow, which makes them hard to target. PIFT borrows interaction patterns from known peptide binders and uses them to guide generation. It steers both building-block choices and 3D coordinates. Peptide guidance improved interface recovery and gave strong docking performance across diverse targets. That matters because it opens a route to hard drug targets.
