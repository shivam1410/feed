A language model can rewrite its own weights to get better. No gradients and no training are needed. It diagnoses its failure and edits the weights behind it. Its own edit made it 6.6 points more accurate on 490 problems it never saw. The gain stays in the weights, so it adds no cost per answer. In a damaged-copy test, it repaired its weights and lifted six image benchmarks from 7% to 70%.
