QDSP is an interpretable machine learning framework for predicting mortality and cerebral palsy risk in very low birth weight infants at hospital discharge. On a cohort of 51 infants, it achieved 92% accuracy and 0.97 AUC, outperforming standard approaches. By combining stability-aware feature selection with differentiable decision structures, QDSP identifies clinically relevant predictors like periventricular leukomalacia and provides interpretable decision paths that clinicians can understand.
