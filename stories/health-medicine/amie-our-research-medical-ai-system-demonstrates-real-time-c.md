Google developed AMIE, a medical AI system capable of conducting real-time clinical video consultations with patients. In a first-of-its-kind study, AMIE demonstrated these consultation abilities in simulated clinical scenarios. This matters as an early proof that AI can engage in real-time medical consultation, though testing remains limited to simulated rather than real patient interactions.
