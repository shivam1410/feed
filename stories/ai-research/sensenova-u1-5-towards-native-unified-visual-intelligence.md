SenseNova-U1.5 is an 8-billion-parameter unified multimodal model designed to perform visual understanding, reasoning, and generation tasks without requiring separate encoder networks or variational autoencoders. The model architecture operates directly on image patches, using reconstruction-based learning combined with carefully curated training data, expert-driven optimization strategies, and on-policy distillation. These techniques work together to achieve high-fidelity visual outputs with strong instruction-following capabilities. By eliminating intermediate encoding stages, the approach potentially reduces computational overhead while maintaining coherent multimodal reasoning. This represents a significant architectural simplification for achieving native visual intelligence across multiple tasks.
