A comprehensive survey organizes robot-learning techniques around two poles: frozen-weight vision-language-action (VLA) models and code-as-policy agents that write executable skills. Analysis of 77 representative systems across six families reveals code-as-policy methods span zero-shot synthesis to self-improving closed-loop systems; only recent systems (ASPIRE, ENPIRE, RoboClaw) combine execution feedback, skill memory, and evolutionary search into open-ended loops. The survey maps the emerging robot-skill economy and identifies open problems: adaptation, cross-embodiment portability, safety verification, composition, and standardization.
