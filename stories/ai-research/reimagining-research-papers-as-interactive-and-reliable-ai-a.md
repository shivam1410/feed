Paper2Agent software converts research papers into interactive AI agents by transforming manuscripts, code, and data into model context protocol-based systems. These artificial agents can reproduce original research findings, answer new scientific questions, and collaborate to generate novel insights. This technology transforms static research publications into dynamic, interactive tools that enhance reproducibility and accelerate scientific discovery by making knowledge directly executable.
