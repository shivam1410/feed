Researchers created X-Tree, a method that finds recurring action patterns in agent training data. X-Tree identifies common sub-skills and teaches agents to reuse them, improving success rates by 4.5 percent on WebArena, 5.8 percent on ScienceWorld, and 4.1 percent on WebShop. Agents learn more efficiently when they recognize that similar tasks share underlying routines.
