Researchers created RLE-Bench to test coding agents on real robotics tasks. The benchmark covers 210 tasks across four workflows: interactive control, policy learning, perception, and mechanical design. It evaluates agents not just on whether they work but on what they build, train, and design. This measures broader engineering capabilities that single-task performance metrics miss.
