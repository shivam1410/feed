Researchers have created textile from fungal filaments that can self-repair and biodegrade completely. This offers a sustainable alternative to conventional textiles, which require intensive chemical processing and create significant waste. The material's self-repair ability extends garment lifespan, while full biodegradability eliminates end-of-life waste, addressing major environmental challenges in fashion manufacturing and consumption.
