Four antibiotic compounds produced by Streptomyces soil bacteria employ a multi-target attack strategy against drug-resistant bacteria. Rather than attacking a single component, these compounds simultaneously assault multiple parts of an essential metabolic pathway in bacteria, making resistance development extremely difficult. This cocktail approach targets bacterial survival mechanisms more comprehensively than traditional single-target antibiotics. The discovery offers promising direction for combating antimicrobial resistance by leveraging natural antibiotic production from soil microorganisms and deploying simultaneous multi-component attack strategies against pathogens.
