Using mechanistic interpretability on brain-to-speech decoders, small neuron groups carry information across different speech forms, with vocalized speech as the best teacher. Early layers are form-specific but later layers overlap, pointing where to share training data. This matters because it enables more data-efficient covert-speech interfaces through shared representations.
