KG-Chronos-2 couples a time-series foundation model to hydraulic domain knowledge for water-surface elevation forecasting. Tested on 4,675 cross sections across 71 reaches, it achieves RMSE of 0.247, reducing error 14.13% versus frozen Chronos-2 and 39.54% versus recurrent GeoFNO. The results demonstrate that augmenting temporal foundation models with project-specific knowledge improves HEC-RAS surrogate forecasting performance.
