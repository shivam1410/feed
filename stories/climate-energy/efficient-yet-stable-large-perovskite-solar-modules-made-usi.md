Researchers developed large-scale perovskite solar modules using lead carboxylate surface treatments achieving high efficiency and reliability. Non-uniform passivator layers—molecular coatings that fix defects and protect surfaces—previously limited module durability. The new approach using chemically stable lead carboxylates outperformed commercial silicon solar modules in yield. This advance addresses a key commercialization barrier for perovskite photovoltaics, which could offer lower costs and higher efficiency than conventional solar technology.
