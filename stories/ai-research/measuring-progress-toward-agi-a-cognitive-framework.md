Google DeepMind has proposed a cognitive framework designed to evaluate progress toward artificial general intelligence (AGI), addressing a long-standing challenge in measuring AI capability development. To operationalize this framework, the team launched a Kaggle hackathon to engage the broader research community in building and validating capability benchmarks. This work aims to establish standardized, measurable criteria for assessing whether AI systems are approaching AGI capabilities, moving beyond subjective assessment toward reproducible evaluation. The framework's development reflects growing recognition that systematic evaluation methods are essential for understanding and tracking AI progress toward more general intelligence.
