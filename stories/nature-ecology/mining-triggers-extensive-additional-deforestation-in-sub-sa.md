Extensive mining operations across sub-Saharan Africa are driving large-scale deforestation of dense tropical forests, particularly for cobalt and other energy transition minerals. Mining's environmental footprint extends beyond direct land clearing to trigger secondary deforestation. This creates tension: minerals essential for renewable energy require extraction that destroys carbon-absorbing forests. Understanding mining's full deforestation impact is crucial for sustainable supply chains and climate goals.
