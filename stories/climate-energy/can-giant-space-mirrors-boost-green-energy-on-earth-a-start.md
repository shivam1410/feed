A startup is planning to launch an array of 50,000 mirror-bearing satellites into orbit to enhance green energy generation on Earth through space-based solar reflection. However, astronomers have expressed deep concern that this massive satellite constellation will severely compromise telescope observations and astronomical research. The conflict highlights tensions between emerging space-based energy technologies and the preservation of astronomical research capabilities. Resolution may require compromise on constellation size or orbital placement.
