A new one-pot skeletal-editing reaction converts isoxazoles into pyrroles by replacing the isoxazole oxygen atom with carbon. The reaction proceeds through an N-propargylic enaminone intermediate and is guided by a computational model that predicts reaction outcomes. This method enables synthesis of pyrroles that are otherwise difficult to access, expanding the toolkit for building complex organic molecules in pharmaceutical research.
