Southern European countries including Spain and France are experiencing record-breaking wildfires and face a warming future as climate change creates progressively hotter, drier conditions resembling California's fire regime. Scientists expect these regions to experience more frequent and intense fires in coming decades, reshaping landscapes, infrastructure, and public health systems as fire seasons expand and severity increases across southern Europe.
