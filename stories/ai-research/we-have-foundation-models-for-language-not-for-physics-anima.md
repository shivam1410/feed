Anima Anandkumar's FourCastNet achieves breakthrough weather prediction using AI. Despite meteorology experts' skepticism about weather's chaos and reliance on supercomputers, her team built a model competitive with decades-old physics simulations. Key achievement: FourCastNet enables accurate weather forecasting on consumer GPUs, democratizing access. This addresses a long-neglected AI frontier—modeling continuous chaotic systems like weather, fusion, and fluid dynamics. Beyond meteorology, FourCastNet demonstrates AI can tackle physics-simulation domains, opening possibilities for complex system modeling and making frontier science accessible without specialized supercomputing infrastructure.
