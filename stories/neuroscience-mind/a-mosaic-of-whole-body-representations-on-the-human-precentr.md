Researchers created a comprehensive anatomical map of the human motor cortex at single-neuron resolution. This detailed atlas documents the precise organization and spatial arrangement of individual neurons throughout the motor cortex, the brain region responsible for planning, initiating, and controlling voluntary body movements. Understanding motor cortex organization at single-neuron resolution reveals how the brain encodes and coordinates motor commands for different body parts. This foundational neuroscience mapping work advances understanding of motor control mechanisms, informs study of movement disorders like Parkinson's disease and stroke, and establishes baseline knowledge for brain-computer interface development.
