Base editing at the PCSK9 gene achieved high efficiency while preserving normal human embryo development. This advance demonstrates that precise genetic editing can target disease-causing variants without disrupting the genetic mechanisms essential for healthy fetal growth. The successful coupling of therapeutic efficiency with developmental safety represents a significant step toward safe genetic interventions. Such work is foundational for developing gene therapies that could treat genetic diseases before birth while maintaining normal physiology.
