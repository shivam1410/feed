Researchers used a video game involving bombs and treasure chests to observe how brains make risky decisions. Neural activity flickered between two regions of the frontal cortex as participants weighed whether to play it safe or take a chance, revealing a neural "tug-of-war" that resolves competing options. This finding illuminates the biological mechanism behind decision-making under uncertainty and could inform understanding of how we navigate risk versus safety in everyday life.
