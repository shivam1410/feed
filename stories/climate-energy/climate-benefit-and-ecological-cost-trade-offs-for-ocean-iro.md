A process-rich global biogeochemical model examined trade-offs between carbon dioxide removal efficiency and ecosystem impacts from ocean iron fertilization across ten ocean biomes. The analysis identifies how different ocean regions balance climate benefits against ecological costs of altering marine productivity. Results inform policy decisions about ocean fertilization as a climate mitigation strategy.
