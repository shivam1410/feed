Several startup companies are advancing the frontier of genetic medicine by targeting epigenetic markers—chemical modifications that control gene expression without altering the underlying DNA sequences. These emerging therapies aim to treat conditions ranging from high cholesterol to rare muscular disorders. Epigenome editing works by reversibly modifying how genes are expressed, offering potential advantages over standard CRISPR gene editing, including greater precision and potentially fewer off-target effects. The approach represents a significant advance in precision medicine, enabling treatment of metabolic and genetic diseases.
