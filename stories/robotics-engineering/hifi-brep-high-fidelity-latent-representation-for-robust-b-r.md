HiFi-BRep advances boundary representation (B-Rep) synthesis for CAD and 3D modeling by introducing a topology-aware encoder paired with a unified single-stage decoder. The key innovation jointly predicts geometry and topology with built-in differentiable validity constraints ensuring generated shapes remain structurally correct. This integrated approach moves beyond sequential pipelines, improving robustness and fidelity of synthesized 3D models. The method is particularly relevant for generative design and automatic CAD generation where accurate geometry and valid topology are critical.
