PhysEvo lets a frozen AI model keep improving at robot tasks by learning from its own failures. A task agent acts while a meta-agent diagnoses why it fails and revises tools and skills. Success on RoboDojo jumped from 47% for the baseline to 62%, and on real robots it hit 84% success, showing the approach actually works in the physical world.
