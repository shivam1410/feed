ViSkill teaches VLM agents to learn visual skills as reusable cards stored in a skill library. Retrieved skills guide both inference and reward shaping, and successful trajectories are distilled back into the library. On benchmarks, it achieved 89% success, rising to 91% with cold-start initialization, outperforming baselines.
