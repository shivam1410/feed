NVIDIA released Cosmos 3, an open omnimodal world model unifying language, image, video, audio, and action using Mixture-of-Transformers with autoregressive reasoner and diffusion generator. Nano (16B) and Super (64B) variants are available; Super fine-tuned versions achieve state-of-the-art open-weight image and video generation. NVIDIA also unveiled Nemotron 3 Ultra, a 550B open-weight language model achieving top US performance, and RTX Spark, a 1-petaflop personal AI supercomputer.
