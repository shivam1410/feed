Analysis of one million diverse genomes identified genetic mutations linked to lower risk of diabetes, heart disease, and related cardiometabolic conditions. These variants are associated with favorable body composition—more muscle, less abdominal fat. The discovery maps specific genetic changes that protect against metabolic disease across diverse populations. This information could guide development of therapies mimicking these protective genetic effects or inform personalized prevention strategies.
