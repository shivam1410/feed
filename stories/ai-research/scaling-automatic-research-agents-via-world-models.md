World Model RL (WMRL) resolves a fundamental bottleneck in training automatic research agents where environment execution dominates training cost and prevents scalability. WMRL replaces real environment execution with a learned world model, eliminating this bottleneck. The approach includes Online Debiasing and Inverse-Variance Denoising to mitigate world model imperfections, and theoretical analysis proves both mitigations improve convergence guarantees. Empirically, WMRL accelerates training 3-4x across various tasks and agent scales while exceeding standard RL baseline performance. This makes automating empirical research with AI substantially more practical.
