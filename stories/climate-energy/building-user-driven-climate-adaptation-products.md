A systematic review demonstrates how user-centered design principles can strengthen co-production approaches for building climate adaptation products. Analysis shows that involving end users throughout design and development improves the relevance, usability, and adoption of climate adaptation solutions. By emphasizing what users actually need rather than expert assumptions, these approaches improve outcomes for communities facing climate impacts. This synthesis of design methodology and collaborative development offers a practical framework for building climate solutions that are more effective and better serve affected populations.
