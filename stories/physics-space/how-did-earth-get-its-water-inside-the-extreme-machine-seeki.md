The Z machine in New Mexico generates incredibly extreme shockwaves that can accurately simulate the intense pressure and temperature conditions found deep within Earth's interior. Researchers use it to recreate the dynamic environment that existed when our planet first formed billions of years ago. These groundbreaking experiments help scientists answer fundamental questions about Earth's origin, including where our planet's abundant water came from in the first place.
