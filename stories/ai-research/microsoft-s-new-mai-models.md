Microsoft released MAI-Thinking-1 (1T total, 35B active) and MAI-Code-1-Flash (137B total, 5B active) trained exclusively on commercially licensed enterprise data without third-party distillation. MAI-Thinking-1 reportedly outperforms Sonnet 4.6 in blind evaluations despite low active parameters. Code-1-Flash rolls out to GitHub Copilot users. Signals potential industry shift toward properly licensed training data and efficient small models.
