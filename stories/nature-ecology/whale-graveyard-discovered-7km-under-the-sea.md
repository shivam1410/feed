Researchers discovered a whale graveyard seven kilometers beneath the ocean surface containing abundant fossils and skeletal remains. The site has already yielded identification of at least one previously unknown extinct whale species. This paleontological accumulation offers significant evidence for understanding whale evolutionary history, deep-sea taphonomy, and mechanisms by which deep-ocean environments preserve and concentrate biological remains across geological time scales.
