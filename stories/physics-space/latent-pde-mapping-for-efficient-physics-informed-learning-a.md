Latent PDE mapping enables physics-informed learning on sparse data by pulling geometry-specific residuals to a predefined latent space via deformation gradients. Tested on the nonlinear, time-dependent Aliev-Panfilov PDE using only 15 geometric samples, it achieved 4–6× reduction in mean relative L2 error on select geometric families with negligible computational overhead, demonstrating efficient generalization across geometries.
