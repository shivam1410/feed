Frustrated by repeated LLM writing clichés like "no fluff" and "no filler," Simon Willison had Claude Fable 5 build a web tool highlighting ten common patterns that signal AI-generated text. The highlighter identifies characteristic phrases frequently appearing in generative AI output, helping readers and editors spot AI-written content. It serves as both a detection aid and a lens for understanding how LLM writing differs from human prose, addressing the proliferation of formulaic AI-generated articles.
