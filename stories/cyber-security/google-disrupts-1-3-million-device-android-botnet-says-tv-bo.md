Google led a takedown that cut off a botnet of more than 1.3 million Android devices, mostly TV set-top boxes. Google says it is highly confident that the maker, Videostrong, put a backdoor in the boxes on purpose. It also says Videostrong sold access to criminals. The criminals used the boxes for ad fraud and to pass other people's traffic through owners' home connections.
