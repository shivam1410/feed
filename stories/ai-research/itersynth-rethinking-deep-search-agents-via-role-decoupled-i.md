IterSynth splits AI search agents into two roles: a Planner that identifies what information to find and a Synthesizer that builds answers from evidence. This separation reduces decision-making noise compared to agents doing both jobs. IterSynth-8B scored 50.7 on deep-search benchmarks, beating prior 8B agents by 4.2 points. Role-splitting helps agents think more clearly on complex questions.
