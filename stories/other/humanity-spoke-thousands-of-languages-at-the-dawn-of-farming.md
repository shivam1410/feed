Agricultural transition reshaped human language. The shift from nomadic hunting-gathering to settled farming around agriculture's origins may have triggered rapid linguistic diversification, creating thousands of languages. Many have since disappeared. This history shows how major lifestyle changes reshape human communication patterns. The arc of linguistic diversity—expansion then decline—mirrors transformations in human settlement and cultural expression across millennia.
