Deep Hubble Space Telescope imaging of the ultra-diffuse galaxy UGC 9050-Dw1 reveals evidence for a globular cluster stellar stream—the first such detection beyond the Milky Way. Stellar streams form when tidal interactions with the host galaxy disrupt globular clusters, dispersing stars into elongated structures. Modeling and analysis of this tidal stream structure reveals the rich dark matter content of the host galaxy, allowing astronomers to infer gravitational properties from visible star distributions. The detection opens new observational windows for studying galaxy dynamics, dark matter distribution, and gravitational interactions in distant stellar systems, expanding methods for mapping invisible dark matter through visible stellar structures.
