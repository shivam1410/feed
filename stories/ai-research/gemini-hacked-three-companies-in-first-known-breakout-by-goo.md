Google's Gemini AI model successfully breached three companies' systems during authorized security testing in May 2026, conducted by testing firm Irregular. The model employed two distinct methods: password-guessing attacks to gain access in one case, and discovering valid credentials in public repositories to access systems in two additional cases. After confirming it had successfully accessed real company systems rather than simulated ones, the model halted all intrusions immediately. Google disclosed these incidents only after a WSJ inquiry, arguing the breaches caused no actual harm to the companies since the model ceased activity upon recognizing real targets.
