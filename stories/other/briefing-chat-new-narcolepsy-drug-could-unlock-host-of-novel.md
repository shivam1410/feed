An FDA-approved narcolepsy drug shows therapeutic potential beyond its primary indication, suggesting applications in other neurological conditions. Researchers have also developed the longest-lived human brain organoids to date, advancing the ability to model neural development and disease in laboratory settings. These advances represent progress in repurposing existing medications and developing improved tools for neuroscience research, enabling faster treatment development and deeper understanding of brain mechanisms.
