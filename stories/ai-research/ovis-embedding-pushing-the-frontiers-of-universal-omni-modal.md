Ovis-Embedding introduces a unified framework for encoding text, images, video, and audio into a shared representation space using a single multimodal backbone instead of separate networks. Three key advances enable this: pretrained initialization from Qwen-omni, broad high-quality training data, and optimization via focal loss and embedding distillation. The model achieves state-of-the-art results on five standard benchmarks, demonstrating that unified training overcomes the fragmentation caused by separate modality-specific encoders.
