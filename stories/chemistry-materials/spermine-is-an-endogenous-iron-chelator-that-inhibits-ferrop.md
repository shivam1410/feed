The enzyme ALDH18A1 drives a non-canonical metabolic pathway producing spermine in hepatocellular carcinoma cells. This spermine synthesis limits iron availability within cells and reduces lipid peroxidation, thereby inhibiting ferroptosis, a form of cell death triggered by iron-dependent lipid damage. Spermine acts as an endogenous iron chelator, revealing a cellular defense mechanism that may explain ferroptosis resistance in certain cancers.
