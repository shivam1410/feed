Single-molecule long-read sequencing of human sperm reveals that substantial portions of non-crossover gene conversions—a mechanism generating genetic diversity—occur before meiosis, not just during it. These pre-meiotic conversions associate with fragile chromosomal sites and vary across individual donors, indicating an overlooked source of genetic diversity in human reproduction. This finding reshapes understanding of how human genetic variation arises, showing meiosis isn't the sole source of recombination. Pre-meiotic conversions occur through distinct mechanisms from PRDM9-regulated recombination during meiosis.
