NIVA is a multimodal foundation model designed to learn coupled Earth system dynamics across atmosphere, ocean, ice, and land components. In proof-of-concept, researchers trained it on ocean-atmosphere interactions using large-scale Earth system simulations. The model accurately captures key climate variability modes and predicts major climate indices, advancing subseasonal-to-seasonal forecasting beyond the typical two-week prediction horizon.
