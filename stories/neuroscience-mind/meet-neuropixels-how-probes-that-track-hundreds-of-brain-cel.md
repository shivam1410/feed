Neuropixels are probes that track hundreds of brain cells at once. They work in rodents and in humans. The tools enable large-scale, single-cell neuronal recordings, which expose how the brain works inside. The article says these probes are revolutionizing neuroscience. That gives scientists a detailed view of the brain at the level of single cells.
