LiveAnimate enables real-time, long-form pose-driven human animation using a 14-billion-parameter video diffusion transformer with specialized training. The system employs bounded attention caching and sequence parallelism to achieve efficient streaming performance. This work addresses the technical challenge of generating realistic, continuous human animation in real time, enabling applications in interactive media, virtual production, and live streaming content creation.
