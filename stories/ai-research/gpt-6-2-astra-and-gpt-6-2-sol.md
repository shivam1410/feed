OpenAI's GPT-6.2 Astra costs $8 per million input tokens and $40 for output, 80% of the price of GPT-6 Astra. Cached input drops to $0.40 per million, down from $1. GPT-6.2 Sol stays at $2 and $10, half the price of GPT-5.6 Sol. Simon Willison tested both with his own prompts. Neither did a good job animating Yonkers as a mech, compared with Claude Fable 5.2 or Gemini 4 Carbon. He sees the cheaper cached input as good news for long agent sessions.
