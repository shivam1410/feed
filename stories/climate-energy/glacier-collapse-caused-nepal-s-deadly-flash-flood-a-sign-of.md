A glacier collapse in Nepal triggered a catastrophic flash flood, illustrating climate change's destabilizing effects on high-altitude mountain regions. Global warming weakens mountain stability through ice melting and altered precipitation patterns, significantly increasing the frequency of landslides and flooding in previously stable high-elevation areas. This tragic incident may signal the beginning of an intensifying pattern of more frequent catastrophic events as warming accelerates globally.
