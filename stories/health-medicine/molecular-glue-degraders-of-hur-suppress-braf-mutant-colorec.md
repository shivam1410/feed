Molecular glue degraders—small molecules forcing selective protein degradation—show therapeutic potential against BRAF-mutant colorectal cancers resistant to standard treatments. These compounds target the RNA-binding protein HuR, which is frequently overexpressed and essential for cancer cell survival in BRAF-mutant tumors. The glue degraders induce chemical interaction between HuR and cellular degradation machinery, eliminating the protein. This approach represents an emerging drug strategy exploiting the ubiquitin-proteasome system to degrade disease-causing proteins previously considered undruggable. Success with HuR degraders could expand this method to other RNA-binding proteins implicated in cancer or neurological disease.
