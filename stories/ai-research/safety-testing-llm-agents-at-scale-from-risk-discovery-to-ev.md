Vera is an automated safety testing framework using a three-stage pipeline: structured risk taxonomies identify potential failures, combinatorial case generation produces test scenarios, and adaptive sandbox execution verifies risks with evidence-based assessment. The approach scales systematic safety evaluation of LLM agents across diverse failure modes before deployment.
