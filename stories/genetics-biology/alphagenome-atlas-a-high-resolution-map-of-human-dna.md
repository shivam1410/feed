DeepMind launched AlphaGenome Atlas, a database that predicts DNA effects. It estimates the effect of every possible single nucleotide variant in the human genome. A single nucleotide variant is a one-letter change in the DNA sequence. The text is brief and gives no accuracy figures. It matters because researchers can look up predicted effects of single-letter DNA changes.
