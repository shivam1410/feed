Bacterial ribosomes can synthesize proteins encoded within their own ribosomal RNA, a self-referential capability challenging conventional genetic code understanding. Normally ribosomes translate messenger RNA into proteins, with ribosomal RNA serving only structural and catalytic roles. This discovery reveals ribosomal RNA functioning simultaneously as catalyst and genetic template, suggesting more complex relationships between RNA molecules and protein synthesis machinery. The finding may illuminate fundamental aspects of early molecular evolution and life's origins.
