Scientists achieved highly efficient base editing at the PCSK9 gene while maintaining normal human embryo development, advancing genome-editing technology. Base editing represents a precise gene-modification technique distinct from traditional CRISPR-Cas9 methods. The success at PCSK9 suggests this approach could target genes involved in disease without disrupting normal developmental processes. This matters because PCSK9 controls cholesterol levels, and this breakthrough demonstrates base editing's potential for preventing hereditary cardiovascular disease while establishing safety parameters for early human development.
