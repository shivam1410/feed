Using single-nucleus multi-omic sequencing, researchers found genetically diverse autism spectrum disorder mutations converge on common disruptions during early brain development. Despite different genetic starting points—various ASD-linked genes—mutations cause transient, stage-specific disturbances in how developing cortex forms during critical windows. Analysis revealed sex-specific differences in gene expression between males and females carrying ASD mutations. This convergence suggests autism arises from shared developmental mechanisms rather than independent genetic pathways, guiding intervention strategies targeting common disrupted developmental processes.
