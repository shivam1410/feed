Bacterial ribosomes possess the capability to synthesize proteins encoded within their own ribosomal RNA sequences, a remarkable self-referential genetic system. This ability reveals unexpected flexibility in genetic information expression and suggests ribosomes can produce their own structural components. The discovery indicates an ancient regulatory mechanism potentially fundamental to understanding cellular machinery evolution.
