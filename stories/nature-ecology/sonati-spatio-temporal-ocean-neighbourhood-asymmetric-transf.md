SONATI is a transformer that fills gaps in sparse ocean measurement tables. It works on local time and space neighbourhoods, which cuts compute compared with global attention. It reports uncertainty using Monte Carlo dropout. On North Atlantic data covering six ocean parameters, it beat classical and transformer methods by at least 12.5% in RMSE. Evaluation used gridded data only, so unstructured data remains untested.
