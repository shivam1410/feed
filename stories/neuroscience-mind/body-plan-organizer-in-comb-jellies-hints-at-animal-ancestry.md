Transplantation experiments across evolutionarily distant animal groups reveal conserved developmental mechanisms that organize body-plan formation. These experiments identify shared molecular principles underlying tissue organization during development. The conservation of these mechanisms across diverse species illuminates fundamental principles of animal evolution and development.
