OpenAI unveiled Jalapeño, a custom inference chip outperforming NVIDIA's GB200/GB300 on real workloads. Rated at 700W but running ~550W in testing, it delivers 1.5–1.9× better work per watt and 1.7–3.6× lower end-to-end latency. Interactive workloads achieve 2.1–4.1× higher performance. Deployment into OpenAI's infrastructure begins year-end with Gen 2 and 3 in development. This shift toward custom silicon reflects the industry's transition from GPU scaling to application-specific performance-per-watt optimization, with implications for AI infrastructure costs.
