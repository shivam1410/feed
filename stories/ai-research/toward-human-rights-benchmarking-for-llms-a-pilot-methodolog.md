Researchers developed HumRightsBench, the first expert-validated benchmark evaluating whether LLMs correctly reason about international human rights law. Adapting legal IRAC framework to IRAP (substituting remedy proposals for conclusions), the benchmark includes authentic scenarios annotated by human rights lawyers worldwide. Evaluation across models shows highly variable performance: overall accuracy ranges 0.339–0.577, with task-specific ranges 0.025–0.774, revealing significant reasoning gaps. This matters because LLMs increasingly mediate legal determinations affecting human rights, yet no standard benchmark existed to assess their reasoning about this essential domain.
