A study of 2,383 GRE test-takers found AI tutoring produced equivalent learning gains to expert human tutoring. In five of seven domains, the best AI tutor outperformed humans. One AI system achieved human-level learning outcomes at 918 times lower cost, $0.0052 versus $4.81 per percentage point gained. This suggests AI can democratize expert-level tutoring.
