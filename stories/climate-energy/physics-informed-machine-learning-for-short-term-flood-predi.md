A physics-informed LSTM adds Trend Alignment constraints, penalizing directional inconsistencies between precipitation and discharge. Testing on flood data shows the model improves Nash-Sutcliffe Efficiency from 0.20 to 0.23 using only 5% training data. Under extreme climate simulations, the baseline LSTM becomes unstable while the physics-informed model maintains directional consistency. This hybrid approach achieves physically plausible predictions in data-scarce and extreme-weather settings.
