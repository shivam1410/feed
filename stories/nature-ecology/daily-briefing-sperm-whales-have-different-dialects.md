Sperm whales in different Mediterranean regions communicate using distinct dialects—varying patterns of clicks and pauses that differ geographically. This discovery reveals that these marine mammals possess cultural differences in acoustic communication, similar to how human languages vary by region. The findings suggest cognitive complexity and social transmission of learned communication patterns within populations. Understanding these geographic dialects provides insights into whale behavior, social structures, and how populations identify and interact with each other in ocean environments.
