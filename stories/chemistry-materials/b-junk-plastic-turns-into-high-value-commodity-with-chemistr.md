Researchers have developed chemistry to convert polyvinyl chloride plastic waste into high-grade motor lubricant, creating significant economic value from discarded junk plastic. In parallel work, engineered yeast strains are helping manufacture a complex cancer drug. These developments demonstrate chemical innovation creating value from waste streams. This matters because converting waste plastic into commercially valuable lubricants makes plastic recycling economically attractive for companies, providing incentive for waste collection while enabling sustainable production of valuable industrial products.
