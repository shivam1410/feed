A modular medical imaging agent outperforms end-to-end vision-language models on spatial reasoning tasks in CT scans by decomposing the problem into three steps: parsing the query, localizing anatomical structures, and applying geometric rules. This structured approach improves reliability and auditability—clinicians can verify each reasoning step. Current AI systems struggle with precise spatial relationships in medical images; modular decomposition enables both better accuracy and explainability for clinical decision support.
