Several start-up companies are actively developing novel medical therapies using a new approach: targeting specific epigenetic markers rather than modifying the DNA sequence itself. These experimental treatments, currently in clinical testing, aim to treat diverse conditions ranging from high cholesterol to rare muscular disorders. Epigenetic markers are chemical switches that control whether genes are turned on or off without changing the underlying genetic code. This approach matters because many diseases stem from epigenetic dysregulation, and drugs targeting these reversible chemical modifications could provide effective treatments for conditions that traditional genetic therapies cannot address.
