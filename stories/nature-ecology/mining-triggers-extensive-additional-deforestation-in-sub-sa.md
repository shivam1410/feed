Mining operations in sub-Saharan Africa are driving extensive deforestation of dense tropical forests, with extraction of energy transition minerals like cobalt playing a particularly significant role. This reveals a hidden environmental cost within the clean energy transition—the very minerals essential for electric vehicles and renewable energy systems directly cause destruction of biodiverse intact ecosystems. The research highlights a fundamental paradox: pursuing sustainability in energy generation comes at the cost of forest ecosystem destruction. Addressing this tradeoff requires careful environmental planning and alternative sourcing strategies.
