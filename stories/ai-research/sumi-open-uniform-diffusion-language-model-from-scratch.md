Sumi is a large-scale diffusion language model trained from scratch using uniform diffusion, demonstrating an alternative approach to the dominant autoregressive paradigm. The model achieves competitive performance on knowledge and reasoning tasks compared to traditional autoregressive models. However, analysis reveals notable differences in commonsense reasoning abilities between the diffusion and autoregressive approaches.
