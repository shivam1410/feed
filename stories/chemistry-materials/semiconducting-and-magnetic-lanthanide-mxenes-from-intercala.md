A novel bottom-up synthesis strategy creates lanthanide MXenes (Ln2CT2)—a new class of two-dimensional materials combining semiconducting behavior with ferromagnetism. By synthesizing from lanthanide intercalated halides, researchers produced materials that exhibit both semiconducting properties and strong magnetic order. This synthesis approach enables controlled creation of two-dimensional materials that integrate contradictory electronic and magnetic properties. The combination of semiconducting behavior and ferromagnetism opens promising applications in spintronic devices, which exploit electron spin for information processing. These lanthanide MXenes expand the materials palette for next-generation electronics and demonstrate how rational design can yield two-dimensional solids with tailored multifunctional properties.
