Push-Wiper reformulates viscous stain cleaning as an aggregation problem, using segmented pushing trajectories generated via Diffusion Policy and executed through hybrid force-position control. Achieves cleaning scores up to 130% higher than baselines. Transfers zero-shot to solid residues, liquid spills, unseen viscous stains, and curved surfaces with varying geometries—all without additional training. The method uses a sponge to progressively gather stains through adaptive pushing sequences, demonstrating strong generalization for practical robotic surface cleaning across diverse scenarios.
