Researchers fused SMILES text representations and graph neural networks via cross-attention to predict drug toxicity while preserving graph-based explanations. With only 0.4 million parameters, it scores 0.987 AUC and 0.906 F1 on clinical toxicity. The explicit graph branch lets researchers see which molecular structures raise toxicity flags. This matters because drug discovery needs predictions you can trust and explain to your team.
