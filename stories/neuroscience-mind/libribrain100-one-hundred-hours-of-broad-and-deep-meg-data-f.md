LibriBrain100 demonstrates that extensive within-subject MEG recordings unlock improved neural speech decoding at scale. The dataset captures 100 hours of broad and deep brain activity during speech tasks, with improvements driven by both rich single-subject data and multi-subject supervised fine-tuning of pre-trained models. This approach scales speech-to-brain decoding beyond smaller datasets, advancing brain-computer interfaces for speech restoration and research.
