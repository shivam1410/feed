Biologists have constructed an updated phylogenetic tree of bats—a comprehensive evolutionary family tree—incorporating newly assembled genome sequences from modern bat species alongside analysis of numerous ancient fossils. The updated phylogeny includes all known bat families and provides refined understanding of how different bat lineages are related to one another. By combining genomic data with paleontological evidence, researchers resolved longstanding questions about bat evolutionary relationships and the timing of major splits in the bat family tree. The fossil record provided crucial temporal anchoring, allowing scientists to date divergence events more accurately. This comprehensive phylogenetic framework advances our understanding of bat evolution, diversification, and biogeography, offering insights into how the diverse array of bat species emerged over millions of years.
