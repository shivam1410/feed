Since 2022, each year a new component of the AI training pipeline flipped from human-designed to model-generated—reward models first, then synthetic data, rubrics, and entire environments. Synthetic approaches now deliver 10% lower quality but 100× cheaper and 10,000× faster than human equivalents. This shift explains recent capability jumps and suggests simulation and synthetic generation are now primary scaling levers, not human annotation.
