Researchers discovered an enormous accumulation of whale remains in the Diamantina Zone of the southeastern Indian Ocean, dated to 5.3 million years ago. This deep-sea whale necropolis reveals complex, specialized ecosystems sustained exclusively by whale carcasses and provides extensive fossil records illuminating ancient deep-ocean biodiversity and whale evolutionary history. The discovery demonstrates how episodic inputs of large organic material from surface waters sustain deep-sea communities across millions of years.
