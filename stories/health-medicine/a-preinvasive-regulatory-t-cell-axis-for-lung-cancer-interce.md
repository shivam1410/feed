Scientists found a regulatory T cell circuit that predicts lung cancer development and can be detected in blood samples. Blocking this immune circuit in the preinvasive stage reduced both tumor incidence and size. Early intervention based on this marker could prevent cancer from forming rather than catching it after diagnosis.
