Two teams used AI to find major flaws in PHP, which runs much of the web. Ryan Austin of Calif found one in August 2026 through OpenAI's Patch the Planet project, using GPT-5.6 Sol. Wiz, now part of Google Cloud, found the other using Gemini 4 Argon, GPT-5.6 Sol and its own tool. The writer notes that disclosures carry some marketing, and how severe the bugs are is debated. Even so, they expect at least a year of similar finds. That is a good sign, since core software is getting its biggest audit ever.
