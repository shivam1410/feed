Multi-component co-evolution enables agentic systems to achieve open-ended improvement by progressively removing fixed human constraints across agents, environments, and evolution mechanisms. The approach reduces human-imposed design boundaries as systems improve. Self-directed evolution enables continuous refinement. This matters because it suggests systems could improve autonomously while remaining aligned with intended objectives.
