Bumblebees are incorporating plastic into their honeycomb structures, revealing extraordinary adaptability in their building strategies. Rather than relying solely on natural materials, these insects can use unfamiliar construction supplies to build honey-storage vessels when conventional resources are scarce. This flexibility demonstrates how insects can dramatically shift their problem-solving approaches in response to environmental changes, suggesting remarkable resilience when traditional materials become unavailable.
