A survey of security leaders shows growing trust in AI agents. In an a16z survey of CISOs at organizations with more than 1,000 employees, 36% said they are comfortable letting AI agents act autonomously on low-risk security actions today. Some 60% expect to be comfortable at that level in 12 months. AI SOC ranked as the top initiative for 2027. It matters because security teams are about to give agents much more authority.
