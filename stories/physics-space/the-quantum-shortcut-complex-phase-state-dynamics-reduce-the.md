Complex-valued hidden states inspired by quantum theory train sequence models faster than real-valued states. At 253M parameters matched to within 0.02%, complex models reach every validation loss in approximately one-third (state-space) to one-half (attention) of optimization steps of real counterparts. The advantage continues widening after learning-rate warmup from 0.321 to 0.354 bits per character. Phase-based information representation and Born-form readouts, though requiring relaxation of strict unitarity, unlock faster training without architectural changes to existing Mamba or Transformer backbones.
