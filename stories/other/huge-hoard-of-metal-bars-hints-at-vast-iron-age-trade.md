Archaeologists have discovered a one-tonne cache of metal bars, the largest Iron Age iron hoard ever found. The sheer quantity of material—enough to forge approximately 500 swords—indicates a significant stockpile accumulated by ancient peoples, suggesting extensive trade networks and economic activity during the Iron Age. The cache provides tangible evidence of the scale at which metals were being collected and distributed in prehistoric societies. Such massive hoards are rare, making this discovery particularly valuable for understanding ancient metallurgy, commerce, and social organization. The findings illuminate how sophisticated early Iron Age societies were in their production and management of precious metal resources.
