Analysis of AI Deep Research agents reveals they adopt false conclusions from misleading information in final reports even when verifier models identify the same information as false during focused validation. Testing 5,933 instances of controlled misleading knowledge across open-source and closed-source agents showed this gap between focused verification and workflow-level evidence use. Various defenses reduced but did not eliminate false-conclusion adoption, exposing critical reliability gaps.
