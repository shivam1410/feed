Neuroscientist Shaira Berg integrates scientific research with artistic expression to explore trauma. Rather than approaching neuroscience through conventional academic channels, she employs visual and cinematic techniques alongside rigorous scientific methods. This interdisciplinary approach enabled Berg to discover her unique niche at the intersection of neuroscience and art, combining the precision of scientific inquiry with the communicative power of artistic media.
