Artificial intelligence-designed synthetic protein assemblies deliver RNA into cells more effectively than natural viral vectors by overcoming evolutionary constraints limiting viral development. These engineered virus-like particles allow optimization beyond evolution's solutions. Superior delivery efficiency could enhance gene therapy applications, reducing required doses and improving treatment efficacy. The achievement demonstrates how computational protein design combined with machine learning creates biological tools exceeding nature's capabilities, opening possibilities for improved therapeutic delivery platforms and safer treatments.
