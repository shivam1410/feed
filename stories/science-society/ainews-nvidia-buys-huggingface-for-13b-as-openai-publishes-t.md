NVIDIA announced acquisition of HuggingFace for $13 billion, representing approximately 80 times HuggingFace's reported $150 million annual recurring revenue and significantly above NVIDIA's initial $7 billion offer from January 2026. The deal signals NVIDIA's strategic bet on open-source AI infrastructure. Simultaneously, Z.ai launched GLM-5.3-Flash, a natively multimodal model with 1-million-token context window, 320 billion total parameters (18 billion active), released under MIT License. Z.ai positioned GLM-5.3-Flash as competitively priced with performance competitive with larger closed-source models on coding benchmarks.
