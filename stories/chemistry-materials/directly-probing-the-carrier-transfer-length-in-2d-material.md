Cross-sectional scanning tunneling microscopy measured a 2-nanometer carrier transfer length in bismuth-contacted monolayer MoS2 transistors, directly determining fundamental scaling limits for sub-10 nanometer 2D electronic devices. This 2 nm boundary marks where device physics transitions from classical to quantum regimes, establishing critical design constraints. For semiconductor manufacturers, the finding shows that metal contacts cannot scale much smaller without confronting quantum effects that degrade performance. This measurement defines realistic physical boundaries for next-generation chip miniaturization.
