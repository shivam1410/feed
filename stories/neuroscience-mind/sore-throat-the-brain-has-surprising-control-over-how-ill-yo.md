Your brain may have more say over how sick you feel than you'd think. It might decode pathogens, the germs that make you ill. It could also store memories of past illnesses. Then it may prepare the body for threats it has seen before. The text frames this as a possibility, not a settled fact. If it holds up, it could change how we think about symptoms like a sore throat.
