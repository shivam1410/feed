ArchEGraph is a benchmark dataset containing 5,481 buildings with 49,326 validated building-weather simulation cases, representing buildings as heterogeneous graphs with aligned geometry, topology, weather, and zone-level thermal loads. The dataset includes over 133,000 space nodes and 1.44 million face nodes reflecting realistic geometric and topological complexity. Two benchmark tasks are defined: graph reconstruction from polygonal meshes to recover topological structure, and topology-informed load prediction using graph structure and temporal weather to forecast zone-level thermal responses. Cross-building and cross-climate generalization experiments assess model robustness across diverse scenarios. This testbed enables ML research on geometry-topology-physics coupling, providing architects rapid design feedback for carbon-neutral buildings.
