Computational reconstruction of the last eukaryotic common ancestor's proteome reveals horizontal gene transfer from diverse bacteria and viruses shaped eukaryogenesis. Early eukaryotes acquired substantial genetic material from multiple prokaryotic sources beyond archaeal ancestors, indicating complex interactions among ancient cell lineages. This reshapes understanding of eukaryotic origins.
