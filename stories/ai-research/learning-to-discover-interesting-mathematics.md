Researchers trained an AI to discover interesting new mathematics instead of just solving existing problems. They defined a theorem's interestingness by the ratio of how long its proof is to how long the statement is, finding this predicts real mathematical utility. The system can now generate novel theorems, pick the most interesting ones, and build a self-expanding verified math library with 70 percent more original discoveries than standard approaches.
