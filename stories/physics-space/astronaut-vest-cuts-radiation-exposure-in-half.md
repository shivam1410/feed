An astronaut vest tested on an uncrewed NASA mission demonstrated a 50% reduction in radiation exposure during solar events. The garment shields wearers from acute radiation effects encountered beyond Earth's protective magnetic field. This protective technology matters because solar radiation is a major limiting factor for long-duration human spaceflight, potentially enabling extended missions and improving crew safety for deep space exploration.
