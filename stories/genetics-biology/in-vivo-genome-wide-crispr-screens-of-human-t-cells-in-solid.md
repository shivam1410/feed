An in vivo model successfully recovers human T cells from within solid tumors, enabling genome-wide CRISPR screening in this clinically challenging environment. By identifying genes enhancing T cell antitumor activity when disrupted, the approach pinpoints therapeutic targets for improving CAR T effectiveness against solid cancers. This tests candidates directly within the tumor microenvironment where they must function, bridging a critical gap in cancer immunotherapy development.
