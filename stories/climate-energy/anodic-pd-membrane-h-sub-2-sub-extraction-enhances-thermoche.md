An electrochemical cell with molten hydroxide electrolyte and palladium membrane actively extracts hydrogen, enhancing ammonia and methylcyclohexane dehydrogenation reactions. The Pd membrane selectively permeates H₂, driving equilibrium toward product formation and simultaneously delivering purified hydrogen. This approach boosts conversion efficiency while producing ready-to-use hydrogen fuel. The technique demonstrates how electrochemical hydrogen extraction can drive thermochemical processes forward, addressing dual challenges: improving chemical conversion and producing clean hydrogen for energy applications.
