Hypernetworks can inject factual knowledge at train-time by generating LoRA adapters that enable LLMs to answer questions about large fact corpora. This decouples injection capacity from model capability, enabling the first rigorous study of hypernetwork scaling laws. The MegaWikiQA dataset contains tens of millions of multi-hop question-answer examples across 39 Wikidata5M domains. Results reveal hypernetwork-based injection exhibits predictive power-law scaling along depth, width, and target-network-size axes. Hypernetworks achieve reliable out-of-distribution generalization at increasing scales, suggesting systematic, scalable approaches to knowledge injection independent of general model capability.
