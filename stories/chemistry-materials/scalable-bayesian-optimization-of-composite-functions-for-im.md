Scalable Bayesian optimization of composite functions (SBOCF) efficiently estimates physical parameters from scientific images in materials characterization. Applied to PACBED patterns used to determine specimen thickness and crystal mistilt in electron microscopy, SBOCF exploits known composite structure of the image-matching objective and intermediate information in simulated images. The method reduces modeled outputs from 24,649 to 11 using patch-level summaries and correction terms. With a 50 simulator evaluation budget, SBOCF dramatically outperformed standard Bayesian optimization, reducing median final sum-of-squared-error by up to 290 times in thick-sample cases. On experimental data, SBOCF produced parameter estimates consistent with previously reported values.
