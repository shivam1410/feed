Researchers built Gan Jiang, a self-learning AI agent for analyzing powder X-ray diffraction data. The system learns from past experience, diagnoses failed analyses, revises its methods, and validates improvements without retraining. On standard benchmarks, it achieved single-phase identification accuracies of 96.30% on MP500, 81.78% on RRUFF, and 40.83% on experimental data, outperforming comparable methods by 20-50 percentage points. This matters because it shows AI agents can build reusable expertise from scientific experience without human intervention.
