Capek 0.5 is a vision-language model designed for embodied robots that organizes learning around execution-centric capabilities: Spatial Reasoning, Temporal Understanding, Action Guidance, and State Verification. The model uses specialized expert networks trained via reinforcement learning, then consolidates them through weight-space merging and routed policy-space distillation. Evaluated at 2B and 35B-A3B scales, the approach enables robots to perceive, reason about, and act on dynamic environments iteratively. This architecture better integrates perception and action than task-specific approaches.
