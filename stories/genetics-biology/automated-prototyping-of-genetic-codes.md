A robotic, cell-free platform enables rapid prototyping of redesigned genetic codes by synthesizing proteins with reassigned codons and non-standard amino acids without altering living organisms' genomes. The automated system accelerates protein engineering by eliminating the need to modify living cells, allowing researchers to explore novel genetic variants and functions in controlled conditions.
