Scientists have created quantum statistical plasmonic metacrystals—engineered materials that control light transmission based exclusively on quantum coherence rather than conventional optical properties like wavelength. These materials generate selective bands of allowed and forbidden photon statistics according to quantum properties. Light transmission depends entirely on the quantum state of photons and their coherence properties. This represents a fundamental shift from wavelength-based optical filtering to quantum-property-based control mechanisms. The breakthrough enables entirely new possibilities for quantum-enhanced photonics and advanced light-matter interactions. Potential applications include quantum information processing, quantum sensing technologies, and experimental platforms where quantum coherence and photon statistics are critical for performance.
