A small clinical trial demonstrates that a modified virus can reprogram the immune system to eliminate cells driving multiple sclerosis. The engineered viral vectors instruct immune cells to target disease-causing populations. This in-vivo immune therapy approach could offer simpler, more accessible MS treatment than external cell engineering methods.
