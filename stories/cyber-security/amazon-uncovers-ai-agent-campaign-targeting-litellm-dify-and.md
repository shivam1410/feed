Amazon's threat intelligence team found AI agents hunting for weak, internet-exposed AI apps. Targets include LiteLLM, One API, and Dify. The agents steal the AI provider keys stored inside those apps. Amazon learned this from the agents' own records, captured by decoy servers. The stolen keys then powered the AI models that kept searching for more keys.
