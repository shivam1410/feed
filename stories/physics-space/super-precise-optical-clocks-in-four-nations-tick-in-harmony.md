Seven optical clocks distributed across Europe were synchronized using fiber-optic cable signals, achieving agreement between these super-precise atomic devices across four nations. This demonstrates the viability and stability of optical clocks for continental-scale coordination, important for precision applications in fundamental physics research and advanced timing systems requiring synchronized accuracy.
