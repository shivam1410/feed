Over 1,000 employees from frontier labs (OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Meta, others—excepting X.ai) cosigned a statement calling for deliberate pacing of frontier AI development. The letter argues AI risks rapid capability advances outpacing human understanding and control. The signers emphasize companies face intense competitive pressure not to unilaterally slow progress and lack international governance tools to deliberately pace development together. They request U.S. government support for an international effort to develop technical and governance measures enabling deliberate pacing. This represents the first major coordinated concern from frontier insiders about capability development speed relative to safety measures. Unlike Musk and Bengio's 2023 pause proposal, this comes from current frontier lab staff, lending credibility to near-term capability acceleration concerns.
