Nancy Grace Roman, honored as 'Mother of Hubble,' spearheaded pioneering programs as NASA's first chief astronomer, ushering in a new era of space-based astronomical science. Her visionary leadership laid groundwork for revolutionary space telescopes and observational strategies. Roman championed ambitious projects and advocated for bold scientific approaches at a time when women held few leadership roles in NASA and astronomy. Her legacy encompasses both the scientific achievements enabled by her programs and her role as a trailblazer expanding opportunities in astronomical research and inspiring future generations.
