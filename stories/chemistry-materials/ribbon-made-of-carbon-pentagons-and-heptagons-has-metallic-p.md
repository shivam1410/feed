Researchers made a carbon ribbon built only from five- and seven-carbon rings. It conducts electrons with metal-like ease. They built it by linking chains of paired five-membered rings that were previously out of reach. That provides a route to highly conducting carbon nanoribbons. It matters because it gives a new way to make such materials.
