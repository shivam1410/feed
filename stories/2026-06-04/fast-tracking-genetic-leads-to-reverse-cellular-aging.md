DeepMind's Co-Scientist—an LLM agent that combines language models with biological knowledge bases—identified genetic factors that successfully rejuvenate human cells. Rather than manually reviewing thousands of aging studies, researchers provided Co-Scientist with cellular aging data, and the agent generated and tested novel hypotheses about rejuvenation factors. This exemplifies how LLM agents accelerate biological discovery by synthesizing scattered knowledge across publications and proposing experimentally-testable predictions. The "fast-tracking" suggests dramatically compressed timelines compared to traditional literature review. Relevant to both LLM agent applications in biology and deeper understanding of protein-level cellular aging mechanisms.
