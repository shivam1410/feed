Researchers developed an automated, cell-free robotic platform for rapid prototyping of redesigned genetic codes. The system enables creation of proteins using reassigned genetic codons and non-standard amino acids without modifying living organisms' genomes. This approach accelerates the discovery and optimization of novel proteins with tailored properties. The technology bridges synthetic biology and protein engineering, enabling faster iteration cycles for designing proteins with custom functions, potentially unlocking new therapeutic and industrial applications.
