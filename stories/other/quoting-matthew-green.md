Cryptographer Matthew Green observes that AI is gaining proficiency in cryptanalysis precisely during a historic transition from RSA/EC to post-quantum cryptography. Green frames this timing as potentially beneficial: robust AI-driven cryptanalysis could validate new cryptographic problems through thorough evaluation, while the same capability risks undermining algorithms if vulnerabilities exist.
