Electric field measurements of the deuterium nucleus—a stable isotope with one proton and one neutron—show no detectable evidence of asymmetry in nuclear structure. The nucleus responds symmetrically to applied electric fields, matching predictions from established particle physics theory and the Standard Model. This result validates current understanding of nuclear physics and confirms quantum mechanics accurately describes nuclear behavior at fundamental levels. The precision measurements demonstrate that theoretical models correctly predict how nuclear structures respond to electromagnetic influences.
