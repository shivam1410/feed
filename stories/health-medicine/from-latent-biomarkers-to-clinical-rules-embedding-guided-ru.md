A pipeline mines decision rules in the latent space of neural networks, then translates them into measurable clinical features. Tested on six clinical datasets, embedding-space rules outperformed raw-feature rules in five datasets with AUROC gains of 0.04–0.23. However, translation doesn't always preserve latent rule quality—one model dropped from 0.98 to 0.72 AUROC—showing that interpretability and accuracy trade differently.
