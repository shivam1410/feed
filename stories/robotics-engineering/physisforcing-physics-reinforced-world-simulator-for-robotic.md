PhysisForcing improves AI models that simulate the physical world for robotic training by enforcing realistic physics constraints during the learning process. The system uses a diffusion transformer framework enhanced with two complementary alignment mechanisms: pixel-level trajectory alignment ensures generated videos show physically plausible object movements, while semantic-level relational alignment preserves correct physical relationships between objects. By combining these constraints, the model produces more realistic embodied video predictions. This matters because accurate world simulation is essential for training robot policies in simulation before deployment in the real world, reducing costly real-world trial-and-error and enabling safer, more efficient robot learning across manipulation tasks.
