A peer-reviewed study published in Nature shows that AMIE, Google's conversational medical AI, performs equally well as human primary care physicians when managing complex chronic diseases. The research tested the AI system on real medical cases with actual symptoms and complications. This groundbreaking result validates AI as a genuine tool for extending medical expertise globally, especially in resource-limited regions facing severe doctor shortages.
