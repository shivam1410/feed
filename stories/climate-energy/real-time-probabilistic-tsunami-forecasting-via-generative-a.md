A conditional diffusion model provides real-time probabilistic tsunami forecasting with calibrated uncertainty quantification. Validated against 2011 Tohoku-oki earthquake data, the generative AI approach faithfully tracks decreasing post-earthquake uncertainty while accurately predicting inundation depth and extent. Shifts forecasting from determinism to probabilism, addressing the critical failure of deterministic predictions that falsely imply safety outside boundaries. Enables next-generation early warning systems that honestly quantify risk rather than provide false certainty for public awareness.
