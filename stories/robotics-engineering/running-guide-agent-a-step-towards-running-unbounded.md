A new AI agent provides real-time audio navigation and live obstacle detection to enable athletes who are blind or low-vision (BLV) to run independently without sighted guides. The system combines dynamic audio directional guidance with immediate hazard detection and alerts, removing accessibility barriers that typically require external assistance or pre-planned safe routes. Real-time processing allows athletes to navigate unfamiliar or changing routes while the AI communicates hazards and directions through audio feedback. This application demonstrates AI's potential to directly address accessibility barriers in physical activities and recreation. Independent running capability could significantly improve quality of life, athletic participation, and confidence for BLV individuals. The technology represents a practical accessibility application.
