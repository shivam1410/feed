Scientists created a massive population-scale map showing chromatin accessibility and gene activity patterns across many immune cell types. They traced these patterns back to genetic variants that cause disease, revealing exactly how genes increase disease susceptibility through changes in immune regulation. This comprehensive atlas bridges the previously mysterious gap between carrying a risky genetic variant and actually developing disease, with clear mechanistic connections that explain the link.
