OpenBioRQ is a benchmark for evaluating agentic AI models on unsolved biomedical research questions—cases where no definitive answer keys exist. Testing reveals significant failures in two critical areas: retrieval-grounded reasoning (correctly using retrieved sources) and tool usage (effectively applying research tools). These findings expose that agents struggle to verify sources and often fabricate citations. This matters because AI agents increasingly support scientific research, and false citations can propagate misinformation in academic communities.
