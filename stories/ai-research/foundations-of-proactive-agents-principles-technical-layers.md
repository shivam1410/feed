Researchers designed principles for proactive agents that assist before users ask. Their framework, called 3T, balances task capability, temporal allocation, and user trust. They built PROACTIVITY-GYM, a simulation testbed with 23 model-harness configurations and persona-conditioned simulated users, revealing that jointly optimizing all three principles matters more than any single one. Misaligned interventions, even correct ones, sharply eroded user trust.
