Google and industry partners dismantled a botnet of more than 1.3 million Android TV set-top boxes. Malware on the boxes rented out users' internet connections to strangers and ran ad fraud. Google's threat intelligence group says Chinese TV box maker Videostrong included the malware and was responsible for it. Experts say the takedown is likely to cut access to more than 10 million other compromised devices. The botnet was named XCPE.
