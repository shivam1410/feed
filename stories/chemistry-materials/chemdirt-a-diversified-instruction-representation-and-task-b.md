ChemDIRT reveals that chemistry language models are highly vulnerable to how problems are presented. The benchmark tests eight chemistry task categories with variations in instruction wording and molecular representation formats (SMILES, graphs, text). Models show substantial prompt sensitivity, representation dependence, and uneven performance across task families. This matters because existing single-format benchmarks overestimate true reasoning capability—models succeeding on one phrasing or notation may fail on semantically equivalent alternatives, limiting real-world reliability in chemistry applications and drug discovery.
