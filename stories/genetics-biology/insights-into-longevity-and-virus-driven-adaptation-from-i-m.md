Comparative and functional genomics of Myotis bats reveal unique adaptations in proteins that interact with viruses and pathways linked to longevity. The research connects two exceptional features of bat biology—their resistance to viral infection and their extended lifespans—through shared genetic mechanisms. These insights may illuminate how some species achieve disease tolerance and extended survival.
