CalTwin addresses reliability threats to medical world models in clinical deployment: covariate shift from fragmented training data across hospitals and scanners, and confidence misalignment where multi-step forecasts overestimate certainty where clinical risk is highest. The lightweight regularization combines Fisher-Information-based shift penalty with confidence misalignment penalty, applied to a GRU-based medical world model's latent transition predictor. Proof steps transfer from classification settings or require adaptation. Evaluation on PhysioNet 2019 Sepsis Challenge demonstrates improved robustness.
