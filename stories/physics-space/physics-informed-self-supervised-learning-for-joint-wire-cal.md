A self-supervised method calibrates scientific particle detectors and reconstructs where particles hit using only geometry and charge consistency, no labeled training data. Tested on real detector systems, it converges stably and improves spatial uniformity. The neural network learns the actual detector response instead of assuming math models. This matters because detectors age and conditions drift, so continuous self-correction keeps equipment accurate and operational.
