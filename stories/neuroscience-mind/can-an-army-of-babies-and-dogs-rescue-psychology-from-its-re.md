Multiple cognitive science laboratories are coordinating and collaborating on large-scale, multilab research projects specifically designed to bring greater scientific rigor to psychology and cognitive research. These extensive team efforts aim to directly address the widespread reproducibility crisis and methodological challenges that have significantly undermined confidence in psychological findings and theories. The coordinated initiatives pool resources and expertise to test fundamental cognitive hypotheses with rigorous, well-powered studies. Why it matters: Systematically addressing psychology's reproducibility crisis through collaborative, highly rigorous, large-scale multilab studies directly strengthens the validity and reliability of psychological science, rebuilds public and scientific confidence in cognitive research findings, and ultimately advances our fundamental understanding of human behavior and mental processes.
