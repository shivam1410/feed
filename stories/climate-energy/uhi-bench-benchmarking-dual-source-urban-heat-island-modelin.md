UHI-Bench introduces the first comprehensive benchmark for dual-source urban heat island modeling, evaluating 20+ baselines across four model families on five tasks spanning 20 cities and nine Köppen climate classes. Cross-city transferability depends more on overlap in UHI regimes than climate zone similarity. Foundation models proved consistently competitive.
