Distinct herpesviruses and anelloviruses chronically reactivate during acute and long COVID-19, with reactivation patterns tracking disease severity, inflammation, and clinical outcomes. These immune signatures may predict disease course and stratify patients, suggesting viral reactivation is a pathological hallmark with prognostic value.
