Farming is reshaping one of the most biodiverse places on Earth. Researchers assessed around 40,000 species in Sumatra, Indonesia, from soil microbes to vertebrates. Intensified agriculture causes species loss and shifts which species live together. These effects show up at several spatial scales. Global demand for agricultural output has grown for decades. That makes this a clear warning for planning land use in biodiverse tropical regions.
