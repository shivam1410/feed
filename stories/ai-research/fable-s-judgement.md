Better results come from letting Fable apply its own judgment rather than dictating workflows. Instead of prescribing 'only use automated testing for larger features,' instruct Fable to decide when testing is appropriate. Similarly, delegate smaller tasks to lower-power models based on the agent's judgment about which model fits the scope. This approach reduces token waste and improves decision quality by leveraging contextual reasoning rather than imposing rigid rules, particularly valuable in the limited time before Fable token pricing increases.
