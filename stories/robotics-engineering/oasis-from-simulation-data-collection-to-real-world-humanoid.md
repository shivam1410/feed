OASIS trains humanoid robots for locomotion and manipulation using simulation data augmented with 3D generative models creating realistic assets. Hierarchical visuomotor policies trained on simulated data achieve better zero-shot real-world performance than training directly on limited real robot data. Simulation-to-reality success shows synthetic training data at scale can exceed limited real-world data, dramatically reducing expensive real-robot development and accelerating humanoid robotics progress.
