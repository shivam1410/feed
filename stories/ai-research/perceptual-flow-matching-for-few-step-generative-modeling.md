Perceptual Flow Matching achieves high-quality generation in few steps by performing flow matching in perceptual feature space rather than raw data space. This approach reduces sampling iterations while maintaining output quality and accuracy. The method dramatically decreases computational cost for generative modeling, making rapid inference practical for deployment scenarios.
