DeepMind created AlphaGenome Atlas, a comprehensive database that predicts the precise cellular effects of every single possible single-letter genetic change in the human genome. The database covers all genetic variants across the entire human genome in one unified and searchable resource. This comprehensive map could help researchers quickly identify disease risks, understand genetic contributions to illness, and discover new therapeutic drug targets.
