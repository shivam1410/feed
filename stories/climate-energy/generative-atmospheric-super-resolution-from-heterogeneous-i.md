A pretrained atmospheric diffusion model was conditioned on sparse, heterogeneous observations (radiosonde, aircraft, surface stations) through composable interfaces that translate each source into likelihood factors. The combined interface reduced RMSE against ERA5 by 9.24 percent across all 13 state variables over the US.
