This paper solves a stochastic control model for startups choosing between multiple activities with different costs and risk profiles to reach milestone targets. The optimal policy uses an efficient frontier curve ordering activities by two measures: riskiness (drift-to-volatility ratio) and cost-effectiveness (drift-to-cost ratio). The framework shows different frontier curve types yield qualitatively different optimal policies. This first-of-its-kind analysis of multi-control stochastic models provides startups with intuitive measures to optimize resource allocation across competing activities.
