Planets' orbits will destabilize when the Sun begins shedding mass, possibly within one billion years. This is far sooner than previously thought. New modeling shows the gravitational disruptions from a dying Sun could unravel our Solar System's delicate arrangement long before astronomers expected. This matters because it drastically shortens estimates for our planetary system's future.
