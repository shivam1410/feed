SyncWorld is an action-conditioned world model that learns to predict visual outcomes of actions in new environments through brief visual calibration episodes. Rather than requiring extensive task-specific training, the model rapidly adapts to environment-specific action-to-visual mappings, enabling zero-shot simulation of unseen robotic tasks and settings. The system supports test-time policy improvement by refining action policies within the learned environment model before deployment. This addresses the critical sim-to-real gap in robotics by enabling rapid visual model calibration in new physical settings without manual retraining.
