Anthropic raised $65B in Series H funding at $900B pre-money valuation, reaching $47B annualized revenue (up from $9B in December), surpassing OpenAI in headline metrics. Most significantly, Anthropic introduced dynamic workflows (ultracode) enabling parallel multi-agent orchestration in Claude Code; this feature reportedly enabled Bun's 750,000 line-of-code rewrite from Zig to Rust in just 6 days.
