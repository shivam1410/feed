Researchers have developed novel chemistry to convert discarded PVC plastic—a common waste stream—into high-grade motor lubricant through controlled chemical transformation. In parallel, engineered yeast strains can assist in producing certain cancer medications. These breakthroughs demonstrate how chemical innovation and synthetic biology enable value recovery from waste and accelerate pharmaceutical manufacturing. Converting plastic waste to functional lubricants addresses environmental contamination while generating economic value from discarded material. Engineered organism-based drug synthesis offers alternative manufacturing routes.
