Extensive mining operations in sub-Saharan Africa are driving large-scale deforestation of dense tropical forests. The mining activity is particularly driven by extraction of key energy transition minerals—especially cobalt—which is critical for lithium-ion batteries in electric vehicles, renewable energy storage systems, and grid stabilization. The scale of forest loss from mining expansion across the region is substantial and accelerating. Why it matters: Mining for minerals essential to the global green energy transition paradoxically causes significant environmental destruction through deforestation in biodiverse regions. This creates a critical conservation dilemma as the world attempts to pursue both rapid climate action and ecosystem protection simultaneously.
