A new interpretability method reveals that transformer hidden states' standard basis already functions as a powerful feature representation without requiring additional training. Individual dimensions encode semantic content through their sign, while magnitudes indicate confidence levels. Dimensions act as independent binary registers, enabling mechanistic interpretability across different architectures. This training-free approach works universally without needing learned rotations or optimization.
