Humanoid robots achieved impressive record-breaking performance in athletic competitions including sprints and similar speed-based events, demonstrating rapid advances in robotics technology. However, these same robots encountered significant difficulties with practical manual tasks requiring fine motor control and precision, such as hammering nails or other construction-related activities. These results reveal both current capabilities in controlled athletic environments and persistent limitations in real-world task execution.
