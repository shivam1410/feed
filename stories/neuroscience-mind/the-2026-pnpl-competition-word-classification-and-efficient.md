The 2026 PNPL Competition uses an expanded MEG brain-imaging dataset (LibriBrain100) for word classification tracks that evaluate cross-subject and within-subject generalization. Competitors aim to decode spoken words from non-invasive brain signals. This advances brain-computer interface research toward clinical viability by improving generalization across individuals and testing whether brain-decoding systems can work reliably for real medical applications.
