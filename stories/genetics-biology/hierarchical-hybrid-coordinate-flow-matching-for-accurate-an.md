Predicting a protein's bound shape from its unbound form matters for drug design. CrypticFlow combines two steps: Cartesian anchor points, then local internal-coordinate fill-ins. Its median C-alpha error is 1.333 angstroms across five training runs. That is the lowest reported at baseline capacity. Local geometry also improves, with 99.58% adjacent-distance fidelity. The predicted shapes stay physically plausible.
