A machine-learning model trained on thousands of electrocardiogram recordings has identified a previously unrecognized group of people at high risk for sudden cardiac death. The deep-learning approach detects patterns in ECG data that standard clinical analysis typically misses, revealing new cardiac risk factors invisible to conventional medical evaluation. This breakthrough in cardiac risk identification could enable early intervention, preventive treatment, and personalized management strategies for vulnerable individuals. The finding demonstrates how artificial intelligence can uncover hidden health vulnerabilities in large medical datasets, with potential to save lives through improved risk stratification.
