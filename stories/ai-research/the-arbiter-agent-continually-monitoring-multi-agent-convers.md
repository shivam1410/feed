The Arbiter Agent is a multi-agent system monitoring framework that detects emergent misalignment through real-time inspection while operating under resource constraints. The system demonstrated effective detection of various misalignment types across different conditions. This approach addresses a critical safety challenge: ensuring that multi-agent systems behave as intended even as they interact and evolve. The framework matters because as AI systems become increasingly complex and agents interact autonomously, the ability to monitor for and identify misalignment in real time becomes essential for maintaining safety and reliability in deployed multi-agent systems.
