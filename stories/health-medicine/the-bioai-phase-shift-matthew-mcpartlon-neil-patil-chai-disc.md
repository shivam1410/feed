AI × pharma crossed a threshold at January's JPM Pharma conference: multiple large AI tools deals announced, signaling pharma's trust in specialized drug-discovery AI. Historically, AI pharma tools companies struggled to convince firms to adopt tools without proof, typically pivoting to build own drug pipelines. Chai Discovery, a 2-year-old OpenAI-backed startup, reached $4B valuation amid broader shift. The change reflects that tools became sufficiently good that drug design teams trust them without requiring internal clinical validation. This matters because it unlocks sustainable software-tools business for AI+pharma, historically blocked by need to prove efficacy on concrete targets.
