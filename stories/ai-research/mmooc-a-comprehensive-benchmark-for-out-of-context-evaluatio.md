MMOOC is a large-scale benchmark that tests whether multimodal language models can simultaneously refuse out-of-context questions while correctly answering in-context ones. Testing across diverse multimodal scenarios, MMOOC reveals that current models struggle to balance these two abilities—they tend to either over-refuse or under-refuse based on context shifts. This exposure of context-sensitivity failures informs robustness improvements and guides safer multimodal model design.
