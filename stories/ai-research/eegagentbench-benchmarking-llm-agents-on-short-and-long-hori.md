A benchmark for testing LLM agents on EEG analysis across six applications, from short 2-second clips to 23-hour recordings. It includes ten standard tools and evaluated 29 frontier models from fifteen families. This matters because it measures how well agents reason about brain signals, use tools, and build multi-step workflows.
