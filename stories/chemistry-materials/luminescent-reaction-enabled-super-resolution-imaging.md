A new chemistry-based super-resolution imaging framework eliminates high-powered laser requirements while enabling three-dimensional, live-cell imaging with high sensitivity and extended observation duration. This luminescent-reaction-based approach overcomes spatiotemporal resolution limitations of traditional reaction-based imaging methods. The breakthrough enables long-term, non-phototoxic cellular observation at near-molecular scales, expanding biological imaging capabilities substantially.
