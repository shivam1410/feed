NVIDIA released Cosmos 3, an open-source family of omnimodal world models unifying language, image, video, audio, and action in a Mixture-of-Transformers architecture. Models range from 16 billion (Nano) to 64 billion (Super) parameters, establishing state-of-the-art performance in open-weight image and video generation. Alongside Cosmos 3, NVIDIA announced Nemotron 3 Ultra (550B parameters) as the strongest open-weight U.S. language model and previewed RTX Spark (1 petaflop personal computer). Together, these represent NVIDIA's push toward accessible physical AI infrastructure.
