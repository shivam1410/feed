Agent skills carry far more reach than users see at install time. A study of 6,309 listings from two marketplaces found that half show evidence of shell-execution capability. About 35% can read files, 31% each can write files or reach the network, and 13% can access credentials. Seven listings in ten carry at least one elevated capability. Only 770 of 6,061 parseable listings declare any pre-approved tools, and that list does not restrict what a skill can reach.
