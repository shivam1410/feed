Principle-driven foundation models trained on radio-frequency data achieve 77.7% average accuracy (91.9% top-3) across 15 diverse tasks using a frozen 1.99M-parameter encoder. The model transfers to audio, images, text, and video without fine-tuning. Performance splits reveal 84.5% on physically-grounded tasks versus 70% on semantic tasks, demonstrating that physical principles enable efficient cross-modal transfer while establishing boundaries between physical and semantic understanding.
