Scientists have recovered the first historical methane record from tropical ice, collected from Nevado Huascarán, a high-altitude peak in Peru. This ice core fills a critical gap in scientific understanding of past atmospheric methane concentrations in the tropics. The data improves researchers' ability to model how much methane tropical sources contributed to global atmospheric methane levels historically and in recent decades. This information refines understanding of the geographic distribution of methane sources and helps predict future changes in tropical methane emissions.
