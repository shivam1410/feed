Researchers demonstrated that Bacteroides uniformis bacteria can mitigate atherosclerosis in mice by producing pentadecanoic acid, an odd-chain fatty acid. This acid directly inhibits HMG-CoA reductase, a critical enzyme in cholesterol synthesis, which helps the liver clear cholesterol more efficiently. This finding matters because it reveals a specific molecular pathway by which beneficial gut bacteria protect against heart disease.
