Researchers using genome-editing technology on human embryos are uncovering fundamental insights about human development, but this scientific progress raises urgent ethical concerns. The work demonstrates what CRISPR and related editing tools can reveal about genes controlling early development. However, advancing capabilities to edit and study human embryos—combined with growing knowledge about embryonic processes—create significant ethical questions about appropriate research boundaries. The scientific community recognizes that ethical frameworks must evolve alongside technical capabilities to address concerns about unintended consequences, equity of access, and appropriate uses of this powerful technology.
