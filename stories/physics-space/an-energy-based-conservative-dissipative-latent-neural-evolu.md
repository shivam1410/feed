Researchers developed a reduced-order model for magnetization dynamics using deep learning. The approach couples a convolutional autoencoder with a structured neural differential equation, motivated by physical principles of magnetic systems. The latent vector field combines antisymmetric and dissipative components. Training requires only trajectory data without physical labels. At inference, encoding once and evolving in compressed latent space enables substantially faster trajectory prediction than traditional micromagnetic solvers.
