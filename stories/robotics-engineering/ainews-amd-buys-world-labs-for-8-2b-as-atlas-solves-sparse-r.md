AMD acquired World Labs for $8.2 billion to own spatial intelligence capabilities. They released Atlas, a model that predicts novel camera views from 2D images, solving the long-standing sparse reconstruction problem in computer vision. This unlocks robotics simulation, architectural visualization, and real-world scene reconstruction. AMD is betting that spatial understanding, complementing language and vision, is essential infrastructure for robotics, design, and autonomous systems.
