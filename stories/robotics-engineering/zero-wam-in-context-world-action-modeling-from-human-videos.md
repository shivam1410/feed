Zero-WAM enables robots to manipulate unseen objects and tasks by conditioning a causal video-action model on in-context human video examples, eliminating the need for task-specific training. An automatically generated dataset and future-chunk prediction objective support generalization. This approach bridges the gap between human demonstration and robotic execution of novel tasks.
