Seven optical clocks distributed across Europe were synchronized using signals transmitted through fiber-optic cable, demonstrating unprecedented agreement between devices separated internationally. This comparison tests optical clock precision at continental scales. Optical clocks underpin GPS systems, telecommunications, and fundamental physics experiments. Successfully coordinating these ultra-precise instruments across nations represents a major technical achievement and foundation for future applications requiring distributed synchronized timekeeping at atomic scales. The international collaboration suggests optical clocks could eventually underpin new global timekeeping standards.
