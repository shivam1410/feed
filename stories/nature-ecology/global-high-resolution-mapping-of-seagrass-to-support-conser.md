Satellite mapping reveals widespread seagrass meadow loss and degradation globally since 2019. Seagrass ecosystems support marine biodiversity, provide nurseries for fish, and sequester carbon. Most meadows lie outside protected areas, leaving them vulnerable to coastal development, pollution, and climate change. Seagrass decline threatens ocean ecosystem services and carbon storage capacity. The high-resolution global mapping makes urgent conservation needs visible and highlights necessity of stronger marine protection policies and climate action to prevent continued loss.
