Researchers discovered a vast whale graveyard seven kilometers beneath the ocean surface, containing abundant whale fossils and skeletal remains that illuminate ancient marine life. The site has already yielded discovery of a previously unknown extinct whale species, providing new insights into whale evolutionary history and deep-sea biodiversity.
