NASA's Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope, launching in the coming months, will revolutionize exoplanet discovery. The instrument could detect 40 times more distant worlds than have been discovered to date—a dramatic expansion beyond the current catalog of several thousand known exoplanets. This extraordinary increase in discovery capacity stems from the telescope's advanced instrumentation and space-based vantage point, which enable detection of fainter, smaller planets orbiting distant stars. The mission promises to fundamentally transform exoplanet science by reshaping our understanding of planetary populations, frequency across the galaxy, and the diversity of worlds.
