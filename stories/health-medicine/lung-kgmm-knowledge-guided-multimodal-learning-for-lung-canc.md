LUNG-KGMM integrates longitudinal electronic health records, radiology reports, chest radiographs, and guideline-derived clinical knowledge for 1-to-6-year lung cancer incidence prediction. The framework includes a leakage-sanitized report processing pipeline and horizon-masked training to handle incomplete follow-up. Testing on MIMIC development cohort showed superior performance over state-of-the-art methods, with validation on the Xiamen Medical Big Data Platform demonstrating cross-cohort portability and identifying need for local adaptation.
