Lead carboxylates passivation improves the performance and stability of meter-scale perovskite solar modules. This technique addresses key challenges in scaling perovskite solar technology to commercially viable panel sizes, offering potential advantages in efficiency and manufacturing cost compared to traditional silicon solar cells.
