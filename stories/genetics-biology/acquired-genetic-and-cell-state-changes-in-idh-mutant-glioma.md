Researchers conducted longitudinal studies of two types of IDH-mutant glioma using transcriptomic analysis to measure gene expression, chromatin analysis to examine DNA packaging, and genomic sequencing. These detailed multilevel analyses reveal how disease progression is shaped by interdependent genetic mutations, epigenetic changes regulating gene expression, and shifts in the tumor microenvironment. The findings demonstrate that glioma progression depends on complex, interwoven factors rather than single mechanisms.
