Artificial intelligence uncovered CRISPR-like DNA sequences hiding in viruses through an unprecedented search effort. Researchers deployed roughly 950 artificial intelligence agents that spent more than 21 hours combing through DNA sequence databases. Finding these gene-editing-like sequences in viruses could reveal how pathogens defend themselves genetically and may open entirely new directions for medical research, vaccine development, and biotechnology applications.
