Adenine base editing—a precise genome-rewriting technique—enables researchers to investigate gene function in early human embryos with minimal disruption. Unlike traditional gene editing creating permanent DNA breaks, base editing makes targeted single-letter changes while preserving genetic integrity. This allows controlled experiments illuminating how specific genes function during early developmental stages. The technique provides a powerful tool for understanding human embryogenesis and identifying genes critical to normal fetal development.
