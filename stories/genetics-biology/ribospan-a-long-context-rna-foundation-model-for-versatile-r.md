RIBOSPAN enables unprecedented long-context RNA modeling by processing sequences up to 10,240 nucleotides in a single model. This bidirectional foundation model produces high-resolution full-transcript representations and supports discrete-diffusion-based mRNA generation and redesign. The long-context capability breaks prior constraints.
