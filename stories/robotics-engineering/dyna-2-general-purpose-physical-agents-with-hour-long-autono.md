Dyna Robotics has a robot that folds napkins for 24 hours straight. It reached a 99.4% success rate in that test. The robot agent, Dyna-2.1, builds on Dyna-2, a model trained on one million hours of human video. Robots can learn new tasks from as little as 45 minutes of demonstrations. The team says it folds thousands of napkins a day at Din Tai Fung, so this is real-world use beyond demos.
