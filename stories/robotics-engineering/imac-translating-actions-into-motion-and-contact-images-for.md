iMaC presents a unified control framework where raw visual images serve as native action representations for embodied AI systems. Rather than translating vision to discrete action codes, the system uses image-based action tokens directly, enabling more expressive and generalized control across robotic platforms. This preserves visual information in action representation, allowing richer communication between perception and control. The method supports more varied and context-aware behaviors than traditional action vocabularies, advancing toward more fluid and adaptable embodied intelligence.
