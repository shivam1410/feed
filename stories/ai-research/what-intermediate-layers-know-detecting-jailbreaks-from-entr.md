Harmful intent in jailbroken large language models is encoded in structured patterns of uncertainty—entropy dynamics—distributed across intermediate layers rather than in final output representations. This finding enables detection of jailbreak attempts by analyzing hidden layer activity. Understanding that malicious intent manifests as intermediate uncertainty patterns, not surface outputs, matters because it suggests new detection and monitoring approaches and reveals alignment mechanisms may need to operate at deeper representational levels.
