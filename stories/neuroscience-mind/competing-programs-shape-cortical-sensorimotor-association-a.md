Multispecies evidence supports a model where two opposing transcriptomically-defined genetic programs compete to pattern the cerebral cortex along a sensorimotor-to-association axis. Induction and exclusion mechanisms drive this regionalization during development. This explains how competing molecular signals establish the cortex's functional organization and may illuminate evolutionary constraints on brain organization.
