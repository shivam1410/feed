Inland waterway shipping produces surprisingly large greenhouse gas emissions relative to the attention it receives, and traffic on these routes continues growing. Researchers identify this as an overlooked opportunity for emissions reduction. Focusing on reducing river shipping's carbon footprint could be a significant climate mitigation strategy that deserves greater attention in climate planning compared to ocean freight and aviation, which receive more policy focus.
