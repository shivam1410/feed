GPT-6 Astra, OpenAI's first model from the Stargate supercluster, decisively outperforms Fable 5.1, saturating FrontierMath at 97.6% and ARC-AGI-3 at 99.9%. These engineering capabilities represent a qualitative shift enabling AI systems to execute complex multi-step technical work—model selection, data labeling, system deployment, and debugging—previously requiring human expertise and oversight throughout.
