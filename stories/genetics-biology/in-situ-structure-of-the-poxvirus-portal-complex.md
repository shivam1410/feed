Cryo-electron tomography reveals the structure of a conserved hexameric portal complex that spans the core wall in vaccinia virus. This portal complex mediates critical viral functions including assembly of virus particles, messenger RNA release, and genome uncoating inside host cells. Understanding the molecular architecture of viral portals illuminates how viruses traffic genetic material and replicate. The structure-function information provides potential targets for antiviral strategies, exemplifying how structural biology can reveal molecular mechanisms underlying viral pathogenesis.
