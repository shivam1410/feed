A tabular foundation model (TabPFN) consistently outperforms classical regression, Random Forest, and rule-based methods across 85 soil spectroscopy tasks, including global spectral libraries with tens of thousands of samples. Applied directly to full spectra, TabPFN surpasses classical baselines without explicit dimensionality reduction. Combined with partial least squares latent variables, it delivers the best overall performance, demonstrating that foundation models unlock value from high-dimensional, collinear spectroscopic data.
