AMD acquired World Labs for 8.2 billion dollars for spatial intelligence capabilities. The team released Atlas, an omni-model that predicts novel camera views from 2D images, solving sparse reconstruction, a long-standing computer vision problem. It combines generative models with multiview geometry and outperforms specialized models. Applications span robotics reinforcement learning, design, real estate, and construction.
