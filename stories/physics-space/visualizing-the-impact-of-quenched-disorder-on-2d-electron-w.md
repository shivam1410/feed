Researchers have developed a technique combining atomic-scale scanning tunnelling microscopy with neural-quantum-state quantum Monte Carlo simulations to study disordered two-dimensional electron Wigner solids. The method enables clear identification of two distinct defect-induced disorder regimes. Wigner solids are exotic quantum states where electrons organize into crystalline patterns. Understanding how disorder disrupts these patterns is crucial for quantum material science and could inform design of more robust quantum systems.
