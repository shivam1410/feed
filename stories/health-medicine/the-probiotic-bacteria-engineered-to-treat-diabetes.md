Genetically engineered probiotic bacteria successfully reduced blood glucose levels in animal trials, suggesting a novel approach for diabetes management. Unlike traditional insulin injections or oral medications, these engineered bacteria colonize the gut and respond directly to glucose changes by releasing therapeutic molecules. This sense-and-respond strategy offers a non-invasive alternative to current diabetes treatments.
