Rigorous clinical trials demonstrate that two drugs can reduce the risk of long COVID—the prolonged illness some people develop after coronavirus infection. Long COVID causes persistent fatigue, cognitive impairment, breathing difficulties, and other debilitating symptoms lasting months or years. Previous evidence offered no clear prevention strategy. These trials provide the first rigorous evidence that pharmaceutical intervention during acute infection can prevent transition to long-term disease. The findings are significant because they offer hope for millions who have suffered and suggest a preventive approach. If results hold across diverse populations, these treatments could transform long COVID management.
