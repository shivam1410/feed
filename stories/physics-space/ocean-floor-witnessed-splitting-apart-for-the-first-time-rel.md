Scientists directly observed mid-ocean ridge expansion in real time, recording several meters of seafloor motion and enormous lava outflows. This represents the first direct observation of active seafloor spreading, providing unprecedented data on plate tectonics processes. The observation advances understanding of oceanic crust formation and volcanic activity beneath the ocean.
