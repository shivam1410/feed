Robo-COP lets a robot's decision-making orchestrator and action policy improve together as the robot actually works. The orchestrator collects data from real actions, fine-tunes the policy when it finds recurring failures, and only adopts updates that actually help. On simulated tasks success climbed from 64.8% to 73.8%, and on three real-world tasks from 38.3% to 50%, turning deployment into learning.
