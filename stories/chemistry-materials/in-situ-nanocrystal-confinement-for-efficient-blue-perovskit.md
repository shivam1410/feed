Researchers achieved efficient blue perovskite light-emitting diodes with external quantum efficiency of 21.8% through in situ polymerization-driven nanocrystal confinement. This method confines nanocrystals during polymerization, improving material structure and light emission. The efficiency metric measures what fraction of input electrons convert to usable light output. Blue LEDs are critical for next-generation displays and advanced lighting. Perovskite materials offer advantages including solution-phase processability and tunable optical properties. This achievement demonstrates improved performance metrics for perovskite-based LEDs, potentially enabling widespread commercial adoption of advanced display technologies.
