Agent benchmarks based on aggregate leaderboard scores fail to capture deployment-relevant performance dimensions and exhibit rank instability. Current evaluation frameworks are poor predictors of real-world agent performance. The field needs new evaluation approaches grounded in predictive validity and robustness to out-of-distribution scenarios.
