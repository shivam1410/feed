Accelerated Arctic glacier melting and changing sea ice patterns are increasing iceberg calving rates into the ocean. As icebergs drift and melt, they deposit rocks (dropstones) on the deep seafloor, fundamentally reshaping benthic habitats and biodiversity patterns. This process extends climate change's ecological impact far beyond glacial regions, dramatically altering ecosystems thousands of kilometers away on the ocean floor and creating unexpected cryospheric-ocean connections.
