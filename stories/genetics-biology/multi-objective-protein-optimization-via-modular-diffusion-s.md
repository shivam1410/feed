MODS designs proteins that trade off several properties at once. It steers a diffusion model during generation, so the generator itself never gets retrained. After each evaluation batch, it updates its fitness predictors. Across six tasks it reached competitive results and substantially outperformed the best baseline on several. It matters because it works with only a few rounds of lab feedback.
