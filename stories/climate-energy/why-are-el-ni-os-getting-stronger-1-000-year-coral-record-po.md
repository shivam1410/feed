Scientists reconstructed Pacific Ocean sea surface temperatures using coral records spanning 1,000 years. The analysis reveals that human-caused climate change is intensifying El Niño events, which bring global weather disruptions. The coral-based temperature reconstruction provides long-term context showing El Niños are becoming stronger than historical norms. This evidence strengthens the scientific consensus linking anthropogenic warming to increased frequency and intensity of these major climate oscillations affecting billions of people.
