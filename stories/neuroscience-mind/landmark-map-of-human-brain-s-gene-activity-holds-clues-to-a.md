Scientists created an atlas of gene expression in the human prefrontal cortex using samples from nearly 1,500 donors spanning infants to centenarians. This age-diverse dataset reveals how genes are active across the entire human lifespan and provides molecular insights into diseases including Alzheimer's. The resource enables researchers to distinguish age-related changes from disease-specific gene expression patterns, potentially identifying intervention points for disease prevention.
