TypeSafe launched Jev, a "System One" model optimized for fast decision-making—classification, routing, scoring—rather than reasoning or code generation. The model runs 100 times faster and costs 200 times less than frontier LLMs, trained through RLCD (calibrated decision reinforcement learning). Designed to complement slower "System Two" reasoning models, Jev offers parallelizable, hallucination-free decisions ideal for real-time routing and classification in hybrid AI systems.
