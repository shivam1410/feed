Chemists are still relying heavily on hazardous, carbon-intensive solvents despite decades of green chemistry development. A 40-year survey of patents reveals these dangerous liquids remain far more common than safer alternatives in actual laboratory practice. Greener options exist but face barriers to adoption. This matters because the solvents chemists use carry both human health and climate costs that could be reduced.
