Researchers have described an AI-assisted attack by a suspected China-linked threat actor. The attacker used PHP2Shell vulnerabilities to breach exposed systems. It was observed on October 25, 2026. The attacker paired PHP2Shell with a kernel privilege escalation exploit to gain root. Per the headline, 25 servers were rooted and a U.S. government-affiliated network was breached.
