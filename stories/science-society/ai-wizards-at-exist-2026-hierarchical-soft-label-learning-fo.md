A hierarchical soft-label prediction system identifies sexism in image-text memes using vision-language embeddings and a lightweight Gated MLP trained with KL divergence and uncertainty weighting. The multimodal approach captures both visual and textual sexism cues. This addresses automated detection of harmful online content in media formats popular on social platforms.
