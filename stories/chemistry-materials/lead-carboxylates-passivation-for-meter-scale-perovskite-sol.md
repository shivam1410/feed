Lead carboxylates passivation improved perovskite solar module stability and performance, enabling meter-scale device fabrication. Perovskites are promising alternatives to silicon solar cells, but material degradation has limited commercial deployment. This passivation technique addresses stability challenges and scales production toward practical, large-area applications.
