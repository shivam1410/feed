Machine-learning interatomic potentials accelerate molecular simulations but depend critically on training data quality and diversity. Researchers introduce Stein Kernelized Molecular Dynamics (SKMD), an enhanced sampling method using interacting particle dynamics to acquire informative training configurations for active learning. SKMD preserves Boltzmann distribution while using a kernel of global atomic descriptors for symmetry-aware configurational similarity. Demonstrated on Müller-Brown potential and MACE functional for alanine dipeptide, SKMD achieves higher model accuracy in fewer training iterations compared to active learning baselines, improving data efficiency for molecular dynamics.
