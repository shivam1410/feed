ReactionAtlas maps chemical reaction networks at unprecedented scale by combining machine-learned generative models with a machine-learned force field. Starting from eight pre-biotic seeds, it discovered approximately 47,000 reactions among 12,000 compounds without hand-crafted rules. Machine-learned transition states matched quantum chemistry references within 0.5 Ångströms in 85% of cases. This enables mapping small carbohydrate chemistry up to C₄H₈O₄ with charge and stereochemical information, providing insights relevant to catalysis and early chemistry research.
