Harness optimization improved by breaking agent mechanisms into reusable plugins. PluginRSI evolves individual components independently and accumulates them in a shared library, then recombines them into new harnesses at each iteration. The evolved plugins successfully transfer to other solver models without further optimization needed. This library-based approach significantly accelerates subsequent optimization rounds, achieving faster convergence on previously unseen tasks despite composition complexity.
