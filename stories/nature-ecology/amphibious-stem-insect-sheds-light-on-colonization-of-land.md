Chosha praecursor, a 300-million-year-old fossil, likely represents an early-diverging insect that helps fill a major gap in the arthropod fossil record. Researchers reanalyzed the specimen using cross-polarized light imaging and phylogenetic reconstruction, finding evidence of insect characteristics preserved in ancient remains. This discovery bridges the "hexapod gap"—a longstanding puzzle where the fossil record lacks intermediate forms connecting earlier arthropods to modern insects. The finding advances understanding of how insects colonized land and diversified.
