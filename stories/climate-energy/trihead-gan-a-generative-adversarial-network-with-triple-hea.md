TriHead-GAN addresses critical data scarcity for carbon emission monitoring in climate policy and regulation. This Transformer-based GAN uses a three-part discriminator supervising distributional authenticity, cross-variable dependencies (CO₂ with co-emitted pollutants), and temporal smoothness. Results preserve realistic step-wise variability of atmospheric measurements better than existing methods, enabling synthetic data generation where real high-frequency city-level monitoring data remain scarce.
