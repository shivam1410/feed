Researchers built a benchmark with alien worlds that have executable rules and flawed manuals, testing whether AI systems genuinely explore and discover new knowledge versus just recalling training data. Two sandboxes contain 31 and 24 discovery targets across 70 tasks each. Testing ten AI systems showed the strongest can learn unfamiliar rules, though exploration can stall. This matters because science requires discovering unknowns.
