Manikomycin, a natural antibiotic isolated from Streptomyces rimosus, demonstrates a previously unseen mechanism of action. The compound binds to the E-site of the bacterial ribosome's large subunit, representing a novel scaffolding approach. Improved laboratory fractionation strategies enabled its discovery, showing that systematic refinement of isolation techniques can reveal antibiotics with entirely new structural and functional properties. This finding illustrates how methodological advances in natural product chemistry continue to expand the toolkit for addressing antibiotic resistance and developing novel therapeutic compounds.
