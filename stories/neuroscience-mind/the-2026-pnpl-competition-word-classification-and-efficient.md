The 2026 PNPL Competition advances brain-computer interfaces with LibriBrain100, expanding prior speech-decoding benchmarks. The dataset includes 33 subjects with ~40 minutes each plus 80 hours of within-subject data. Two complementary tracks target within-subject performance maximization (Deep) and cross-subject generalization with minimal data (Broad). This matters because practical BCIs must generalize to new users from minutes of data, directly targeting the gap between research and deployment.
