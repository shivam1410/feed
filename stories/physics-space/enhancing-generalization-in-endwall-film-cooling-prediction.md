Scientists built SDNO, a physics-enhanced neural network predicting turbine endwall cooling effectiveness. Applying the superposition principle, it decomposes cooling layouts into independent sub-problems, solves each, then combines them. Trained on layouts with 1 to 5 cooling holes, the model generalizes to unseen configurations, potentially accelerating turbine design optimization.
