Researchers have enrolled the first participant in a clinical trial of cellular reprogramming therapy aimed at rejuvenating aging cells. The technique represents the first-in-human application of a method designed to reverse cellular aging. This landmark trial marks an important step toward potentially transforming how doctors treat age-related diseases and extending human healthspan.
