Scientists achieved single-molecule infrared absorption spectroscopy by detecting photon recoil from a trapped molecular ion—eliminating the need to destroy the molecule to analyze it. This non-destructive approach enables single-photon-sensitive measurements of molecular vibrational transitions with unprecedented sensitivity. The technique opens new possibilities for studying the detailed structure of individual polyatomic molecules.
