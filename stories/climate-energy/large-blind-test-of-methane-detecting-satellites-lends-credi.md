Twelve methane-sensing satellites were put through a year-long blind test. The test used controlled releases of methane in the Arizona desert. Many satellites detected and measured the releases accurately, and instruments built specifically for methane sensing performed best. It matters because it gives more confidence that satellite emissions data can be trusted.
