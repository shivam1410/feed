Google introduced AMIE, a medical AI system designed for real-time clinical video consultations. The system was evaluated in a first-of-its-kind study using simulated clinical settings rather than live patient care. AMIE demonstrates AI's potential to assist with medical consultations through video, though deployment in actual clinical practice remains to be established. This represents progress toward AI-augmented telemedicine capabilities.
