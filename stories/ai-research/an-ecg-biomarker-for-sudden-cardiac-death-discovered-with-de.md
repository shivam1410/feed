A deep-learning algorithm trained on electrocardiogram waveforms identified a novel biomarker—a visible pattern in ECG recordings—that predicts sudden cardiac death more accurately than existing clinical methods. The biomarker's visibility on standard ECG tests makes it immediately practical for clinical use without additional specialized equipment. This advance could enable earlier identification of high-risk patients, allowing preventive interventions before fatal cardiac events occur. The superior accuracy demonstrates how machine learning can extract predictive patterns from conventional medical data that human analysis may overlook.
