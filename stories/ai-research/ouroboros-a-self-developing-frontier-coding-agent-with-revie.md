Ouroboros is a self-developing agent that evolves its own tools, prompts, context assembly, and core implementation through reviewed commits, which become the runtime for subsequent work. Two evolution modes drive improvement: recursive free evolution (where improvement itself is a task) and experience-driven evolution (where ordinary work exposes bugs and inefficiencies). Benchmarks show strong performance: 86.74% on Terminal-Bench 2.1, 90.69% on OSWorld-Verified, state-of-art on CL-Bench. Hope, a 161-day live deployment, demonstrates sustained evolution under human social interaction. A critical design challenge: maintaining guardrail authority as the agent rewrites itself.
