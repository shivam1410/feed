Nancy Grace Roman, NASA's first chief astronomer, spearheaded pioneering programs that fundamentally transformed the field of astronomy. Her visionary leadership established foundational approaches to space-based observation that remain central to modern science. Recognizing her extraordinary contributions, the newest space telescope has been named in her honor—a prestigious recognition of her enduring impact. Roman's work revolutionized how scientists observe the cosmos and opened entirely new research possibilities. Her legacy continues to drive astronomical innovation and inspire researchers worldwide.
