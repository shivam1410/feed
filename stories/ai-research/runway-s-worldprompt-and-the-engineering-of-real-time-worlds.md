Runway's GWM Worlds 2 generates real-time interactive 720p video at 24 fps. WorldPrompt is a new input format allowing users to fix the first frame and specify timestamped events or actions that occur in the generated world, with actions even updatable in real-time. Runway is valued at $5.3 billion following a February 2026 funding round.
