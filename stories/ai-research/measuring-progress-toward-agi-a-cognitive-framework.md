Google DeepMind introduced a cognitive framework for evaluating artificial general intelligence (AGI) and launched a Kaggle hackathon to develop capability benchmarks. The framework provides structured methodology for assessing AGI progress against cognitive milestones. Crowdsourced benchmark development aims to build standardized measures of AGI capability across diverse evaluation dimensions.
