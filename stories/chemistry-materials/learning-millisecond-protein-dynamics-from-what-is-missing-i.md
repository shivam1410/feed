Scientists developed methods to extract protein dynamics occurring at millisecond timescales by analyzing missing features in NMR (nuclear magnetic resonance) spectra. This reveals how proteins move and change during function. Understanding these dynamics clarifies protein mechanisms and could improve drug development by identifying vulnerabilities in disease-causing proteins.
