A lightweight deep learning framework addresses atmospheric compensation in passive long-wave infrared hyperspectral imaging by jointly estimating transmittance, atmospheric path radiance, and downwelling spectrum from multi-range radiance measurements. The approach enables accurate atmospheric correction without active illumination. Improved passive infrared imaging quality enhances applications in remote sensing, thermal surveillance, environmental monitoring, and scientific observation while avoiding the power requirements and detection signatures of active systems.
