Greg Brockman, OpenAI president and co-founder, observed that employees strongly prefer direct human contact over AI-mediated interaction. When ChatGPT reaches out on Slack asking for help with tasks employees would willingly do if asked by a human colleague, they object—reinforcing how much people value human relationships. Employees want AI to return time for deeper collaboration and connection, not insert itself as an intermediary layer. It matters because it signals a critical constraint on AI adoption: systems succeed by enhancing human collaboration, not replacing human-to-human contact.
