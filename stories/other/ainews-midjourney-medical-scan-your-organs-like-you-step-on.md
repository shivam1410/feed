Midjourney announced an ultrasonic medical imaging device featuring 358,000 transducers arranged in a 70-centimeter diameter ring with 40 system modules. The company positions it as the first new whole-body imaging modality in 50 years, providing radiation-free full-body scanning through ultrasound-based CT. While the launch generated significant technical and media interest, substantial questions remain about practical clinical readiness and real-world utility.
