Google DeepMind embedded invisible digital watermarks into AI-designed proteins while keeping all their biological functions and capabilities completely intact. Scientists can now uniquely track synthetic biology created by AI, identifying exactly which engineered organisms came from algorithms rather than natural evolution. It's an important security measure for synthetic biology, creating a practical way to verify the origin and authenticity of AI-designed living organisms in the wild.
