NS-RIS is a scalable algorithm for learning hidden quantum Markov models (HQMMs) that uses Newton-Schulz orthogonalization on the Stiefel manifold, avoiding costly matrix decompositions. Critically, this is the first method to demonstrate HQMMs consistently outperforming classical HMMs on non-quantum-generated data. On synthetic HMM-generated benchmarks, NS-RIS improves evaluation metrics by an average of 38.5% (up to 50.6%) compared to both standard EM and state-of-the-art HQMM method COSM. On HQMM benchmarks, it improves 18.9% over COSM. The method establishes finite-time stationarity guarantees and opens practical applications for quantum-inspired models.
