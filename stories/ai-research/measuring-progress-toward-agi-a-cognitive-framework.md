Google DeepMind has proposed a cognitive framework specifically designed to evaluate progress toward Artificial General Intelligence (AGI), moving beyond existing benchmarks. The framework provides structured criteria for assessing AGI capabilities. DeepMind also launched a Kaggle hackathon inviting researchers to develop capability benchmarks aligned with this framework. Establishing objective measures of AGI progress is critical as AI systems become increasingly sophisticated, helping distinguish genuine breakthroughs from incremental improvements and guiding research investment and safety considerations in AI development.
