OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra for hard projects and GPT-6.2 Sol for everyday work, both in the API. Astra costs $8 per million input tokens and $40 for output, 80% of the old price. Its cached input price is $0.40, down from $1. Sol stays at $2 and $10, half the price of GPT-5.6 Sol. On Simon Willison's mech animation test, neither model morphed Yonkers well, unlike Fable 5.2 and Gemini 4 Carbon.
