Hybrid diffusion architecture with heterogeneous attention mechanisms scales efficiently for long-context visual generation. Chimera (11B parameters, 2B activated) combines KDA with O(N) complexity, multi-head latent attention, and modality-aware convolutions. System is 1.7× more compute-efficient than dense baseline and 7.3× more efficient overall. Model extrapolates zero-shot from 5-second video training clips to 30-second sequences without length-specific fine-tuning, demonstrating scalable approach to high-resolution, long-duration video synthesis.
