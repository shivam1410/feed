Researchers have developed a method to read text from ancient unopened scrolls from Herculaneum without unrolling them. By creating and burning their own papyrus, scientists reverse-engineered how ink produces glowing signals, enabling decipherment of scrolls sealed by volcanic ash nearly 2,000 years ago.
