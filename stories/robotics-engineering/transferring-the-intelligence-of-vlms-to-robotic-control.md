RoboDawn enables vision-language models to control robots through a simple interface of discrete translation, rotation, and gripper commands. Using in-context learning with minimal demonstrations, RoboDawn achieves strong zero-shot performance without task-specific robot training. On RoboTwin 2.0, zero-shot success rates reached 53.2%, improving to 73.6% with a single demonstration, exceeding trained baselines like π0.5 at 46.0%. The approach demonstrates that VLM intelligence generalizes from digital to physical robotic domains.
