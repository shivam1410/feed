Computer scientists built a massive simulation of academic science using AI agents as researchers. They ran experiments with 40,000 virtual researchers generating 1.2 million peer reviews across multiple simulated worlds. Their simulation revealed that rejected papers encourage too much resubmission, increasing reviewer burden, and that funding inequality can grow even without obvious early advantages accumulating.
