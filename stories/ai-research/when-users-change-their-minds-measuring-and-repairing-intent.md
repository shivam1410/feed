IntentFlux reveals that language model agents struggle when users change their mind mid-conversation instead of stating the full task once. Task scores drop from 0.476 to 0.384 when superseded intent remains in the context. StateForge maintains explicit tracking of active requirements and lifts performance from 0.367 to 0.467, but doesn't fully close the gap, showing state estimation is only part of the problem.
