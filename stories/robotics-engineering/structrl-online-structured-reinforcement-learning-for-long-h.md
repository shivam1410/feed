StructRL improves long-horizon robot learning by decomposing tasks into verifiable subtasks with intermediate rewards granted only after each prerequisite completes. This structured supervision beats sparse terminal rewards that can't distinguish early failures from rollouts making partial progress. Tested on RoboCasa365 and LIBERO-Long benchmarks, it consistently outperforms online RL baselines for multi-step manipulation.
