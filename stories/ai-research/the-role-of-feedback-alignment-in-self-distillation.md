Self-distillation effectiveness depends on structural alignment between feedback and solver reasoning. Step-aligned critique—feedback targeting specific reasoning failures—substantially outperforms binary rewards and reference solutions. This finding clarifies why certain distillation strategies work better and guides optimization of student-teacher learning dynamics.
