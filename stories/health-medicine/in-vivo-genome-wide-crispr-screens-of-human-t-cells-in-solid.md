An in vivo model successfully recovers human T cells from solid tumors, enabling genome-wide CRISPR screens to identify genes improving CAR T cell antitumor activity. This approach identifies therapeutic targets directly in the tumor microenvironment rather than in simplified in vitro conditions. Findings guide improvement of CAR T cell immunotherapy effectiveness.
