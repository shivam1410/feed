Three scientists won the 2026 Nobel Prize for developing optogenetics, a revolutionary technique that uses light to control and manipulate neurons. The method lets researchers switch specific neurons on and off with remarkable precision, turning them on command. This revolutionized neuroscience because it opened entirely new ways to understand how brain circuits work, how different brain regions function together, and what causes various brain diseases.
