A plasmonic metamaterial operating at terahertz frequencies exhibits strong, ultrafast time-dependent modulation and transitions into a photonic time crystal regime with reduced losses. This demonstrates feasibility of time crystals in photonic systems where optical properties repeat periodically in time rather than space.
