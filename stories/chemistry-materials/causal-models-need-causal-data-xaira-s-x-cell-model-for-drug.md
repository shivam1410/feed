Xaira Therapeutics' research reveals that scaling improvements for predicting gene expression changes plateau when models reach an information bottleneck. Training on a single, small dataset caused test loss to flatten at 1.5B parameters while training loss continued dropping, with the 3.1B model falling off the established scaling trend. However, information-rich data collection from cellular experiments—approximately 30x more experimental data—enabled the model to continue scaling with parameters and compute. This demonstrates that for biological prediction tasks, the limiting factor isn't parameters or compute but the information density of training data.
