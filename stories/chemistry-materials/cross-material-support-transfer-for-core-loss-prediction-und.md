A transfer-learning method that borrows information from related materials to predict power loss when test data comes from different waveforms than training data. On material D, where trapezoids were rare in training but common in tests, it cut error from 20.39% to roughly 1 percent. This matters because deployed systems face mismatches between what hardware can measure and what it produces.
