Single-cell genetic mapping in inflammatory bowel disease patients reveals that disease-associated genetic variants operate differently across distinct cell types. Variants identified at the individual cell-type level—particularly distal variants located in enhancer regions—align more frequently with genome-wide disease associations than variants identified at the broader tissue level. This cell-type-specific precision approach provides substantially deeper insight into how genetic factors influence disease susceptibility and risk mechanisms. The findings suggest that cell-type-resolved genetic analysis should become standard for understanding complex genetic diseases and their underlying mechanisms.
