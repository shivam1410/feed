Ethan He (xAI, formerly NVIDIA Cosmos lead) proposes that video model intelligence derives primarily from underlying LLMs rather than video training data, arguing the next frontier is video agents—systems that plan, generate, edit, critique, and iterate—rather than incremental generation improvements. He parallels this to coding AI evolution: from single-shot output optimization to multiturn orchestration where planning and refinement dominate. This suggests generative video's roadmap mirrors code's trajectory toward agentic systems.
