BFN-RL presents a unified generative framework applying Bayesian Flow Networks to offline reinforcement learning. Unlike diffusion models requiring categorical reformulation for discrete tasks, BFNs iteratively evolve distribution parameters natively handling both discrete and continuous trajectory spaces. The approach generates future state sequences via a categorical planner, then converts generated state pairs into actions using an inverse-dynamics model. Evaluations across discrete planning and continuous control demonstrate effective trajectory generation across data modalities, establishing BFNs as a versatile foundation for offline planning problems.
