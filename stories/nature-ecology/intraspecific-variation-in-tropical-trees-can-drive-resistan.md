Research confirms that distinct populations of the same tropical tree species exhibit different levels of drought resistance. This intraspecific genetic variation indicates tree populations can adapt differently to water stress based on local evolutionary history. Population-level diversity provides genetic material for climate-driven natural selection without requiring species invasion. This finding complements research showing species turnover also drives forest-scale drought adaptation.
