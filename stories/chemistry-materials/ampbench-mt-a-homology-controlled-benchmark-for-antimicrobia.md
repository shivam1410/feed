AMPBench-MT, a homology-controlled benchmark for antimicrobial peptides, evaluates 161 endpoint-specific models across binary recognition, species-conditioned potency, and safety assays. Results reveal high binary classification performance does not predict assay-relevant outcomes; frozen protein-language embeddings form a leading error cluster. Spectrum and toxicity labels expose that precision-recall metrics mislead under sparse negatives. The benchmark demonstrates that AMP evaluation must move beyond recognition leaderboards toward endpoint-aware evidence auditing aligned with actual therapeutic requirements.
