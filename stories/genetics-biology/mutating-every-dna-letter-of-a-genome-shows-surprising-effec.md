Researchers systematically mutated every letter of a virus's DNA genome and found that top artificial-intelligence models failed to accurately predict the biological consequences of these complete genomic rewrites. Despite their sophistication, leading AI systems could not reliably forecast whether mutations would affect viral properties. This comprehensive study reveals fundamental limitations of current AI in understanding complex biological systems and suggests that computational prediction of genetic effects remains a major unsolved challenge.
