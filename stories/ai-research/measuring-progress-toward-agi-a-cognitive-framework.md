Google DeepMind proposed a cognitive framework for evaluating progress toward artificial general intelligence (AGI) and launched a Kaggle hackathon to build capability benchmarks. The framework provides a structured approach for assessing AGI development by defining key cognitive capabilities and measurement methods. The hackathon invites researchers and developers to contribute practical benchmarks. This initiative reflects efforts to move beyond speculative discussions by establishing measurable, science-based criteria for measuring progress in AGI research.
