A permutation-equivariant neural operator plans collision-free trajectories for entire spacecraft swarms in a single forward pass. Trained on ten spacecraft without optimal-trajectory labels, it generalizes zero-shot to 1,000 spacecraft navigating over 11,000 debris objects, matching optimal-control accuracy, evading worst-case threats, and reducing inter-swarm proximity several-fold—demonstrating that physics-grounded operator learning scales better than classical trajectory optimization.
