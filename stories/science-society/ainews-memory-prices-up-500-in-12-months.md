Memory chip prices have increased 500% in 12 months, with 128GB DDR5 kits reaching 10 times their historically lowest prices. Hyperscalers have locked in nearly all of 2027's global DRAM production capacity through advance deposits. DRAM chips are now worth over half as much per kilogram as solid gold. Moore's Law's decades-long trend of falling hardware prices has reversed for memory. This severe shortage threatens AI scaling and infrastructure expansion, forcing unprecedented demand commitments and reshaping hardware commodity markets.
