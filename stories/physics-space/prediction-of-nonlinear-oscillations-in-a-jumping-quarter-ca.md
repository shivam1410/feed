Reservoir computing via echo state networks predicts dynamics in a jumping quarter-car model prone to bifurcations and chaotic motion. The trained network qualitatively reproduces period-doubling routes to chaos, captures geometric structure of periodic and chaotic attractors, and reconstructs phase-space trajectories across regimes. Results demonstrate that reservoir computing is feasible for data-driven prediction of nonlinear vehicle dynamics with discontinuous contact loss.
