Researchers found lithium ions grouping together in battery electrolytes, the liquid that carries charge. They combined machine-learning simulations, spectroscopy, electrochemical data and quantum calculations. The clusters have no anions attached, and they had not been studied before. This sharpens the atomic-level picture of how lithium-ion batteries work.
