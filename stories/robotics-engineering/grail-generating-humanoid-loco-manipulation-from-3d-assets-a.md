GRAIL generates realistic humanoid movement data by combining 3D digital assets with video foundation models. Rather than relying solely on expensive real robot recordings, the system synthesizes diverse manipulation and locomotion behaviors from simulations and video priors. This synthetic training data transfers effectively to real robots in practice. The approach solves a fundamental robotics challenge: robots trained purely in simulation often fail in reality due to differences between virtual and physical environments. GRAIL bridges this sim-to-real gap by grounding simulation data with video-based priors, enabling cost-effective robot learning before deployment.
