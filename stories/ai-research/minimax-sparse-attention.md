MiniMax Sparse Attention enables large language models to process ultra-long contexts efficiently through blockwise sparsity patterns and GPU-optimized execution. By selectively attending to blocks of tokens rather than every pair, the technique achieves significant speedups while maintaining reasoning performance. This addresses a fundamental bottleneck: quadratic attention complexity in standard transformers. By preserving performance while reducing compute, MiniMax unlocks practical ultra-long-context reasoning, reducing document chunking and enabling better handling of lengthy inputs.
