Recent research challenges a foundational laboratory assumption: Bunsen burners, long thought to protect workbenches by wafting bacteria away, actually fail to provide this sterilization benefit. This discovery could force reconsideration of safety practices in modern labs. Separately, the United States is shifting substantial biomedical research initiatives toward military application. In surprising behavior, orcas have been documented actively bashing boats—not from territorial aggression, but apparently for enjoyment and play. These findings highlight broader scientific evolution: questioning long-accepted practices, reorganizing research priorities, and revealing unexpected animal sophistication.
