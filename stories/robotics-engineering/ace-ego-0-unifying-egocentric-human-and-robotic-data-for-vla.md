Training embodied AI agents requires vast amounts of motion data, but most datasets come from either human egocentric videos or robot trajectories—each with different structures and quality levels. ACE-Ego-0 unifies both data sources through a reliability-aware training approach that adjusts how heavily each data point influences learning based on its quality and relevance. The Vision-Language-Action pretraining framework leverages heterogeneous data without requiring manual alignment, automatically learning from both human demonstrations and robot experiences. Downstream evaluation shows this combined approach improves performance on embodied AI tasks more than using either data source alone, suggesting significant untapped value in heterogeneous training data.
