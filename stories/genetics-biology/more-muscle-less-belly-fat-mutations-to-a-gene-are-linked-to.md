A large-scale analysis of one million diverse genomes uncovered genetic mutations associated with beneficial body composition—more muscle, less visceral fat—and substantially lower risk of type 2 diabetes, heart disease, and other cardiometabolic conditions. These findings identify genetic pathways controlling energy storage that could inform new preventive or therapeutic approaches to metabolic disease.
