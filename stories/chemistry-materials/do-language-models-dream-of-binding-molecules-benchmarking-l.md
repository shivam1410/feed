Researchers benchmarked general-purpose language models on 3D drug molecule design tasks requiring spatial reasoning about protein structures and constraints. While LLMs still underperform specialized diffusion models, they show promise handling multiple spatial constraints simultaneously—anchor fragments, pharmacophore points, and protein-ligand interactions—using a novel 3D-Fit benchmark. Results suggest LLMs could scale to heterogeneous drug design problems, though significant performance gaps remain compared to established baselines.
