Two archaea, a kind of microbe, have now been grown in the lab. They come from a lineage closely related to the ancestor of eukaryotic cells, the complex cells with membrane-bound organelles. Their simple internal layout and elaborate surface structures offer clues. It matters because it helps explain how complex cells first arose.
