OpenAI used an unreleased model to resolve the Navier–Stokes existence and smoothness problem, one of the seven Millennium Prize Problems with a $1M bounty since 2000. Mathematicians Tristan Buckmaster (NYU) and Levent Alpöge (Anthropic) had worked on related problems for a year using Claude and Codex, achieving a breakthrough August 15th. OpenAI's team began work after learning of their progress, raising concerns about whether the AI model was trained on or had access to their coding sessions.
