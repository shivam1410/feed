Raygun, a new generative AI system, enables precise protein engineering through probabilistic sequence encoding derived from language model embeddings. The framework allows researchers to miniaturize proteins and modify their properties while maintaining their native three-dimensional architecture and preserving functional integrity. This computational approach bypasses the traditional need to design proteins entirely from first principles. Researchers can now adapt and augment existing natural proteins for specific applications. The ability to reduce protein size while sustaining biological function could accelerate development of new therapeutics, biosensors, enzymes with customized capabilities, and molecular tools. The breakthrough represents substantial progress in AI-assisted protein design, potentially enabling faster drug discovery and novel biotechnology applications.
