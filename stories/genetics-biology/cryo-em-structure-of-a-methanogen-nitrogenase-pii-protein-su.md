Using cryo-electron microscopy, researchers solved the structure of a nitrogenase supercomplex from a methane-producing archaeon and revealed how PII proteins drive its assembly. PII proteins control nitrogenase activity in response to nitrogen levels in the cell. This matters because understanding how methanogens regulate nitrogen fixation could inform efforts to optimize microbial metabolism for biotechnology.
