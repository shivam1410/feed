Morris is the only known Northern Gannet (Morus bassanus) in the entire Pacific Ocean, an unusually isolated occurrence for the species. The bird appeared at the Farallon Islands off San Francisco's coast 14 years ago and subsequently made Pillar Point Harbor in California its permanent home. Easily identifiable by its distinctive white plumage and yellow head, Morris frequents the harbor near the commercial pier, typically in the company of smaller black Brandt's cormorants. The bird has become a local celebrity and attraction, remarkable for representing an entire species presence in an ocean otherwise devoid of its kind.
