Researchers discovered that transformers like those in ChatGPT exhibit a hidden linearity. When they combine different text inputs, the model produces a mathematical superposition of the individual responses instead of blending them. This fundamental property is built into the transformer architecture itself. By fine-tuning, they restored the effect and created a method to generate two separate coherent continuations from a single forward pass, like thinking two thoughts at once.
