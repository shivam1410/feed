Researchers traced a neutrino detected at the South Pole to its origin in a distant galaxy during the Universe's early star-forming era. This galaxy actively produced stars when the cosmos was young. Identifying neutrino sources across cosmic distances represents a breakthrough in multi-messenger astronomy, connecting particle observations to galaxies.
