BioHub announced ESMFold 2, an open scientific engine for protein structure prediction, design, and discovery built on large-scale protein sequence models. ESM systems are trained on millions of protein sequences using simple next-token objectives but discovered they also learn biological structure and function without explicit instruction. Performance improves predictably with additional compute, and inference time scaling also works. ESMFold 2 achieves state-of-the-art results on protein interactions, especially antibodies critical for therapeutics. The team released an atlas of 6.8 billion predicted protein structures and 1.1 billion structures, making protein design tools accessible to researchers worldwide.
