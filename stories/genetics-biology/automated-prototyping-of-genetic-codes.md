Scientists developed a robotic, cell-free platform that rapidly prototypes newly redesigned genetic codes without modifying living organisms. The system enables researchers to synthesize proteins with reassigned codons and incorporate non-standard amino acids that don't exist in natural life. This breakthrough bypasses the technical challenges of reengineering actual living cells, allowing faster, safer experimental testing of novel genetic systems and protein designs. Potential applications include engineering proteins with enhanced catalytic properties, novel enzymatic functions, or distinctive medical and therapeutic utilities. The platform substantially accelerates synthetic biology research and transforms biotechnology.
