Researchers developed an in vivo model to recover and analyze human T cells directly from solid tumors, enabling genome-wide CRISPR screening to identify genetic targets that enhance CAR T cell antitumor activity. The approach addresses a major limitation of CAR T cell therapy—poor effectiveness against solid tumors compared to blood cancers. By identifying which genes regulate T cell function within the tumor microenvironment, this work could accelerate development of more effective engineered cell therapies and improve outcomes for patients with solid malignancies.
