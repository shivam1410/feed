Lunar spacecraft identified two massive shield volcanoes on the Moon, revealing evidence of more extensive volcanic activity than previously documented. Shield volcanoes form from widespread lava flows over long periods, indicating the Moon experienced significant geological activity. This discovery reshapes understanding of lunar evolution, internal heat sources, and geological timelines. These findings inform planetary formation models and support future lunar exploration strategies, including resource identification and site selection.
