Ruff v0.16.0 expanded default linting rules from 59 to 413—the first change since v0.1.0. The linter's total rulebase grew from 708 to 968. New defaults catch severe issues like syntax errors and runtime failures, substantially increasing strictness without configuration. Projects face hundreds of new violations requiring fixes after upgrading.
