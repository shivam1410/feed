SPACE is a framework that explains why a short video was flagged as harmful diet content. It uses sparse autoencoder features from a large multimodal model. On 3,711 labeled TikTok videos, it reached a harmful-category Macro F1 of 0.8507, the highest among the benchmarks tested. Its top-scoring moment overlapped a true harmful span in 88.0 percent of harmful test videos. Stakeholders found the evidence report useful for reviewing and documenting decisions.
