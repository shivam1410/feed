Machine learning screening identified promising antibiotic compounds against drug-resistant Neisseria gonorrhoeae, a sexually transmitted bacterium resistant to nearly all standard clinical antibiotics. Antibiotic resistance in this pathogen represents a major public health threat as treatment options become severely limited. The artificial intelligence tool rapidly screens chemical libraries to identify compounds with activity against this difficult target. This demonstrates how computational screening accelerates drug discovery for challenging infectious pathogens where conventional methods are slower. Finding new therapies is critical as resistance spreads globally.
