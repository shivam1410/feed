Single-nucleus RNA sequencing created a cellular atlas capturing molecular changes in the prefrontal cortex across the human lifespan in healthy and diseased states. This technique examines gene expression in individual cell nuclei, revealing how different brain cell types change with age and disease. The dataset provides unprecedented resolution into brain aging mechanisms and disease-specific molecular signatures, helping identify cellular changes driving cognitive decline and therapeutic targets.
