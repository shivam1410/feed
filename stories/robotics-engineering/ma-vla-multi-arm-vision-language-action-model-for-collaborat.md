MA-VLA enables multi-arm robotic collaboration by assigning atomic actions to individual arms and leveraging training-time action permutations to generalize to unseen coordination patterns. Multi-arm tasks require coordinating multiple effectors simultaneously; this method decomposes the action space per arm and learns transferable coordination through synthetic permutation during training, allowing the model to handle novel arm combinations without retraining.
