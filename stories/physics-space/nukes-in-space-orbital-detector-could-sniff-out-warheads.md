A shoebox-sized detector satellite could identify hidden nuclear warheads in space by detecting their telltale radiation signatures. This capability enables verification of the Outer Space Treaty and monitoring of weapons compliance in orbital regions, providing a practical tool for international arms control beyond Earth's atmosphere.
