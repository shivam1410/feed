Temporal-Distance JEPA improves latent world model planning by mining directed temporal cost signals directly from demonstration trajectories rather than relying solely on learned embedding geometry. On Two-Room manipulation tasks, TD-JEPA achieves 100% success compared to LeWM's 97.4%; on OGB-Cube tasks it improves performance by 14.2 percentage points. By extracting reward-free planning signals from data trajectories, the method enables more accurate multi-step predictions for robotic control. This advances manipulation by better aligning learned representations with actual task success metrics.
