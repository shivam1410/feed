Traditional AI efficiency metrics focus on isolated training runs or single predictions, but this ignores broader environmental cost. This position paper advocates applying life cycle assessment to AI systems—accounting for energy impact across development and deployment, including embodied costs of hardware manufacturing, training, and inference. As AI pipelines grow complex, only full life cycle accounting properly quantifies resources and downstream environmental impact needed at scale.
