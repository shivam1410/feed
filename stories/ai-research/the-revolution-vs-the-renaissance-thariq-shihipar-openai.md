AI can make software cheap enough to produce by the thousand and still give you more time to make one thing feel right. At AI Engineer CODE, Thariq Shihipar shows how those two futures can coexist. In his fighting game, he edits the size of a rock in the /human folder and immediately tries the throw. He also built a Mac Dock that switched modes, used it, and realized he cared more about noticing when he got distracted. We get into what Thariq changed his mind about with coding agents, how a decision model can choose from actions a designer defines, and who gets to decide which experiments deserve the compute.
