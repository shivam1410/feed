MALOQ trains machine-learned operators to predict density functional theory Hamiltonian and density matrices at reduced computational cost, enabling electronic-structure calculations previously infeasible. The system scales from few atoms to 100,000-atom systems with large basis sets across diverse elements. Testing on Alps supercomputer achieved 30 percent reduction in training time per epoch and enabled inference on 12,000-atom material graphs, making previously impossible quantum materials simulations computationally practical.
