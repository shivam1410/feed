A peptide signaling pathway involving RGF family members controls pollen resilience to cold stress in tomato and rice plants. Modulating this molecular signaling axis prevents yield losses when crops experience unexpected cold events. This physiological mechanism offers genetic breeding and engineering targets for developing climate-adapted crop varieties. As growing seasons face increasing temperature volatility due to climate change, enhancing cold resilience becomes crucial for global food security and agricultural sustainability.
