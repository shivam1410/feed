Where pumas patrol, vehicles are significantly less likely to strike deer on roads, suggesting that puma predation reduces deer populations or alters their behavior to decrease dangerous crossings. This overlooked ecological benefit of large predators highlights how maintaining apex predators improves human safety alongside ecosystem balance.
