Entangle teaches a robot a new task from one human video, with no task-specific fine-tuning. It takes that video plus the robot's current view and predicts future visual states and actions. The team turned robot videos into matched human contexts for training. On an Astribot S1 it averaged 83.5/100 across six unseen tasks. It reached 69.5% success on seven unseen RoboTwin tasks, 22.6 points above Zero-WAM. It matters because robots may learn new jobs from a single demonstration video.
