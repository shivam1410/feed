In aging cells, mitochondrial acetyl-CoA fuels epigenetic modifications that drive inflammatory gene expression, amplifying senescence. Blocking SLC25A1, a mitochondrial transporter, reduces these changes. Targeting mitochondrial acetyl-CoA metabolism could suppress the inflammatory cascade accumulating with senescent cells and delay age-related dysfunction.
