PixWorld unifies 3D reconstruction and generation in pixel space using diffusion, eliminating latent-space method limitations. The approach applies direct image-level supervision with geometry-aware feature alignment. By working directly in pixel space rather than learned compressed representations, the method achieves better geometric consistency and reconstruction quality.
