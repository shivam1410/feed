Microtomography and phylogenetic analysis of Urokodia aequalis, a fossil from China's Chengjiang biota, revised understanding of how chelicerate anatomical structures—including chelicerae (fangs) and book gills—evolved. The reconstruction shows transitional features linking early arthropods to modern chelicerates. This fossil clarifies a critical gap in understanding major arthropod group origins.
