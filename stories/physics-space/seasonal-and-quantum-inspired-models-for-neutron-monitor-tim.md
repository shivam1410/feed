Researchers tested eight different forecasting models on neutron monitor data, including deep learning and quantum-inspired approaches. The quantum-inspired KAN variant (QiKAN) won with the lowest errors. But here's the surprise: a simple seasonal baseline was nearly as good. For periodic scientific signals, models with strong seasonal priors can match or beat fancy deep learning.
