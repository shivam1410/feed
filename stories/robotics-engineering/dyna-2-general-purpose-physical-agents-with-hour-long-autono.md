Dyna Robotics' models reached a 99.4% success rate folding restaurant napkins for 24 hours straight, including recovering after pulling over the whole stack. The Dyna-2 model was pre-trained on one million hours of human video and combines future video prediction with robot actions. Robots can learn new tasks from as little as 45 minutes of demonstrations. The company says its robots fold thousands of napkins daily at Din Tai Fung. It matters because long-horizon reliability is what moves physical AI from demos into production.
