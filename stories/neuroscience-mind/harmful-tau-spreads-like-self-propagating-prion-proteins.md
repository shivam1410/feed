Structural studies confirm that tau protein, which causes progressive damage in Alzheimer's and other neurodegenerative diseases, spreads like a prion by acting as a misfolding template. The misfolded protein convinces healthy tau molecules to adopt the same abnormal shape. This important finding helps explain how neurodegenerative diseases progressively damage the brain over years through self-propagating chains of protein misfolding, suggesting new treatment targets.
