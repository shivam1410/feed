Declarative Attention enables language models to identify and prioritize relevant context regions during reasoning, dramatically reducing the number of key-value cache reads required. This selective attention mechanism maintains accuracy while substantially decreasing computational cost. The technique addresses a critical efficiency challenge in large language model inference by allowing models to skip reading most KV cache entries. This approach could substantially reduce the computational resources and energy required to operate large language models, improving deployment feasibility.
