Spatial transcriptome analysis of a human embryo at Carnegie stage 6 reveals three distinct developmental trajectories from epiblast toward amnion, primitive streak, and axial mesoderm. Critically, haematopoiesis initiates before gastrulation from the hypoblast rather than epiblast, revealing earlier and alternative origin of blood cell formation.
