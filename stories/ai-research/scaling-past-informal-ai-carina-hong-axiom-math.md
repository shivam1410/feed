Axiom, a seven-month-old startup, solved eight of twelve problems on the Putnam exam, a legendary mathematics competition where the median score is 0 or 1 point. While other AI systems have achieved higher scores given unlimited time, Axiom showed strong performance within the time limit. The article reflects on how AI has progressively dominated elite competitions, and notes that by mid-2026, AI coding ability is reshaping the world. However, Axiom's CEO argues that coding ability, while necessary for AGI progress, is not sufficient; surprising gaps in other domains remain. She emphasizes the importance of verified AI as a bottleneck.
