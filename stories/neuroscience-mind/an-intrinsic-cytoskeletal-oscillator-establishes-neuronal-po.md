The ARP2/3 complex works together with actomyosin proteins in an oscillatory program at the neuronal soma to establish neuronal polarity and characteristic architecture. This rhythmic protein activity governs how neurons develop their distinctive shape and orientation, revealing a fundamental mechanism underlying neuron development.
