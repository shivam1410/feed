WeatherNext 3 is DeepMind's most advanced and accurate global weather AI forecasting model, now integrated into Google Search, Gemini assistant, Maps applications, Google Maps Platform for developers, and Google Cloud services. Broader access to better weather predictions improves planning for transportation, agriculture, emergency management, and countless other weather-dependent activities and operations.
