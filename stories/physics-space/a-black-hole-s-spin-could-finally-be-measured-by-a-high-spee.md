A fast-moving star orbiting extremely close to Sagittarius A*, the galaxy's central supermassive black hole, may enable the first direct measurement of the black hole's spin through gravitational effects on the star's trajectory. Successful spin measurement would advance black hole physics understanding and constrain galaxy formation models. The article also notes research into exoskeletons enhancing human movement, representing parallel advances across scientific domains.
