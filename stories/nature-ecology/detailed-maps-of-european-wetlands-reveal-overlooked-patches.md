Researchers used machine-learning algorithms to analyze satellite imagery and produce high-resolution maps of European wetland environments. The automated analysis identified wetland patches that had been previously overlooked, revealing the full extent of these ecosystems across the continent. This mapping effort provides a valuable resource for conservation planning and wetland management. The project demonstrates how artificial intelligence can enhance environmental monitoring and help protect biodiverse habitats by making their locations and boundaries visible to land managers and researchers.
