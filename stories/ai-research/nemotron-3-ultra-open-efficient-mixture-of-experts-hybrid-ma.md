Nemotron 3 Ultra is a 550-billion-parameter language model featuring a hybrid Mamba-Attention architecture designed for efficient reasoning at scale. The combination of Mamba (efficient local pattern capture) and Transformer-style Attention (global context modeling) achieves high inference throughput and extended context handling through specialized training. This architectural fusion addresses a fundamental tradeoff in large language models: maintaining reasoning capability while preserving computational efficiency—critical for practical deployment in agentic systems requiring both speed and nuanced understanding.
