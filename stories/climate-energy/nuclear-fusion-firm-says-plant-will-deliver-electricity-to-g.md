Commonwealth Fusion Systems has published detailed design papers for its ARC fusion power plant, intended to demonstrate electricity generation for the grid. The company has released technical specifications outlining the plant's architecture and approach. However, significant questions remain unresolved: the technology's timeline to deployment, its economic scalability, and whether the commercialization timeline is realistic given the novelty of the approach.
