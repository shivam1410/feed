HarvestBench evaluates LLM agents' moral decision-making through a farm simulation where agents choose between causing animal harm (free) or preventing it (costly in fuel). Results show kill rates are price-sensitive and vary based on system prompting across different models. This benchmark tests whether LLMs make ethically consistent choices under economic pressure, revealing that model behavior changes with both cost structure and instructional framing.
