DeformSmith is a framework that automatically generates deformable 3D assets suitable for robotic manipulation. It uses hierarchical agentic construction and physics-grounded testing to progressively build and refine geometry, material properties, and physical models. Evaluated on 4,675 cross sections with improvements over baselines including PhysGen3D and PhysX-Omni, it produces assets with superior visual quality and physical plausibility for robot simulation.
