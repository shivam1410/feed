A hybrid framework combines nonlinear time-series modeling (NARX) with large language models for predicting Greenland iceberg discharge—a chaotic system with limited observability and extreme events. The LLM uses physics-informed prompts to encode glacier dynamics and environmental drivers, correcting systematic errors from the NARX component. This fusion bridges data-limited climate forecasting with knowledge-driven reasoning, allowing the system to interpret unmodeled factors and produce interpretable residuals, addressing rare extremes that traditional models miss.
