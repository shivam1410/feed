Scientists use two fundamentally different methodologies to estimate how many people die from heatwaves, with each approach producing potentially divergent results. Current severe heatwave events affecting Asia and Europe are highlighting the challenges of accurately tallying mortality. These methodological differences can produce significantly different estimates of lives lost, underscoring the importance of developing standardized approaches for counting climate-related deaths. Understanding how scientists assess heatwave mortality is crucial for accurately measuring climate change's human health impacts.
