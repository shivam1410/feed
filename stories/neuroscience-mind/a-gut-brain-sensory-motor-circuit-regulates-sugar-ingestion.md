Fruit flies have a gut-to-brain circuit that controls how much sugar they eat. Sugar-sensing nerves in the gut activate brain cells. Those brain cells switch off motor neurons that tighten the foregut, so the muscles relax. That lets food move into the crop and keeps the flies feeding. The circuit also regulates how much they take in. It shows how gut signals steer eating.
