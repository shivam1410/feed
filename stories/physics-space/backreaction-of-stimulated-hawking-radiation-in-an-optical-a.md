Using fiber-optic systems mimicking black hole physics, researchers provided theoretical and experimental evidence for Hawking radiation—radiation predicted to emit from black hole event horizons. The study examined backreaction: how generated radiation affects the optical pump driving the system. This laboratory analogue enables testing black hole predictions in controlled conditions.
