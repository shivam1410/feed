Morris is a Northern Gannet photographed at Pillar Point Harbor in California—a location that has made Morris a local celebrity for an extraordinary reason. Morris is the only known Northern Gannet in the entire Pacific Ocean, making its presence unique in the region. The bird originally arrived at the Farallon Islands off San Francisco's coast fourteen years ago. Since then, Morris has established a permanent home at Pillar Point Harbor, where it is easily spotted thanks to its distinctive appearance: a white body with a yellow head that clearly distinguishes it from the smaller black Brandt's cormorants that frequent the same waters near the commercial pier.
