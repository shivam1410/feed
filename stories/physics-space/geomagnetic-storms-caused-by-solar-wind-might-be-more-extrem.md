Geomagnetic storm severity was thought to plateau beyond a threshold solar wind strength, suggesting an upper limit to intensity. However, this apparent ceiling reflects measurement uncertainty rather than true physical limits—observed values regress toward the mean, creating an illusion of saturation. Storms may actually reach more extreme levels than previously modeled. This reassessment has implications for space weather forecasting and infrastructure resilience, suggesting enhanced preparedness is needed.
