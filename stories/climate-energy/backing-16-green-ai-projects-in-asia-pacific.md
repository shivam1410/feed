DeepMind is backing 16 organizations across the Asia-Pacific region that are using frontier AI to tackle climate change, agriculture, and biodiversity challenges. These projects apply cutting-edge AI tools and methods to solve pressing regional environmental problems. Scaling and expanding AI applications for conservation and adaptation could help these rapidly developing regions better adapt and respond effectively to major climate change impacts.
