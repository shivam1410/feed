Climate and econometric modelling reveal that human-induced climate change is shifting the burden of childhood malaria in Africa. This research combines climate data with economic analysis to examine how climate change alters malaria transmission patterns. The findings indicate shifting disease distribution, which has significant implications for public health planning and disease management strategies in affected regions.
