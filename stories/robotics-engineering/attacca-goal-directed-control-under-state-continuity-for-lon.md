Trains visual policies for long-horizon tasks by learning on complete search-to-interact sequences with goal images from different scenes. Behavioral-phase conditioning teaches the policy to distinguish Search, Approach, and Interact stages. Matters because embodied agents must handle continuous task chains where each step starts from wherever the previous one ended, not from fresh scenes.
