Three individuals with highly sensitized immune systems—who typically reject any donated organ—successfully received kidney transplants using transformative CAR-T cell therapy. This breakthrough is significant because these patients previously had very limited survival options. CAR-T therapy modified their immune cells to tolerate donor organs, overcoming the biological barrier that made transplantation impossible. This advance could expand transplant access for highly sensitized patients who represent a particularly vulnerable population.
