Elorian launched Glimpse, its first visual reasoning model. It also co-released DecoyCount, a benchmark testing whether models really count objects or rely on visual shortcuts. Per the accompanying paper, frontier models scored below 50%. The best was GPT-6 Astra at 43.17%. Elorian's CEO Andrew Dai said Glimpse scored 50.48% in an internal test.
