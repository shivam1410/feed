Trapped-ion quantum processors demonstrated substantial improvements in logical error rates using quantum error correction combined with error detection and post-selection. The approach achieved 11-fold to 800-fold error-rate reductions compared with unencoded physical qubit baselines across different configurations. This experimental validation of error-correction scalability represents a critical milestone toward practical, fault-tolerant quantum computers capable of useful real-world applications.
