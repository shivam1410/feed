SHAPER is a training-free framework that improves embodied agents by evolving reusable skills and context-code around a frozen foundation model through environment interactions. The approach avoids expensive retraining while leveraging pre-trained capabilities. The framework generates improvements through environmental rollouts. This matters because it enables efficient adaptation of robots to new tasks without computational overhead of full retraining.
