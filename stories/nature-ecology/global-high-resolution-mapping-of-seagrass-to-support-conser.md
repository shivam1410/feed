Global high-resolution satellite mapping reveals that seagrass meadows have experienced widespread loss and degradation since 2019, with the majority of surviving seagrass located outside formally protected marine areas. These coastal ecosystems are critical for carbon storage, fish nurseries, and coastal protection against storm surge and erosion. The finding matters because it demonstrates that current marine conservation efforts have failed to adequately protect seagrass habitats, leaving them vulnerable to climate change, coastal development, and pollution. The research underscores the urgent need for expanded marine protection, improved conservation strategies, and stronger climate policies.
