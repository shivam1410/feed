BrainJanus represents the first unified model integrating brain activity, vision, and language into a single framework—a significant breakthrough connecting neuroscience with AI. The model uses a tokenized representation and autoregressive architecture to create an 'Omni space' enabling bidirectional mapping between neural activity and sensory stimuli. This achievement connects three previously separate domains—neuroscience, computer vision, and language models—through shared computational principles. The breakthrough suggests fundamental mathematical principles underpin perception across biological and artificial systems, advancing both neuroscience and AI.
