RicciBind combines two geometric techniques to predict protein-ligand binding affinity more accurately. Ricci curvature captures the local tightness of atomic interactions within protein and ligand molecules, organizing them hierarchically. Optimal transport (OT) then aligns protein and ligand clusters across the two different molecular domains, discovering higher-order interaction patterns beyond local neighborhoods. This dual approach—local curvature awareness plus global cross-domain alignment—enables the model to characterize complex binding mechanisms that existing methods miss. Results demonstrate superior predictive performance and generalization across binding affinity benchmarks and virtual screening tasks. This matters because accurate affinity prediction directly accelerates drug discovery by narrowing candidate compounds for experimental validation.
