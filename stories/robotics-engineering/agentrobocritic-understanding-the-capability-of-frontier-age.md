Frontier AI agents can drive robots directly, one low-level action at a time. A new critic checks each action against the agent's stated intent. The agents recover well from local slips and handle language grounding across scenes, but they struggle with grasping and alignment. The team released 12,844 annotated actions and an open critic model, whose failure-mode accuracy rose from 32.58% to 74.24%. That makes cheap, action-level analysis possible.
