DeepMind has released AlphaGenome Atlas, a high-resolution map of human DNA. It predicts the effect of every possible single nucleotide variant in the human genome. Those are the smallest single-letter changes in DNA. Having predictions for all of them, not just a sample, is the big deal. Researchers can look up a variant instead of testing each one.
