The Muon optimizer outperforms Adam in large language model training by reducing curvature penalties through lower normalized directional sharpness, with advantages particularly pronounced during middle and late training stages. Benefits amplify under data imbalance and heterogeneous curvature conditions, suggesting broader applicability. More efficient optimizers reduce training time and compute costs for large models, making state-of-the-art AI development faster and more sustainable while democratizing access to advanced model training.
