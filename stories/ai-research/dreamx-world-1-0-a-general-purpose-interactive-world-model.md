DreamX-World 1.0 is an interactive text-and-image-to-video generation model producing long-form video content with user-controlled camera movement and persistent scene elements. Using specialized encoding, training, and optimization methods, it generates coherent multi-frame sequences maintaining visual and spatial consistency. This capability enables creative applications from dynamic visualization to interactive content creation with fine-grained control.
