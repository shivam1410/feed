WorldToken fuses diverse robot sensor inputs—camera images, joint positions, force feedback—into unified world tokens processed by a causal Transformer, with a diffusion model generating actions. Scaling and temporal-context analyses on RoboCasa and RMBench demonstrate effectiveness for robotic imitation learning. This approach addresses processing heterogeneous sensor data, enabling robots to learn generalizable manipulation skills from demonstrations. Temporal-first modeling represents a promising direction for robot learning.
