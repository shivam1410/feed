Post-mortem brain tissue analysis from African American, Latin, and white individuals identified cell-type-specific molecular signatures associated with Alzheimer's disease and cognitive impairment using single-nucleus RNA sequencing and ATAC-seq analysis. The study discovered that these signatures are shared across diverse population groups despite genetic differences. Cell-type-specific patterns show conservation across populations. This finding suggests that Alzheimer's disease pathology operates through similar molecular mechanisms regardless of population background. The research advances understanding of disease mechanisms and supports development of therapeutics relevant to all populations. It demonstrates the importance of including diverse populations in biomedical research.
