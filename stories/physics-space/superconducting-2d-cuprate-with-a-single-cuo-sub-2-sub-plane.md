Researchers fabricated a high-quality pristine monolayer cuprate superconductor containing a single CuO₂ plane, isolating this material to its minimal structural unit. Study identified two distinct quantum critical phenomena emerging between the Mott insulating phase and superconducting phase. This minimal system clarifies the underlying mechanisms of cuprate superconductivity.
