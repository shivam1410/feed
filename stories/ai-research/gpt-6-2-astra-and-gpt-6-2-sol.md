OpenAI released two new models. GPT-6.2 Astra is for hard projects, and GPT-6.2 Sol is for everyday work. Astra costs $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens, which is 80% of its predecessor's price. Cached input fell to $0.40 per million, from $1. Sol stays at $2 and $10, half the price of GPT-5.6 Sol. Simon Willison found that neither new model turned Yonkers into a mech as well as Claude Fable 5.2 or Gemini 4 Carbon. The cheaper cached rate helps long agent sessions.
