Study of long-horizon planning in foundation model agents across three developmental stages reveals that explicit world models via chain-of-thought state transitions enable stronger generalization than atomic skills alone; suboptimal trajectories severely degrade long-horizon performance because errors amplify over steps. Post-training via GRPO and on-policy distillation shows different effectiveness regions—OPD outperforms GRPO in low-quality, long-horizon settings by maintaining consistent update directions. Distinguishing general planning patterns from task-specific knowledge helps identify where post-training interventions succeed or fail.
