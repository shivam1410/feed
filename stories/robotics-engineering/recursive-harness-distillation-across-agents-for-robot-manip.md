Researchers developed Recursive Harness Distillation, where skilled robot agents distill learned intervention strategies into playbooks for less capable agents. Real-world manipulation success improved from 37.3% to 64.0%. The playbook boosted performance across different agents and tasks without retraining models, showing robots can systematically accumulate and share knowledge.
