Since 2005, volcanic eruptions and extreme wildfires have systematically increased stratospheric water vapor, reveal observations and models. These episodic aerosol perturbations were previously overlooked as a driver of upper-atmosphere humidity changes. This finding reshapes understanding of how episodic surface events propagate into the upper atmosphere and affect climate variables.
