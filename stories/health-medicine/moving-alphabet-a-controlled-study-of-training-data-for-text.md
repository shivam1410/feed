Training data quality—not just model size—is critical for text-to-video generation. Using a controlled testbed (Moving Alphabet) with procedurally rendered letters, researchers found three key insights: balanced content distribution matters for generalization, caption quality directly impacts model performance and efficiency, and even classifier-free guidance cannot fully recover from poor pre-training data. These findings highlight that video-text alignment depends on data curation, not just compute scaling, suggesting the field should prioritize pre-training data science.
