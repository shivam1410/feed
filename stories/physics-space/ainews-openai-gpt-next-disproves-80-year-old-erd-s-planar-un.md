OpenAI's general-purpose language model (reportedly GPT 5.6) solved an 80-year-old unsolved mathematics problem—the Erdős planar unit distance conjecture from 1946—using extended reasoning for under 32 hours of compute at under $1000 cost. The model generated 125 pages of detailed mathematical reasoning, discovering novel construction families that improve upon square-grid solutions. Importantly, this general-purpose model achieved the result without domain-specific mathematical scaffolding, proof assistants, or specialized training, unlike prior AlphaProof/Lean-style systems. The breakthrough suggests long-horizon reasoning may generalize beyond mathematics.
