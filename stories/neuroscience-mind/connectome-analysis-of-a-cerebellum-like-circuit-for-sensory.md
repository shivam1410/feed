Connectome analysis, electrophysiology, and computational modeling of electric fish neural circuits reveal how distributed synaptic plasticity across multiple network layers cooperates to enable fast, accurate, and noise-robust sensory learning. These cerebellum-like circuits demonstrate sophisticated plasticity mechanisms that achieve precise sensory prediction despite neural noise, illuminating fundamental learning principles applicable across neuroscience and artificial intelligence.
