Researchers created ResearchTrails, a dataset mining git commit histories to capture the messy process of research exploration. They extract structured trajectories showing successive changes to methods, experiments, and ablations. These intermediate steps contain signals about research thinking that final papers never reveal. This could help AI systems learn from discovery itself, not just its products.
