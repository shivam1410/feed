DMM is a decentralized pathfinding system where agents refine their action intents through repeated communication rounds, inspired by diffusion model denoising. Instead of each agent picking an action once independently, they iteratively adjust choices to achieve compatible joint actions. It solved 1,598 of 1,600 test cases and scales to over a million simultaneously acting agents in obstacle-filled spaces.
