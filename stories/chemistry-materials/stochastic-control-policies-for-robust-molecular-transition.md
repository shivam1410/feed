Stochastic control policies improve molecular transition path sampling by learning probability distributions over control forces rather than deterministic policies. Two variants (FS-TPS and LaS-TPS) were tested across three biomolecules from small (alanine dipeptide) to large (BBL protein). Stochastic approaches consistently increased transition success rates and path quality while reducing seed-dependence compared to deterministic baselines.
