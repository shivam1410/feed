OpenAI released GPT-6.1 Terra, a fast, efficient model. Its focus is visual reasoning, computer use and clearer writing. OpenAI calls it a significant step up from GPT-5.6 Terra. It sits mid-lineup, like Sonnet at Anthropic. OpenAI's Ted Sanders said it is probably better than Astra and Sol at technical communication. It is similar on vision and computer use, but much faster. For API users, it is a cheaper middle option than Sol and smarter than Luna.
