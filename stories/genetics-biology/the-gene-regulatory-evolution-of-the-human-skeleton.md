Using hybrid human-ape skeletal cells and massively parallel reporter assays, researchers identified how cis-regulatory elements (gene switches) differ between species and control skeletal development. Variations in these regulatory DNA regions, not the protein-coding genes themselves, explain why human skeletons differ dramatically from primates. This reveals that gene regulation is the primary driver of human skeletal uniqueness.
