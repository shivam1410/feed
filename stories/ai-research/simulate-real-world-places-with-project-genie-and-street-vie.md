Google is integrating Project Genie, an AI world-generation system, with nearly 20 years of Street View imagery to enable creation of virtual environments anchored in real geographic locations. This combination allows users to build realistic simulated worlds grounded in authentic visual data from real places. By combining generative AI with vast real-world geographic data, the system enables location-specific simulation and immersive environment creation.
