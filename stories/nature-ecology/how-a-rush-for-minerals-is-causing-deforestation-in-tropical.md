Mining causes significant deforestation in tropical Africa. An analysis of mining sites in sub-Saharan Africa from 2001 to 2020 quantified forest loss caused by mineral extraction. This research is critical for conservation planning and policy, particularly in biodiversity-rich regions where mining expansion threatens endangered ecosystems and carbon stores.
