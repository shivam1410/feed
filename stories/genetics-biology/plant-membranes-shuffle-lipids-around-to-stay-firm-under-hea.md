Rice plants rapidly adjust the composition of lipids in their cell membranes when exposed to high temperatures, counteracting the weakening and leakiness that heat normally causes. This lipid reshuffling mechanism helps plants survive heatwaves, offering insight into how crops might adapt to climate-driven temperature extremes.
