A physics-chemistry-informed neural network (PCINN) predicts surface coverage in spatial atomic layer deposition roughly 50,000× faster than CFD—~7 ms per query. Trained from only 30 cases across four orders of magnitude, it achieves R²_log=0.998. The interpretable hybrid architecture hard-codes surface kinetics, enabling robust identifiability of adsorption energy and desorption rate, making real-time operating-window scans feasible for high-throughput ALD manufacturing.
