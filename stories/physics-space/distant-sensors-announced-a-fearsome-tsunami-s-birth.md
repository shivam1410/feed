Distant seismic sensors detected an underwater volcano collapse in real time and traced how the eruption triggered a destructive tsunami. By analyzing seismic data, scientists pinpointed the exact moment and mechanics of the volcanic failure, revealing the causal chain from eruption to tsunami generation. This demonstrates the power of global seismic monitoring networks to identify rare catastrophic events and provide crucial early warning. Understanding these trigger mechanisms improves tsunami forecasting and hazard assessment, potentially saving lives through faster evacuation and better coastal preparedness.
