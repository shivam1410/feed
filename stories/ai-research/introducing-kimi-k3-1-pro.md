Moonshot released Kimi K3.1 Pro through its API and promises full open weights by November 4, 2026. It costs $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, half the price of Claude Opus 5.5. Artificial Analysis scored it 55, ahead of Claude Fable 5.1, GPT-6 Astra and Gemini 4 Argon at 53. Only Claude Opus 5.5, at 58, scored higher. The author calls it the first soon-to-be open weight model to score this high against the frontier.
