This work tackles distilling reasoning from large to small language models by framing it as constrained reinforcement learning rather than supervised mimicry. Instead of averaging over reward hacking or using soft KL penalties, it enforces a worst-case constraint on teacher agreement at every step of the reasoning chain. The derived unaugmented constrained MDP avoids expensive dual solvers and test-time teacher dependence.
