European wetlands face severe fragmentation, limiting ecological function and resilience. Scientists used satellite imagery and machine learning to map six wetland types and identify land-use disturbance patterns across multiple countries. Analysis reveals restoration needs are unevenly distributed—some areas require immediate intervention while others remain relatively intact. Fragmentation results from agricultural conversion, urban expansion, and hydrological modification. Understanding spatial fragmentation enables targeted, cost-effective restoration strategies. Policymakers can prioritize resources where ecological recovery potential is highest rather than applying uniform approaches across diverse landscapes.
