A comprehensive review identifies conserved mechanisms shared between human antiviral immunity and bacterial anti-phage defense systems. Both humans and bacteria employ ancient, common strategies for pathogen sensing, signal transduction, and immune effector functions. These universal principles reveal fundamental rules governing host-pathogen interactions and demonstrate that viral defense mechanisms are deeply conserved across all domains of life.
