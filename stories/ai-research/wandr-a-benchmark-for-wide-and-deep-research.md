WANDR is a 500-task benchmark for research agents requiring breadth—discovering entities meeting specified criteria—and depth via coordinated web searches. Tasks range from dozens to thousands of records, represented as qualification key hierarchies. Rather than static gold answers, task-specific judges verify records by refetching cited pages and evaluating against evidence. Soft and hard precision, recall, and F1 scores assess factual quality, coverage, and completeness. Evaluated on six production systems, WANDR remains far from saturated, supporting workflows like market mapping, due diligence, literature review, and talent sourcing.
