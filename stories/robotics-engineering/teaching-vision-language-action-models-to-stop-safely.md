Robots can be told to refuse a task, but they may still finish it if they take over midway. This team built SAFI to fix that. It teaches the robot to keep its arm still and its gripper steady when a task is prohibited. On LIBERO tests, the banned task succeeded only 1.3% of the time after takeover. There were no recorded physical incidents, and other skills stayed strong. A robot that drops an object is not fully safe, even if it stops.
