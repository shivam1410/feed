World Pilot enhances Vision-Language-Action models by incorporating dynamic scene evolution and trajectory priors from a World-Action Model. This integration enables superior performance in zero-shot out-of-distribution manipulation tasks—physical scenarios the model hasn't encountered during training. By leveraging prior knowledge about environment changes and object movement, World Pilot helps robots manipulate objects effectively in novel situations without requiring task-specific training.
