EgoTools is a new dataset and benchmark for teaching AI to understand tool use in videos. It has 100 hours of real egocentric footage with 1,000 QA pairs testing perception, geometry, procedure, and reasoning. Top models score 66.9% overall but only 51.7% on grounding tools visually. Most of the performance gap comes from failing to connect what they see to how tools work.
