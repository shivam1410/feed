Exquisite early Cambrian fossils of two bryozoan species—Protomelission gatehousei and Dayingomelission hexaclitia—preserve remarkably detailed soft-tissue anatomy and fine skeletal microstructure, enabling definitive identification of these organisms as true bryozoans. The discovery indicates that the evolutionary origin of the bryozoan phylum extends deeper into the Cambrian period than previously apparent. The exceptional preservation of anatomical details provides rare windows into early animal body plan organization. Why it matters: These high-fidelity fossils significantly refine our understanding of animal evolution during the Cambrian explosion and demonstrate that complex bryozoan body plans evolved far earlier than previously recognized, providing crucial fossil evidence for reconstructing early animal diversification and phylum origins.
