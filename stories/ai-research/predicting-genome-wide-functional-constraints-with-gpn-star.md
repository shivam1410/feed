GPN-Star, a phylogeny-aware genomic language model, predicts how genetic variants affect function across entire genomes and multiple species. Trained on whole-genome alignment data, the model scales flexibly to handle vast genomic datasets. It enables researchers to anticipate functional consequences of mutations without exhaustive experimental validation. This tool accelerates identification of disease-causing variants and understanding of evolutionary constraints on genes, applicable across diverse organisms.
