HypoAD, an atlas of the human hypothalamus in ageing and Alzheimer’s disease that combines high-resolution magnetic resonance imaging from 458 individuals with single-nucleus RNA sequencing of 614,403 nuclei, reveals that metabolism- and circadian-regulating subregions, and their inhibitory neurons and microglia, are particularly vulnerable.
