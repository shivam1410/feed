NeuroPB scales neural decoding for brain-computer interfaces by transferring knowledge from large-scale motor behavior data to limited paired neural-behavioral recordings. A motor encoder pretrained on behavioral data is aligned with neural activity using paired samples, then optimized to reconstruct continuous movement. Achieves 11% R² improvement on center-out and 8% on random-target trajectory decoding versus training from scratch. Robotic trajectory pretraining achieves performance comparable to primate pretraining, demonstrating kinematic structure transfers across biological and artificial systems.
