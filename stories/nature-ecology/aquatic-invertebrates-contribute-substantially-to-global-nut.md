Analysis of aquatic invertebrate nutritional content reveals their global production is a rich source of nutrients crucial for human health. This work provides a foundation for better recognition of these valuable invertebrate nutrients in conservation, management, and policy efforts. The finding matters because it highlights aquatic invertebrate production as an underutilized source of essential micronutrients. Better integration of invertebrate food production into policy and management could help address global micronutrient deficiencies and sustainability challenges.
