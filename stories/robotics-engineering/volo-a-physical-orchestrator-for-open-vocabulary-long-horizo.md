VoLoAgent integrates vision-language models with robotic systems to enable open-vocabulary long-horizon manipulation tasks by combining language understanding with physical control. The system interprets varied natural language instructions and executes complex multi-step robotic operations without task-specific training. This advancement matters because most robotic manipulation requires extensive hand-coding or task-specific programming. VoLo bridges the gap between language understanding and physical action, advancing robots that can follow diverse human instructions for different manipulation objectives. This enables robots to generalize across tasks and environments using natural language as a unified control interface.
