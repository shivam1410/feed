Researchers trained neural networks to learn Lagrangian and Hamiltonian physics equations from damped oscillator data. Both network types successfully captured the oscillators' behavior despite energy loss and time-dependent equations. This extends physics-informed neural networks beyond textbook systems into realistic scenarios with dissipation.
