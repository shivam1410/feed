RoboDojo is a comprehensive benchmark designed to standardize evaluation of generalist robot manipulation policies across both simulated and real-world environments. The benchmark spans diverse manipulation tasks and multiple evaluation dimensions, enabling researchers to systematically assess how well policies transfer from simulation to reality and generalize across different robot configurations and gripper types. By providing a unified evaluation framework, RoboDojo addresses the fragmentation in robot learning research where different teams use incompatible tasks and metrics, making progress difficult to measure and compare. This standardization accelerates development of more capable and broadly applicable robotic manipulation systems.
