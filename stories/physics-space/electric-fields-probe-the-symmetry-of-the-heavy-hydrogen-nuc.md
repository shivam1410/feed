Physicists probing deuterium nuclei—the nuclei of heavy hydrogen atoms containing one proton and one neutron—with electric fields have found no evidence of nuclear asymmetry. Deuterium nuclei respond to electrical fields in ways fully consistent with conventional theories of particle physics and nuclear structure. This finding confirms existing theoretical predictions about how atomic nuclei behave under external electric forces. While confirming established theory rather than overturning it, this work validates current models of nuclear physics and strengthens confidence in fundamental understanding of particle behavior.
