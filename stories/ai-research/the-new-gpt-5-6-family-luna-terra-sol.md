OpenAI released three new GPT-5.6 models—Luna, Terra, and Sol (priced $1/$6, $2.50/$15, and $5/$30 per 1M input/output tokens respectively)—all featuring one-million-token context windows and February 2026 knowledge cutoff. On Agents' Last Exam, an evaluation of long-running professional workflows across 55 fields, GPT-5.6 Sol scored 53.6 compared to Claude Fable 5's 40.5—a 13.1-point advantage. Smaller models Terra and Luna also outperform Fable 5 at roughly one-sixteenth the cost, emphasizing efficiency. However, Fable 5 dominates SWE-Bench Pro with 80% versus Sol's 64.6%, revealing domain-specific strengths. These releases demonstrate OpenAI's push toward more cost-effective agentic AI.
