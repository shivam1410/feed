A Moderna-developed personalized mRNA vaccine successfully prevented melanoma recurrence in trial participants, demonstrating the therapeutic potential of cancer vaccines tailored to individual tumors. Results support using genetic sequencing to design patient-specific treatments targeting unique cancer mutations. This strategy could extend beyond melanoma to other cancer types, potentially transforming oncology through customized immunotherapies. The advance represents a major step toward personalized medicine and precision cancer treatment.
