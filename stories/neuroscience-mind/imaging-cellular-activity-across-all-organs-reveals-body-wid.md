A whole-body imaging system captures cellular activity throughout an entire zebrafish, revealing how organs coordinate through distributed circuits. The technique records dynamic cellular responses across all major organ systems simultaneously, identifying previously unknown body-wide functional networks. This comprehensive mapping of intercellular communication demonstrates how local cellular processes integrate into coordinated biological systems. The findings illustrate how organs communicate and synchronize through distributed neural and physiological pathways.
