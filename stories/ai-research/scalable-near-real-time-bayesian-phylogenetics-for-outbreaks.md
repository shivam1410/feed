Delphy enables public health authorities to perform near-real-time and scalable Bayesian phylogenetic analysis of viral outbreak data with state-of-the-art accuracy and minimal friction. Traditional phylogenetic methods are computationally intensive and slow, limiting response speed. Delphy makes analysis accessible and rapid, allowing public health bodies anywhere to identify transmission patterns, track viral evolution, and respond faster to emerging threats. The software works directly on outbreak data without excessive technical barriers. During pandemics or epidemics, faster transmission chain analysis directly improves public health response effectiveness. The tool democratizes access to advanced outbreak analysis capabilities globally, enabling evidence-based rapid response.
