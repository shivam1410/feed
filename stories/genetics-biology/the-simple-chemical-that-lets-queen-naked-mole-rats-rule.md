Scientists have solved a long-standing evolutionary mystery by identifying the chemical mechanism maintaining the extreme social hierarchy in naked mole rat colonies. The discovery reveals how a single compound produces and maintains the reproductive suppression and social structure characteristic of these eusocial mammals. Understanding this molecular control system provides insight into how mammalian social hierarchies evolved and function at a biochemical level. The finding connects molecular biology with behavioral ecology.
