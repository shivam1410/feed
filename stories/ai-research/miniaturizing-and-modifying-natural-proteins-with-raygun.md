Researchers introduced Raygun, an AI framework that miniaturizes and modifies natural proteins while preserving their function. The system uses probabilistic sequence encoding derived from language model embeddings within a generative AI approach. By converting proteins to probabilistic representations, Raygun can design smaller, modified versions while maintaining native architecture and functional integrity. Protein miniaturization is valuable for drug development, synthetic biology, and biotechnology because smaller proteins are easier to manufacture, deliver to target tissues, and engineer for specific applications. This advance demonstrates how language models and generative AI can accelerate protein engineering and open new possibilities for therapeutic biology.
