Nik Suresh documents widespread AI mania disconnecting corporate strategy from competence and reality. An executive who had never used ChatGPT created an AI-centric strategy for a $2B+ organization. Engineers rewrite entire codebases in unfamiliar languages to boost performance metrics. Vendors avoid honest capability assessments because contradicting customer executives' 100x productivity claims risks contract cancellation. This misalignment creates perverse incentives: credibility depends on echoing inflated expectations, not delivering realistic outcomes. The phenomenon reveals how organizational fear of AI irrelevance, combined with prestige pressure and vendor-customer mutual reinforcement of hype, warps decision-making at scale. Reality increasingly diverges from strategy as executives make commitments without technical grounding.
