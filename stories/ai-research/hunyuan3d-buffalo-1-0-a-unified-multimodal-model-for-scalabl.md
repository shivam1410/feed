Hunyuan3D-Buffalo 1.0 unifies 3D understanding, generation, editing, and part-based generation in one architecture. Trained on an 87M-scale corpus with 25M understanding samples, 50M text-to-3D pairs, and 12M editing pairs, it combines a vision-language model with a diffusion transformer for high-fidelity synthesis. The VLM provides semantic and spatial understanding while editing preserves source structure. Experiments show state-of-the-art 3D generation and editing performance with strong understanding capabilities.
