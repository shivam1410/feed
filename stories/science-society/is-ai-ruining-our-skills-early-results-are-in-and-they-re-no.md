Studies reveal reliance on artificial intelligence tools is degrading professional skills in physicians and software engineers. Research shows measurable declines in diagnostic reasoning among doctors and coding abilities among programmers using AI assistance regularly. The concern centers on 'skill atrophy'—erosion of core competencies when practitioners outsource cognitive work. While AI tools boost short-term productivity, this dependency weakens underlying expertise needed to recognize tool failures, catch errors, or handle novel problems. Findings suggest growing tension between AI adoption and professional capability maintenance.
