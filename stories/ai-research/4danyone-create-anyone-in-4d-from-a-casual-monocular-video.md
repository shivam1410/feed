4DAnyone reconstructs dynamic 4D human figures from a single monocular video by generating multiview-consistent videos—synthetic video views that maintain spatial and temporal consistency across different viewing angles—and lifting this multiview data into 4D Gaussian Splatting representation. The method overcomes scaling bottlenecks through reference and target context designs that stabilize training. Starting from a single camera angle, the system synthesizes a 360-degree video representation and converts it into a 4D model capturing both human shape and motion dynamics. This technology could enable 3D human capture from casual smartphone video for applications including animation, virtual try-on systems, motion capture, and digital twins without specialized multi-camera studio setups.
