RoboTok improves robot learning by retrieving relevant human manipulation videos from the internet. The system uses a latent motion space derived from 3D hand trajectories to identify relevant demonstrations. These retrieved human videos train robot policies more effectively than synthetic or limited datasets alone. This approach leverages the vast repository of human manipulation videos online to bootstrap robot dexterity without requiring new human demonstrations.
