World Tracing introduces generative 3D geometry prediction extending beyond visible surfaces to predict complete structure including hidden or occluded regions. Using a diffusion transformer with pixel-space flow matching, the method generates pixel-aligned geometry predictions. By completing hidden surfaces alongside visible ones, the model creates fuller 3D scene understanding—important for robotics, simulation, and reconstruction where occluded geometry affects interaction and planning. This represents a shift from vision-based reconstruction toward generative completion of unobserved structure.
