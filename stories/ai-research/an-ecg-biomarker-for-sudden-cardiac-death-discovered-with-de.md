A deep-learning model trained on electrocardiogram waveforms has identified an easily visible biomarker that predicts sudden cardiac death more accurately than current clinical methods. The breakthrough's practical value lies in simplicity: the biomarker can be readily detected on standard ECG readings, making it deployable across healthcare settings without requiring specialized equipment. Sudden cardiac death claims hundreds of thousands of lives annually, often striking without warning in seemingly healthy individuals. This AI-discovered predictor could transform risk stratification, allowing clinicians to identify high-risk patients and deploy preventive interventions before life-threatening events occur, with detection feasible immediately.
