WEAVER is a multi-view world model that learns to simulate robotic environments with high fidelity and temporal consistency using flow-matching loss for physical accuracy. The model excels at evaluating action success, improving existing policies, and planning at test time. World models enable robots to reason about how actions affect their environment. By achieving both visual fidelity and physical consistency, WEAVER demonstrates that world models directly improve robot decision-making and manipulation without separate policy training.
