LLM agents can learn during a task without retraining the whole model. ReTTT has the agent reflect on feedback, keep lessons in an explicit memory, and update a small add-on module during the episode while the backbone stays frozen. It was tested on ALFWorld, ScienceWorld, and SWE-bench Lite with two model families. It beat trajectory-based test-time training in all six settings, improving task success by up to 12.14 percentage points. It matters because failed attempts turn into more useful guidance.
