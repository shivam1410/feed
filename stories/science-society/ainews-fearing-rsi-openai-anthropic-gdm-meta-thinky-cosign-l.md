Over 1,000 employees from most major frontier AI labs—OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Meta, and others excluding X.ai—cosigned a statement warning that leading companies could be close to automating AI research itself. The signers express concern that capability development is accelerating beyond humanity's ability to understand or control resulting systems. Unlike a 2023 pause proposal that industry leaders ignored, this statement acknowledges competitive pressures preventing unilateral slowing and calls for U.S. government support developing international technical and governance tools to deliberately pace frontier-wide AI progress rather than race without oversight.
