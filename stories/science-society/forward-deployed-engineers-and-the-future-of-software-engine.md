Sierra's head of agent engineering describes the emerging "agent engineer" role as combining systems integration, agent development, customer operations knowledge, and end-user experience understanding. Forward deployed engineering is defined more by accountability to customers than by specific skillsets. The role reflects convergence of product and customer-facing engineering.
