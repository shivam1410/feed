Genetically modified probiotic bacteria reduce elevated blood glucose levels in animal trials, demonstrating a new biological strategy for diabetes management. This engineered approach harnesses modified gut microbiota as a therapeutic vector. The bacterial therapy demonstrates efficacy in animal disease models. The work represents a departure from conventional pharmaceutical treatment of diabetes, offering an oral intervention strategy delivered through living organisms. This approach suggests potential for non-invasive, microbiome-based therapies in managing glucose control.
