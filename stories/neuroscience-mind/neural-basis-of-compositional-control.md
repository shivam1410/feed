During flexible decision-making tasks, the human brain dynamically blends multiple control policies rather than continuously updating one. Brain imaging shows the hippocampus estimates hidden states, anterior cingulate cortex switches between policies, and orbitofrontal cortex provides value-based context. This reveals how the brain achieves behavioral flexibility—composing different strategies and switching between them based on task demands rather than running a single adaptive controller.
