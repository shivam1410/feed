Astronomers confirmed that jets shooting out from black holes create a halo effect in the surrounding gas cloud, exactly as physicists had predicted decades ago. The observation matches long-held theoretical expectations about these extreme cosmic objects and their interactions. It provides new evidence about how black holes actively influence and shape the hot gas in their galactic environments through their incredibly powerful jets of matter and energy.
