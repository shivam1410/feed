Google DeepMind is launching an Accelerator program in the Asia-Pacific region to support green technology development and address environmental risks. The region is economically vital but highly vulnerable to climate change impacts. While green technologies are gaining adoption, accelerated development and deployment are needed. This program aims to bridge the gap between technological potential and real-world implementation, supporting entrepreneurs working on climate solutions in a region critical to global stability.
