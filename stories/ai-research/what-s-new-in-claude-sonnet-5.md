Claude Sonnet 5 delivers performance close to Opus 4.8 at lower advertised prices, with 1 million token context and 128,000 maximum output tokens. Adaptive thinking is enabled by default. However, a new tokenizer increases token counts by approximately 30% for identical text versus Sonnet 4.6—effectively offsetting the price benefit. The model removes sampling parameters (temperature, top_p, top_k) and maintains identical per-token pricing as Sonnet 4.6 ($3/million input, $15/million output) with introductory discount until August 31st. The capability improvement is significant, but the tokenizer change represents a hidden cost increase.
