Satellite imagery detected accelerated movement of a glacier-rock mass just days before it collapsed and triggered a deadly flash flood in Nepal. The images captured the warning signs of the imminent disaster. This capability demonstrates how satellite surveillance of unstable mountain terrain could provide early warning systems for similar catastrophic events, potentially saving lives through timely evacuation and response planning.
