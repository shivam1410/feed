DataPrep-Bench introduces the first unified benchmark measuring how effectively LLMs prepare training data across the complete pipeline—data construction and quality evaluation. Spanning six domains, the benchmark released Data-Construction-Skill, a skill-guided agent improving baseline performance by nearly 20 points on Llama-3.1-8B finance tasks, plus the Distributional Alignment Score (DAS) for evaluating dataset quality. This matters because training data quality fundamentally determines LLM capabilities, yet no standardized methodology existed to measure preparation effectiveness before this work.
