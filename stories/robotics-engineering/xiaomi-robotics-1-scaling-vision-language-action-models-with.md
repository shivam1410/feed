Xiaomi-Robotics-1 is a vision-language-action model trained on over 100,000 hours of real-world robot manipulation trajectories via UMI devices, enabling out-of-the-box performance on diverse mobile manipulation tasks in unseen environments. A scalable auto-labeling pipeline annotates trajectory clips with natural language descriptions of state transitions. Model performance improves consistently with increased training data and parameters; this scaling behavior transfers to post-training fine-tuning on complex dexterous tasks with high data efficiency.
