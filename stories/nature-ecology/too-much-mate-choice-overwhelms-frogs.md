Female tree frogs demonstrate markedly reduced selectivity in mate choice when exposed to large choruses of calling males, a phenomenon reflecting sensory overload effects on reproductive decision-making. This shows how information abundance can impair decision quality even in biological contexts. The finding matters for understanding how environmental conditions such as increased population density or acoustic pollution in natural habitats can disrupt successful breeding behaviors and potentially compromise population sustainability.
