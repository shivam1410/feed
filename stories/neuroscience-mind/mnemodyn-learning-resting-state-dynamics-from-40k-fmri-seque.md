MnemoDyn applies dynamical-systems modeling to resting-state fMRI, trained on 40,000 sequences. Using multi-resolution temporal modeling of brain parcellations, it outperforms transformer baselines while remaining compute-efficient and generalizing across populations and scanning protocols. Performance on small-sample studies improves accessibility of fMRI research.
