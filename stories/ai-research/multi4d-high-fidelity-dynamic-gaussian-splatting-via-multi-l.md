Dynamic 3D Gaussian splatting methods traditionally face a trade-off between maintaining motion consistency across frames and achieving high visual fidelity in rendered output. Multi4D addresses this tension through a novel multi-level competitive allocation framework enabling adaptive specialization of Gaussian components. Different components efficiently specialize in their domains, improving temporal coherence and visual quality simultaneously. The framework optimizes resource allocation across hierarchical levels, systematically reducing conflicts between motion and quality objectives. This approach improves 3D video generation for visual effects, virtual reality, and interactive media.
