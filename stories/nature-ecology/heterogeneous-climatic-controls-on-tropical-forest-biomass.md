Tropical forest carbon storage depends on complex, region-specific interactions between climate, topography, and soil—not climate alone. Analysis across three major tropical regions shows aboveground biomass responds differently to climatic factors depending on landscape and soil properties. Understanding these multi-factor interactions is essential for predicting how climate change will alter forest carbon storage capacity.
