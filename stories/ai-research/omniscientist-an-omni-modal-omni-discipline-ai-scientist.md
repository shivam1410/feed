OmniScientist is an autonomous AI system conducting multidisciplinary scientific research across diverse data types. It works end-to-end from heterogeneous raw evidence—text, images, audio, videos, structured data—using autonomous agents with lifecycle-wide perception to generate novel findings. This matters because scientific discovery increasingly crosses traditional discipline boundaries and valuable evidence exists in non-text formats. By automating evidence integration and hypothesis generation across modalities, OmniScientist accelerates discovery and removes human biases from interpretation, potentially uncovering insights humans might miss across interdisciplinary research domains.
