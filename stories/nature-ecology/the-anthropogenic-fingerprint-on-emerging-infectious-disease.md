Emerging infectious disease outbreak risks exist almost everywhere worldwide, but the highest risks are found in landscapes where human populations, livestock farms and fragmented natural ecosystems coexist together. Unfortunately, detection and data collection on actual outbreaks remain severely limited by significant inequalities in healthcare access across the globe. Understanding these high-risk landscapes where disease emergence is most likely is crucial for anticipating future pandemic threats.
