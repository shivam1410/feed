Tropical tree species and populations within the same species show variable tolerance to drought conditions. Research reveals that genetic variation in drought resilience exists not only between different species but also within populations of a single species. This intraspecific variation is significant for conservation planning and forestry management, as it suggests that certain tree populations may be better adapted to future climate conditions and drought stress than others.
