GyroNovo sequences unknown peptides from sparse mass spectra by using decoder errors to guide which missing fragments are worth recovering. It incorporates physics-aware attention with rotary embeddings to encode how peptides actually fragment. This tackles a key challenge in proteomics: identifying unknown peptides from experimental spectra without relying on a reference database for comparison.
