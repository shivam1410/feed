Researchers identified geometric stability as a distinct measure of neural representation quality, quantifying how consistently pairwise stimulus distances remain across trials. Unlike temporal stability (consistency of firing patterns over time) and decoding accuracy (predictability from neural activity), geometric stability reveals how neural populations organize information about stimulus relationships independently. This metric shows neural geometry itself encodes meaningful information separate from moment-to-moment firing patterns.
