Bilayer materials exhibit rich properties from interplay of strong intra-layer bonding and weak inter-layer van der Waals interactions, but discovering new materials computationally is expensive. Researchers developed a two-stage framework: MatterSim-D3 efficiently generates DFT-quality bilayer structures from monolayer building blocks, while BDIP-Net (Bilayer Dual-Interaction Potential Network) predicts properties by explicitly modeling intra-layer and inter-layer interactions separately. Testing across homobilayers, heterobilayers, and twisted bilayer systems showed the workflow reproduces expensive DFT results while BDIP-Net consistently outperforms existing graph neural networks.
