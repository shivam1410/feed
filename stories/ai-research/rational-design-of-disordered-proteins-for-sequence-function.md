Researchers developed GOOSE, a method enabling design and functional testing of thousands of disordered protein region sequences. Disordered proteins—regions lacking rigid three-dimensional structure but performing critical cellular functions—are poorly understood compared to structured proteins. GOOSE systematically reveals how amino acid sequences relate to biological function in these proteins. Understanding disordered regions advances protein engineering and reveals new regulatory mechanisms in an understudied area of molecular biology.
