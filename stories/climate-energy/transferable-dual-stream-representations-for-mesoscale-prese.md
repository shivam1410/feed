EddyFlow uses physics-informed representation learning for kilometer-scale sea surface temperature downscaling, addressing the critical challenge of preserving physically meaningful multi-scale structure. Deep learning models often produce overly smooth outputs missing mesoscale variability critical to ocean dynamics. Trained on Gulf of St. Lawrence and evaluated on Bay of Fundy and Gulf of Mexico, EddyFlow reduces zero-shot RMSE by 21%, achieves 85.6% skill versus persistence on unseen domains, and maintains near-ideal spectral fidelity (PSD ratio ≈1.00).
