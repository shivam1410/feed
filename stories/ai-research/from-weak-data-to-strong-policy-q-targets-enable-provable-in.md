Meet QTPT (Q-Target Pretrained Transformers), a new take on in-context reinforcement learning. Instead of just copying behavior from weak training data, it learns to estimate action values from context. This makes it much more robust when trajectories are messy or suboptimal. In tests, it jumped accuracy from 57% to 70% on math problems using small model ensembles.
