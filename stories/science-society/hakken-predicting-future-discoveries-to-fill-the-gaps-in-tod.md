Hakken is a knowledge prediction system discovering novel scientific relationships not deducible from existing knowledge. It combines transformers trained on temporal knowledge graphs from publications with language model semantics. Applied to biomedical research, Hakken scored 1.5 million candidate hypotheses on aging. Critically, two predictions were confirmed with significant implications for drug discovery and repurposing. This demonstrates AI's potential to accelerate scientific discovery by identifying promising research directions that humans might miss.
