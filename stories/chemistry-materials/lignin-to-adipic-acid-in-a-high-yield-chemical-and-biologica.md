A chemical and biological redox process converts lignin from poplar into adipic acid at high yields, mirroring petrochemical refining procedures. Adipic acid is a valuable industrial bioproduct used in manufacturing. This two-step process demonstrates a scalable pathway transforming lignocellulosic waste into useful chemicals, advancing sustainable bioproduction while reducing petroleum dependence and maintaining yield efficiency.
