Single-cell genetic mapping of inflammatory bowel disease identified distal genetic variants enriched in enhancer regions that control gene expression. These variants detected at the individual cell-type level co-localized more frequently with genome-wide association study findings than variants identified through tissue-level analysis. Cell-type resolution reveals that IBD genetic risk is shaped by variants active in specific cell populations, providing deeper mechanistic insights than population-level studies.
