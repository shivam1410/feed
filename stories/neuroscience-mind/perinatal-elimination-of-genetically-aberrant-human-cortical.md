Developing human brains carry many genetic errors before birth. Single-neuron sequencing showed that widespread genomic abnormalities are common in the womb. Around birth, most of those faulty neurons are largely eliminated. The findings point to genome quality control during development. It matters because it suggests the brain filters out damaged cells early in life.
