NVIDIA is acquiring HuggingFace for $13 billion, approximately 80 times the company's $150 million annual revenue—roughly double NVIDIA's initial $7 billion offer from January 2026. HuggingFace has doubled its customer base in 2026 alone, establishing itself as the primary open-source AI model hub. The acquisition signals NVIDIA's commitment to the open-model ecosystem and occurs amid competition from other fast models and discussion about Western open AI. This consolidation centralizes control over model distribution infrastructure in a critical AI player.
