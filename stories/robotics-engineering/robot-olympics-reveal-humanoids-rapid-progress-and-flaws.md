Humanoid robots competing in 'Robot Olympics' demonstrated rapid progress by setting records in athletic events including sprints. However, the same robots revealed significant limitations when attempting practical manual tasks such as hammering nails, indicating that athletic capability does not translate to real-world manual work. The results showcase both advances in humanoid robotics and persistent challenges in developing robots for versatile practical application.
