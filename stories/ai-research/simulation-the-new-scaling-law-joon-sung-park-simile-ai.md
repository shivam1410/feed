Simile AI's $2 billion Series B reflects confidence in behavioral simulation. The company achieves 85-99% accuracy simulating human behavior versus human focus groups. Methods: long-form interviews, observational data, randomized controlled trials, population and individual-level modeling with post-training on causal decision mechanisms. Evolved from generative agents (Smallville, 2023) where AI characters showed memory and emergent behavior. Now deployed with Fortune 100 clients like CVS. Broader implication: could we simulate billions of people to test decisions before implementation?
