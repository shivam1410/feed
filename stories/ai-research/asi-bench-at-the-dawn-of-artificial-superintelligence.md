ASI-Bench, built by 40+ experts over 31,000+ hours, benchmarks 60 research projects across 11 scientific domains to evaluate autonomous scientific capability. Results reveal current AI's critical limitation: performance drops sharply from 50.91 with full guidance to 29.10 when only methods are specified and 26.62 when AI determines methods independently. Across 18 state-of-the-art configurations, this pattern persists. Current AI excels executing provided procedures but struggles substantially when independently designing research approaches. These findings show artificial superintelligence requires autonomous scientific methodology design and original knowledge creation—capabilities current systems lack.
