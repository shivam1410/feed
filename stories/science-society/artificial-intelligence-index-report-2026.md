AI technology is advancing faster than governance frameworks, evaluation methods, and educational systems can keep pace. The ninth AI Index Report highlights this critical gap, tracking how systems test reasoning, safety, and real-world task execution while exposing measurement reliability challenges. New chapters examine AI's economic value, labor market effects, and expanding influence in science and medicine. The report frames institutional readiness—governance structures, evaluation methods, education systems, and data infrastructure—as essential infrastructure that currently lags AI capability, making preparedness frameworks a central concern.
