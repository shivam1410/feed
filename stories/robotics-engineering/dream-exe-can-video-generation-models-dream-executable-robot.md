Video generation visual quality doesn't predict executable robot motion. When video models were tested on robotic manipulation tasks, visually impressive outputs often failed to translate into physically feasible movements. This gap reveals that visual fidelity and physical plausibility require separate evaluation, challenging assumptions about model transfer to physical domains.
