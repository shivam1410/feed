Scientists compiled the Great Barrier Reef Microbial Genomes Database containing genetic sequences from prokaryotic, viral, and eukaryotic organisms recovered from reef seawater samples. This comprehensive genomic resource documents microbial communities inhabiting one of Earth's most biodiverse ecosystems. The database serves as a foundation for studying marine microbial community composition, functions, and environmental interactions. Researchers can use these genomes to understand disease dynamics, nutrient cycling, and ecosystem resilience. The resource supports both fundamental research into reef ecology and applied management efforts monitoring reef health and predicting responses to environmental stressors like warming and acidification.
