Google DeepMind proposes a cognitive framework for evaluating artificial general intelligence across multiple dimensions rather than a single metric, and launches a Kaggle hackathon to crowdsource development of capability benchmarks. The effort aims to set consensus standards for measuring AGI progress and defining which capabilities matter before we approach AGI.
