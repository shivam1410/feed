The Great Barrier Reef Microbial Genomes Database is a comprehensive genomic resource compiling complete genetic sequences of prokaryotic, viral, and eukaryotic genomes extracted from seawater samples collected throughout the Great Barrier Reef. The database provides fundamental genetic information about the microbial communities inhabiting one of Earth's most biodiverse ecosystems. By cataloging the full genetic diversity of microscopic organisms, the resource enables researchers to study marine microbial ecology, community structure, and ecological function within coral reef environments. This supports ecosystem-level research, conservation management, and understanding how microbial communities sustain reef health and resilience under environmental pressures.
