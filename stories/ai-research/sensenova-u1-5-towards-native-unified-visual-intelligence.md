SenseNova-U1.5 is an 8-billion-parameter unified multimodal model performing visual understanding, reasoning, and generation without traditional encoders or VAEs. The design uses patch reconstruction, curated training data, expert optimization, and on-policy distillation to achieve high fidelity. Its native multimodal approach processes images directly without separate feature extraction layers. This matters because unified models are more efficient than pipeline approaches; eliminating encoder bottlenecks improves speed and reduces memory requirements.
