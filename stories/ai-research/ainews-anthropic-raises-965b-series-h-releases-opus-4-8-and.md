Anthropic announced a Series H funding round raising $65 billion at a $900B pre-money valuation, with backing from hyperscalers and memory-industry investors. The company reported $47B annual revenue run-rate, up from $9B in December, now leading OpenAI in several headline metrics excluding compute and non-coding benchmarks. Anthropic released Opus 4.8, addressing community concerns and achieving state-of-the-art performance on economically relevant benchmarks. Claude Code's Dynamic Workflows feature (called "ultracode") enables massively parallel multi-agent orchestration, exemplified by a 750,000-line Bun codebase rewrite completed in 6 days. These advances position AI-assisted development at unprecedented scale.
