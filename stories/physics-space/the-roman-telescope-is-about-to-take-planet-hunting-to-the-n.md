NASA's Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope, preparing for launch, will revolutionize exoplanet detection capabilities. The instrument can discover approximately 40 times more distant planets than current telescopes have identified. This massive expansion in detection capacity will enable astronomers to build a comprehensive census of planetary systems and potentially identify worlds with characteristics suitable for life. The Roman telescope represents a transformative leap in humanity's ability to explore the cosmos and understand planetary diversity across the universe.
