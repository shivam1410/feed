Researchers grew boron carbon nitride into a complete wafer-scale semiconductor using a novel dual-chemical precursor system that had never been attempted before. This high-mobility p-type material shows superior performance compared to silicon in certain electronic applications. It's a promising building block that could fundamentally revolutionize 2D electronics technology and enable future computer chips to be more efficient, faster, and smaller than what current silicon-based technology allows.
