Qwen-Drive-1.0 is a vision-language foundation model designed for autonomous driving that unifies 3D perception, visual question-answering, and motion planning through shared learned representations and staged training. The unified architecture enables vehicles to interpret scenes, reason about driving scenarios, and generate control decisions from a single model.
