Google's Empirical Research Assistance automates solving scientific problems by reducing them to optimization challenges. Once researchers formulate a score function, which is the hard part, the system finds code that maximizes it. Many previously stubborn scientific challenges from climate change to fusion research can be written down as scoreable tasks. This pattern recognition unlocks automation on problems that seemed fundamentally unsolvable.
