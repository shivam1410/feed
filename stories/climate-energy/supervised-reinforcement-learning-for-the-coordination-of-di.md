Distributed energy resources like solar panels are crucial for decarbonization but difficult to coordinate due to uncertainty and modeling complexity. Supervised Reinforcement Learning pre-trains coordination policies on demonstration data, then fine-tunes offline for performance enhancement and online for real-world adaptation. Experiments show significantly improved results compared to benchmarks, achieving cost-efficiency even with low-quality demonstration data. Better DER coordination enables faster renewable energy integration into power grids.
