As Arctic icebergs melt, rocks they transport accumulate on the deep seafloor, creating surprising biodiversity hotspots. These dropstones provide hard substrate that harbors corals, sponges, and specialized Arctic species in the deep ocean. Climate-driven iceberg discharge paradoxically generates deep-sea habitats and extends polar ecosystems' influence worldwide.
