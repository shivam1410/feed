Optimal training-set selection for machine-learned potentials depends on budget size: structural coverage outperforms model-disagreement selection at 5% data retention, but selection flips at 20%, with witness-selected models reducing force errors by 0.46–5.89%. Testing on MACE models on carbon and MD17 datasets shows this budget-dependent crossover improves computational efficiency when data selection matches available resources.
