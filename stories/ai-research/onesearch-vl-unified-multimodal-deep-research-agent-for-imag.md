OneSearch-VL unifies image and video research through visually grounded evidence graphs that link visual details to external facts. Trained on 110K supervised examples and 10K RL examples, it outperformed Qwen3-VL by 20 points on new image benchmarks and 18 on video. Preserving dependencies improves multimodal reasoning.
