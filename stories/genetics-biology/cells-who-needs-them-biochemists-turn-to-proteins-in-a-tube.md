Scientists are synthesizing proteins without using living cells, relying instead on cell-free transcription and translation systems. These lab-based systems allow researchers to build biosensors and engineer synthetic cells entirely outside any organism. This matters because it removes the cell's messy complexity and opens entirely new ways to design and engineer biology in the lab.
