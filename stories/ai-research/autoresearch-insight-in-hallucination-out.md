AutoResearch is a two-stage autonomous system improving research reliability. The first stage generates research ideas; the second grounds them through evidence-based execution, reducing hallucinations. Integrating idea generation with systematic validation produces more reliable results than idea generation alone. This addresses a fundamental challenge in research AI—distinguishing genuine insights from plausible but incorrect hypotheses. The framework improves research productivity while maintaining scientific rigor.
