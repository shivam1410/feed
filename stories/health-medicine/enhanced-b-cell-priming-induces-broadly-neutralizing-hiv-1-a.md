Enhanced B cell priming—optimized immune cell stimulation—successfully produced broadly neutralizing antibodies against HIV-1, targeting the apex region of the virus. This technique improves upon standard vaccination approaches by inducing more potent and broadly protective immune responses. The result accelerates development of effective HIV preventive therapies.
