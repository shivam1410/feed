Deep-foam photolithography uses controlled light-induced polymer foaming to create high-resolution microstructures with tunable properties. The technique produces multifunctional structures with programmable optical, wetting, and fluid-handling characteristics suited for advanced applications. By combining foam generation with photopolymerization, researchers achieve precision manufacturing of complex microarchitectures. This biomimetic printing platform enables fabrication of sophisticated materials with integrated functionality, opening pathways for engineered surfaces and devices.
