Anthropic listed on Nasdaq under $ANT and raised $81.3B, the largest IPO in history. It priced at $83, opened at $111.50, and closed at $97.17. Its Class F shares carry 50.1% of voting power on most shareholder matters. In a founders' letter, Dario and Daniela said Anthropic would pace training and deployment based on whether it can keep systems safe and under human control. Roughly a trillion dollars of shares could free up over the next year as lockups expire, which the newsletter expects to weigh on the stock.
