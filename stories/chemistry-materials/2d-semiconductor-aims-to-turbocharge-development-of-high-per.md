Scientists developed a groundbreaking two-dimensional semiconductor that conducts electricity using positively charged holes instead of electrons, representing an entirely new approach to materials science. This ultrathin material has unique properties that could enable significantly faster and more efficient electronic devices than existing options. The breakthrough might dramatically accelerate development of next-generation computing and electronics technology beyond what current semiconductor approaches can achieve.
