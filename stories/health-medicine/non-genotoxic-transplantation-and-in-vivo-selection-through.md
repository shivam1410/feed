Researchers used epitope editing to modify immune markers, enabling non-toxic conditioning that selectively enriches edited blood-forming stem cells. The approach supports durable cell engraftment while preserving cell diversity and boosting production of fetal hemoglobin. This gentler stem cell therapy addresses sickle cell disease and β-thalassemia without the toxicity of traditional chemotherapy-based conditioning.
