Deep Hubble Space Telescope observations of the ultra-diffuse galaxy UGC 9050-Dw1 reveal the first globular cluster stream beyond the Milky Way. Analysis and modeling of this extragalactic stream probes dark matter distribution and structure within the host galaxy. This discovery extends our understanding of galaxy assembly and dark matter to systems outside our own galaxy.
