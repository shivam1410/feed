A glacier collapse in Nepal's high-altitude mountains triggered a deadly flash flood, exemplifying climate change impacts on mountain regions. Global warming is destabilizing high-altitude areas by accelerating glacial retreat and weakening geological structures, increasing catastrophic failure risk. The incident demonstrates how glacier collapse generates destructive floods with severe consequences for downstream communities. Scientists warn that climate-driven destabilization could trigger increasingly frequent landslides and floods in mountainous areas worldwide.
