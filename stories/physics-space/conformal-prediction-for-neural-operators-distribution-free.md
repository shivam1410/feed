Researchers applied split conformal prediction to Fourier Neural Operator (FNO)-based physics simulation for the first time, providing distribution-free prediction intervals with rigorous finite-sample coverage guarantees—addressing a key requirement for safety-critical engineering applications. They introduced normalized conformal prediction leveraging MC Dropout uncertainty to produce adaptive-width intervals (tighter in low-uncertainty regions, wider where uncertain). Full-scale experiments on steady-state heat conduction achieved 89.1% empirical coverage at alpha=0.1, demonstrating both rigor and practicality for real thermal management systems.
