Scientists developed a way to control CRISPR gene-editing enzymes using light and sound, allowing them to edit genes only in specific tissues rather than affecting the whole body. This precision approach targets exactly the cells you want to change while leaving others untouched. The breakthrough could make gene therapy much safer and more effective by preventing unwanted edits in other parts of the body where they might cause harm.
