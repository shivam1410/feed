Guava is a harness framework for embodied tool use enabling compact language models to perform complex manipulation tasks without requiring extensive training data. The system combines high-level reasoning capabilities with external specialized modules that handle specific manipulation subtasks. By decomposing complex manipulation into reasoning plus modular tools, Guava achieves strong performance with minimal task-specific training.
