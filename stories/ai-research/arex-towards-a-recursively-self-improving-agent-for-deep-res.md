AREX is a family of recursively self-improving research agents alternating between evidence gathering and constraint-based verification. The system identifies unresolved claims and launches targeted follow-up research guided by partial verification. AREX learns an autonomous context-update tool compressing interaction history into compact improvement states preserving verified evidence and unresolved constraints. Instantiated as 4B dense and 122B-A10B Mixture-of-Experts models trained via agentic mid-training and long-horizon RL with key-step emphasis. The discovery-verification asymmetry—costly discovery but decomposable verification—enables recursive refinement for deep research over extended horizons.
