PermaVid addresses video editing consistency challenges by using multi-modal memory banks that separately encode appearance and geometric structure, enabling coherent video generation respecting both visual identity and spatial relationships across time and viewpoint changes. This approach enables more natural, seamless video editing across long temporal horizons, improving the video editing experience.
