Amateur astronomers are using AI to hunt for planets. The tools help them spot exoplanet candidates that nobody had flagged before. The results are mixed, though. Some of the candidates look far more credible than others. Hobbyists can add real value, but they still need to sort the solid leads from the weak ones. It's a useful look at where AI helps science and where it still needs checking.
