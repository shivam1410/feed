RoboTok retrieves relevant human manipulation videos from internet-scale video data using a latent motion space derived from 3D hand trajectories. These retrieved demonstrations improve robot policy training for dexterous manipulation tasks. Leveraging human videos at web scale provides robots with diverse manipulation examples without manual annotation, potentially accelerating learning of complex physical skills.
