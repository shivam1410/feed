Astronomers discovered S301, a rapidly orbiting star near the Milky Way's central black hole Sagittarius A*. This star's proximity and orbital characteristics make it a sensitive probe of the black hole's spin through relativistic measurements of its orbits. Previous methods for measuring black hole spin were limited; this star offers a new avenue for directly measuring Sagittarius A* rotation through detailed observations of its orbital dynamics. The discovery advances tests of general relativity in extreme gravity environments and improves understanding of supermassive black hole properties. Such measurements help refine knowledge of black hole physics.
