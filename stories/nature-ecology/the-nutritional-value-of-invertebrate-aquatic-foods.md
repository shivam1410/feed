Aquatic invertebrates including insects and crustaceans are exceptionally nutrient-dense foods, collectively supplying sufficient annual vitamin B12 and selenium to meet requirements for over 5 billion people. These organisms provide concentrated micronutrients in small food volumes. Farmed aquatic invertebrates represent a sustainable, nutritionally efficient food source for addressing global micronutrient deficiencies and food security challenges.
