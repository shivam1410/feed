Google DeepMind proposes a cognitive framework designed to evaluate artificial general intelligence (AGI) and is launching a Kaggle hackathon to build capability benchmarks. This initiative addresses a critical need in AI development: establishing systematic, measurable criteria for assessing progress toward AGI. The framework and associated benchmarks will enable researchers to move beyond subjective assessments and establish concrete standards for evaluating AGI capabilities. This matters because as AI systems grow more powerful, the field needs agreed-upon metrics to gauge genuine progress and ensure responsible development toward advanced AI systems.
