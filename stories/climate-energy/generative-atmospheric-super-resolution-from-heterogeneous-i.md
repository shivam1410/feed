Researchers developed composable interfaces to combine sparse atmospheric observations—balloons, aircraft, ground stations—with a diffusion model that reconstructs gridded weather states. The multi-source interface reduces error by 9.24% compared to balloons alone across all 13 atmospheric variables. This matters because observations scatter everywhere but weather prediction needs regular grids.
