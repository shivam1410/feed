Neural recordings during a video game involving bombs and treasure chests reveal how the brain decides between risky and safe choices. Researchers observed neural activity flickering between two frontal cortex regions as subjects weighed tricky decisions. This tug-of-war between competing neural systems provides insight into the decision-making process at the neurological level. Understanding these mechanisms could illuminate disorders affecting risk assessment and decision-making abilities in patients with neurological or psychiatric conditions.
