TriGlue formulates molecular glue design as ternary complex generation, addressing a critical computational drug discovery gap. The biology-inspired framework decomposes into interface estimation using SE(3)-equivariant modules to predict protein-protein interfaces from unbound monomers, and interface-conditioned ternary flow matching to jointly generate the molecular glue and predict rigid-body transformations for assembly. Generates chemically valid molecules and plausible ternary complexes, demonstrating that generative modeling accelerates molecular glue discovery for targeted protein degradation.
