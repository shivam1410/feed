Using LARES-2, LAGEOS, and GRACE satellites, researchers measured Earth's frame-dragging effect—a subtle distortion of spacetime predicted by general relativity—with unprecedented precision. The measurements strongly confirm Einstein's theory and constrain alternative gravity theories that predict different effects. Frame-dragging occurs because rotating objects like Earth slightly twist the fabric of spacetime around them. Beyond testing fundamental physics, the precise measurements also improved understanding of Earth's tides and gravity variations. This demonstrates that satellites can detect relativistic effects once considered too subtle to measure directly.
