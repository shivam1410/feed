Astronomers discovered a four-carbon 'true sugar' molecule in interstellar space, significantly expanding evidence of complex organic chemistry beyond Earth. This particular sugar represents a milestone in understanding prebiotic chemistry in the cosmos. Sugars are essential building blocks of life, and this finding demonstrates that complex organic compounds can spontaneously form in the harsh environment of space despite extreme cold and radiation. The discovery helps explain how the chemical precursors required for life might naturally arise in the universe.
