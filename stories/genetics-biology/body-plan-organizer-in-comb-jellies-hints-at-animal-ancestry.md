Transplantation experiments across evolutionarily distant animal groups revealed conserved developmental mechanisms—biological processes functioning similarly across species—underlying tissue organization during body-plan formation. These findings in comb jellies and other animals indicate shared developmental principles, providing insights into early animal evolution and suggesting common ancestry among diverse body plans.
