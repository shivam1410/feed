Google DeepMind has launched the Asia-Pacific Accelerator program to address environmental risks and climate change vulnerability affecting the region. The Asia-Pacific region serves as a major global economic engine but faces significant exposure to climate hazards and environmental challenges. The initiative capitalizes on growing momentum in green technology adoption to develop and scale solutions for environmental risk mitigation and climate adaptation. The program leverages AI and innovation capabilities to support sustainable development and climate resilience in a region critical to both global economics and environmental outcomes.
