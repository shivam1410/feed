ACE-Data-0 comprises 150 hours and 17 million video frames of embodied AI data capturing 75,000 interaction episodes across 200 task categories from 50 participants in 2 home environments. Using synchronized recording across table and room scales, it captures egocentric and external video, full-body motion, hand tracking, object geometry and trajectories, audio, and tactile signals spanning atomic manipulation through long-horizon household activities.
