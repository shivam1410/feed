mRNA-lipid-nanoparticle vaccines can be engineered to generate better immune responses against difficult pathogens. The review examines how modifying vaccine components—including the RNA sequence, lipid formulation, and delivery system—allows fine-tuning of immune activation. This modular approach matters because infectious diseases vary in their immune-escape strategies, so tailoring the vaccine response could improve efficacy across diverse infections.
