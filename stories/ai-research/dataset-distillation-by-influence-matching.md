Influence Matching (Inf-Match) improves dataset distillation by aligning final training outcomes rather than mimicking training processes. Using a differentiable sample-level influence estimator running in linear time, Inf-Match achieved 31.5% accuracy on Tiny-ImageNet (10 samples per class), a 4.7% improvement over prior methods. On vision-language tasks with 200–1000 samples on Flickr30K, it outperformed baselines by 2.5% on retrieval metrics. The method enables efficient model training by learning synthetic datasets whose cumulative effect on converged parameters matches real data, yielding better accuracy-efficiency tradeoffs.
