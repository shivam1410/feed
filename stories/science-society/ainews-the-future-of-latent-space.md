Latent Space is merging its daily newsletter and Discord community after three years of AINews reaching 200k subscribers. The swyx-authored newsletter will consolidate with the platform while new topic-specific shows launch. The merger aims to reduce spam and create one coherent technical news hub. This matters because fragmented AI coverage through newsletters, Discord, and Twitter becomes unmanageable.
