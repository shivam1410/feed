Nearly 70 percent of websites need to update PHP to close two flaws. The bugs let attackers take full control of a server. Experts say working exploits exist and are already being used in the wild. PHP powers Drupal, Magento, Joomla and WordPress. Reuters reported that government sites in Europe went offline after the disclosure. Cloudflare saw some exploitation and added firewall rules at 3:02 am UTC. OpenAI, which helped find the bugs, called them among the most impactful security issues in recent memory.
