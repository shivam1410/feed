Mechanist is an autonomous AI system that discovers internal mechanisms generating intelligence in AI models. The system generates hypotheses about model operation, then performs causal interventions—controlled experiments testing hypotheses by modifying model components. This mechanistic understanding enables improvement in both safety (identifying dangerous behaviors and causes) and performance (strengthening beneficial mechanisms). This matters because current AI systems are largely opaque; understanding decision-making processes is crucial for intended behavior. By automating mechanism discovery, Mechanist advances interpretability and controllability of advanced AI systems, improving safety and alignment.
