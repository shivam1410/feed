A star in close orbit around Sagittarius A*, the supermassive black hole at the Milky Way's center, may enable precise measurement of the black hole's fundamental spin property through analysis of its orbital dynamics. This observation offers an unprecedented opportunity to directly measure black hole characteristics otherwise difficult to quantify. The finding matters because it demonstrates how stellar dynamics can empirically probe black hole properties, advancing our understanding of these extreme cosmic objects.
