Scientists are engineering modified microorganisms to manufacture and deliver anticancer drugs directly to patients. This approach harnesses bacteria as biological factories producing therapeutic compounds deployed within the body. Rather than traditional chemical synthesis, engineered bacteria serve as programmable drug-delivery vehicles. This biotechnological strategy potentially offers more targeted treatment with fewer side effects, representing a shift toward living therapeutics for cancer management.
