For decades, the blood-brain barrier has prevented most drugs from reaching the brain, blocking treatments for brain cancers and neurodegenerative diseases. Two competing technologies—nanoparticles and exosomes—are emerging as solutions. Nanoparticles, engineered at molecular scales, and exosomes, naturally occurring cell-derived vesicles, can be designed to cross the barrier while carrying therapeutic cargo. Researchers are developing these carriers to deliver medications while protecting brain tissue. Success would unlock treatment options for previously intractable neurological conditions that have impeded progress for decades.
