Two children died from gene therapies administered in China, prompting urgent examination of safety practices and regulatory frameworks. Strengthened disclosure laws could ensure future adverse events are quickly reported to the public and scientific community, improving transparency. China's reputation for responsible conduct of emerging gene therapy research now faces scrutiny. The incident highlights risks inherent in early-stage therapeutic interventions and the importance of robust informed consent, ethics oversight, and rapid safety reporting systems when deploying novel treatments on human subjects.
