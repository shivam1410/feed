CONFLUX synthesizes high-fidelity chest CT scans using 3D latent diffusion with controllable clinical attributes. The model uses metadata conditioning to control specific anatomical features and reinforcement learning refinement for output optimization. This approach enables customizable medical image synthesis useful for training data generation, clinical simulation, and research while maintaining diagnostic quality.
