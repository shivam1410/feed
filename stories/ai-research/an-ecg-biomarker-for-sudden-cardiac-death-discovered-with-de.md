A deep-learning model trained on electrocardiogram waveforms has identified a visible ECG biomarker that predicts sudden cardiac death more accurately than existing clinical methods. The AI found a pattern in electrical heart activity that clinicians can see and measure. This matters because sudden cardiac death kills hundreds of thousands annually; a more accurate predictor could enable preventive interventions in high-risk patients.
