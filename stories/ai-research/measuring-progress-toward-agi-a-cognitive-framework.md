Google DeepMind has proposed a cognitive framework for evaluating artificial general intelligence (AGI) and is launching a Kaggle hackathon to build capability benchmarks. Rather than relying on a single metric, the framework uses multiple cognitive dimensions to assess progress toward AGI. By engaging the research community through the hackathon, researchers can contribute to developing standardized benchmarks that measure different aspects of AGI capability. Defining and measuring AGI progress remains challenging because current systems demonstrate uneven strengths and weaknesses. A comprehensive evaluation framework could advance research by clarifying what AGI actually means and establishing measurable milestones for progress.
