NeoMME introduces small bidirectional multimodal encoders using masked discrete diffusion pretraining, achieving strong visual document retrieval with high compression of late-interaction embeddings. By operating at smaller scale than typical multimodal models, it balances efficiency and capability. This matters because production retrieval systems need efficient multimodal encoding to process both text and images at scale, making compact encoders practically valuable.
