Global food systems can be restructured sustainably by 2050 while feeding the world, according to economic simulations. Researchers modeled transformation toward healthier diets emphasizing plant-based foods, improved agricultural productivity, and halving food waste. Results show this creates a fundamentally different global agricultural landscape—different crop mixes, altered land use, and shifted production regions. Critically, models show this transformation is economically feasible through market mechanisms and policy incentives, not requiring system collapse. Findings suggest sustainable, nutritionally adequate food systems are achievable with coordinated changes.
