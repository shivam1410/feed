AlphaFold, the AI protein-folding system developed by DeepMind, has been applied to strengthen a key photosynthesis enzyme. Scientists used this computational approach to engineer crop variants that tolerate higher temperatures, directly addressing a critical challenge as global warming threatens agricultural productivity. The technique targets a fundamental biological process—photosynthesis itself—to make crops more resilient to heat stress. By using computational design to create stronger enzyme variants, researchers aim to develop food crops that maintain productivity in warming climates without conventional breeding timelines. This approach combines artificial intelligence with agricultural science, offering a practical solution to near-term food security challenges by potentially protecting yields as temperatures continue rising globally.
