Researchers created a framework that automates environment redesign in reinforcement learning by enabling the policy itself to analyze failures and propose configuration changes. This self-improving approach achieved superior results compared to larger proprietary models and static-environment baselines. The framework demonstrates that allowing agents to participate in shaping their own training environment leads to more efficient learning and adaptive optimization.
