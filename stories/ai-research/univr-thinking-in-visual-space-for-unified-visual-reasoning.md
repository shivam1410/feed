UniVR investigates learning complex reasoning, fine-grained physics, and long-term planning directly from visual demonstrations without text. It introduces VR-GRPO, an RL paradigm using complementary global and step-level rewards to enforce logical coherence and physical consistency throughout reasoning without task-specific heuristics or image-text pairs. The approach is trained and evaluated on VR-X, the first comprehensive benchmark curated from 16 diverse sources spanning long-horizon manipulation, spatial puzzles, and physical reasoning. UniVR achieves up to 25% improvement on VR-X and its superior visual reasoning also boosts performance on various multimodal understanding benchmarks. This matters because it demonstrates the potential of learning world knowledge through visual reasoning alone, advancing agents toward more general embodied cognition.
