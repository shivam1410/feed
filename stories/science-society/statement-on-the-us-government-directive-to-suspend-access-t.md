The US government issued an export control directive on June 14, 2026, requiring Anthropic to suspend all access to Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models for all foreign nationals, whether inside or outside the United States. The government cited national security concerns related to a potential jailbreak technique that essentially asks the model to analyze code for vulnerabilities and propose fixes. The directive was delivered abruptly at 5:21pm ET. While other Anthropic models remain available, this action represents a significant precedent for government-mandated AI model restrictions. The specific jailbreak demonstrated appears to be a narrow technique, and analysis suggests similar capabilities already exist in other publicly-available models.
