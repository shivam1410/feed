Researchers created AgenticBBO-Bench to test LLM agents on black-box optimization, where each evaluation is expensive. Agents span five domains: synthetic functions, hyperparameter tuning, databases, chip design, and molecular design. LLM agents outperformed traditional optimizers in four of five domains, suggesting they're useful for hard engineering problems.
