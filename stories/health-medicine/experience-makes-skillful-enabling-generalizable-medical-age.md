SkeMex is a self-evolving framework enhancing medical agents through structured skill memory that distinguishes valuable experiences and manages retention based on contextual utility. The system learns which experiences remain useful over time and which become obsolete, improving long-term clinical reasoning. Specialized memory management helps medical agents avoid outdated or incorrect knowledge while retaining critical diagnostic and treatment patterns, advancing toward more reliable AI clinical assistants for healthcare settings.
