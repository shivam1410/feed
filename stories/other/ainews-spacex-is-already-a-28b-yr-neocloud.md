SpaceX has become a major AI compute provider through GPU rental deals, with its latest contract with Reflection AI joining previous agreements with Anthropic and Google. This annualizes to approximately $28 billion yearly, roughly double Coreweave's current revenue despite Coreweave's $60 billion post-IPO valuation. SpaceX's rapid expansion into cloud computing infrastructure represents a significant shift in the AI industry's compute landscape, competing with established providers. This vertical integration allows SpaceX to monetize excess launch infrastructure while positioning itself as critical AI infrastructure.
