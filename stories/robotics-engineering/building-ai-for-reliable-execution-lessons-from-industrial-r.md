Standard Bots builds AI-native industrial robot arms for tasks like machine tending, welding, and assembly. It raised $200 million at a $1 billion valuation. Customers include NASA, Amazon, and Lockheed Martin. Its approach is a shared base model that customers adapt with demonstrations and fine-tuning. The largest model has a few billion parameters, which is small by frontier standards. Its Head of AI says data quality matters far more than raw volume.
