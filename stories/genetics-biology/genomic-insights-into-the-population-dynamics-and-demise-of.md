An expanding surge of genetic data extracted from Neanderthal skeletal remains is reshaping understanding of these ancient hominins. New genomic insights are disproving long-held assumptions about Neanderthal population dynamics, behavior, and extinction, while simultaneously generating fresh questions challenging existing models. This influx of ancient DNA has revealed unexpected complexity in Neanderthal genetics—including gene flow with modern humans and within Neanderthal populations—previously invisible to paleoanthropology. The revised picture is far more nuanced than earlier frameworks suggested. As genetic technologies continue improving, each new Neanderthal genome potentially rewrites chapters of human prehistory, suggesting our ancestors' story is richer.
