MatrAIx is a large-scale evaluation framework employing 8.3 billion simulated persona agents in interactive environments to assess AI system capabilities across diverse domains. Rather than testing AI systems against uniform, static user profiles, MatrAIx draws on diverse persona records to simulate varied user types and behaviors. This approach tests AI performance across many domains and user personalities simultaneously, revealing broader generalization patterns than single-persona evaluation. By simulating such large numbers of persona variants, the framework provides unprecedented coverage of user-behavior space and domain diversity. This comprehensive evaluation methodology enables researchers to identify robustness gaps and failure modes across heterogeneous user populations more thoroughly than conventional single-scenario testing approaches.
