Meta-learning for drug discovery that conditions on auxiliary assay data to handle assay heterogeneity. Linear attention over large datasets plus exact attention over scarce task context enables scaling without losing precision. Improves few-shot bioactivity prediction on ChEMBL and BindingDB. Matters because individual assays have too few measurements; learning to borrow from related data makes early-stage screening more reliable.
