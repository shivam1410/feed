Accurate flood forecasting is essential for disaster mitigation, but purely data-driven models struggle in data-scarce environments and may violate physical principles. Researchers propose Physics-Informed Machine Learning (PIML) framework incorporating hydrological knowledge directly into an LSTM loss function through a Trend Alignment constraint penalizing directional inconsistencies between precipitation and discharge. Results show the physics-informed model outperforms baseline LSTM, improving Nash-Sutcliffe Efficiency from 0.20 to 0.23 when trained on only 5% of available data, while maintaining directional consistency when standard models become unstable.
