NASA's Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope is poised to revolutionize exoplanet discovery with unprecedented detection capabilities, potentially discovering 40 times more distant worlds than have been identified to date. The telescope's advanced design and exceptional sensitivity enable detection of extrasolar planets on a scale previously impossible. This dramatic multiplier in exoplanet discoveries will substantially expand humanity's understanding of planetary systems throughout the galaxy.
