Who Framed Roger Rabbit featured a pelican riding a bicycle in one early scene. The pelican was animated while the bicycle was real. The filmmakers filled the bicycle wheels with water to add stability, then set it rolling and guided it along with a cable. It's a clever example of the ingenuity behind the 1988 film's blend of animation and practical effects.
