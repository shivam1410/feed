Analysis from the CheckMate 77T trial identifies potential predictive biomarkers indicating which resectable non-small cell lung cancer patients benefit from perioperative nivolumab immunotherapy. These biomarkers could enable precision treatment selection—giving therapy to likely responders while sparing unnecessary exposure in non-responders. Biomarker-guided optimization improves outcomes by concentrating treatment and reducing toxicity burden, a key goal in personalizing cancer care.
