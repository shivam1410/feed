Scientists developed a catalyst-free plastic upcycling method using microdroplet interfaces to generate hydroxyl radicals, requiring only water and oxygen under mild conditions. This approach enables efficient polymer processing and chemical conversion without traditional catalysts or complex infrastructure. Waste plastics are converted to valuable chemical products. The simplified methodology could accelerate scalable plastic waste conversion. Reducing infrastructure requirements and chemical inputs makes the process more practical for industrial deployment. This breakthrough addresses the critical challenge of plastic waste management while creating value from waste streams through chemical upcycling.
