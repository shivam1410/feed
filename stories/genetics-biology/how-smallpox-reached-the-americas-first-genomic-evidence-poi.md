Genetic analysis of viral sequences from Chilean mummies provides the first genomic evidence that smallpox was introduced to the Americas from Europe. This molecular evidence confirms historical records linking European contact to disease transmission in the Western Hemisphere. The findings combine archaeological samples with modern genetic sequencing to establish the origin and timing of this catastrophic disease introduction.
