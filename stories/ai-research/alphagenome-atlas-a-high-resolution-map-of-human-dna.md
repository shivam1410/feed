DeepMind built AlphaGenome Atlas, a database that predicts how every possible single nucleotide variant affects the human genome. Instead of testing mutations in the lab, researchers can assess genetic impact computationally. This accelerates understanding disease risk and enables faster personalized medicine approaches.
