Caltech professor Anima Anandkumar developed FourCastNet, an AI weather prediction model that matches physics-based simulations despite skepticism from experts. Completed within a year, it now runs on consumer-grade GPUs, making accurate short-term weather forecasting accessible. Her work highlights an overlooked frontier: continuous physical systems like weather, fusion, and fluid flow are chaotic, multi-scale challenges that the AI community has largely neglected. With only tens to hundreds of thousands of available examples in these domains—far below what modern transformers expect—this field demands new approaches beyond simple scaling.
