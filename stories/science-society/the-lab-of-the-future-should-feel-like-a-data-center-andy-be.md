Lila Sciences is building an autonomous laboratory powered by AI and robotics, operating 24/7 to generate experimental data at massive scale. The facility contains over 10 trillion experimentally validated scientific reasoning tokens, spanning biology, chemistry, drug discovery, and materials science. The thesis: treating a lab as an infinite token generator could produce a general scientific reasoner capable of tackling any problem—an ambitious bet on scaling experimental data like language models scaled text.
