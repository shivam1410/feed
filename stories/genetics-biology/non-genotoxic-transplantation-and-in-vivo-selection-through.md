Researchers used epitope editing of the KIT protein to enable antibody-based conditioning that selectively enriches BCL11A-edited blood cells without causing DNA damage. This non-genotoxic approach supports durable cell engraftment while preserving clonal diversity and enhancing fetal hemoglobin production. This therapeutic strategy shows potential for treating sickle cell disease and β-thalassemia, genetic blood disorders caused by defective hemoglobin.
