Action-conditioned world models enable zero-shot simulation: SyncWorld learns environment-specific action-to-visual mappings via brief visual calibration episodes, then simulates robotic environments it has never encountered. Policies improve at test-time across diverse unseen physical settings. This substantially reduces data engineering burden for robot deployment to new contexts, enabling generalization without task-specific retraining.
