EEGJev is a fast EEG decoder that answers questions about a recording. It scores candidate answers in one pass, so changing the question changes the target. Across 13 test sources, it picked the right attribute label with 99.70% accuracy, against 49.20% under uniform choice. Its 95th-percentile response time was shorter than NeuroLM's on matched questions.
