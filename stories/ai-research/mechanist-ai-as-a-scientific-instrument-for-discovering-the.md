Mechanist is an autonomous agentic system designed to discover and control the internal mechanisms that drive intelligence in AI models. The system generates hypotheses about how model components function, performs targeted causal interventions to test these hypotheses, and measures resulting performance changes. This mechanistic approach enables fundamental understanding of how intelligence emerges in neural networks and practical improvements to model safety and performance. By treating model internals as controllable mechanisms rather than black boxes, Mechanist enables systematic investigation of what drives intelligent behavior.
