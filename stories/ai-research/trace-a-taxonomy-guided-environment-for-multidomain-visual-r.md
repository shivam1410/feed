Trace provides a procedurally generated environment for vision-language reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards. It factorizes task construction into scene grammars and executable task programs, with a shared semantic state determining rendered images, prompts, answers, and verifier states. The environment comprises 1,000 tasks over 277 scene grammars and 11 visual domains with controlled variation. RLVR on 64,000 Trace instances improves macro-average across 24 external benchmarks by 3.51 percentage points for Qwen2.5-VL-3B and 4.06 points for 7B, providing evidence that procedural training transfers beyond generated task distributions.
