Aquatic invertebrates—insects, crustaceans, mollusks, and other small aquatic animals—are exceptional nutrition sources, remarkably dense in essential vitamins and minerals. These organisms provide vitamin B12 and selenium at concentrations high enough that global aquatic invertebrate production could meet annual requirements for over 5 billion people. Unlike conventional livestock, they require minimal land, water, and feed, making them environmentally sustainable alternatives. Their exceptional nutritional density combined with low environmental footprint makes them promising for addressing future food security.
