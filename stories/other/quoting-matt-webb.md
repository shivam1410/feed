Matt Webb developed Galactic Compass 2, an augmented reality application, and needed to implement quaternion rotations for 3D object manipulations. Rather than asking ChatGPT to write the code outright, he used it as an interactive tutor to understand quaternion mathematics—a mathematical concept he'd previously struggled to grasp through reading textbooks and conversations with mathematician friends. This collaborative educational approach succeeded where traditional learning methods had failed. Webb emphasizes that outsourcing thinking to AI doesn't halt learning; rather, it prompted him to achieve deeper understanding of necessary concepts, positioning AI as a learning accelerant rather than a replacement for human intellectual effort and growth.
