January 2024 saw four major AI-for-Pharma tool deals announced at the JPM Pharma conference, with OpenAI-backed Chai Discovery, valued at $4B despite being only two years old, playing a central role. This marks a fundamental business shift: AI pharma tools are now trusted enough by drug design teams for direct adoption. Previously, AI pharma companies typically abandoned tool development to build their own drug pipelines—the only viable path since selling tools required clinical proof. Now improved AI capabilities enable pharma teams to trust tools directly, unlocking entirely new commercialization pathways beyond pipeline ownership. This phase shift represents fundamental change in how AI-pharma integration works and creates new business models.
