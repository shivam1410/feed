Long-WAM scales robot control to use longer visual history while staying within real-time latency limits. By pretraining autoregressively on robot video, longer histories actually pay off: on RoboCasa success jumped from 63.3% to 78.7% with 19.2 seconds of context. On dynamic cup stacking it hit 95% success where other methods failed, proving that viewing past frames helps robots understand ongoing motion and task progress.
