RAIBO2, an energy-efficient quadruped robot, completed a full marathon distance in 4 hours, 19 minutes and 52 seconds on a single battery charge. The robot achieved a cost of transport of 0.25—significantly surpassing the human benchmark of 0.37. This achievement demonstrates substantial progress in robot locomotion design and energy management, with implications for developing practical autonomous systems capable of sustained operation without frequent recharging.
