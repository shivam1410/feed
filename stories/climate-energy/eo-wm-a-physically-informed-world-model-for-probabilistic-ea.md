EO-WM introduces a video diffusion transformer for multispectral Earth Observation forecasting that incorporates physically informed conditioning to better capture weather-driven uncertainties in land-surface dynamics. The model leverages physics-based frameworks to improve prediction of satellite-observed environmental changes affected by weather patterns. This approach combines machine learning with domain knowledge to handle the complex uncertainties inherent in forecasting Earth observation data, particularly for understanding how weather systems drive changes in land surface characteristics. The integration of physical constraints into the model architecture enables more accurate and interpretable forecasting of satellite imagery across multiple spectral bands, which is important for applications in climate monitoring, agricultural assessment, and disaster response.
