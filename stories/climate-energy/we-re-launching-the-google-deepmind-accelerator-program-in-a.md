Google DeepMind is launching an accelerator program in the Asia-Pacific region to address environmental risks and climate vulnerabilities. The initiative targets a region experiencing rapid economic growth alongside significant climate exposure including sea-level rise, extreme weather, and ecosystem disruption. The program aims to support and scale green technologies gaining momentum across Asia-Pacific. This effort reflects recognition that developing regions need accessible, scalable climate solutions for both mitigation and adaptation to environmental threats.
