Packora, a flow-based generative model, predicts molecular crystal structures by jointly determining atomic coordinates and lattice parameters from molecular graphs. Supporting multi-component and organometallic crystals with flexible conditioning, Packora outperforms baselines on all six generation benchmarks with faster convergence and superior experimental form recovery. This accelerates discovery in pharmaceuticals, agrochemicals, and organic electronics.
