Monkey King Bang (MKB) is a unified scientific multimodal foundation model supporting six scientific branches: DNA, RNA, proteins, small molecules, earth science, and medical images. Built around a shared Transformer backbone with modality-specific encoders, adapters, and decoders, MKB generates native outputs including biological sequences, molecular strings, meteorological fields, and segmentation masks. Training follows a two-stage curriculum: first aligning modality components with the frozen backbone, then consolidating them with language using mixed scientific and general corpora. The model achieves competitive performance on biological and molecular benchmarks while producing high-fidelity outputs for weather forecasting, biological generation, and medical-image segmentation.
