Google's AMIE, a conversational medical AI system, has demonstrated real-time clinical video consultation capabilities in a first-of-its-kind study conducted in simulated healthcare settings. AMIE can conduct interactive diagnostic and therapeutic conversations with patients in real-time, mirroring primary care physician workflows. Its ability to engage patients through video consultations while providing clinical guidance could expand access to medical expertise in underserved regions. The simulated study validates that AI can handle complex consultation dynamics—patient concerns, follow-up questions, and clinical reasoning—in interactive settings. This technology represents progress toward AI-augmented healthcare delivery that improves access.
