PlannerForge is an LLM-agent framework that automates scenario-based testing for autonomous driving systems by unifying generation, selection, modification, and planning stages. The framework improves performance across both commercial and open-source motion planning models. This matters because systematic, AI-driven testing can accelerate the validation of autonomous vehicle safety—a critical bottleneck in real-world deployment.
