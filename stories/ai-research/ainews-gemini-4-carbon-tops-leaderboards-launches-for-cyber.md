Google DeepMind announced Gemini 4 Carbon, two weeks after Argon. It is aimed at cyber work and first goes only to trusted cyber defenders. Google claims scores of 88.2% on SEC-bench Pro for finding vulnerabilities, 54.8% on ExploitGym, and 71.0% on CWE-bench for patching. The model reportedly took the top spot on Artificial Analysis's consolidated benchmark. Wider developer access is promised soon.
