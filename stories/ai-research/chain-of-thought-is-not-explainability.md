A position paper argues that chain-of-thought explanations are not real explainability. Models often write reasoning steps that don't match the hidden computation behind their answer. The authors reviewed 1,000 recent chain-of-thought papers. About 21.7 percent treated it as a load-bearing interpretability tool. They say this is risky in medicine, law, and autonomous systems, so chain-of-thought shouldn't count as interpretability without extra verification.
