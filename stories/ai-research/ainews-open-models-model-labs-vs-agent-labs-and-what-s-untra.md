Sarah Guo's framework clarifies the emerging AI landscape by distinguishing between open models, model labs (focused on foundational model development), and agent labs (focused on applied AI products). The key insight: agent labs earn sustainable competitive advantage by doing 'unglamorous work'—translating customer reality so models can act on it, building domain-specialized engineering and tools, and maintaining relationships. This translation work is difficult to copy and does not end after deployment. The framework also highlights the shift in 2024-2026: while open models seemed less relevant in 2024, by 2026 they have become strategically important. This matters because it clarifies where AI value actually accrues: not primarily in model training, but in the hard engineering and domain integration work that makes models useful for real problems.
