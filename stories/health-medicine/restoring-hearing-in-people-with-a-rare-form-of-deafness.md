After decades of dedicated research, scientists finally achieved the first gene therapy that successfully restores hearing in people with inherited deafness caused by genetic mutations. The therapy works by correcting the underlying DNA defect responsible for profound hearing loss. This breakthrough offers genuine hope to affected individuals and may open doors to similar genetic treatments for other rare inherited conditions.
