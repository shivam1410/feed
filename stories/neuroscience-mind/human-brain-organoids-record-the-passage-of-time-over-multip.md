Human brain organoids cultured for over five years in the laboratory continue maturing and demonstrate the capacity to track the passage of time through intrinsic cellular programs. The organoids follow human-specific developmental schedules independent of organismal context, showing that neural tissue can self-organize and sustain biological timekeeping in culture. This reveals fundamental properties of human brain development and timing mechanisms.
