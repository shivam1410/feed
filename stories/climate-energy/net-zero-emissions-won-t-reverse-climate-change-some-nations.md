Reaching net zero emissions may not be enough to reverse climate change. Some countries are now looking at 'net negative' goals instead. Once temperatures pass the 1.5 degree C target, bringing them back down would mean pulling massive amounts of carbon dioxide out of the air. That is why nations are starting to plan for removal, not just cuts.
