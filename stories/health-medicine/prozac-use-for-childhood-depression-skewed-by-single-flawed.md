A small flawed trial from Iran ended up swaying meta-analyses in favor of giving antidepressants to depressed children, researchers found. The single poorly-designed study influenced multiple large reviews. This matters because doctors base treatment decisions on these summaries, so one bad trial can mislead the treatment of vulnerable kids.
