Researchers built SAKIKO, an auditing tool that checks whether tweaks to LLM internals actually fix problems or just shuffle errors around. They found that interventions achieving a net gain of 55 actually broke more than half of the decisions that were already correct. This warns us not to trust quick metrics when steering AI systems internally.
