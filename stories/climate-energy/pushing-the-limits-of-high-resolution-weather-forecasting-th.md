BaguanHR improves high-resolution weather forecasting by using variable-wise super-resolution to synthetically expand training data. This data-scaling approach overcomes the limitations of transferring coarse-resolution models and demonstrates strong scaling benefits. Better high-resolution forecasts enable more precise weather predictions with practical benefits for agriculture, water management, and disaster planning.
