Moonshot has released Kimi K3.1 Pro through its API. The full weights are promised by November 4, 2026. It costs $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. That matches GPT-6.1 Sol and is half the price of Claude Opus 5.5. Artificial Analysis scored it 55, just behind Opus 5.5 at 58 and ahead of Fable 5.1, GPT-6 Astra, and Gemini 4 Argon. Simon Willison tested it with a pelican-on-a-bicycle SVG prompt across three reasoning levels.
