Roads in areas where pumas patrol experience fewer deer-vehicle collisions. Pumas naturally reduce deer populations through predation, lowering the number of animals crossing roads and causing accidents. This reveals an unexpected ecological benefit of large predators—improved human road safety—demonstrating how wildlife management decisions affect human welfare and predators' broader ecosystem role.
