Research on language model reasoning reveals distinct reasoning operations occupy geometrically separable regions in hidden neural representations. This structure emerges consistently across model layers and depends on reasoning context. The finding provides mechanistic insight into how transformers perform multi-step reasoning, suggesting reasoning is composed of distinguishable computational operations rather than being monolithic. Better understanding these operations could enable improved interpretability of AI reasoning and more targeted improvements to reasoning capabilities.
