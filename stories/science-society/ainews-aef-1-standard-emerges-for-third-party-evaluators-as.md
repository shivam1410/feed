Frontier AI companies (Xai, OpenAI, Anthropic) are adopting AEF-1 standard for third-party safety oversight. Anthropic commits unilaterally to embedding independent evaluators like METR as ongoing employees to verify safety practices, report incidents, and assess alignment of models and training pipelines. The standard represents a precedent-setting shift toward embedded regulatory-style auditing in AI safety.
