SkillForge manages a robot's skill library the way evolution works: new skills are tried, good ones stabilize, bad ones retire, and harmful ones are removed. Across benchmarks it achieved up to 7.8% improvement over baselines while keeping the library compact. They also released SkillFurnace, a dataset of 5,000+ annotated training examples and skill lifecycles to help researchers study when skills should persist or retire.
