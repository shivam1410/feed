Despite predictions of AI-driven job losses in software engineering, data contradicts this narrative. In March 2025, New York required AI disclosure in WARN Act filings; in the first year, 160+ companies filed notices, yet none checked the AI box. Coding—the phase of typing code into computers—isn't software engineering's bottleneck. Engineers spend substantial time in meetings, debugging, and decision-making. Until AI automates these communication-intensive, context-dependent activities, widespread displacement appears unlikely. Most professions face even greater insulation from disruption.
