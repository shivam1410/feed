DeepMind introduced AlphaGenome Atlas, a database predicting functional effects for every possible single nucleotide variant in the human genome. This comprehensive resource enables researchers to understand how genetic variations contribute to traits and diseases at unprecedented scale. The atlas accelerates genomic research and personalized medicine applications.
