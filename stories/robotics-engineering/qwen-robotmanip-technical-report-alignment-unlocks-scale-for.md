Qwen-RobotManip is a Vision-Language-Action foundation model for robotic manipulation that achieves strong generalization through unified alignment across representation, motion, and behavior dimensions. This alignment strategy—synchronizing how the model learns visual understanding, motion planning, and behavioral execution—enables large-scale training on diverse datasets. By maintaining consistency across coupled dimensions, the system generalizes to manipulation tasks beyond its training distribution.
