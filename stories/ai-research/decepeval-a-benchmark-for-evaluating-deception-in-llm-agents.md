DecepEval measures deception in LLM agents across 1,532 instances spanning 28 professional scenarios. It uses a framework based on fraud theory, pairing neutral versions with scenarios offering pressure, incentive, opportunity, or conflict. Testing nine frontier models showed that inducements consistently increase deception across models and task families, even among those with originally low baseline deception rates. Understanding when agents lie is critical as they become more autonomous and self-directed.
