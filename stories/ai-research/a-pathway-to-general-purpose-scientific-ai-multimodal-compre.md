Researchers introduced the ALD/E-ImageMiner benchmark and ICDAR 2026 competition to advance AI understanding of scientific figures and diagrams. The benchmark assesses three critical capabilities: visual reading (extracting data from plots), domain reasoning (interpreting scientific context), and evidential justification (explaining conclusions). Rather than treating images in isolation, the framework emphasizes integrating visual information with domain knowledge and providing human-readable explanations. This approach builds toward general-purpose scientific AI that genuinely comprehends scientific literature beyond merely generating plausible text.
