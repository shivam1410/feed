Biochemists are now making proteins without cells, synthesizing them directly from raw molecules in a test tube using cell-free transcription and translation systems. They're using this approach to design biology-based sensors and develop synthetic cells. This bypasses the complexity of living cells entirely. Cell-free systems could enable faster, cheaper ways to engineer biology and create new medical technologies.
