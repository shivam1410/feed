OpenAI just released two new models, and the top one is cheaper. GPT-6.2 Astra is built for the hardest projects. It costs $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output, which is 80% of the old Astra price. Cached input dropped to $0.40 from $1. GPT-6.2 Sol is for everyday work at $2 and $10, half the price of GPT-5.6 Sol. Simon Willison's Yonkers-to-mech animation test didn't impress either model. Cheaper cached input matters most for long agent sessions.
