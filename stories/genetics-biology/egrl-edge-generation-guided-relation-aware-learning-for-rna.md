EGRL framework improves RNA-protein interaction prediction using graph neural networks enhanced with three key innovations: implicit meta-path learning without handcrafted paths, multi-relation-aware attention for adaptive pattern fusion, and a graph generator predicting soft edges for unknown molecules. The method achieves competitive performance on benchmark datasets and notably strong generalization in cold-start scenarios with unknown molecules, reaching an AUROC of 0.867. By jointly training with primary task loss and auxiliary generator loss, EGRL handles data sparsity and learns to generalize beyond training molecule sets.
