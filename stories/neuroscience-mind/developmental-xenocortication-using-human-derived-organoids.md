Researchers transplanted human-derived neural organoids into mice to create xenocortication models that enable detailed analysis of neurodevelopmental circuits and behavior. This approach allows scientists to study human brain development mechanisms using living animal systems. The integration of human neurons provides insights into developmental processes difficult to observe in traditional laboratory models, enabling circuit-level and behavioral analysis of human neurodevelopment.
