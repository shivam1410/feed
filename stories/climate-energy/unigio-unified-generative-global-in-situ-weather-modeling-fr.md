UniGIO generates missing weather data from sparse in-situ observations and unifies forecasting, imputation, and generation under arbitrary missing ratios. Using Observation Mixer and Event Aligner components, it diffuses discrete observations into continuous spaces; Adaptive Temporal Mixer tracks temporal dependencies and chaotic local weather through a mixture-of-experts structure. This enables direct modeling of global in-situ weather dynamics from natively incomplete observations.
