Researchers have developed an insulator-free topological photonic waveguide system featuring four inequivalent photonic valley half-semimetals arranged in parallel, cyclic configuration positioned very close together. This architecture achieves robust, unidirectional light transport across multiple pathways with 100% spatial efficiency. The topological design ensures light travels along these multi-lane highways without loss even in closely-packed arrangements. This photonic advance could enable more efficient optical communication and quantum computing systems.
