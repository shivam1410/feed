A decade after its launch, the human genome synthesis project remains worth pursuing. The initiative aims to construct a complete human genome from chemical building blocks. Revisiting this ambitious goal highlights both scientific and practical reasons for completion, including applications in medicine and biotechnology. The project represents one of biology's grand challenges, combining technological innovation with fundamental questions about genomic engineering and synthetic biology.
