Here's a wild finding: C. elegans's complete connectome, implemented as neural network reservoirs, often lost to random networks in benchmark tasks. Scientists tested the biological wiring from different ages and measurement methods against equally sized random networks. The takeaway: how you wire inputs and outputs matters more than having the real biological connectivity.
