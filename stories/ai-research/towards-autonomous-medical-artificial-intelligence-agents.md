An artificial intelligence agent powered by a large language model operated within a simulated electronic health record system and performed autonomous clinical tasks. The system independently took patient medical histories, ordered diagnostic tests, interpreted test findings, diagnosed medical conditions, and proposed treatment plans. Notably, the AI agent's performance exceeded experienced human clinicians in a controlled study while adhering to established safety standards and clinical guidelines. The agent operated in a sandboxed environment isolating it from real patient data. This demonstration suggests potential for AI to support clinical decision-making and improve efficiency in healthcare, though deployment would require rigorous validation.
