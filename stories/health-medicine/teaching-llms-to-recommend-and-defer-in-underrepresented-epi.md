MANANA adapts LLMs to local medical practice by learning prescribing guidance from small patient cohorts in Ugandan pediatric epilepsy care. The system discovered that many LLM errors reflected distribution-miscalibrated defaults rather than comprehension failures. MANANA converts observed errors into auditable prompt memories and outperforms classical ML and prompt baselines. Bayesian prompt averaging generates uncertainty signals enabling selective prediction—automatically handling straightforward cases while deferring uncertain ones—improving top-3 prescription accuracy by 4-8 percentage points.
