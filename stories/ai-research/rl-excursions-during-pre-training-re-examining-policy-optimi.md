Reinforcement learning proves effective very early in LLM pre-training, often matching the standard SFT→RL pipeline by intermediate checkpoints. Targeted pre-training data composition is a stronger lever than model scale. Parallel averaging of RL and SFT objectives outperforms sequential approaches while preserving general capabilities—a property lost under SFT alone. These results suggest integrating RL deeper into the training pipeline rather than reserving it only for post-training.
