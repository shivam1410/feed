Language model agents are now asked to run ongoing business operations, not single tasks. SMB Bench is a 90-day wholesale simulator with 18 paired tasks and 859 frontier runs. Only 3 of 16 frontier models beat a fixed buying rule. Many noted the problem in their notes but did not change purchases in time. A trained open model followed customer-protection rules more consistently and had fewer severe-loss quarters. Its extra profit stayed uncertain.
