A quantum computer using trapped ions reached 98 qubits and can connect any two quantum bits to each other, achieving performance levels that conventional computers cannot match. This represents progress toward scalable quantum computing by solving the connectivity problem that has limited previous designs. High qubit count with flexible connectivity brings practical quantum advantage closer.
