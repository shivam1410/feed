WorldCrafter is a video world model that learns implicit 3D-aware memory to maintain consistency across long horizons and changing viewpoints. The system uses a pose-conditioned readout that integrates historical observations into the video generation process without explicit depth calculations. Combined with temporal context and distillation, WorldCrafter enables minute-scale scene exploration from a single image or text prompt while preserving visual quality and spatial consistency.
