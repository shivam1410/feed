Wearable leg sensors miss freezing of gait when the legs are almost still. Adding EEG brain signals as an input raised a detection score from 0.663 to 0.740, and 9 of 10 patients improved. EEG is impractical for daily wear, so the team also tested it only during an early training stage. Training the sensor model that way lifted its score from 0.663 to 0.705. The deployed detector still needs only the leg sensors. Each setting was run five times.
