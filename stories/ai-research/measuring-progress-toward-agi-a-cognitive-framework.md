Google DeepMind proposed a cognitive framework for evaluating artificial general intelligence and launched a Kaggle hackathon to develop capability benchmarks. AGI, systems matching human-level intelligence across diverse domains, remains theoretical with no consensus on measurement approaches. This framework provides structured criteria for systematic assessment. The hackathon crowdsources benchmark development, engaging the broader research community. Creating measurable, agreed-upon benchmarks is crucial for tracking AGI progress and enabling responsible development. This work addresses a critical gap in AI research.
