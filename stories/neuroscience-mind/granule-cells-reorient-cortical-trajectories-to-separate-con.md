Simultaneous imaging of premotor cortex and cerebellar granule cells in mice learning two skills simultaneously shows that neural trajectories generalize across tasks in cortex but cleanly separate and reorient apart in granule cells. This division of labor suggests the cerebellum specializes in context-specific computation while cortex favors generalizable representations. The complementary coding strategies—generalization in cortex, separation in cerebellum—may enable flexible learning while preventing interference between distinct skills and contexts.
