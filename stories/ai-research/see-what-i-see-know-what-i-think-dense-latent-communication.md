Heterogeneous multi-agent systems can efficiently share knowledge through aligned KV-cache communication, outperforming text-based inter-agent messaging with significantly lower computational costs. When agents built on different architectures communicate through the model's internal numerical representations rather than through human-readable text, coordination improves substantially. This matters because agent-to-agent communication often becomes a bottleneck in complex multi-agent systems. The discovery suggests that computational efficiency and coordination quality can both improve by bypassing natural language as an inter-agent medium.
