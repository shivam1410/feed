Analysis of biomedical papers archived in PubMed from December revealed that approximately 90% showed detectable signs of artificial intelligence assistance, substantially exceeding previous estimates of large language model integration in scientific publishing. This dramatic prevalence reflects rapid and extensive adoption of AI tools throughout research workflows. The finding matters because such widespread AI involvement raises important questions about research quality assurance, methodology consistency, potential biases introduced by AI systems, and the necessity for clear disclosure guidelines regarding AI use in scientific publications.
