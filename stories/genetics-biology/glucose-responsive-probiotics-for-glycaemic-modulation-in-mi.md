Engineered probiotics equipped with synthetic glucose-responsive circuits transiently colonize the gut and automatically secrete therapeutic factors when blood glucose rises. Tested in mouse and primate models, these living therapeutics enable sense-and-respond diabetes control without external intervention. This approach demonstrates autonomous living systems that detect disease conditions and deliver treatment automatically. It advances therapeutic microorganisms and precision medicine applications.
