In May, an internal OpenAI model found a counterexample to Erdős's unit distance conjecture, open since 1946. Many experts had tried to prove it. The article asks which math problems language models can solve, and why. One simple model treats difficulty as the shortest chain of insights, with work between each one. Experts need fewer hints because they know more background. The author uses W.T. Gowers's commentary on the unit-distance result. That matters because knowing which problems fit models helps decide where to point them.
