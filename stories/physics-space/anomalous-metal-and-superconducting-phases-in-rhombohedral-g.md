Rhombohedral graphene on a WSe2 substrate exhibits coexisting superconducting (zero-resistance) and resistive regions that vary with temperature, magnetic field, and current, creating an anomalous phase diagram. This unexpected superconductivity in stacked graphene opens new research directions toward room-temperature or higher-temperature superconductivity. The phenomenon reveals exotic quantum phases in layered materials.
