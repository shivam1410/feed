Francis Halzen won the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics for his South Pole neutrino observatory that detects cosmic particles from the Universe. His detector has caught neutrinos and helped unlock mysteries about the cosmos. Neutrinos barely interact with matter, so his ingenious instrument represents a breakthrough in observational physics.
