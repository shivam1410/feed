A world model can project how older adults' health changes over time. It was trained on US Health and Retirement Study data, then tested without refitting in England and China. In the US test set, mortality AUROC was 0.89 at the first horizon and 0.84 at the fifth. In England, mortality AUROC ranged from 0.867 to 0.881. Simulated trajectories kept the expected risk order across action regimes. Tracking how risk unfolds, not a single snapshot, is the point.
