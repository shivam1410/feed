The Amazon rainforest may be approaching an irreversible tipping point sooner than scientists expected. Intense droughts and fire triggered by a "super" El Niño event could push this critical ecosystem past recovery. This matters because the Amazon absorbs vast amounts of carbon and harbors immense biodiversity; collapse would accelerate climate change globally and devastate ecosystems millions depend on for water, food, and livelihood.
