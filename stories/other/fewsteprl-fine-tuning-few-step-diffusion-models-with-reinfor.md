Reinforcement learning objectives for diffusion models are reformulated for few-step samplers through x-space transformation, gradient decomposition, and anchor adaptation, yielding modular few-step variants of several methods and showing reward-gradient methods excel with differentiable rewards while DiffusionNFT-style methods achieve high rewards in fewer iterations.
