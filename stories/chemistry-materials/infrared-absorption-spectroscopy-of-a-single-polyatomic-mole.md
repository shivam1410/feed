Researchers achieved single-molecule infrared absorption spectroscopy by detecting photon recoil from trapped polyatomic molecular ions. When a molecule absorbs infrared light, the recoil from photon absorption slightly changes the ion's motion—a signal detectable by monitoring the ion's position. This single-photon-sensitive measurement enables direct observation of molecular vibrational transitions in individual molecules without destroying them. The technique opens new possibilities for studying molecular structure, quantum dynamics, and potentially chemical reactions at the single-molecule level.
