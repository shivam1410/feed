Signal will now restrict screenshots by default, to stop AI agents from reading private chats. The change covers Android and iOS. It applies to agents built into iOS 27, and to Android versions where Signal can detect an AI assistant trying to read a chat. Signal admits the protection is incomplete. For users, it's a real but partial shield against AI assistants reading their messages.
