Agents-A1, a 35-billion-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model, achieves performance equivalent to trillion-parameter systems through long-horizon trajectory scaling and heterogeneous agent ability scaling. Training combined supervised fine-tuning, domain-level teacher models, and multi-teacher distillation across multiple stages. This efficiency breakthrough demonstrates how strategic architectural choices and sophisticated training techniques can dramatically compress model scale while maintaining output quality.
