Research addresses lead carboxylates passivation in meter-scale perovskite solar modules, advancing scaling from laboratory prototypes toward commercial viability. Perovskites offer promising photovoltaic efficiency but require optimization for durability and manufacturing at scale. This work focuses on passivation strategies to improve module performance and stability. Reaching meter-scale production represents a critical step in transitioning perovskite solar technology from research demonstrations to practical renewable energy deployment.
