CardioState-JEPA develops a unified cardiac representation by learning from three physiological signal types: ECG, PPG, and PCG. The system uses cross-modal delay alignment to predict masked physiological states, learning how different cardiac signals relate. Training on multiple modalities simultaneously improves classification performance across all three signal types. This multi-modal approach reflects cardiac physiology's complexity, leveraging complementary information from different measurements. The technique demonstrates that integrating diverse physiological data sources creates richer clinical representations, potentially improving cardiac disease detection and diagnosis accuracy.
