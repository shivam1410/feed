Ontologies—structured representations of classes, properties, and relationships in a domain—are resurging in AI agent design to provide logical guardrails for otherwise probabilistic LLMs. UC Berkeley professor Frank Coyle argues reliable agents need this structured knowledge foundation. Neo4j CEO Emil Eifrem describes three ontology types: business-facing (organizational concepts), technical (enterprise metadata and data assets), and execution trace ontologies for agentic systems. Ontologies trace to Aristotle and appear throughout AI history; their revival reflects recognition that combining LLM probabilistic reasoning with explicit logical constraints enables smarter substrates for scaling agents reliably. Agents coordinating across enterprise systems need structured guardrails beyond statistical inference.
