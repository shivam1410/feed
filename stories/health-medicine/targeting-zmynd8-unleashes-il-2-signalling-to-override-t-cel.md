The protein ZMYND8 enforces CD8+ T cell exhaustion by suppressing IL-2R–STAT5 signaling. Deleting ZMYND8 restores IL-2 signaling, boosting effector-like T cell states and markedly enhancing both antiviral and antitumor immunity. This offers a potential therapeutic target to reverse immune exhaustion in cancer and chronic viral infections by reactivating critical immune pathways.
