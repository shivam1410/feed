A clinically annotated organoid resource from 256 cancer patients across five cancer types reveals genetic dependencies controlling cell survival. Each organoid pairs with matched patient tumor genomic, transcriptomic, and epigenomic data, creating multi-layered molecular maps. Researchers identified subtype-specific genetic vulnerabilities and discovered how cancers evolve under treatment pressure. Organoid-tumor correlations validate that lab models capture real patient disease mechanisms, bridging mechanistic research with clinical data and accelerating personalized cancer medicine development.
