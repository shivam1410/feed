NASA's Juno spacecraft observed relativistic electrons being accelerated at Jupiter's bow shock—the boundary where solar wind meets the planet's magnetic field. These observations revealed a universal scaling law directly relating shock size to the maximum energy cosmic rays achieve. This fundamental relationship applies across planets and throughout the cosmos, unifying understanding of particle acceleration mechanisms operating in diverse space environments and predicting maximum energies.
