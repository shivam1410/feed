PARTS is a reinforcement learning framework that refines pretrained robot policies by targeting failure-prone subtasks in long-horizon manipulation. Rather than collecting full-task demonstrations, PARTS uses agent-generated verifiers to provide local rewards during real-world RL rollouts, enabling efficient practice at bottlenecks. On bimanual manipulation, success improved from 32% to 61%, and on single-arm tasks from 50% to 95%, using only tens of minutes of real-world rollouts per task.
