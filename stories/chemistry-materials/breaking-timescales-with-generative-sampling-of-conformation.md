A new generative framework reconstructs rare biomolecular transition pathways efficiently by bypassing traditional computational bottlenecks. Rather than requiring predefined reaction coordinates or exhaustive brute-force sampling, this committor-guided method reveals how biomolecules transition between states, including underlying thermodynamic and kinetic details. The approach achieves this at acceptable computational cost. This breakthrough accelerates molecular simulation, enabling researchers to model conformational changes crucial for protein function, drug binding, and enzyme catalysis without years of supercomputing time.
