AuditFlow, a graph-grounded multi-agent framework, achieves 82.09% accuracy in automated financial audit verification—outperforming previous methods by nearly 15 percentage points. The system combines symbolic reasoning with AI agents to validate complex financial reports against US-GAAP taxonomy and XBRL data. Removing deterministic verification dropped accuracy to 17.91%, proving structured logic remains essential: AI alone cannot reliably replace systematic audit checks.
