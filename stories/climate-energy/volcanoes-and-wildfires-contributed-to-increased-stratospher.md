Satellite observations and climate simulations reveal that moderate volcanic eruptions and extreme wildfires have increased stratospheric water vapor since 2005. Aerosol effects from these events explain approximately one-third of observed stratospheric humidity trends—a contribution comparable to surface warming's impact. This identifies previously underappreciated drivers of atmospheric water-vapor changes with implications for climate modeling and prediction.
