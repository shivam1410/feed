European countries including Spain and France are experiencing record-breaking wildfires and scientists predict they will develop fire regimes increasingly similar to California's as climate change promotes hot, dry conditions. This shift represents a fundamental change in European fire patterns, challenging fire management infrastructure designed for historical frequencies.
