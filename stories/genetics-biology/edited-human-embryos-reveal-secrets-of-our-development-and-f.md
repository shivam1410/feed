As scientists advance genome-editing technology capable of modifying human embryos to correct genetic diseases, leading researchers urgently call for comprehensive ethical frameworks to establish clear boundaries. While embryo editing offers therapeutic potential, it raises concerns about unintended consequences, equity, and societal implications. The pace of scientific progress is outstripping ethical and policy discussions, creating pressure for immediate engagement. Researchers emphasize ethical deliberations must occur across scientific, philosophical, policymaking, and public stakeholder communities before widespread embryo editing becomes feasible.
