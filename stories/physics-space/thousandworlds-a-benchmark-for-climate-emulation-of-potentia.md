Searching for life on distant exoplanets requires understanding their climates—the same molecule signals life on one planet but abiotic chemistry on another. Climate models provide this understanding but demand millions of computational core-hours per simulation, creating a bottleneck. ThousandWorlds introduces a machine-learning benchmark containing approximately 1,800 climate simulations from five different global climate models, mapping eight planetary parameters to three-dimensional atmospheric fields including temperature, humidity, winds, clouds, and radiation. The dataset includes three nested subsets of increasing difficulty, from single-model regression to multi-model prediction with missing data. Gaussian process methods achieved best performance, offering a new direction for accelerating climate modeling.
