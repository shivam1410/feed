TreeSeeker is an inference-time framework that improves performance on complex reasoning tasks through structured tree search, using branch-and-return control to balance exploration and exploitation. The systematic trial-and-error process occurs after training without requiring model retraining. By exploring a tree of possibilities and deepening promising branches, TreeSeeker generates better solutions than single-pass generation. This matters because many hard reasoning problems require searching through possibilities rather than direct generation. The approach is broadly applicable: improving reasoning without changing the underlying model.
