Chamaileon is a framework for protein binder design addressing limitations by handling multi-target and multi-state binding requirements simultaneously. The method uses In-Context Complex Co-Design (I3CD) for context-aware sequence-structure co-modeling and employs Mixture-of-Paths Sampling (MoPS) during inference to optimize sequences across diverse conformational landscapes while alleviating scarcity of multi-conformational training data. Evaluated on the newly constructed CROSS benchmark, Chamaileon effectively generates protein sequences adaptable to multiple targets and conformational states. This advances function-oriented protein design by enabling researchers to engineer binders working across multiple biological contexts simultaneously, unlocking more sophisticated therapeutic applications.
