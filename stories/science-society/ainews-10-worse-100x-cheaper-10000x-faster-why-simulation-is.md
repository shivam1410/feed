Since 2022, AI pipeline components have progressively flipped from human-made to model-made: first reward signals (InstructGPT), then rubrics (Constitutional AI), then data, then entire workflows. Synthetic replacements are 10% less capable but 100× cheaper and 10,000× faster. Each transition began at a frontier lab where synthetic versions became load-bearing, then productionized elsewhere. This accelerating shift suggests future AI systems will be trained almost entirely on model-generated outputs rather than human labor, fundamentally reshaping how intelligence is produced at scale and where value concentrates.
