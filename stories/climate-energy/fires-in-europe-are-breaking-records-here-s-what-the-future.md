Europe is experiencing record-breaking fire events, with scientists warning that Spain, France, and other countries face future fire regimes resembling California's pattern. Climate change is creating hotter, drier conditions favorable for wildfires. As global temperatures rise and drought intensifies across southern Europe, fire risk will escalate, fundamentally altering fire ecology and requiring adaptation in fire management strategies.
