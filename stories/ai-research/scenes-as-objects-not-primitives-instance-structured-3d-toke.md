Researchers developed a feed-forward framework that decomposes 3D scenes into instance-structured token groups directly from multi-view images without requiring 3D annotations. This approach enables object-level reconstruction, segmentation, and manipulation from standard 2D image inputs. By treating scene objects as distinct tokens rather than primitive geometric shapes, the method simplifies 3D scene understanding. This eliminates the need for expensive 3D ground-truth data, making 3D scene understanding more practical for applications requiring robotic manipulation, scene editing, and spatial reasoning.
