As artificial general intelligence (AGI) approaches, researchers examine potential pathways to artificial superintelligence (ASI)—systems exceeding human-level capabilities across all domains. The paper argues AGI development may inevitably lead to superintelligence through multiple possible mechanisms and cascading improvements, representing a transformative societal change with profound implications for governance, ethics, and global coordination. The work emphasizes that preparation for ASI requires interdisciplinary collaboration spanning technology, policy, social science, and ethics. Understanding these pathways is critical for developing safeguards and policies before superintelligent systems emerge.
