DeepMind is funding 16 organizations across the Asia-Pacific region to deploy advanced AI on environmental problems. The funded projects tackle climate change, agriculture, and biodiversity conservation. By putting frontier AI directly to work on real-world challenges in this region, the initiative tests whether machine learning can accelerate solutions to nature's biggest problems and demonstrate AI's potential for good.
