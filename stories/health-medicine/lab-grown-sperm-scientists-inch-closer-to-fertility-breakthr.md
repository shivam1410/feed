Scientists have grown immature human sperm cells from stem cells, nurturing them on mouse kidney tissue. This lab-based reproduction marks a major step toward artificial sperm production. The breakthrough could eventually help infertile men, bypass genetic defects, and reduce reliance on donor sperm for assisted reproduction.
