A now-patched flaw in Apple's audio service let apps on iPhones and iPads record audio even after the user tapped "Don't Allow" on the microphone prompt. The first security updates with the fix came out on August 17. Neither Apple nor the two researchers who found the flaw report that it was used in attacks. Nik Tsytsarkin of Meta Red Team X published the technical details last week. Mustafa
