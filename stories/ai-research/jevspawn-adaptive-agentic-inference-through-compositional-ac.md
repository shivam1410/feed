JevSpawn extends fast decision models to handle dynamic tasks where actions must be discovered from natural language. It uses parallel action spawning coupled with feedback-driven selection and representation revision to adapt as tasks unfold. This matters because Jev-style models need action choices specified upfront, but real-world autonomous tasks require discovering and adapting actions on the fly.
