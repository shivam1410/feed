EpiFlow presents methods to improve disease forecasting by incorporating wastewater viral loads (WVL), which signal disease burden earlier than clinical reporting. The framework characterizes WVL predictability using entropy measures, analyzes its leading-indicator behavior through causality tests accounting for temporal dynamics, and handles reporting delays through simulations. Applied to COVID-19 hospital admissions forecasting across Virginia and its health regions, incorporating WVL improved prediction accuracy compared to baseline models using only traditional burden indicators. During high-prevalence periods, multiple indicators track disease effectively, but WVL reliability grows as reporting fatigue and low prevalence reduce clinical data quality. Early wastewater signals enable faster public health response.
