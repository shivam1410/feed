An AI builder turns a scientific task description into a complete working lab: integrated models, tools, data, and a controller all behind one interface. Documented 28 delivered systems across seven domains from molecular and genomic prediction to clinical decision support. All passed validation on held-out tests. Ten contain trained models; others assemble search, analysis, and language-model workflows. This matters because science needs working systems delivered and verified, not prototypes.
