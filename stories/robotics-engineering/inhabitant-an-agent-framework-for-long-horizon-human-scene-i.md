Inhabitant is an agent framework where a language model plans long human-scene interactions. It specifies motions spatially, measures how far results drift, and revises its control. It reaches high task completion on the Inhabit benchmark. Checking and correcting its own movement is what lets it handle long tasks. That makes virtual characters more reliable in complex spaces.
