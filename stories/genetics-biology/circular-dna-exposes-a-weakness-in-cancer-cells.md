Cancer cells harbor circular DNA outside the nucleus that represents both a threat and an opportunity. These repetitive sequences break easily, making them vulnerable to therapy, though they may also help tumors adapt and evolve. This matters because it reveals a potential weakness to exploit while also suggesting a mechanism that might make cancers harder to treat.
