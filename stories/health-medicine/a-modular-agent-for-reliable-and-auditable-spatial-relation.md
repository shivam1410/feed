A modular medical AI agent outperforms end-to-end vision-language models at verifying spatial relationships in CT scans by decomposing the task into three steps: parsing the query, anatomically localizing relevant structures, and applying geometric rules. This structured approach leverages domain knowledge and logical reasoning rather than relying solely on learned visual patterns, improving accuracy and reliability in clinical spatial reasoning tasks.
