Google DeepMind used agentic AI to formally verify key cryptographic guarantees of the Signal protocol. The proof-of-concept took just four weeks. The team used a new AI framework called AlphaProof Code. The work independently checks cryptography that protects huge volumes of messages on Signal, Google Messages and WhatsApp. It also shows DeepMind's formal verification capabilities.
