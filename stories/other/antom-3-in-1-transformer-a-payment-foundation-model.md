Ant International built a payment model that can spot risky transactions. It has about 7 billion parameters, and it learned from internal payment data first. When reused on a different business, pretraining lifted the target metric by 34.5 percent. With only a quarter of the labels, it beat a model trained from scratch on all its labels on several metrics. In a four-month live test on its payment platform, the overall loss rate fell 37.88 percent against the older XGB-Online system.
