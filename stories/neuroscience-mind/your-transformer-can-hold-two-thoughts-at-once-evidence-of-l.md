When input text streams are linearly combined in a Transformer, the model outputs a superposition of the individual token distributions. This happens at the arithmetic level, not as an emergent property. Fine-tuning can restore or reduce this linearity. A guided decoding procedure can disentangle superposed outputs to generate two coherent continuations from a single forward pass. This matters because it reveals hidden structure in how neural networks reason.
