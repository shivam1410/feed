A Google DeepMind scientist explained how AI weather models can predict cyclones earlier than traditional forecasting methods. These AI systems process vast amounts of real-time weather data far faster than humans can, spotting patterns and trends that traditional models miss. By providing cyclone warnings hours or days earlier, the technology gives communities crucial extra time to evacuate residents and prepare emergency response.
