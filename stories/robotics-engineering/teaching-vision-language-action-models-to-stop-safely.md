Robots can be taught to stop a forbidden task partway through, and a method called SAFI does this. Older policies could still finish a suppressed task after taking over midway. On the evaluated LIBERO tasks, SAFI cut target success after takeover to 1.3 percent. It recorded no physical incidents or action saturation. It matters because robots need a safe response when they must stop mid-task.
