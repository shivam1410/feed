Even huge black holes can't seem to explain dark matter. The idea was that they could supply the invisible pull holding galaxies together. If they did, they would leave a bigger imprint on cosmic radiation. That imprint is the check, and they don't pass it. It matters because it narrows the search for what dark matter really is.
